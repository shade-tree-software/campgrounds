Weather Finder

Searches the next {{ forecast_days }} days of forecast across the campground database for somewhere with the weather you're after — a comfortable range, or simply cooler (or warmer) than it's going to be at home that same day. Narrow it to the dates you're actually free, or leave them blank for the whole {{ forecast_days }} days.

{# `hidden` here mirrors the default mode (range), so the fields that mode doesn't use aren't painted before syncMode() runs and hides them. #}
{# step is measured from `min`, NOT from zero — with min=5 step=10 the valid values were 5, 15 ... 245, 255, so the field rejected its own default of 250 and offered 245/255 instead. step=5 off a min of 5 makes every multiple of 5 valid, including 250 and the 500 cap. #}
{# One field holding both ends of the range, because it's one question. Bounds are the forecast horizon (server-supplied) — there's nothing to search outside it. Leaving them empty searches the whole horizon. #}
