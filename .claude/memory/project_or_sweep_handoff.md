1. **park_type `dnr` = BLM (federal) in OR, NOT a state DNR** — OR has no state DNR. Huge BLM inventory: Roseburg district N.Umpqua (Susan Creek/Millpond/Cavitt Creek/Scaredman/Lone Pine/Sharps Creek), Steens/Frenchglen (Page Springs/Fish Lake/South Steens/Jackman/Chukar), Leslie Gulch/Slocum Creek, Gerber Reservoir, Prineville district (Chimney Rock/Trout Creek/Macks Canyon), Coos Bay district (Loon Lake/Sixes River/Edson Creek/Smith River Falls/Vincent Creek). **BUT verify per entry** — a few `dnr`-tags turned out Oregon Dept of Forestry state-forest (state) or county (local).
2. **Oregon Dept of Forestry state forests = `state`** (Tillamook/Clatsop/Santiam SF: Gales Creek, Jones Creek, Nehalem Falls, Henry Rierson Spruce Run, Elk Creek). BUT **Nestucca River Recreation Area (Dovre/Fan Creek/Alder Glen) = BLM federal, NOT ODF** — my add instructions wrongly listed Dovre/Fan Creek under ODF; agents correctly overrode to BLM. Alsea/Blodgett-area (Big Elk) = Siuslaw NF.
3. **Federal DOMINATES** (359/594) — vast USFS (Deschutes/Willamette/Umpqua/Mt Hood/Rogue River-Siskiyou/Fremont-Winema/Ochoco/Malheur/Umatilla/Wallowa-Whitman/Siuslaw NFs), BLM, NPS (Crater Lake Mazama Village), USACE (Cottage Grove/Dorena/Fall Creek/Columbia John Day: LePage/Rufus). NOTE: the `stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=park.profile&parkId=<N>` profile IDs the add agents wrote are sometimes best-guess/wrong — the ReserveAmerica deep link in the same `website` field is the reliable one.
