When a booking engine (Campspot etc.) offers both a "Van" and a "Motorhome" rig type, EKKO books
as a Motorhome, and the price gate uses the cheapest site type that lists Motorhome. A cheaper
van-only site does not rescue a park (1473 Cayuga Wine Trail dropped on this, $65 > $50).

**Why:** AWH once booked a "van" site at Fort DeSoto (FL); on arrival they were told EKKO could
not stay there even though it fit the site.

**How to apply:** only when AWH is certain the operator doesn't care may a van type count. A park
with NO motorhome type at all (1518 Rancho Pines) is a question for AWH, not an automatic drop.
Relates to [[feedback-price-gate-proximity-exceptions]].
