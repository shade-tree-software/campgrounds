AWH 2026-11-10, shown twelve held Missouri upgrade leads with distances (Pulltite, both Ruark Bluffs, Wheatland, Thomas Hill and others; sites 10-45 m from the water): "Keep the Missouri waterfront values as is." Then, when I recorded that as "a minority of shore sites does not raise a value": "if there are a few sites that are clearly waterfront, you can count the campground as waterfront with a caveat."

**Why:** the number of sites is not the test; whether they are clearly on the water is.

Then: "10-35 m across open grass counts as clearly waterfront. You can upgrade those ones." (Ruark Bluff East/West, Pulltite, Ranacker, Shawnee Trail raised.)

Then AWH pointed to the About panel on the campground map as the definitive wording: on the water = at least some sites within about 50 m with nothing between but open ground (no road, parking lot, day-use area or tree screen); a thin line of trees, a slope or a road = a view. Six more leads changed under it.

**How to apply:** a few sites within about 50 m of the water across open ground, nothing between, seen on imagery = upgrade without asking, with the note saying how many and which sites. Farther back, screened, or unclear stays at the stored value and is logged in `audit/verify/waterfront_leads.md`.
