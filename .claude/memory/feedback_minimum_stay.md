A campground that requires a multi-night minimum on every stay (American Way RV Park, Mineral Wells WV: "$50 daily, 4 day minimum") does not count as a nightly campground and is skipped (AWH 2026-09-30: "4-night min does not count").

**Why:** EKKO trips move most days; a park that won't sell one night is a weekly/monthly park wearing a daily rate.

**How to apply:** skip with kind_of_no "not nightly (N-night minimum)" when the minimum applies to all stays. Weekend 2-night and holiday 3-4-night minimums (River Ridge, Groves' 2-night non-event minimum) are normal and do not disqualify. Related: [[feedback_exclude_seasonal_residential]].
