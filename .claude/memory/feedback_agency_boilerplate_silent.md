Under the 2026-10-22 rig-length-from-reviews rule (docs/campground-curation.md), agency boilerplate counts as **silent** (keep), not vague (AWH 2026-10-22). The vague tier, which needs a recent review of a 23-ft+ rig, applies only when the agency singles out THIS campground ("small trailers only", "limited access for trailers and RV's").

**Why:** Michigan DNR's "sites for tent and small trailer use" is on 99 of its 115 state forest campground pages (14 say "small and large trailer"; "small" is never defined in feet). Read as vague, it would have dropped most MI state forest campgrounds, because rustic camps rarely have recent rig-size reviews. Culhane Lake 420 is the case that raised it.

**How to apply:** before calling agency wording vague, count how many of that agency's pages carry it (same method as [[reference-agency-policy-research]]). Explicit limits (4WD, high clearance, "motorhomes not recommended") still drop.
