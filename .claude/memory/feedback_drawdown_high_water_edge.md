At a reservoir, measure waterfront distance to the beach/high-water edge (the vegetation/sand line), never to the drawn-down waterline. An open unused apron within ~50 m of that edge earns lakefront. A treeline, road, day use or bluff in between still makes it a view (AWH 2026-11-22: raised Angostura Hat Creek 15759 to lakefront, sites 96E/98E/100E/101E named in the note; Sheps South 15763 followed on the same ruling).

**Bank test (AWH 2026-11-23):** on the water only if a camper can easily reach the water and launch a kayak. Pathfinder Sage/Weiss pads 1-2 m up a gentle bank (to ~40%) = walkable, lakefront; Diabase 13584 pads 2-3.7 m up a ~30 deg bank = too high and steep, kept lakeview (also Weiss's west-shore shelter). Written into the curation doc's bluff/bench clause. Creekside is the exception: an easy walk down is enough (Caledonia 655 sites 131/133, 2.2-2.75 m over 21-28%). A booking-site field that contradicts the site's own photos and other fields (Caledonia 133's '7 ft overhead clearance') is an error, not a limit (AWH 2026-11-23).

**Why:** the kit lessons p3-551 (Pathfinder) and p3-572 had drifted from docs/campground-curation.md and made verifiers downgrade on drawdown. AWH sided with the curation doc.

**How to apply:** when a kit lesson contradicts the curation doc, the doc wins. Fix the lesson (done in kit/sd and kit/wy lessons.txt) instead of following it. Name the qualifying sites and the drawdown caveat in the note. Related: [[feedback_waterfront_unsure_downgrade]], [[feedback_missouri_waterfront_declined]].
