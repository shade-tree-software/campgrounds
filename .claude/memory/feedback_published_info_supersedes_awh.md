If the campground's or agency's own website clearly publishes information that contradicts older
--AWH note text, I may rewrite the note and remove the outdated part — no need to ask (first
applied to Red Haw IA, 2026-10-20: site count, booking cutoff, FCFS option).

**Why:** AWH's notes are firsthand but dated; the operator's current published facts are the
better record once they clearly disagree.

**How to apply:** only a clear statement on the operator/agency's own page supersedes — never
silence, an aggregator, or two official pages that disagree with each other. Keep the rest of the
--AWH text verbatim; put the new facts in my own --Claude-signed text with the source date. In
phase 7 a verifier lists each removed phrase + source quote in `awh_superseded` and apply.py then
allows the rewrite. Refines [[feedback-attribute-note-edits]] and [[feedback-clean-up-notes]].
