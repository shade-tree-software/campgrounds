A campground that turns up mid-pass and isn't in the database (e.g. Riverbrook NH, sold through Baker River's Campspot listing; Cross Creek, Cairo GA) gets vetted and added by me under docs/campground-curation.md: inclusion criteria, the waterfront gate with a satellite look, and the price gate. It's not a question for AWH.

**Why:** AWH 2026-10-16: "Why do you need my input on Riverbrook and Cross Creek? Do we need to update or clarify our inclusion criteria so that you can make the decision yourself?" The criteria already decide these. Parking them as "add?" left them unanswered for days.

**How to apply:** ask AWH only when the criteria really don't cover the case (a named judgement call in the doc, or a new kind of place). Record the lead's outcome either way (added with evidence, or the kind of no). Related: [[feedback-add-with-caveat-not-withhold]].
