EKKO (2023 Winnebago EKKO 22A on a Ford Transit chassis) is about 7 ft 2.5-3 in wide at the body (dealer specs) and an estimated ~8 ft 1 in (~97 in) mirror to mirror (Ford's Transit figure; not measured). AWH 2026-10-28: Browns Park's Swinging Bridge (8 ft 6 in at its narrowest, 20-ton limit) is fine with the mirrors folded.

**How to apply:** a width restriction of ~8 ft 6 in or more passes; something narrower than ~8 ft 1 in needs the mirrors folded, and under ~7 ft 4 in excludes. Related: [[user_ekko_height]], [[user_ekko_weight]], [[user_ekko_no_4wd]].
