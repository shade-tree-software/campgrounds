AWH 2026-10-21: "EKKO is under 10.5 feet tall."

**How to apply:** a tunnel, underpass or canopy limit of 11 ft or more on a campground's access road is not a problem; keep the entry. Harpers Ferry Campground (815, Bakerton Rd's 11-ft one-way tunnel) was kept on this. Flag a posted clearance only when it is under ~10.5 ft.
