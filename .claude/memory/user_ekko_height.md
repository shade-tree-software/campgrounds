AWH 2026-10-21: "EKKO is under 10.5 feet tall."

**How to apply:** a tunnel, underpass or canopy limit of 11 ft or more on a campground's access road is not a problem; keep the entry. Harpers Ferry Campground (815, Bakerton Rd's 11-ft one-way tunnel) was kept on this. Flag a posted clearance only when it is under ~10.5 ft. Related: [[user_ekko_weight]], [[user_ekko_no_4wd]], [[feedback_min_site_length]].

**AWH 2026-11-10:** "Drop anything with less than 10.5 feet height clearance." That includes a posted 10 ft on a site's own booking page even when imagery shows an open lot (Bums Inn 15203, Cove Road Landing B dropped); I had kept those as a possible page default. Drop and report, don't ask.
