EKKO weighs about **9,500 lb empty** and has a **gross vehicle weight rating of 11,000 lb** (AWH 2026-09-29). Length is 23 ft ([[feedback_min_site_length]]).

**How to apply:** a posted 5-ton (10,000 lb) bridge or road limit excludes a campground, since EKKO travels loaded above it. Frozen Head's Big Cove (TN) was excluded on exactly that ([[project_remaining_work_map]]). A limit of 6 tons (12,000 lb) or more is a pass.
