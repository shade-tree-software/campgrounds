The private-gap standards stand as they are (AWH 2026-10-02, after Wapocoma prompted a "what are we missing" check). RV Life + Good Sam are the sources; a 3* or $$$ RV Life row is screened out by design, and its Good Sam twin goes with it.

**Why:** a private campground listed on neither RV Life nor Good Sam is itself a red flag. An OSM-only park is fine ONLY if it is active, has good Google reviews, a valid phone and published prices - AWH expects that to be a small percentage, so it is not worth a systematic pass.

**How to apply:** don't build an OSM third source into private_gap_list.py or back-fill done states. If an OSM-only private park comes up ad hoc, judge it on that bar. Measured for context: WV had 89 named OSM campsites on neither directory, ~15-20 plausibly RV campgrounds (most were scout subcamps, trail sites, horse/church camps); the public ones among them (Paden City, Lewis County, Brooks Hill) had already been judged and excluded by the local pass. Wapocoma itself is on RV Life as "Wapacoma" (3*, $$$) - screened, not missed. Related: [[project-private-gap]].
