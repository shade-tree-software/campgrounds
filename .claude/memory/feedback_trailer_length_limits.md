When an agency posts a trailer length limit (e.g. FishMT's "Trailer Length is 20ft" on Montana
fishing access sites), treat it as applying to motorhomes as well, unless the source says
otherwise. A limit under ~23 ft therefore excludes EKKO under [[feedback-min-site-length]].

**Why:** AWH 2026-10-20: "Lots of campgrounds have trailer length limits ... I have never been able
to figure out if that applies to motorhomes. In the absence of any additional information, we
should probably assume that it applies to motorhomes as well."

**How to apply:** an entry whose only length evidence is a sub-23-ft trailer limit is a criteria
failure. I may drop it myself and report which ones (the decision is made). A source that
distinguishes motorhomes, or gives a separate RV/vehicle length, overrides it. First applied to
the Montana FAS entries, 2026-10-20.
