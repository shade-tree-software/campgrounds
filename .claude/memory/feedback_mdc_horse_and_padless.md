AWH 2026-11-09, answering three phase-7 asks:

- **A horse camp that can't be told apart from the general camp means drop.** Castor River CA 13813: MDC's map labelled the trailhead camp "Horse Camping Area (Trail Head)", and nothing clearly separated it from a general camp. The area's other camp, Trace Creek (15639), stays.
- **No pad is needed.** Coldwater Access 14006: camping along a dirt interior track with no gravel pad was kept, with a caveat in the note (park on the field edge; soft after rain).
- **A general campground with an OHV side stays, with a caveat.** Cobb Ridge 1922: most of its visitors ride. The note now warns about engine noise and trail traffic. See [[feedback_offroad_parks_out_of_scope]].

**Why:** the curation doc excludes horse camps, but a mixed trailhead camp is only out when it is plainly the horse camp. An agency-designated roadside spot is still designated camping.

**How to apply:** in a brief, ask the verifier to say whether a horse-labelled camp is physically distinct from a general one. Without a clear distinction it is a drop. Don't hold padless designated roadside camping as a question; write the caveat.

**Split check (2026-11-09):** before splitting, search the DB for an existing entry within ~1 km of the split point. Deer Ridge already had 13743 on the north-tract camp, and a duplicate split (15644) had to be removed. Related: [[feedback_split_multi_campground_parks]].
