AWH 2026-11-11 ("make it so", after asking for a better name than "coastal woods"): the value is now **`coastal walk`** - an easy walk (up to about a mile) to the shore of an ocean, a Great Lake or a large saltwater sound/bay, with or without a view.

**Why:** "coastal woods" was coined as the non-dune alternative to `coastal dunes` and only described one setting. AWH's three examples: Hammonasset Beach CT (open grass, ~1,000 ft behind a small dune on Long Island Sound - big water that "looks and feels different" from an inland lake), Cape Henlopen DE (woods, a mile on trails), Twelvemile Beach MI (woods, filtered views).

**How to apply:** any setting counts; the park's own day-use beach, parking and park roads may lie between; a highway, town, rail line or residential strip still rules it out; never inland lakes. `coastal dunes` keeps its stricter rule (the campground's own dune field, no day-use complex between). Still excluded from the map's "On the water" filter; weather_finder ranks it tier 1; map colour unchanged (#6b8e23). All 100 entries renamed and Hammonasset 212 set to it.
