AWH 2026-10-30, two rulings the same day:
- Dropped Sugar Mill RV Park (4648, Thomasville GA, Good Sam Park at $40, passing every written gate): "Drop Sugar Mill due to very poor Google reviews."
- Kept Blackwater Ranch (753, Hipcamp, $52.79 with Hipcamp's fee, gateway 46 km) after AWH found it at 5 stars from 48 Google reviews, then: "For campgrounds that appear to be borderline on price like blackwater, you can use Google reviews as a tiebreaker."

**Why:** a park that passes the written gates can still be a bad place to pull in for the night, and one a little over the line can still be worth it; Google's review stream is where both show.

**How to apply:**
- When vetting or verifying a private/hipcamp park, give its Google Maps rating and review count alongside the gate facts.
- Borderline price (a few dollars over US$50, or just past the 40 km gateway radius): I may decide it myself on the reviews - strong (about 4.7+ on a few dozen reviews) keeps it, mediocre or poor drops it. Report which way and why. No exact cut-off has been set; Blackwater (5.0/48, $2.79 over) is the worked keep.
- A markedly poor rating on a park that otherwise passes: flag it as a drop candidate for AWH with what the reviews complain of.
