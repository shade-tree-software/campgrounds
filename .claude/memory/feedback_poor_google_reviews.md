AWH 2026-10-30 dropped Sugar Mill RV Park (4648, Thomasville GA, Good Sam Park at $40, passing every written gate) with "Drop Sugar Mill due to very poor Google reviews." The verifier's flag had been only the residential question; AWH looked up the reviews themself.

**Why:** a park that passes price, presence and long-stay tests can still be a bad place to pull in for the night, and Google's review stream is where that shows.

**How to apply:** when vetting or verifying a private park, give its Google Maps rating and review count alongside the gate facts. Flag a markedly poor rating (with what the reviews complain of) as a drop candidate for AWH rather than dropping it myself - no numeric line has been set. Relates to [[feedback-require-live-web-presence]] and [[project-phase7-verification]].
