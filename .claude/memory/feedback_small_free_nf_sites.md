AWH 2026-11-12 (Six Mile Lake 5495, Chippewa NF): "small free primitive national forest campgrounds or dispersed sites can stay in with very little evidence, such as camper reports, as long as the evidence doesn't suggest that campers have found the site closed or locked up. just add a caveat so users know to call the forest service before setting out."

**Why:** forests drop small free sites from their web pages without closing them; the absence of an FS page is not evidence of closure.

**How to apply:** keep such an entry with a note line telling users to call the forest before setting out (forest office number in `phone`). A camper report of a padlock, gate or overgrown sites (Tamarack Point 5498) is the opposite signal: those AWH dropped, with West Seelye Bay 5504. Paid or larger FS campgrounds still need the usual sources. Related: [[feedback-campground-vetting-discipline]], [[feedback-add-with-caveat-not-withhold]].
