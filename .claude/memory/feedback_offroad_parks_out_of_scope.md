Off-road parks are not in scope (AWH 2026-10-02, settling the AL private-gap holds Boggs & Boulders and Northwest Alabama Mountain Ride). Skip them with kind_of_no "out of scope (off-road park)"; don't hold them for AWH.

**Why:** AWH ruled the question rather than per-park; the campground exists for the riders, not as a transient RV stop.

**How to apply:** applies to private off-road parks found by the private gap ([[project-private-gap]]). An ordinary campground that merely sits near ATV trails is judged normally. Existing DB entries of this kind (Mud Pit ATV Resort 1211, Busco Beach 4307, Sabine ATV Park 7768, Stony Lonesome OHV Park 13389, county OHV parks, federal OHV campgrounds) were NOT removed — ask before treating the rule as retroactive. Rule is in docs/campground-curation.md "Never add".
