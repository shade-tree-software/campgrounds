Off-road parks are not in scope (AWH 2026-10-02, settling the AL private-gap holds Boggs & Boulders and Northwest Alabama Mountain Ride). Skip them with kind_of_no "out of scope (off-road park)"; don't hold them for AWH.

**Why:** AWH ruled the question rather than per-park; the campground exists for the riders, not as a transient RV stop.

**How to apply:** applies to every owner (AWH 2026-10-02, after seeing the list): off-road parks, OHV areas and OHV staging/trailhead campgrounds are out; a general campground that also has an OHV side stays. 58 existing entries removed that day (audit/offroad_scope/decisions.jsonl has each reason, plus the 14 borderline ones kept as general, e.g. St. Joe SP, Mack Lake ORV, the Alberta PRAs, Lizzie and Charlie's). Rule is in docs/campground-curation.md "Never add". Related: [[project-private-gap]].

**Big Scrub 4731 (USFS Ocala NF) KEPT by AWH 2026-10-21.** It's a 46-site Forest Service campground that is "perennially popular with off-road vehicle enthusiasts", with a trailhead inside that gives riders campsite-to-trail access. So a full-service agency campground with its own OHV trailhead still counts as general. Only a camp that exists for the riders is out (the Wolf Den Run and St. John's Rock drops, 87 and 64).

**Mack Lake ORV 3756 (USFS Huron-Manistee) KEPT again by AWH 2026-10-25** when phase 7 re-raised it (FS: "primarily used by ORV trail riders"). It has 41 lakeside family sites, a swim area, and "ORVs may not be used within campgrounds", and the trailhead is listed as a separate area. It was already on the 2026-10-02 kept-as-general list (audit/offroad_scope/decisions.jsonl), so check that list before asking about an ORV-named campground.

**PA state forest ATV lots, AWH 2026-11-10:** "Drop sites that are specifically named as ATV parking." A reservable campsite inside a lot the agency names as ATV trail parking is out even though anyone may book it (Piney Mountain 15268, Big Flat 73, Buchanan CV013/SH035/SH032 dropped). I had kept five of these with a caveat and asked; don't ask again.

**AWH 2026-11-10:** a state forest campsite the agency places in a snowmobile parking area drops too (Sproul SS016), and so did SS017 beside a gas compressor station. Same day: a page giving a 25-ft vehicle limit beside shorter driveway/spur figures stays with a fit caveat (HY040, CR020).

**AWH 2026-11-10 (later):** campsites that are part of an ATV trail system drop even when not in a parking lot (Sproul WS011-013: "ATV tracks run right through... doesn't sound very appealing. Drop them."). A roadside site on a road ATVs share stays with the wording in the note (my reading; SS003 kept).
