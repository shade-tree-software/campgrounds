AWH 2026-11-12 (Sherin Memorial Park 5689): "Amperage and location of water are nice to know if they are readily available on a website, but we don't need to make a phone call for that sort of thing. Just knowing that electricity is available at the sites and water is available somewhere is enough to close out that campground for now as long as there's nothing else we're waiting on."

**Why:** calls cost AWH's attention; they are for facts that decide keep/drop, waterfront, or the basics (open, rate, season), not refinements.

**How to apply:** when building or pruning `audit/verify/phone_list.md`, don't list an entry whose only gaps are amperage, water-at-site vs. a spigot, or similar refinements; record the close in `curation_note`. Related: [[feedback-phone-call-source]], [[project-phase7-verification]].
