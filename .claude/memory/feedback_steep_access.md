AWH 2026-11-10, answering the PA state forest asks: "Drop sites with severe driveways and steep grass access." Also: "Sites that will fit EKKO and are listed for trailers can be kept even if they don't specifically mention RVs."

**Why:** EKKO is two-wheel drive ([[user_ekko_no_4wd]]) with a long rear overhang; I had kept these with caveats and asked each time.

**How to apply:** an agency grade of Severe on the driveway takes the site out; so does steep grass access (my working line, not AWH's number: about 8% or more on the elevation model, or agency wording such as "not recommended in wet conditions"). A gravel driveway graded Moderate stays with the measured grade in the note. Take the site out of a multi-site entry's count and name; drop the entry when no site is left; report, don't ask. Applied to 15198, 15208, 15332, 15215, and to sites inside 15200 and 15224. Related: [[user_ekko_height]], [[feedback_min_site_length]], [[feedback_offroad_parks_out_of_scope]].

**Same day:** walk-up-only sites with no stated procedure stay with a note to call the district office (Akersville 15225); an agency's own name replaces ours (Jonas, Kerkeslin). Rules are written into docs/campground-curation.md.

**NARROWED by AWH 2026-11-10 (later), after I asked about three Elk sites on measured grades:** "In general, no need to measure the grade or take a close look at the road surface. Keep anything unless the owner/operator/agency mentions an issue with the road, in which case you can use our rules to decide whether to either put a warning in the note or drop it." All three kept (15240, 15243, 15244). So: no 3DEP profiling of driveways or access roads, no road-surface research, no asks built on my own measurements. The rules above apply only once the agency or operator raises the issue (a Severe grade on the page counts as the agency raising it - confirmed by AWH 2026-11-10). The "about 8% on the elevation model" line is retired.
