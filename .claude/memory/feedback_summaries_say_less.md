Across a day of corrections to the trip day-summaries, every single one AWH
made was **cut this, it says nothing** — never "add more". The specific
offenders, in his words:

- "with nothing on the day but the drive" → just stop after the drive.
- "barely out of town" → "'Fireworks at Franklin Park in Purcellville' is good.
  'barely out of town' is unnecessary."
- "and no need to move the camper at all" → "is unnecessary."
- "I don't think it's necessary to draw attention to a bare day."
- "sometimes it's still too heavy on the stats."

**Why:** these are all commentary about the *record* or about the *absence* of
something, rather than about the day. They make a thin day conspicuous instead
of letting it be short, and they are what padding to a word count produces.

**How to apply:** when generating prose from data anywhere in this project,
write the facts and stop. Never set a minimum length — every floor I set
(25, 22, 8 words) went on to reject text he had already approved, and six words
can be a complete entry. Check generated text for: remarks on what the record
holds, glosses on a small distance or on not moving, and padding clauses added
to reach a length. Relates to [[project_travelogue_capture_gap]].
