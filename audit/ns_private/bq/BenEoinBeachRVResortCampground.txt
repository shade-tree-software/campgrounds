    Ben Eoin Beach is one of the oldest and most beautiful resorts in Cape Breton, NS. Founded in the early 1960’s by Malcolm S. and Shirley MacDonald, it has continued to be owned by their family and operated by the Manager Alexis MacDonald & Vice-President Walter MacDonald.

    Located on a peninsula on the beautiful Bras d'Or Lakes it has 30 amp services, pull thru sites, waterfront & beach front sites are available, each a with breathtaking view. The scenery and service is the best. There are Coin operated showers, washrooms, laundromat, wood, ice, camping supplies. Lovely beach swimming, a multi-purpose court, canoe and kayak rental. There are 58 overnight sites + 149 seasonal. It has boating facilities with an excellent boat launch available. The new dog park is a treat for the whole family! An extensive daily recreation program for the children, with Halloween in July and Christmas in August. The 6:00 pm hayride is a family favourite. There is something for the whole family. It is a secure facility with night security to ensure your quiet nights. There is the Ben Eoin Beach Snack Shak onsite for those day and night-time munchies.

    Sydney is only 20 miles away and is a central location for touring the Island. Baddeck, and the famous Alexander Bell Museum is an hour away. While with us, visit Ben Eoin Lakes Golf Course or the famed Fortress Louisbourg nearby. Watch the bald eagles soar overhead as you have your morning coffee. Customers can follow us on Facebook Ben Eoin Beach Campground Recreation and Ben Eoin Snak Shack .
