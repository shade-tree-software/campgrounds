Book Your Next Dream Camping Trip with a COANS Member


Campground Owners Association of Nova Scotia (“COANS”) members strive to offer you a premier selection of RV Parks and Campgrounds, each with a focus on the highest quality facilities and services. 
Whether you are just beginning to discover the special pleasures of Nova Scotia or are returning again to enjoy one of your favorite vacations, we invite you to explore our camping destinations in our beautiful Province.
Camping allows you to become part of the landscapes, seascapes, and culture of the area you choose to venture into, and Nova Scotia has it all!
Our members offer a wide variety of settings for your stay; do you prefer an oceanfront park or a secluded forest site? Looking for a very remote location, or perhaps a park offering you convenient access to our capital city of Halifax?
COANS Private Campground owners are dedicated to providing you with a safe, secure, high-quality camping experience.  All of our members are licensed as campground businesses – insuring that you will always be able to find a safe and comfortable location for a night, a week, or a season!

About Us
We’re Committed to Giving You the Adventure You Seek
What Drives Us

At the Campground Owners Association of Nova Scotia (COANS), our mission is to promote exceptional camping experiences throughout Nova Scotia.

We strive to support our member campgrounds by fostering a strong community, advocating for their interests, and showcasing the beauty and adventure our province has to offer to campers of all ages and interests.

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1982, Campground Owners Association of Nova Scotia (COANS) has been at the forefront of enhancing Nova Scotia’s camping industry for over four decades.

Our association represents privately owned campgrounds and RV parks across the province, working tirelessly to ensure high standards of quality and service.

Over the years, COANS has grown into a trusted resource for campground owners and campers, building lasting partnerships and a vibrant community committed to outdoor adventure and excellence.

Why Choose a COANS Campground?
EXPERIENCE TOTAL REST AND RELAXATION THAT YOU DESERVE
Enjoy exceptional service with friendly and knowledgeable owners and pristine facilities. COANS Members go above and beyond to make your stay pleasant and memorable, reflecting our genuine care for your comfort.
TRAVEL LUXURIOUSLY WHILE SUPPORTING SUSTAINABLE TOURISM
Since 1982, COANS has championed ethical values and environmental stewardship. By staying with one of our members, you support sustainable tourism and contribute to a legacy of respect for our planet and communities.
EXPLORE NEW LOCATIONS YOU’LL LOVE
Enjoy a diverse range of campgrounds, from tranquil lakesides to breezy beaches and cozy forests. COANS Members offer the perfect setting to relax, reconnect, and create lasting memories.
WAKE UP TO NATURE’S UNSPOILED BEAUTY
Experience Nova Scotia’s breathtaking landscapes with stunning sunrises, peaceful campfire evenings, and close encounters with nature. Our pristine scenery promises a rejuvenating escape and unforgettable serenity.
BECOME PART OF A CLOSE-KNIT CAMPING COMMUNITY
When you camp at a COANS Campground, you will be embraced by the warmth of Nova Scotians. Feel like part of their family with every interaction, making your stay memorable and filled with heartfelt connections.
EMBRACE THE JOY OF ENDLESS EXPLORATION
Delight in a world of adventure and relaxation. Whether hiking trails, paddling lakes, or enjoying local flavors by the campfire, our activities promise joy, excitement, and lasting memories.
