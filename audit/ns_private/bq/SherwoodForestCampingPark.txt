Welcome to the Camping Season!

   
Once part of an English gentleman’s 400 acre estate,  Sherwood Forest Camping Park is centrally located in Coldbrook, Nova Scotia.  The park is ideally suited to today’s camping family, whether travelling with tent, trailer, or motorhome.


Our natural pine forest has been carefully preserved and provides an excellent setting for 223 spacious sites.  A spring-fed trout brook, large fresh water pond and variety of wildlife add to the outdoor experience.  Our attractions section of this site provides you with an overview of the in-park activities and the local attractions.  The young campers particularly enjoy random encounters with Robin Hood and his band of Merry Men during the hayrides.  The park is a base from which you can easily and conveniently visit the many scenic and historic points of interest in the Annapolis Valley.

Come and join us for a day, week, month, or as one of our many seasonal campers.  Your Host, the Shaw Family, welcomes you!
