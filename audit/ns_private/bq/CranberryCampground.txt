CAMPING SEASON: Victoria Day Long Weekend through Thanksgiving Long Weekend (Seasonal Camping).
Regular camping is available through the end of September.

Family-owned and operated since 2005, Cranberry Campground offers spacious campsites, waterfront views, modern amenities, and a welcoming atmosphere in beautiful Merigomish, Nova Scotia. Come fall in love with our place!

Where Memories Begin!

The best camping memories begin with space to enjoy them. Whether you are staying for a weekend or the entire season, our spacious 48' by 52' campsites offer comfort and convenience. Choose from 20 amp, 30 amp, or 50 amp full-service sites, including pull-through and waterfront two-way options. Enjoy beautiful views of Merigomish Harbour, campground-wide WiFi, and convenient firewood delivery right to your site. For those who prefer a more traditional camping experience, our peaceful tent camping area offers a quiet retreat surrounded by nature.

Reservations and Cancellation policy for sites and cottages

All reservations must be guaranteed with a valid credit card. Full payment is required at the time of booking.

For a full refund, cancellations must be made at least five days prior to your arrival date. Refunds will be credited to the original credit card used for the reservation, less a $20.00 administration fee.

Refunds are not provided for cancellations made within five days of arrival, early departures, inclement weather, illness, or personal emergencies.

As we have a limited number of campsites, this policy allows us to offer availability to other guests whenever possible.

Check out time is 11am

Check in time for campsites between 1pm-8pm

Check in time for cottages between 3pm-8pm

Early arrival or late departure is subject to a $10.00 charge.

Visitors:
All visitors must check in to the office upon arrival and pay the appropriate fee.

Day visitors $5.00

Overnight visitors $10.00

We do not allow visitors after 8pm. If you think you will have a guest during your stay with us, please make them aware of this policy.

THINGS TO DO/ATTRACTIONS

While staying with us, it will be our pleasure to help offer many activities for you and your family to enjoy in the nearby area! Here are just a few ideas:

Riverside International Speedway

The biggest, baddest, loudest, fastest racin’

Arisaig, Nova Scotia

Arisaig Provincial Day Park – an excellent family outing, beautiful hiking trails and fossil hunting

Big Island Beach

Just seconds away, a beautiful, not so crowded sandy beach awaits you! This long sand bar is easily accessible yet there is plenty of space for solitude or a long stroll along the shore.

Sunrise Trail

Nova Scotia’s scenic Northumberland Shore is a tourist destination noted for it’s warm ocean water, fog-free summers and it’s hospitality.

Read what this happy kayaker thinks

For the kayaking/canoeing enthusiast: Lots of breathtaking views! Cranberry Campground is the perfect place to have as “home base”

Pictou County Tourist Association

Check out the “Things to Do” page for lots of attractions and events that found around the county that are sure to amaze, entertain and even walk you through our county’s history!
