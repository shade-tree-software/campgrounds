Where to Stay in Nova Scotia Frequently Asked Questions
When should you book accommodation in Nova Scotia?

For summer travel and popular destinations such as Halifax, Peggy’s Cove, Lunenburg, and Cape Breton Island, it’s best to book accommodation several months in advance. July and August are the busiest travel months, and sought-after waterfront hotels, seaside inns, resorts, cottages, and campgrounds can fill up quickly. Booking three to six months ahead is often recommended, especially for oceanfront properties, Cabot Trail accommodations, and unique stays. Travelling in spring or fall generally offers more availability and flexibility.

Fire Restrictions in Nova Scotia

Burning restrictions are in place in Nova Scotia from March 15 to October 15 each year. No domestic brush burning or campfires are allowed between 8 am and 2 pm during wildfire risk season. All provincial, municipal, and private campgrounds follow the burning restrictions. Burning restrictions in national park campgrounds are under the jurisdiction of Parks Canada.

The fine for violating burning restrictions is $25,000.

Restrictions are updated daily at 2 pm on the BurnSafe map.
