Discover Cheticamp

Traditional Acadian Heritage & Natural Beauty on Cape Breton Island

Cheticamp, Nova Scotia
Founded 1785
~3,000 Residents

Experience the authentic charm of this historic Acadian fishing village, gateway to Cape Breton Highlands National Park, offering whale watching, cultural festivals, pristine beaches, and stunning highland trails.

Local Attractions & Activities

From the rugged beauty of Cape Breton Highlands to the rich Acadian culture, Cheticamp offers an incredible variety of experiences for outdoor enthusiasts, culture seekers, and nature lovers.
