Folklore 'as it that there many years ago in the fog, ye could 'earrr a ship comin' into the basin. The fog been thick, an' the air been still. Ye could 'earrr the waves 'ittin' the bow o' the ship an' the brass bells ringin' in the air as they moved from side to side.

 it be believed this here ship 'ad attacked several small, coastal towns along the atlantic coast an' been full o' treasures. In the distance ye could 'earrr the laughter an' celebration from the gentlemen o' fortune on board the SS golden pillager bein' led by their fearless leader, Cap'n Jaggarrr.

 As the fog thickened, the sounds started to lesson. No laughter been 'eard, no waves splashin' along the bow o' the ship. Suddenly, it been quiet.

 As the fog lifted in the early mornin' there been no ship or debris. It be believed the ship down to Davy Jones' Locker into the night with all on board. As the tide washed away there was gold coins found scattered along pebble beach 'owever, no wood, no supplies, nothin'. Only gold.

Many 'ave said o'er the years that there the SS Golden Pillager been a ghost ship an' that there Cap'n Jaggarrr ne'er really existed…. But what about the gold?

It be said in the thick fog ye can still 'earrr the brass bells ringin' in the distance. It be also said that there some nights ye can 'earrr the thump from Cap'n Jaggars wooden leg 'ittin' the rocks as 'e walks the beach at night lookin' fer 'is treasure an' callin' fer mac, 'is macaw parrot. Many people o'er the years 'ave spotted a green macaw in the trees that there seems to vanish as quickly as 'e appeared. Could it be mac?

Every now an' then a gold coin washes on the shore o' pebble beach, still today.

Why Jaggars?
We came to Nova Scotia to look at properties because the beauty, the people and the connection was something that we wanted to be part of. 
We have always been RV campers and have visited many campgrounds over the years throughout Canada and the US. Being a camper and seeing the diverse options for people made us really want to become part of this industry, not as campers but as owners. 
In December 2019 we found exactly what we were looking for. We began the process of purchasing this great property throughout a pandemic. We were persistent because we knew what we wanted and what could be done with that property. 
The previous owners had neglected it for many years and failed to disclose some of the major infrastructure issues that we only discovered after taking ownership in July 2020. 
Since then we have been working to fix the infrastructure, pool, piping and building repairs. 
Our long term goals are adding additional wooded tenting sites with and without service, gathering area on the beach,  and more. 
If you are interested in a season spot here, please give the office a call.  Our seasonal spots all come with 30amp, water, and sewer. 
We are looking forward to welcoming you to Jaggars Point Oceanfront Campground Resort.

Terms and Conditions

Cancelation Policy and Booking Terms and Conditions 

RV and tent site cancelation policy.

We require a credit card to hold your reservation at the time of booking. No charge is applied until the date of check in. If you are unable to provide a credit card you will be required to etransfer full reservation payment to hold your booking.

We require 48 hours notice to cancel a reservation. A $20.00 admin fee per site will be charged to the credit card on file. Long weekend reservations require 7 days notice to cancel. No show reservations are 100% in penalty and your card on file will be charged.

Reservations canceled within 48 hours of arrival are charged for 50% of the reservation fee.

Dogs are permitted and must be tethered at all times. You are required to clean up after your dog and dispose of it properly. We do have disposal stations throughout the park. Lots that are not cleaned properly may result in a $25.00 site clean up fee. 

Rustic Cabin and Wildwood Trailer Policy

Check in time is 2:00PM and check out time is 11:00AM

We require a credit card to hold your reservation at the time of booking. We have a minimum two night stay requirement.

We require 7 days notice to cancel your booking. Long weekend or concert event weekends require 14 days notice to cancel.

Group Bookings

If multiple sites (3 or more) are booked, we require 7 days notice to cancel the reservation. There will be a $20.00 per site admin fee charged to the credit card on file for each site booked.

Cancellations within the 7 day arrival time are subject to one night fee per site booked. Cancelations within 48 hours of arrival time are in full penalty.

Additional Booking Terms and Conditions

Our rentals (Cabin and trailer) are non smoking. There is a $250.00 fee charged to your card on file if the rentals need to be deep cleaned. Small dogs are permitted with permission at the time of booking.
There is a $20.00 per night fee for dogs.
We do not provide early check out refunds for weather conditions and situations out of our control. A future booking credit may be offered and is up to the owner’s discretion. No refunds are provided if a camper is asked to leave the park due to unruly behavior.
We ask that you leave your site the way you found it. Any damage to power poles, fire pit, picnic table or site area will result in a repair fee up to $250.00. Please do not place anything in the septic system other than toilet paper. This includes baby wipes, diapers, paper towel.
There is a strict speed limit within the park of 10kms. You are provided one warning. If a secondary warning is required, you will be asked to leave your vehicle in the parking area by the main office. There is a four way stop by the pool area, please be vigilant there as children are playing.
We have a strict noise policy. Quiet time is at 11:00PM at which time we ask for music to be turned off or low and voices to be kept at a comfortable level as to not disturb fellow campers.
