About Us

Welcome to Valley R&R Campground, your perfect getaway for relaxation and adventure! Have fun camping while enjoying our top-notch amenities and friendly atmosphere. Join us for unforgettable experiences and create lasting memories in the heart of the great outdoors!

Whether you’re here for the Apple Blossom festival, Berwick Gala Days, one of the many soccer or softball tourneys, or just simply sightseeing through our beautiful province, come stay with us at Valley R&R Campground !

 

As of 2025, it will be our 20th season as the owners of the campground. Our goal is to provide a great camping experience with a laid back and friendly environment. We are happy to see many familiar faces returning to our campground. We strive every year to meet a new goal, and continue expanding the park.

 

Although cannabis is legal in Canada, we continue to prohibit cannabis usage within the campground, unless you have a medical card.

 

Hope to see you soon!

Scott & Lindsay

Reservations/Check In & Check Out

Check out time is 11 am and Check in time is 3 pm.

Reservations will only be accepted if backed up by any of the following:

 

Visa, MasterCard, Visa Debit  or MasterCard Debit  in the amount of 1 night stay.

 

All reservations over a long weekend must be for a minimum 3 night stay.

​

Must be 19 years or older to reserve a site.

Cancellation Policy

Sites:

A $6.00 administration fee will apply to any reservation that is cancelled or shortened prior to the cancellation date, with the exception of the cabin, which is a $15.00 administration fee charge.

 

Cancellation date for the period:

May 15th to June 21st and End of Labor Day to October 14th  is 48 hours prior to your scheduled arrival.  June 22nd to End of Labor Day Long weekend is 1 week prior to your scheduled arrival.

Any reservation not cancelled prior to the cancellation date the credit card on file will be charged for 1 night plus tax when the reservation was for 5 days or less. On reservations covering 6 days stay or more the cancellation fee will be 2 nights.

 

Long Weekends:

Any reservation cancelled within one week of their arrival a 2 night fee will be charged to the credit card on file. No exceptions.

 

No Shows:

If you do not show up for your reservation, the full amount of your stay will be charged to your credit card.

Campground Rules & Regulations 

1) No refunds will be issued at any time for any given circumstance.

​

2) QUIET TIME IS 1:00 am. ANY excessive noise at any time will not be tolerated, so please be courteous of your neighbours! If you are asked to quiet down, you will be subject to eviction and will not be welcome back at the campground. No refunds will be given in this event.

 

3) For security, noise or emergency situations please call 538-8888

 

4) The Speed Limit is 10km/h. Please adjust accordingly, heavily traffic areas you should be driving much slower! A warning will result in the parking of you vehicle out front of the store, and you will not be permitted to drive within the park perimeters. Kids are playing so PLEASE slow down! No vehicle should be operated under the influence of alcohol.

 

5) Dogs are welcome but must be on a leash and cleaned up after. Excessive barking is not tolerated. Please be considerate of others.

 

6) All RV’s must have some sort of reception/connection device between their sewer line and our receiving line to prevent a backup in the sewer system. If you do not have one, they are available to purchase at the store which will meet this requirement.

 

7) Kids MUST be supervised by an adult, while swimming, playing on playground, or just running in the campground. Kids are to be on their own lot by 11pm.

 

8) No black or grocery bags are to be used, clear bags only!

 

9) All vehicles must be registered at the office. One vehicle may remain on site; everyone else is to park on grass in front of pond by front office.

 

10) One family per site, that is 2 adults and 3 children permitted. $6.00 per extra person/per night, as permitted by office staff.

 

11) No fireworks or fire crackers are permitted

 

12) Valley R&R Campground  is not responsible for lost or stolen property.

 

13) Entry to Wheaton’s FIELDS or Corn fields is not permitted.

 

14) Bike helmets for bicycles MUST be worn by both adults and children!

 

15) Valley R&R Campground will not be responsible for stolen property, damages or injury of any kind.

 

16) No drugs of any kind allowed on the property, INCLUDING CANNABIS (unless you have a medical marijuana card and you can show us this card to allow usage in a designated area)

 

17) Whoever signs this waiver is responsible for all persons on site.

 

Breaking of these rules can lead to eviction.

You can reach us at the campground, Local: 1 (902) 538-8888 Toll Free: 1 (888) 363-8882 or through our Contact Form at the bottom of our web page

Recycling & Disposal

FOOD WASTE: (green cart) No plastic of any kind please including; forks, bags and Styrofoam

 

ONLY peelings and leftover food including:

 

tea bags, coffee grounds & filters, egg shells, food soiled paper,

paper towel or box board, i.e., pizza and KFC boxes.

 

NOTE: To help reduce maggots please wrap your compost in old

newspaper etc.

 

GARBAGE: ONLY CLEAR BAGS MAY BE USED!!!

plastic wrap, plastic utensils, dirty plastic, plastic containers other than

1, 2 or 4

cardboard soiled by other than food

dog droppings & kitty litter

dried out paint or stain cans, with lids removed

chips bags & candy wrappers

Styrofoam products, disposable coffee or slushie cups

lids from all beverage containers, salad dressing and glass jars, broken

glass

liners from cereal boxes

feminine hygiene products and baby diapers

scouring pads, latex gloves, toothpaste tubes

 

PAPER PRODUCTS: (deposited directly into the recycle station)

 

clean cardboard and newspaper

clean box board, i.e., core from toilet paper or paper towel, cereal or

cracker boxes

 

CLEAN RECYCLABLES: (deposited directly into the recycle station)

 

clean milk cartons and bags

clean glass jars, lids go to garbage

clean plastic bottles, lids go to garbage

all clean plastics numbered 1, 2 or 4

clean plastic bags

clean tin cans and their lids

 

NOTE: to clean containers simply rinse with water, your old dish water is preferred

 

REFUNDABLE CONTAINERS: (please remove lids before depositing in

the recycle station)

 

all soft drink, juice, water ( 4 litre and smaller) and alcohol containers

 

NOTE: Proceeds from refundables will go to the Plantation Kids Committee.

Our Facilities

Welcome to the Valley R&R Campground facilities page! Here, you'll discover a range of activities and conveniences that will make your stay even more enjoyable.

Recreation Hall

Our Recreation Hall is a great social meeting place and also provides: A Gym, Dances, Bingo, Pool Table, Arcade Games, Showers & Washrooms

Pool

Swimming Times: 10am until dusk

Adult Swim (18 and up): 12pm - 1pm  &  5pm - 6pm

Times may change subject to pool maintenance.

General Store

Our General Store has hot coffee in the mornings, and we carry hard ice cream, slushies, chips, pop, candy, bread, eggs, ice and other necessities just in case you run short. We also have some replacement parts for your camper / trailer. You can also find campground maps, flyers and other event listing in the Store.

 

Early Season Store Hours:(May 15 - June 30) 
9am - 7pm (mon - thur) & 9am - 10pm (weekends) 

 

Peak Season Store Hours: (July 1 - Labour Day) 
9am - 8pm (mon - thur) & 9am - 10pm (weekends)

 

Late Season Store Hours: (Labour Day - Oct 15) 
Closed (mon - thur) & 9am - 8pm (weekends)

 

Store Hours subject to change: Check our Facebook link for up to date info.
