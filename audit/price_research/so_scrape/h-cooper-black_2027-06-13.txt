2027 MASTER NATIONAL RETRIEVER CLUB EVENT
Interested in booking a stay for the 2027 Master National? Please note the following:
The reservation window opens at midnight 13 months in advance of the arrival date (e.g. to arrive 10/8/27, book your reservation on or after 9/8/26 at 12 a.m.)
All reservations have a 14-night maximum
To ensure fairness in reserving campsites, reservations cannot be transferred or changed more than 12 months in advance of the arrival date
South Carolina State Parks has a strict policy against subletting sites; you cannot book a site and then sell or transfer it to someone else. The person who reserved the site must occupy the site.
Reservation name changes are not permitted. Reservations must be canceled and rebooked in the name of the new reservation holder.
