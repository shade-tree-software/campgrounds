Campus RV Park
15 Reviews
Add Review
Campus RV Park provides full hookups and concrete pads for RVs up to 55 feet on the grounds of a large church complex in Independence, Missouri. The park operates as a private facility with reservations available at $35-55 per night, offering both back-in and pull-through sites along with standard amenities including restrooms, showers, laundry, and WiFi access.
The campground sits close to downtown Independence, placing campers within walking distance of local attractions like the Truman Library and historic courthouse. Several reviewers note the proximity to train tracks, with occasional horn sounds during overnight hours. The urban setting means limited privacy between sites, though the concrete pads provide level parking.
Kevin H.
reports excellent internet connectivity, noting "wifi access point right here. 66m down, 24m up."
Clean facilities consistently earn positive mentions from visitors. The shower house maintains hot water and tidy conditions, while the laundry room provides convenient on-site washing options. A community fire ring serves the entire campground, though individual sites lack fire pits. The church campus setting creates a different atmosphere than typical commercial RV parks, with occasional events and regular church activities nearby.
Access proves straightforward via paved roads, though some reviewers mention the surrounding neighborhood doesn't immediately suggest an RV park location. The facility works well as a staging point for Kansas City area visits, sitting roughly 30 minutes from downtown and 15 minutes from Arrowhead Stadium.
Nathan T.
notes the location's convenience for local events, stating "there is a very clean shower house and bathroom."
Campus RV Park suits travelers prioritizing full hookups and urban convenience over natural settings, particularly those attending Kansas City area events or exploring Independence's historical sites.
Description
Welcome to Campus RV Park, a clean, quiet, and conveniently located RV park in the heart of historic Independence, Missouri. Whether you're staying for a night, a weekend, or an extended visit, you'll enjoy spacious concrete sites, full hookups with 30/50-amp service, and a peaceful setting that's just minutes from many of Kansas City's most popular attractions.
Our park offers modern amenities designed to make your stay comfortable, including clean restrooms with hot showers, a guest laundry facility, wide paved roads, and plenty of open green space to relax. Despite being close to shopping, restaurants, and major highways, Campus RV Park is tucked away from the hustle and bustle, creating a private, relaxing atmosphere for travelers.
We're conveniently located near the Harry S. Truman Presidential Library & Museum, Arrowhead Stadium, Kauffman Stadium, Worlds of Fun, GEHA Field, downtown Kansas City, and many of the historic sites that make Independence a favorite destination.
Whether you're passing through, exploring the Kansas City area, or visiting family and friends, Campus RV Park provides a safe, welcoming place to park, unwind, and recharge.

Easy Access to City
This isn't our typical "go to" type campground; we stopped here because we were having some trouble with our truck and needed to have it looked over before heading out further west. The RV park is really close to downtown Independence, Missouri and has everything we needed for the night. It's within walking distance of everything in town, has full hook-ups, wifi, bathrooms, and a laundry facility.
It is what you might expect for an RV park so close to downtown, a little lacking in outdoor aesthetics. But, the bathrooms were clean, the showers very hot, and the laundry room was tidy! Also, the manager was super nice and accommodating.
Independence has some cool stuff to check out and has a very midwestern, small-town feel to it, even though it is basically a suburb of the greater Kansas City area. There is a lot of charm and some interesting architecture in the immediate area. The Truman Library, the Community of Christ Church, the downtown theater and court house are all interesting and worth a look. We had some time to kill and made the rounds to these various places. If the weather would have been warmer, it would have been a very bikeable area, too.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are there photos available of Campus RV Park?
Yes, photos of Campus RV Park are available online through various camping websites and platforms. These images typically showcase the concrete pads, layout of the sites, nearby attractions like the church view, and the campground's facilities. Photos help potential visitors get a sense of the site spacing, shade coverage, and overall setting of this urban campground in Independence, MO before making a reservation.
What amenities are available at Campus RV Park in Independence, MO?
Campus RV Park offers full hookups on level concrete pads with both back-in and pull-through sites available. Amenities include clean shower and bathroom facilities, on-site laundry, and high-speed WiFi (with speeds up to 66Mbps down/24Mbps up). The campground features a community fire ring and is located close to downtown Independence with convenient access to shopping, restaurants, and local attractions. The park is well-maintained with good hookups and some shady spots, though sites tend to be on the smaller side.
What do reviews say about Campus RV Park?
Campus RV Park receives consistently positive feedback for its central location, just 30 minutes from downtown Kansas City and 15 minutes from Arrowhead Stadium. Guests appreciate the level concrete sites, exceptionally clean facilities, and convenient no-stop registration process. Many highlight the friendly staff and great views, including an interesting church nearby. Some mention the train noise at night and occasional sirens due to the urban location. The campground's proximity to downtown Independence makes it popular for those exploring local shopping, food options, and events like the Santa Caligon fair.
