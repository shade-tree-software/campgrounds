Welcome to High Plains Homestead
Glampground and RV Park
Step back in time at High Plains Homestead, an 1880s frontier experience nestled in the beauty of northwest Nebraska. Trade noise for stillness and schedules for sunsets as you unwind in a place designed for rest, reflection, and reconnection. Make yourself at home and enjoy all the beauty of God’s creation here in the badlands!

FAQ
To reserve a space at Angleton RV Park & Resort, what do I need to do?
Reservations can be made through reservation using our simple call booking service. Just pick out the dates that suit you best and secure your place.
When is the Angleton RV Park & Resort operating from?
Angleton RV Park & Resort’s hours of operation are 8:00 am – 6:00 pm (Monday – Friday) and 8:00 am – 1:00 pm (Saturdays); we are closed on Sundays. Should you need to change your arrival or departure times, kindly inform us in advance.
Can I bring my pets along when camping at this park?
Absolutely! This is an RV park that accommodates pet owners as well. Additionally, there are specific zones within the facility where animals may play and exercise unleashed; however, they must be supervised closely while outside their designated areas for safety reasons.
