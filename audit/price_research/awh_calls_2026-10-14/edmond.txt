Online Campsite Reservations
Rental Information:
All sites at Arcadia Lake have a 14-day maximum stay limit. You may stay up to 14 nights at this park, then you must leave for at least seven days prior to returning.
There is a two-night minimum on weekend bookings and a three-night minimum on holiday bookings. For booking purposes only, weekends include Friday or Saturday and holidays include Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, and Spring Break.
Your rental includes two vehicle passes for the duration of your stay. Any additional guests must pay the day-use fee for each day they are in the park.
Watercraft are not covered in your rental. A pass must be purchased at the time of entry for any watercraft brought into the park. You will need a pass for each day you are in the park. There is a 150-boat limit on Arcadia Lake. If the Lake is at capacity, you will be asked to wait in a holding area until a spot opens.
Electric sites may have a total of two camping units, made up of two tents or an RV/Travel Trailer and one tent.
Primitive sites may have a total of two camping units, made up of two tents or a Pop-up tent/camp van plus one tent. Electrical units in a primitive site must always remain on the pavement. Generators must be turned off during quiet hours.
Check-in/Check-out procedures:
You may not occupy your site until 3:00 pm the day of your arrival. Upon arrival, you must stop at the Fee Collection Station and pick up your parking permits. If you arrive outside of office hours, please stop back during the posted times.
Be sure to log in and “check-in” upon arrival.
Check-out time is no later than 1:00 pm on the day of your departure. Please remove all personal items from the site by this time. We must be able to prepare the site for the next guest arriving at 3:00 pm.
Cancellations:
If you cancel your reservation more than 14 days prior to the first night of your stay, you will get a refund less a $10 cancellation fee and any applicable processing fees.
If you cancel your reservation within 14 days of the reservation, no refund will be given.
Any cancellations due to weather will be subject to the cancellation policies.
Discounts:
Discounts are only available when booking with staff. Online rentals and reservations are not discounted. All discounts require approval with proper documentation of eligibility.
Make an Online Reservation:
Central State Park
Edmond Park
Scissortail Campground
Campsite reservations at Central State Park can be made in advance and on the same day. Edmond Park and Scissortail Campground bookings can only be made on the same day.
