CAMPGROUND CLOSURE NOTICE:
The campground at Montgomery Bell State Park is currently closed for renovation. We appreciate your patience and look forward to seeing you when the renovation is completed. If you have any questions, please call (888) 867-2757.
LAKE WOODHAVEN DRAINED:
Please be aware that Lake Woodhaven will be drained starting August 31st, 2026, for infrastructure updates. Access to the lake will remain open initially, but visitors should keep an eye on our channels if that will change during this draining. Thank you for your patience and understanding. If you have any questions, please contact the park office.
