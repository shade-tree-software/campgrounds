**ATTENTION CAMPERS:
If you are traveling with a trailer or RV, please use Hwy. 52 to Hwy. 136, due to inaccessibility to RVs from the south entrance of the park.
CAMGROUND CLOSED FOR RENOVATIONS:
Starting July 7, 2025, the campground at Standing Stone State Park will be closed for renovations. This closure is expected to last until late 2026. We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience and understanding. If you have any questions about your upcoming reservation, please call 1 (888) 867-2757.
RECREATIONAL FIELD TEMPORARY CLOSURE:
Beginning in March 2026, the recreational field below the park dam at Standing Stone State Park will undergo capital improvements. This area will be closed during construction. The closure is expected to last into mid-2027. Thank you for your patience and understanding. If you have any questions, please call the park office.
