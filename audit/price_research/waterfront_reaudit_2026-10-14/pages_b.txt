Our Amenities
High Island RV Park is half a mile from the beach, but far enough away from Crystal Beach to avoid the noise from loud parties. If you're looking for a place to live and relax close to the beach, but not on the beach, High Island RV Park is for you...and if you love birds, it's the only place you should be!

Plenty of Parking
Every space we have comes with enough room to park your vehicle next to your campsite. Additional parking is available throughout the park on a first come first serve basis.
Full Hook-Ups
All our spots are slabbed and come with 30/50 amp electrical, water and sewer connections. If you need one of our pull-through spots, click the reserve button and choose spot 8-12.
Laundry Room
Our laundry room has 3 washers, 4 dryers and a change machine for convenience. The room is air-conditioned year round with space to sit and wait for your clothes to dry!
Bathroom & Shower
Recently remodeled, our bathroom is air-conditioned, always clean and available for use by all tenants. Why shower in your RV when you can use ours? Text us for the door code!
Free Wifi
Wifi is available throughout the park to all guests for FREE. We have the best system that is offered to High Island, and even with the park full we've not had any complaints.
EV Charge
Need to charge your Tesla or any other electric vehicle? Look no further! Our park sits on a side street off the main route in and out of Crystal Beach. Stop by to get charged up!
