Cheney State Park
Font Size: + -
The park features 29 miles of asphalt roads and parking areas; a park office; 2 marinas; 223 reservable electrical hookup sites with water; over 400 primitive camp sites; 4 trailer dumpstations; 6 boat ramps with 22 launching lanes; 4 courtesy docks; 2 fish cleaning stations; 7 modern pit toilets; 9 showerhouses; 1 shelter with restrooms; 2 large group shelters; 2 medium group shelters; 29 small picnic shelters; a large shelter and a restroom; 3 nature trails including a 5 mile bicycle/walking trail; and 12 modern cabins.
Camping in Winter
Beginning in mid- to late-October, we turn the water off for the winter seasons to all utility campgrounds, bathrooms/shower houses, and dump stations. The utility campgrounds still have electric available, and the park is still open to camp during the winter. The only campground with both water and electric available during the winter is Smarsh Creek campground, and it is only sites 96-115. All sites during winter are first come first serve, no reservations or online payments are available during winter you must stop by the park office to pay or utilize the self pay for single-night use only. Call our park office if you have any questions 316-542-3664.

Cabin Reservation Information
Cheney State Park has 12 cabins available for rent year-round, that sleep between 4-10 people depending on the cabin. Cabins #1-9 are located on the West Shore, while Cabins #10-12 are located on the East Shore.
Cabins #2 Kingfisher, #3 Nuthatch, #4 Mockingbird, #5 Pelican, #6 Kestrel and #7 the Roadrunner sleep up to 4 adults with a Full-sized bed and a twin bunk bed. Cabins 2-8 come with a bathroom/shower, microwave, toaster, dorm sized fridge, air conditioning/heating, and outdoor accommodations such as an outside hydrant, grill, picnic table, and fire ring. Cabin #8 Nighthawk has same amenities as cabins 2-7 aside from screened in porch (has a deck) and has a full-sized bed, single bunk bed, and roll away single bed.
The other six cabins are ADA accessible, Cabins #1 Heron, #8 Nighthawk, #9 Osprey, #10 Crappie, #11 Walleye, and #12 Catfish. Cabins #1, #8, & #9 have decks not screened in porches.
Cabin #1 Heron is a one-bedroom cabin with a full-sized bed, single futon couch, double futon bed, and a single murphy bed. Cabins #9 Osprey, #10 Crappie, #11 Walleye, and #12 Catfish are all two-bedroom cabins with full sized bed, two sets of twin bunk beds, and two double futons. Cabins 1,9,10,11 and 12 have all the same amenities as cabins 2-8 (aside from screened in porches) but have full sized kitchens which include a full-sized fridge as well as a stove and dining table with 4 chairs.
