Kanopolis Alert
Beginning May 15, 2026, the Kanopolis Lake Information Center that is operated by the US Army Corps of Engineers will be closed to the public and pass sales will be by appointment only. There are no closures at Kanopolis State Park.
Located on the Prairie Trail Scenic Byway northwest of Marquette along K-141, about 5 miles north of the K-4/K-141 junction (or) about 7 miles south of the K-140/K-141 junction east of Ellsworth
The first state park in Kansas, Kanopolis State Park is situated in the rolling hills, bluffs and woods of the scenic Smoky Hills region of the state. From the towering Dakota sandstone bluffs to the caves and crevices of Horsethief Canyon, the park and surrounding area offer rugged beauty and stunning vistas. The park is split into two areas north and south of the dam and there are two federal parks, as well.
Kanopolis offers more than 30 miles of trails open to hikers, mountain bikers, and horseback riders, 15 of which are on the Smoky Hill Wildlife Area. The 3,500-acre lake and 12,500-acre wildlife area provide anglers and hunters abundant fish and game. Nestled near the Smoky Hill River toward the northwest portion of the wildlife area is Faris Caves, which were carved by early pioneers and served as milk house, school house, and living quarters.
Wildlife viewing and photography opportunities are plentiful. In the winter, bald eagles and ospreys fish the river. Songbirds and wildflowers brighten the landscape throughout the area.
Kanopolis State Park also manages nearby Mushroom Rock State Park, a picnic area with unusual geologic formations that amaze first-time and return visitors alike.

Cabin Reservation Information
Kanopolis State Park is offering six deluxe cabins as a year-round alternative camping opportunity. These are especially beneficial for individuals who enjoy the park experience but would rather not “rough it.” The cabins feature easy access to the reservoir’s fishing, boating, water-skiing, etc. opportunities. They are also close to Smoky Hill Wildlife Area which offers excellent hunting opportunities in the area.
ADA Accessibility:
Arapaho Cabin is only partially accessible
Cheyenne Cabin is not accessible
All other cabins are fully accessible
Our cabins are arranged in the following order from east to west in the Little Bluestem Cabin Area in the Langley Point Area (South Shore) of the State Park:
Cabin Amenities:
Heated and air-conditioning
Sleeps five to six people with one full-size bed, a full-size futon, a pull-down--“Murphy-type" twin-size bed, and a couch); beds are fitted with a mattress only (no linens)
Full kitchen (refrigerator, stove, microwave), table and chairs.
Kitchen utensils, pots, pans, dishes, bedding, and towels are not included. Please bring your own.
Check in time is 3p.m. or after and check out times are at noon everyday, year-round.
Exterior amenities include a picnic table, fire ring, charcoal grill (you must bring your own charcoal), dog kennel with dog house, water hydrant, and an excellent view of the lake. Please leave the cabins in the same condition as you found them for the next visitors. We invite you to write in the journal located in the cabin and tell us and the next visitors where you are from and about the Kanopolis State Park experience you had. (Please keep it “G” rated). Smoking and pets are not allowed inside the cabins.
