Webster State Park
Font Size: + -
Print
Webster State Park is a 880-acre prairie setting of rolling hills and spacious skies where visitors often see deer, turkeys, quail, pheasants, and many wildflowers.
3,700 acres of open water offer great fishing in Webster Reservoir and it's a stopping place for many migratory waterfowl and shorebirds. Ituality walleye, crappie, white bass, and channel catfish.
Webster Wildlife Area encompasses an additional 8,018 acres mostly surrounding the Solomon River west of the reservoir. A variety of wildlife habitats are developed and maintained to enhance wildlife.
Campfires must be contained in campfire rings.
Quiet hours are from 11 pm to 6 am.

Cabin Reservation Information
Webster State Park offers two modern rental cabins located on the north side of Webster Reservoir. These cabins offer great views of the lake and opportunities to stay at the lake without a camper or sleeping on the hard ground in a tent. These cabins are available all year long so you can come out and play in the water or relax on the beach or rent one for a late season hunting excursion.
Pintail Place is located on the reservoir’s north shore providing an excellent vantage overlooking the beautiful Webster reservoir. The modern log cabin will appeal to those who want to experience the great outdoors but also enjoy modern conveniences. The modern cabin sleeps up to six people. The kitchen has a small stovetop (no oven), small refrigerator/freezer, and microwave. (Coffee maker, pots & pans, dishes, and eating utensils are not provided.) Picnic table, grill, and fire ring are provided for you to enjoy with your family and friends. Cabin has a modern bathroom with showers as well as air conditioning and heating. You will need to bring your own bedding, towels, & toiletry items. No pets are allowed in the cabin and smoking is prohibited in the cabin.
The Bluewing Bungalow is a one bedroom modern cabin is equipped with a full-size bed and a futon, along with a Murphy bed and futon in the living area. The modern cabin sleeps up to five people. Kitchen has a small stove with oven, refrigerator/freezer, and microwave. (Coffee maker, pots & pans, dishes, and eating utensils are not provided.) Picnic table, grill, and fire ring are provided outside for that perfect night under the stars. Cabin has a modern bathroom with shower as well as air conditioning and heating. You will need to bring your own bedding, towels, & toiletry items. No pets are allowed in the cabin and smoking is prohibited in the cabin.
