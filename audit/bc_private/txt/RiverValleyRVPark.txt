Policies / Rules/ Regulations
Thank you for visiting our Website.
We have monthly sites available for 2026.
CHECK IN: 1:00 p.m. CHECK OUT: 11:00 a.m.
Please read the rules and at the bottom of the page is the reservation form!
Pets must be kept on a leash at all times and must not be left unattended.
Generators are not to be used without management approval.
Do not leave air conditioner on when not at site.
No car or trailer washing or repairing in campsites.
Use of proper  screw in  sewer hoses, elbows and adapters only.
Protect Picnic Tables when using Barbecue.
The speed limit in the park is 8 km/5 miles per hour
Visitors of Registered Guests -  RULES: Must report to office. Visitors must leave the park by 10:00 p.m. Overnight visitors must register and pay at the office. Must park in designated area.
No children under 19 allowed at the river without parent supervision .
Quiet hours: 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Please keep voices, radios, stereos and TV's low enough as to not to disturb your neighbors at all times.
In case of severe infractions of the Park rules, the management reserves the right to evict the offending party without refund of registration fees.
Personal Property - River Valley RV Park is not responsible for the loss of personal property by theft or damage.  Please secure your belongings
Only Registered vehicles within park.
CANCELLATION POLICY
No refunds.
No refund for early departure or eviction.  To qualify for a refund you must cancel within 30 days of your reservation or there is no refund.
PET INFORMATION
Dogs must on a leash  (6' max)  at all times. They are not to be left alone on site.
Pick up after your pets. Some areas are   pet free   zones.  Owners who have aggressive animals will be asked to leave the park.
I have read the rules and regulations. I acknowledge that failure to follow the Park Rules and Regulations may result in eviction from the park. I also acknowledge that updated rules will be provided when i arrive at the park.
For long-term guests there are long-term rules and regulations that are provided.

Management has compiled these rules for everyone to remain safe and to try and make your time here pleasant. We may at any time implement additional rules and regulations. We also reserve the right to select our clientele and their guests.
