Welcome to Springs RV Resort, where we are excited to have you join us for a night, a season, or as an owner of your own piece of BC paradise. Picture yourself enjoying our refreshing pools, soaking up the sun, and sharing quality time with loved ones. With Phase 3 now complete, we are ready to welcome you this summer!
We are confident that you will agree: life is truly better at Springs RV Resort.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is the daily rate?
Rates vary depending on time of year and lot chosen. The secure reservation portal will display the daily rate for your chosen stay. Daily rate includes 2 adults, 1 RV and up to 2 vehicles parked on your rental site.
Are there any additional fees?
Additional Guest Parking spaces $5/day. Additional adults or children $10/day. Children under 6 are free. Pets $7/day. Boat trailer parking $20/day.
Are there any maximum or minimum stays?
Long Weekends: A three-night minimum stay is required.   Cancellations for Long Weekend Reservations are non-refundable
June,   July & August Weekends: A two night minimum stay required.
When are the Pools and Hot Tubs open?
Hot tubs: will open daily 10 a.m to 10 p.m. mid-May through Thanksgiving.
Pools: Family Pool, Adult Pool/Hot Tub open annually 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. for the May long weekend and remain open through early September. Please check your reservation notices for updates as in some years it may open earlier if the weather cooperates!
What time is Check-In? Check-out?
Check-in between 1 p.m. and 8 p.m.    For late check-ins, please make arrangements with the office prior to arrival. A $35 late check-in fee may apply.
Check-out prior to 11 a.m. on the departure date.
What is the Cancellation Policy?
Reservations that are canceled 14 days or more prior to your arrival date, will be given a  refund less a $45 cancellation fee. (Booking fees are non-refundable)
Cancellations or changes to a reservation within 13 days of your arrival date are non-refundable.
Cancellations for Long Weekend Reservations are non-refundable
Date Changes to your reservation require notification 14 days or more prior to your arrival date and are dependent on availability. A $15 Date Change fee may be applied.
Does the Resort have free WiFi?
There is an open WiFi system which is perfect for emails and general internet searches. If you want to stream movies or other content, you have the option to upgrade for a fee to a daily package.
Can I bring a pet?
Yes the Resort is very pet-friendly with a beautiful park with dog run and forest walking trails at the foot of the mountain.
Are the lots fully serviced?
All lots are serviced with municipal water and sewer. Family lots have 30 amp service, Premium lots have 50 amp service and Phase 3 Premium Rental lots have either 50 or 100 amp service.

Thank you for choosing Springs RV Resort as your vacation destination.
There are some important details we want you to know before you make your booking.
We do not accept tent trailers, tents. RVs – motorhomes, 5th wheels, trailers, travel vans and truck campers only please.
Check-in is between 1:00 pm and 8:00 p.m.
If you expect to arrive later than 8:00 p.m., please call the office at 1-604-796-9767 in advance. Late check-ins may be subject to a $35 fee.
Cancellation Policy and Reservation Change Policy:
Cancellations made within 14 days of your scheduled arrival are non-refundable.
Cancellations made more than 14 days in advance are subject to a $ 30. cancellation fee + $15 booking fee ($45). The remaining balance will be refunded to the credit card on file.
No changes to your reservation within 14 days of your scheduled arrival.
Changes to your reservations more than 14 days in advance may be subject to a $15. change fee.
No-shows and early departures are charged the full reservation amount.
To make a reservation, please check the box to confirm that you have read and understood the criteria above.
