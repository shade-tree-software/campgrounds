About FINGER LAKE RESORT
Your Gateway to  Nature  and  Relaxation
Located just an hour south of Vanderhoof, British Columbia and two hours from Prince George,  Finger Lake Resort  offers the tranquility of untouched wilderness.
As the sole access to the pristine 9 km-long Finger Lake, our cozy, pet-friendly family resort is your destination for adventure, relaxation, and scenic beauty.

Camping at Finger Lake
Escape to the peaceful beauty of  Finger Lake , where towering trees, fresh mountain air, and the sound of nature create the perfect backdrop for your next camping adventure.
Whether you’re here for a  weekend getaway, a family reunion, or hunting season , our cozy, well-spaced campsites offer the  perfect mix of comfort and wilderness.
Breathe in the Fresh Air, Unwind by the Fire
Reconnect With Nature
There’s nothing like  waking up to the sounds of birdsong, spending the day exploring the outdoors, and ending it by a cozy campfire under the stars.  Whether you’re here for a weekend escape or an extended stay, Finger Lake offers the perfect blend of  peace, adventure, and relaxation.

Camping in British Columbia
A True Nature Experience
Tucked along a gentle slope overlooking Finger Lake, our campground offers  10 spacious campsites , designed for both tent campers and RV travelers.
Here, you’ll wake up to birdsong, spend your days exploring the great outdoors, and wind down beside a crackling campfire under a sky full of stars.
Boat Rentals
Your Cozy Campsite Awaits
Settle into a peaceful spot surrounded by nature, where you can relax, explore, and enjoy the fresh air. Our campsites are designed for both camping in a tent or parking your RV.  The well-equipped sites at Finger Lake provide everything you need for a comfortable stay.
Each Site Includes:
A Fire Pit - perfect for roasting marshmallows or warming up on crisp evenings
A Picnic Table - enjoy meals outdoors with your family and friends
Drive-Through Access - available upon request for longer 5th wheels and motorhomes
A Looped Road - so there’s no tricky backing required for most RVs
For those who love  fishing, hiking, and wildlife spotting , Finger Lake is the perfect basecamp for adventure. Whether you prefer  lazy afternoons by the water or early morning hikes , there’s something for everyone here.

We understand plans can change, so here’s what you need to know before you book:
Booking Info
Please note for cabin and campsite rentals:
Cabin Bookings
Minimum 2-night stay required
Holiday Long Weekends
Minimum 3-night stay required for both cabins and campsites
May Through October
No refunds for early departures – pay for what you book
Cancellation Policy
If you cancel your reservation:
14+ days before arrival:
25% of total reservation charged
14 days to 72 hours before arrival:
50% of total reservation charged
Less than 72 hours or no-show:
100% of reservation charged
Remaining balance is due at check-out unless otherwise arranged.
