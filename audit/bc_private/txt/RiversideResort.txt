Welcome Home
Providing high-quality, attainable housing in
amenity-rich communities  Canadians are proud to call home.
As Canada’s largest and most long-standing modular housing developer, we are redefining attainable living with vibrant, professionally managed communities that Canadians are proud to call home. From elegant townhomes to modern bungalows, our flexible designs blend functionality and style to create high quality, exceptional value housing. Available for purchase on a land lease basis, where residents buy their home outright and rent the land it is situated on, Parkbridge is expanding housing options across the country, and providing a shorter path to homeownership for many Canadians.

Parkbridge Resorts celebrates best-ever survey results
February 10, 2026
The Parkbridge Resorts’ annual survey results are here – and our team is thrilled to share we have received some of our highest-ever customer satisfaction scores!
With 30 resorts across Canada, Parkbridge Resorts offers overnight and seasonal RV sites, as well as cabins, yurts, and tent camping.
Vacationers are asked to rate their entire overnight experience – from the helpfulness of our resort teams to the quality of the grounds.
Here are a few highlights from the 2025 survey:
•  Highest Ever Satisfaction Score:  93% of respondents rated their experience as 4 out of 5 or higher
•  Record-Breaking NPS:  Our Net Promoter Score is up 7.4 points from last year
•  Outstanding Team Performance:  Overall Experience & Overall Satisfaction with the team were both ranked at 4.5 out of 5 stars
Starting 2026 strong in BC:
• Our Overall Experience & Overall Satisfaction with the team has already grown to 4.8 out of 5 stars
• Three of our BC resorts – Gallagher Lake, Rondalyn, and Oceanside - started the year with perfect 5 out of 5 star scores for Overall Experience
There’s still time to book an adventure this winter in BC. Visit   Parkbridge Resorts   for our availability.
