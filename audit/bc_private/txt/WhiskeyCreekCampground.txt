Welcome
A peaceful and serene experience awaits you at Whiskey Creek RV Campground located in a wooded setting at the foot of Mount Arrowsmith and only minutes to Little Qualicum Falls, Cathedral Grove and Cameron Lake.
We are located on Highway 4, 18 kms west of Parksville on beautiful Vancouver Island, British Columbia, Canada.
If you are traveling to Long Beach, Tofino or Ucluelet, let us be your resting point.  We are 45 minutes from the Nanaimo ferry and only 2 hours from the west coast.  Once you discover all the great many things to do in the local area, we'd be happy to have you for an extended stay!
