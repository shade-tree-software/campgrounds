Located off the coast of Vancouver Island, BC— Kyuquot Sound offers some of the world’s best salmon and halibut fishing.
Kyuquot Sound, BC, CANADA [ 50.0160° N, 127.3544° W ]
Walters Cove Resort gives saltwater fishing enthusiasts like you first class access to this pristine, largely undiscovered destination. Kyuquot Sound presents unparalleled prospects for anglers and is conveniently located just minutes from the Fishing Resort.
While the rugged west coast of Vancouver Island will fill your long days of fishing with adventure, your evenings will have you winding down with first class service, mouth watering meals, and a quality of relaxation you may not have forgotten the taste of.
Our Vancouver Island Fishing Lodge is ready to receive you, no matter your point of origin.

There are three ways to get to us for
your vancouver island getaway
1.   Fly-IN   FISHING Trips
Accessing Walters Cove Resort has never been more effortless. Depart from the Seair Seaplane Terminal, located adjacent to Vancouver, BC's International Airport's South Terminal, and embark on a remarkable one hour and fifteen-minute flight to the resort's dock. Additionally, if you are traveling by car to Vancouver, we offer complimentary parking at Seair for the duration of your fishing getaway, making your journey all the more convenient.

2.   DRIVE-IN   FISHING CHARTERS
Many guests like to spend some time exploring Vancouver Island in addition to saltwater fishing at Walters Cove Resort. We offer the option of driving in on select dates, so you can travel at your own pace. You can get to Walters Cove by driving to Fair Harbour where we will pick you up by boat and bring you on a scenic 25-minute ride to the lodge. We provide free parking in a supervised lot in Fair Harbour, which is owned and operated by us as well!
