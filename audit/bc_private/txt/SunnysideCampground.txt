Sunnyside Campground is now open for the season. For 2026 camping inquiries, please contact  sunnyside@cultuslake.bc.ca .
About Sunnyside Campground
Cultus Lake is a sparkling gem in the crown of the Fraser Valley. A short drive from Vancouver, it is a piece of paradise in the Lower Mainland. With beautiful beaches and a large and clean freshwater lake surrounded by spectacular mountain views, it is a very special place.
Sunnyside Campground, which is owned and managed by Cultus Lake Park, offers seasonal and overnight campers the opportunity to experience all of the magic of Cultus Lake – and everything it has to offer.
Sunnyside Campground provides campers with 65 acres of campsites – including waterfront, view and regular tenting, as well as full hook-up sites. A friendly, welcoming place with a focus on family fun, Sunnyside Campground is located within walking distance of everything Cultus Lake Park has to offer. This includes the world-famous Cultus Lake Waterpark, golf course, mini-golf, a putting course, boat rentals, hiking, ice cream spots, restaurants, pubs, shops and so much more.
We invite you to come and have fun on our beautiful beaches, to sit amongst the trees or splash in the clear, clean water of Cultus Lake – there’s something for everyone at Cultus Lake Park! Please  click  to make a reservation.

Sunnyside Campground Rates & Fees
All Sunnyside Campground sites include one (1) fire pit and one (1) picnic table.
Campground rates are subject to change at any time.
We require full payment in high season at the time of booking.
Please note, we require a three (3) night minimum booking on long weekends and a two (2) night minimum booking on regular weekends.
Sunnyside Campground’s reservation system is available online and we accept Canadian currency. Please  click  to make a reservation.
