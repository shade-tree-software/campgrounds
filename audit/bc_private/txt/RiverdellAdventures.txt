About Us
Wild River Adventure Tours started from a passion for the scenic wilderness of Tumbler Ridge, and everything it has to offer. Today, we are a small family-run touring company with years of experience in the area. We want you to feel the same passion we feel every time we look at Kinuseo Falls or hike up Titanic Rock.
