RV Sites on Qualicum Bay
Nestled right by the ocean on Qualicum Bay, near Qualicum Beach, ROAM offers both standard and premium waterfront RV sites with stunning coastal views and direct beach access.
Book Your Site
Qualicum Beach RV Sites
ROAM's RV sites offer comfort and adventure for peaceful getaways or family trips, all with a gorgeous ocean backdrop. Many of our sites are waterfront, offering stunning views and soothing wave sounds.
Choose from sites that  accommodate vehicles up to 32 feet  ( varies by site ).
Enjoy pet-friendly sites and amenities like 30-amp hook-ups, water and sewer connections, Wi-Fi, and more ( varies by site ).
Facilities include an on-site  laundromat  and outdoor bathrooms with coin-operated showers. Don't miss our  outdoor barrel sauna  during your stay!
Whether you're planning a tranquil escape or an exciting family trip, ROAM is the perfect base for your  coastal adventure , Enjoy a home-away-from-home experience with the ocean at your doorstep.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are your terms of service?
Please  visit this page  to view ROAM's terms of service.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please  visit this link  to view ROAM's cancellation policy.
Is there Wi-Fi available at the resort?
Yes. The password will be provided in your booking confirmation email.
Where do I park?
Please stay within your designated RV site area.
Is smoking allowed at the RV sites?
No. Smoking of any kind (tobacco, vape, marijuana) is  strictly prohibited  in all areas of ROAM Beach Resort, including cabins and RV sites, to ensure the comfort and safety of all guests.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in time is 1PM, Check-out time is at 11AM.
Are there grocery stores or restaurants nearby?
Yes, there are grocery stores, convenience stores, and restaurants within a short driving distance of the resort where you can purchase essentials and groceries or enjoy a meal out. There is a pub, cafe, ice-cream, and a food truck within walking distance.
Are children allowed at the resort?
Absolutely! ROAM is family-friendly, and children of all ages are welcome to join in on the fun.
Are pets allowed at ROAM?
Pets are welcome, but are not automatically accepted and must be approved in your booking. Additional fees may apply.
Is there a maximum vehicle length?
Yes, each site has a maximum vehicle length that ranges from 18 to 32 feet depending on the site. Please see the  list of sites above  for specific maximum lengths.
Are there any nearby hiking trails or outdoor activities?
Yes! There are numerous hiking trails, beaches, and outdoor activities within close proximity to the resort, including Horne Lake Caves, Lighthouse Trail, and Forbidden Plateau, to name a few. Check out our  Things to Do  page for more.
