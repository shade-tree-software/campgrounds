Year-Round RV Camping
RV Camping
Whistler RV Park offers full hookups for both back-in and pull-through RV sites, including sewer, water, and electricity. Each site provides ample space to accommodate all pullouts, ensuring that RVs of any size are welcome. All sites are equipped with a picnic table and a fire pit for your convenience and enjoyment.
The nightly rate includes up to two adults and an unlimited number of children in your immediate family. Additional adults are charged at $10 per night. Pets are welcome for a fee of $5 per pet, per night.
Please note that tents are not permitted on RV sites unless prior approval has been obtained from management.
May 1st to Oct 1st
$75 – $94 (plus taxes) per night on all weekdays and weekends (excluding Holidays / long weekends)
$95 – $105 on all Holidays / long weekends during this time period.
All weekend bookings during May to the end of September, require a 2 night minimum and a 3 night minimum on all long weekends.
October 1st to May 1st
$70 per night (plus tax) excluding Good Friday and Thanks giving
$95 – $105 (plus tax) per night for Good Friday (March 29th)  and Thanksgiving (Oct 14th)
30 day discounted rates available for October – May. $1350 for 30 days! Email or call for inquiries on other longer stay deals.

Tent Camping
Book Tent Space Now
Our small tent sites and Small RV or Tent sites are nestled within a serene forest setting, surrounded by trees that offer a tranquil camping experience.  Small RV or Tent sites are ideal for smaller vans, truck campers, Bolers, and tent trailers.  Small Tent sites are ideal for small to medium tents only.
Each site is equipped with a picnic table and fire-pit for your convenience. Additionally, our tenting area includes a full washroom facility with complimentary hot showers and easily accessible water taps. The nightly rate covers accommodation for up to two adults, with no limit on the number of children in your immediate family.  Please note, each site allows for only one tent. A maximum of two adults is permitted per site.  For groups of four or more adults (excluding teen children), two sites must be booked. We strive to maintain a family-friendly environment, where all guests are expected to respect one another.
Pets are welcome for a fee of $5 per pet, per night.
Please note that our tent sites are closed during the winter months.
May 1st to September 30th
$55 per night (plus taxes)   everyday in June, July and August.
$57 on all Holidays / long weekends during this time period.
$30 per night (plus taxes) Sunday to Thursday and $55 per night (plus taxes) Friday and Saturday May & September.
$30 per night (plus taxes) October.
We only have 1 group site #206 all other tent/small RV sites are a max of 2 adults and 1 tent per site.
Family Group Site with no services #206
$130 per night (plus taxes) May 1st – November 1st.
The Family Group Site is nestled in a wooded area, offering a peaceful retreat. This site includes a picnic table, fire pit, a full washroom facility with complimentary hot showers and easily accessible water taps.
When booking, please indicate whether you will have a small RV and/or tents in the notes section.  The site accommodates a maximum of 6 adults and up to 8 children.  It can accommodate up to 3 small RVs (25 feet or under) or up to 6 tents.
Over Flow RV Site with no services
$55 per night (plus taxes)   everyday in June, July and August.
$57 on all Holidays / long weekends during this time period.
$30 per night (plus taxes) Sunday to Thursday and $55 per night (plus taxes) Friday and Saturday May & September.
$30 per night (plus taxes) October.
The Flow Sites are located through the gate at the back of the park, numbered 1 through 5, and offer plenty of sun and open sky exposure. These are non-serviced sites, each equipped with a picnic table, fire pit, and 24-hour access to restrooms, water stations, dishwashing facilities, and complimentary hot showers.
The maximum occupancy is 4 adults, with no limit on the number of children in your immediate family.
Sites #1 to #3 can accommodate RVs up to 27 feet in length.
Site #4 can accommodate RVs up to 40 feet in length.
Site #5 can accommodate RVs up to 25 feet in length.
