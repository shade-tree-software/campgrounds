RV SITES
Located minutes away from Spider Lake on beautiful Vancouver Island, Lily Pond RV offers 10 full hook up RV sites on an 8.7 acre aquatic farm producing chicken eggs (Organic Fed, Corn/Soy Free) and a variety of lettuce, basil, herbs and Japanese Koi.
Full Service Sites
All of our sites are equipped with 15, 30 & 50 amp power, filtered deep-well water and septic.
Modern Facilities
We offer newly built, modern, private washrooms, private shower facilities and a coin-op laundry room. Endless hot water showers are included with your stay.
wifi
Blessed to have high-speed fibre internet in the Spider Lake Community. Wifi is strong and fast throughout the property with a custom network, also included with your stay.

The Farm
THE Lily Pond
5 spring-fed ponds throughout the property will be home to 36 different varieties of Hardy Water Lillies in June of 2023. We’ll find our favourite flowers and what grows best in our local area. It will be a sight to behold in the coming months, Claude Monet would be proud.
The Grounds
A mix of pasture, ponds, blueberries, seaberries and perennials. Constantly evolving and growing.
The Aquaponic Facility
Home to 40,000 gallons of recirculating living water, countless Japanese Koi and thousands of romaine lettuce. Sustainable agriculture with the marriage of aquaculture and hydroponics.

Lily Pond RV Policies
As a small private farm in the Spider Lake Community, we value the peace this area affords us. We want our guests to enjoy the same feelings of peace and the connection with nature throughout their stay. We’ll strive to do our part to make your stay enjoyable and ensure the high standards we set for ourselves.
General Policies
Reservations must be made in advance of arrival.
Please use either the online check-in or check-in at the farm stand prior to setting up at your site.
Please use the parking spaces provided in front of your site and refrain from parking in any unoccupied sites. New guests may be arriving shortly.
Speed limit on the property is 5km/hr.
Tow vehicle must be onsite. Third parties cannot drop off RVs.
Any RV with non-standard window coverings will not be accepted for setup.
For a month stay or extended stays:
Please ensure your RV is in good working order and less than 15 years old.
Limit of (2) humans max per site.
Limit of (2) small animals upon approval.
Unfortunately we cannot allow any tents to be setup. RVs only.
ATV use - Please respect your neighbours, our neighbours and the gravel roads. Prefer guests to stage their ATVs at the trails and not within the property.
Noise
No loud music at any time. We’re music lovers ourselves but please keep volumes low as to not disturb the neighbouring guests.
Please respect your neighbours and ours.
Generators are not permitted unless there is a power outage.
Quiet time is from 11pm to 8am.
Pets
We are a pet-friendly property, but pets must be walked on a leash, cleaned up after and may not disturb other guests or the farm animals.
Pets are the responsibility of the owner. Management reserves the right to restrict disruptive or aggressive breeds.
Dog fences are only permitted for short stays of up to 3 days. Any extended stays longer than 3 days, dog fencing is not permitted as damage to the grounds/grass occurs.
Maximum of 2 pets per site.
Guests
A maximum of 4 guests are allowed per site.
All guests must vacate the site prior to quiet time.
Please let us know if you are having guests that require additional parking.
Fires
Fires are allowed according to the current RDN bylaws, guests must use the fire pits provided.
Please do not burn wood found in the surrounding forest, firewood is available for purchase. Alternatively, feel free to bring your own.
Arrivals/Departures
Check in Time is 2pm, please call to inquire if you need an earlier check in.
Check out time is 11am, late checkouts are hard to accommodate with incoming guests.
Please avoid any check-ins after sunset. If you have a late ferry and it’s unavoidable - please let us know and we can bring a light out for setup.
Sites
Please maintain the site in good, clean condition during the duration of your stay.
Please do not setup any structures that interfere with lawn/site maintenance.
Be aware that we do get bears in the area from time to time, please do not leave out any food in your campsite.
Active farm work
Sometimes we need to make a little noise in order to run the farm effectively. Boilers need wood, pastures need mowing occasionally, things need to get built. We will try our best to complete these tasks between the hours of 11am and 2pm to keep our disturbance to a minimum. We thank you for your understanding.
The Ponds
Pond fencing is in place surrounding the RV sites to limit access and provide a safe experience for our guests. Please ensure all children are supervised at all times in the natural pond, wetland areas and throughout the RV grounds.
