Frequently asked questions
what are your rates?
Our rates use a dynamic pricing system, meaning prices may vary depending on season, demand, length of stay, and how far in advance you book. Some dates—such as long weekends—may carry premium pricing, while quieter periods or early bookings may see reduced rates (often even cheaper than previous management!) Extended stays may also qualify for automatic discounts. The most accurate rates will always appear on our booking platform  here . For occasional sneak-peek offers and limited promotions, be sure to follow along on our social media channels.
Where are you located?
We are located at 423 Highway 6, just outside of Cherryville, BC
Is there wi-fi at the campground?
We are working to get wi-fi available across the campground, but for now, free wi-fi is available at the Gold Panner Apothecary in the front office. Telus network gets great reception throughout most of the property.
Do you have an rv dump station?
Yes! The rv dump station is free for registered campers or $10 for those just passing through.
Are there showers & flush toilets?
Yes! We have one small wash house with flush toilets and coin-operated showers. Wash house is available seasonally (approx. May long weekend through Thanksgiving) and is unavailable during winter dry camping months.
