Beachside Camping At Cox Bay In Tofino
Welcome to Surf Grove
Surfer or not, you'll love it here
First class camping, year-round
Tofino’s top rated campground, and the only campground at Canada’s #1 Surf Beach, Cox Bay. Surf Grove is camping, but better. What does that mean?  It means an easy and seamless online booking process. It means warm, attentive service—real humans, here to welcome you, then help you with anything you might need during your stay. It means high-quality, carefully considered amenities—designed with families,  surfers, hikers, and adventurers of all kinds in mind. Not to mention our best amenity, Cox Bay—but we can’t exactly take credit for that, can we? Welcome to Tofino camping, elevated.
Camp at Surf Grove
We’re the only Tofino campground at Cox Bay—Canada’s favourite surf beach. Each of our 167 fully-serviced RV campsites, 75 semi-serviced campsites, A-Frame Cabins and our Surf Grove RV Rentals are just a stone’s throw from sand and surf. So getting to and from the beach (with or without surfboard) couldn’t be easier.

Experience Tofino
Tofino. There’s nothing like it. This is a place for adventures, set against one of North America’s most stunning natural backdrops. Climb mountains, catch waves, watch wildlife—it’s all here for you. Start planning your Tofino camping getaway.

Camping at Cox Bay
Camping at Surf Grove
Wake Up to Rich Forest & Salty Ocean Air
One of the many things we love about Tofino camping is that it’s about slowing down. You tend to notice more here—like the smell of campfire, lush forest, salty ocean air. It’s in the taste of a perfect s’more or the first refreshing sip of post-surf IPA. You notice sounds like crashing waves and laughing kids. And you’re more able to really take in—to fully appreciate— what’s in front of you: some of Canada’s most spectacular natural beauty, right on your doorstep. Claim your spot at Surf Grove, and experience it all for yourself.
Full-Service RV Camping Sites
These fully serviced RV campsites, our closest sites to Cox Bay, have everything you need for an elevated Tofino camping experience. So pull in, hook up, and start making memories.
The Groves Site Info
Semi-Service Tent Camping Sites
Classic tent camper? These semi-serviced campsites are perfect for you. Sites in the Low & High Tide Loops can also accommodate guests with vans, truck campers, roof top tents, and smaller RVs that don’t require water and sewage hookups.

RV Rentals
We totally get that transporting a camper onto an island, then across it, might not be for everyone. Or maybe you’re flying in, or don’t have your own RV. Good news: we’ve got you covered with Surf Grove RVs—all hooked up, stocked with essentials, and ready to move into.

A-frame Tent Rentals
Enjoy a simple, weather-protected camping experience in one of A-frame tents. Located on campground sites and supported by Surf Grove’s shared campground facilities, these tent-style shelters offer a cozy place to sleep while keeping the experience rooted in classic campground accommodation.

A-Frame Tents
Tofino Camping, But Dry
Take Shelter In A Hard-Sided A-Frame Tent
A weather-protected take on classic tent camping, our hard-sided A-frame tents offer a simple, comfortable campground stay while still relying on shared campground facilities. Perfect for guests looking for a cozy camping experience at Surf Grove. Skip the set-up and settle into your campsite in your A-frame camping shelter, perfect for camping in whatever west-coast weather Mother Nature has in store.
Elevated comforts include a queen-size bed with lumberjack-chic bedding, under-bed gear storage and pleasant bedside lighting. A-frame tents do not include private washrooms, kitchens, or cooking facilities and are supported by Surf Grove’s shared campground amenities. Our A-Frames can accommodate up to two adults, but up to four adults are permitted to stay in the campsite.  This site is not pet friendly.

Surf Grove Policies
The following policies ensure that Surf Grove is safe, fun and fair for everyone—guests, staff and locals (human and otherwise).
Reservations
For Bookings Made Within 120 Days of Arrival  :
Prepayment of the entire balance is required at the time of booking.
For Bookings Made More Than 120 Days Prior to Arrival  :
Prepayment of 50% of the total balance is required at the time of booking.
The remaining balance will be automatically charged to the card on file 119 days prior to the arrival date.
If the automatic charge cannot be processed, the reservation will be canceled should the final payment not be received within 14 days. A standard $35 cancellation fee will apply.
Any changes to your reservation must be received   7 days before arrival in to order avoid penalties.
If you wish to extend your stay, and your site is available, please notify reception before 11:00 a.m. on your scheduled date of departure.
Occupancy Rules    Prices are based on two adults per site. Additional adults are $10 per person per day.
Maximum 4 adults per campsite. The maximum occupancy on a campsite is 6 people if there are children 16 and under in the group.   4 adults + 2 kids = Okay!
2 adults + 4 kids = Okay!
2 adults + 5 kids = Book 2 Sites!
5 adults = Book 2 Sites!
Children 16 years old and under are allowed at no additional charge provided that the total number of occupants does not exceed 6 people per site, with no more than 4 of the total occupants being adults.
RV Rental Sites have a maximum occupancy of 2 adults and 2 children 16 years old or younger. No pets. No exceptions.
To lock in a specific site number during the reservation process, there is a fee. If you choose to not lock in your site number and pay the lock-in fee, you will be assigned to the section you requested, but we are unable to guarantee your selected site number.
Cancellations & Changes
Any cancellations received within 7 days of the scheduled arrival  will result in a forfeiture of your entire payment. Cancellations received before this 7 day period are subject to a $35 cancellation fee, the remainder is to be refunded to the credit card on file.
For any reservations where an auto-payment applies to a remaining balance 119 days prior to arrival, If the automatic charge cannot be processed, the reservation will be canceled should the final payment not be received within 14 days of the attempted charge. A standard $35 cancellation fee will apply.
Any dropped nights within the 7 day period prior to your arrival will result in a forfeiture of payment for each night dropped. In the occasion that a night is dropped and it causes a reservation to be less than our minimum night stay, this will result in a forfeiture of payment for this dropped night. Any nights dropped outside of the 7 days prior to arrival that do not result in your stay becoming less than our minimum required stay are eligible for a full refund.
In the case of any no-shows or early departures, guests will still be responsible for the full amount of their reservation.
Cancellations will only be accepted by email to info@surfgrove.com. Cancellations will not be accepted by phone.
We do not give credit or refunds due to inclement weather. The fun continues rain or shine!
During a pandemic you accept that you are travelling at your own risk knowing fully that travel restrictions and provincial changes can be made any time. If any changes are made municipally, provincially, or federally that prevent you from travelling you accept that our standard cancellation policy still applies.
Reservations modified after booking must continue to satisfy all booking requirements at the time of modification. This includes seasonal minimum stays, arrival and departure restrictions, occupancy limits and other operational requirements.
Rental RV reservations may not be modified if the requested change would result in a one-night gap in the campground reservation calendar during our peak season.
Campers
Campsite permit holders must be at least 19 years of age.
The permit holder is responsible for the conduct and behaviour of their family and other guests.
Where space permits, a maximum of three pieces of shelter equipment are allowed per site—only one of which may be a travel trailer, tent trailer, camper van, motor home or self-propelled camping unit.
Check in time is 2:00 p.m. Check out time is 11:00 a.m.
Check in time in our RV Rental sites is 4:00 p.m. Check out time is 11:00 a.m.
If you wish to change campsites, the change must be cleared with Guest Services prior to occupying the site.
Management reserves the right to refuse entry to any person or animal without notice or reason.
Management reserves the right to refuse service to anyone for violating campground rules, destructive behaviour or creating a disturbance. To protect our campers, trespassers will be prosecuted.
Site Policies
Surf Grove is a family campground. We ask that everyone behaves in a respectful manner.
Music must be off between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and 9:00 a.m. Loud music is not permitted at any time.
Quiet time is 11:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. Excessive noise is not permitted at any time.
Visitors must be off-site by 10:00 p.m. The permit holder is responsible for his or her guests.
Generators are permitted only in the case of a power outage. They must be turned off between the hours of 10pm and 8am.
Any after-hours emergencies can be referred to our onsite security at 250-266-3503. In the case of an acute health emergency, please call 911.
Anyone who fails to comply with campground regulations will be required to vacate the property immediately. No refunds will be provided.
Surf Grove is not responsible for any loss of or damage to any personal or private property. Please keep all valuables stored and locked away.
Environment
Cutting or chopping trees on or around the Surf Grove property is prohibited, as is the burning of any Surf Grove property. Firewood is available for purchase at Guest Services.
We do everything in our power to protect our surrounding woodlands. Please remove any ropes or debris used for tying tarps. Do not attach ropes or affix anything to any young and sensitive trees or bushes.
Do not forage for wood or damage the bushes or other flora. Roasting sticks are available to purchase at the office.
Inflatable hot tubs and pools are not permitted as they use an excessive amount of water in order to fill. Tofino’s water supply is limited and no excessive use of resources is permitted on campground property.
Fires on Surf Grove property
All fires must be contained to a fire ring.
Fire rings are not to be moved.
All fires must be monitored by an adult.
Fire rings are not to be used for burning garbage.
Please only use CLEAN firewood (no metals, nails, paint, stains or other chemicals). Bundles of firewood are available for purchase at Guest Services.
On Cox Bay Beach, fires of any kind are prohibited. This is strictly enforced by law, and may result in being required to vacate Surf Grove Campground.
Pets
Limit of 2 pets per campsite.
All pets must be registered at time of check in.
Pets of non-registered guests are not permitted.
Pets are permitted in “ Pet Friendly “ sites only and not in any of our Surf Grove Rental RVs or A Frames.
A One Time charge of $20.00 per pet will be charged to the guest’s account.
Only pets that are friendly and up to date with their vaccinations are permitted on property.
Pet’s must be leashed at all times while on Surf Grove Property.
I/We acknowledge that our pet/dog has had no prior aggressive incidents towards other animals or humans.
Cox Bay is considered a public area and Tofino By-Law prohibits unleashed pets on all local beaches.
We ask for your cooperation in not leaving your pet unattended in your site for extended periods of time.
Pets that bark excessively, act in an aggressive manner or disturb other guests or resort staff will not be tolerated.
Owners must pick up after their pets immediately. This includes Surf Grove property & on the Beach.
Pet owners are reminded that Cox Bay serves as a natural habitat to many migratory shorebirds and to please ensure their pet is under control at all times. Disrupting the daily routines of the resident wildlife is not tolerated and can result in a fine.
Vehicles
All vehicles must be registered at Guest Services. A valid parking pass should be on display at all times.
We are a family campground. Kids are at play throughout our property. Therefore, a speed limit of 8km/h (5mph) is enforced.
Parking for one vehicle (or two motorcycles) is included in your campsite rental. Additional parking passes can be purchased for $10.
Vehicles are not to be parked on any grass or roadway, or in other campsites. Additional parking is available near the Guest Services office.
Washing of vehicles on Surf Grove property is not permitted.
Use of dirtbikes or ATVs is prohibited on Surf Grove property.
Garbage and food waste
Please use our garbage, recycling and compost services.
Cleaning fish on Surf Grove property is not permitted. Your fish must be cleaned, and the remains disposed of, before you return to the campground.  Any fish or garbage odor in individual campsites is a serious bear attractant, and therefore poses a danger to all campers.
Campsites must be kept clean and free of food scraps.  We have bears for neighbours, and unfortunately    a fed bear is a dead bear.
To ensure our property is safe for both guests and local wildlife, our staff will remove any food or garbage that is left unattended and/or is not stored in a vehicle or other safe location.
Beach use
Absolutely no tenting is permitted on Cox Bay Beach. This is strictly enforced, and may result in being required to vacate Surf Grove Campground.
Fires of any sort are prohibited on Cox Bay Beach. This is strictly enforced by bylaw, and may result in being required to vacate Surf Grove Campground.
Please clean up after yourself. Remember: leave only footprints.

A message from Daniel
I’m Daniel, the founder of Schnerp.
It started with one campsite I couldn’t get. I watched my dates vanish into a 60,000-person queue, and the only advice anyone had was to keep refreshing the reservation page and hope someone cancels.
That felt like the wrong job for a person but the perfect one for a computer. So I built a small tool to watch the calendar for me and let me know the moment something opened. It worked. I got the trip.
Then a friend asked me to set up an alert for him. Then his friends. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a favour and became the thing I do. Now it’s for people I’ll never meet, every one of them facing the same “sold out” screen that once stopped me. The reason has never changed: someone should be watching, even when you’ve looked away.
More than a million alerts later, I’ve learned that sold out doesn’t mean forever. Surf Grove sits on Canada’s #1 surf beach, so of course it books up. But life happens, people cancel, and the surf trip you’d written off is usually still within reach. That’s why I’m proud to be Surf Grove’s official waitlist: the moment your dates open up, you’ll be the first to know.
So go live your life. I’ll keep an eye on the calendar.
