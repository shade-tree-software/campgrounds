Welcome to Gorge Harbour
Gorge Harbour Marina Resort is a waterfront destination on Cortes Island, B.C., at the gateway to Desolation Sound.
Whether you arrive by land or sea, our year-round marina resort offers marina moorage, waterfront accommodations, glamping domes, RV sites, camping, and resort amenities in one of British Columbia's most spectacular coastal settings.
​
Located along the northwest shore of Gorge Harbour, guests can relax by the outdoor pool and hot tub, explore the Discovery Islands, or simply enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding rainforest and ocean.
​
Proudly 100% owned by the Klahoose First Nation, we invite you to experience authentic coastal hospitality where comfort, adventure, and connection come together.

BOOKING POLICIES
BOOKING POLICY
A deposit of 30% of the total booking amount, including applicable taxes, is required at the time of booking.  Balance is due at check-in.
CANCELLATION POLICY
A deposit of 30% of the total booking amount, including taxes, is required at the time of booking.
Deposits are non refundable for cancellations made within:
• 30 days of arrival for Harbour House, Lodge Rooms, Cottage, Domes, and Trailers
• 14 days of arrival for RV Sites and Tent Sites
The remaining balance is due at check in and is non refundable.
RATES & TAXES
All rates are in Canadian Dollars, per person based on double occupancy, unless otherwise stated.
All rates and fees are subject to tax at 5% GST and 11% Hotel Tax (8% PST and 3% MRDT).
Should other taxes or fees be required they will be advised. All rates, taxes or fees are subject to change without prior notice.
MINIMUM STAY
All accommodations have a 3 night minimum stay.
We accept stays for 1 or 2 nights with an $85 surcharge.
Harbour House accepts stays for 1 or 2 nights with a $120 surcharge.
CHILDREN & FAMILY STAYS
Children are welcome at Gorge Harbour Marina Resort. We’re happy to host families and encourage you to contact us directly with any questions about accommodations, amenities, or planning a stay with little ones. We’re here to help make your visit comfortable and enjoyable for all ages.
PET POLICY
We love animals, but to ensure the comfort and safety of all guests, pets are not permitted in the Lodge Rooms or Cottage.

RV SITES & CAMPING ON CORTES ISLAND
Open year-round, Gorge Harbour Marina Resort offers a camping experience surrounded by rainforest, mature trees, and the natural beauty of Cortes Island. Whether you're travelling in your own RV, setting up camp on a tent platform, or looking for a glamping getaway, you'll enjoy easy access to resort amenities and the relaxed atmosphere of Gorge Harbour.
FULL-SERVICE RV SITES
Choose from 21 full-service RV sites, each equipped with 30-amp power, water, and sewer connections. Sites are numbered RV 1-21 and offer a mix of treed, settings throughout the resort.
TENT PLATFORMS
Experience camping with a little extra comfort on one of our seven elevated tent platforms. Built on spacious 18 ft x 18 ft wooden decks, these walk-in only sites are located in a peaceful, vehicle-free area surrounded by nature.
Tent platforms are identified as Sites A-F and share access to a nearby washing area with power available for charging devices. Washrooms, coin-operated showers, and laundry facilities are located a short walk away in the main lodge washhouse.

RESORT GROUNDS & OUTDOOR SPACES
Set on more than seven acres of waterfront greenspace, the resort is surrounded by rainforest ravines, mature trees, and expansive grassy areas within a historic apple orchard. Deer are frequently spotted throughout the property, adding to the peaceful atmosphere.
​
Guests can gather at the campground gazebo for potlucks, BBQs, happy hours, and family reunions, or spend the evening around the waterfront campfire area overlooking Gorge Harbour.
IMPORTANT INFORMATION
Please note that there is no communal cooking or dishwashing station available for RV and tenting. RV and tenting guests are encouraged to use their onboard kitchen facilities and campers should bring their own cooking equipment.
