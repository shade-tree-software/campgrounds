Embrace the Great Outdoors in Sunshine Valley
Located just 15 minutes east of Hope, BC, Sunshine Valley RV Resort & Cabins is your ultimate year-round destination for outdoor fun and relaxation. Surrounded by the majestic Cascade Mountains, our resort offers 110 large, 50-amp serviced sites and ten cozy, fully-equipped cabins, ensuring a comfortable stay no matter the season.
Adventure Awaits Every Season
No matter the season, Sunshine Valley brings the outback to your front door! Whether you’re looking to hit the slopes or explore the trails, you’ll find it here:
WINTER WONDERLAND
Enjoy skiing, snowboarding, and snowshoeing at nearby  Manning Park . Our trails also transform into perfect routes for snowmobiling.
SUMMER EXPLORATIONS
Hike through miles of scenic trails, ride ATVs or dirt bikes, or take a leisurely boat ride on nearby waterways. In the colder months, these waters become a picturesque ice skating haven.
Resort Amenities for Ultimate Comfort and Fun
Our resort is equipped with all the amenities to enhance your stay:
POOLS AND HOT TUBS
Dive into our indoor and outdoor heated pools or relax in the hot tubs available year-round.
RECREATION
Enjoy access to fire pits, a fitness centre, and a recreation hall with a wood-burning fireplace.
CONVENIENCE ON SITE
Visit our General Store for necessities including beer, wine, and liquor, or utilize our propane filling station.
COZY CABIN GETAWAYS
For those who prefer a more enclosed setting, choose from our 10 mountain-style furnished cabins
LEARN MORE
Take in the view from dip our heated pool!
Sunshine Valley Cabins: Your Home in the Wild
For those who prefer a more enclosed setting, choose from our 10 mountain-style cabins.
FULLY FURNISHED COMFORT
Each one and two-bedroom cabin is fully furnished with a wood stove, full kitchen, and all the essentials—just bring your food and clothes!
STAY CONNECTED
Free WIFI is available in each cabin for up to two devices, ensuring you stay connected while enjoying the wilderness.
Some pet-friendly cabins are currently offered.
Discover the charm of Sunshine Valley’s deluxe mountain cabins, where the spirit of camping meets modern comfort and convenience.

What activities are available at Sunshine Valley RV Resort & Cabins?
Guests can enjoy skiing, snowboarding, hiking, ATVing, dirt-biking, snowmobiling, and boating depending on the season.
What types of accommodations are available?
We have 110 large 50-amp serviced sites and ten cabins available year-round.
Are the pools and hot tubs available year-round?
Yes, our indoor pools and hot tubs are heated and available year-round.
Can I host an event at Sunshine Valley RV Resort & Cabins?
Yes, our Great Room is available for private functions, including weddings and other gatherings.
Are there any rental restrictions I should be aware of?
Our cabins come with free WIFI for up to two devices, and pets are generally welcome, though restrictions may apply.

Pipeline Update as of end of August 2023
The Trans Mtn pipeline is mostly finished installing the pipeline through Camperland. The route was through the Bridal Falls Waterpark, past the Mini Golf through the overflow parking area, through the dog park and through the upper area of Shady Lane which affected 28 full-service sites.
The status is as follows.
The tenting area and the access to the Waterpark from Camperland is complete.
The Parking lot next to the playground is fully paved now.
Sites 133 to 136 and 124 to 128 have been fully restored.
The dog run has been reseeded and the grass is starting to grow.
We are waiting for Hydro to finish their work so that the next 12 sites can be completed.
The roadways that were damaged by the heavy equipment are now fully paved.
We will be using fencing to divide the sites instead of cedars (as they tend to die a lot). This work will be done over the next few months.
This means that we are still short of 12 sites awaiting BC Hydro.
We appreciate your patience and understanding.
