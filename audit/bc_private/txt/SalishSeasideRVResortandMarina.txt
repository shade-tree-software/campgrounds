BIG RIG HAVEN
The park has been completely redesigned to be truly "Big Rig Friendly". It comprises 37 pads of which all but a very few will accommodate the largest Class A Motorhomes. Most sites are waterfront with spectacular views around Victoria's harbour.
View Sites
FLOAT HOME RENTAL
L’eau Rider offers the perfect base to explore all that Victoria has to offer. From your dock, you can kayak or paddleboard through both the inner and outer harbours, and when you're ready for a meal in town, hop on a Victoria Harbour ferry—your taxi to the best local dining!

MOORAGE
Enjoy Victoria’s West Bay. We offer nightly, monthly and annual moorage in our unique float home village.

Our Nation’s History
Located on a small peninsula across from the Victoria’s inner harbour, our resort is a world-famous destination to explore unique indigenous history, local shopping, sightseeing, and some of the most vibrant outdoor activities.

Getting Here
Salish Seaside RV Resort and Marina
445 Head Street, Victoria BC V9A 5S1
FROM NORTH VANCOUVER ISLAND
From Up Island
Take the #1 Highway south. Exit at the McKenzie Avenue/Admirals Road interchange, follow Admirals Road south and turn left onto Lyall Street. Stay on Lyall Street, we are at the very end.
FROM THE COHO FERRY (DOWNTOWN)
When exiting the ferry terminal, turn left onto Belleville Street. Continue on Belleville until you merge onto Blanshard Street (highway 17). Continue on Blanchard St for around 6KM and take exit 7 heading west on McKenzie Avenue. Once you cross the Trans-Canada Highway, McKenzie Avenue will turn into Admirals Rd. Continue until you hit Lyall Street and take a left. Stay on Lyall Street and we are at the very end.
FROM SWARTZ BAY (SIDNEY)
Take the Patricia Bay Highway south, come off at highway exit 7 and turn right (west) onto McKenzie Avenue. Once you cross the Trans-Canada Highway, Mackenzie Avenue will turn into Admirals Rd. Continue until you hit Lyall Street and take a left. Stay on Lyall Street and we are at the very end.
