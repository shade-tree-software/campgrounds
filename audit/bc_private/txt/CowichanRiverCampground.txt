Cowichan River Campground Information
To help make your stay a pleasant one, we have prepared some guidelines for your convenience and would appreciate your cooperation.
Thank you for taking the time to read these rules. We shall endeavor to make your stay a pleasant one!
CHECK-IN,   2:00 P.M.
CHECK-OUT,   11:00 A.M.
Please note:  you do not have to check-in at the office, you can go straight to your site. Security will come around and check your ID and reservation as per your terms and conditions on campspot.com at time of booking.
Office Hours
Monday to Friday: 8:30 A.M. – 4:30 P.M.
Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 A.M. – 2:00 P.M.
QUIET HOURS
From 10:00 P.M. to 8:00 A.M. Radios and T.V. must be at level (at all hours) that NO ONE is disturbed at ANY time.
No loud parties or other excessive noise will be tolerated. Guest Curfew: 10:00 P.M. No ball teams, or players.
The management reserves the right to restrict entry to any objectionable person or persons who may cause a disturbance or become a nuisance.
VEHICLES
Park posted SPEED LIMIT of 10 km/h must be observed at all times. PLEASE CAUTION YOUR GUESTS.
Do not block or park your vehicles in vacant sites. The site could be assigned to someone checking in. If your vehicle blocks a site, you will be charged for that site.
GUESTS
You are responsible for your visitor’s conduct and observance of the park rules. The right to restrict entry to any objectionable people or persons who may cause a disturbance or become a nuisance, is reserved. The management is sole judge of the existence of cause for such action. Inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.
We reserve the right to establish a per-day-rate to be charged for guests.
NOTICE to GUESTS
This property is privately owned, and management reserves the right to refuse service to anyone and will not be responsible for accidents or injury to guests or for loss of money, jewelry or valuable of any kind.
CHILDREN
Children must be supervised at all times at the washrooms and never be left unattended.
PETS
ALL DOGS must be LEASHED everywhere on the property at ALL TIMES. They must not be left unattended, must be cleaned up after, must be kept quiet and must be kept out of the washrooms. Failure to follow these rules will result in you being asked to leave!
GARBAGE
Please refer to the campground map for garbage bin locations. Note: disposal of garbage at Cowichan River Campground is only for patrons staying here. There is a recycle bin near the cabins; please see the map.
SERVICES
Tampering with park electric service or connections is strictly forbidden. Please contact the office if you encounter any difficulties.
SEWER
Please SECURE the sewer discharge hose with the proper fitting so that it doesn’t fall into main sewer line, or spill onto the ground.
CAMPFIRES
Fires are permitted in assigned fire pits ONLY (when no fire ban is in place). Firepits are for firewood and paper only, NO burning of building/construction materials, garbage or plastics is allowed.
OTHER
Please treat the washrooms as if this "IS" your “home away from home” for the enjoyment of everyone. A washroom door code will be emailed to you once you have booked and signed all terms and conditions on Campspot.com
Violations of campground rules & regulations, Terms & Conditions on campspot.com at time of booking, complaints, suggestions, etc.: The management encourages the direct communication of violations of any rules and regulations between the occupants. These rules are reasonable, and reasonable people will adhere to them for the common good.
When giving out your address for a taxi, pizza delivery, or to guests or friends, please specify that you are in the R.V. park to avoid delays.
If something is in need of repair—please tell us!
If you need to contact us after office hours for Security, an emergency or problem, please call 250-252-6614
