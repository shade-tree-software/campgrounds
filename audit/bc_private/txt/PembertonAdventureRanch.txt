about us
Farm life,
mountain air, memories that last.
Pemberton Adventure Ranch is a working farm and outdoor retreat set against the dramatic peaks of the Pemberton Valley, just a short drive from Whistler. Choose your adventure: unwind in a premium glamping retreat, arrive in your RV and settle in for the long haul, or pitch a tent and sleep under a sky full of stars. With wide open pastures, mountain views, and fresh country air, this is BC's wild side done right.
Stay your way at the ranch
Pemberton sits in the Pemberton Valley, surrounded by Coast Mountains and glaciers. Within a short drive:
Joffre Lakes Provincial Park
Whistler Blackcomb
Pemberton Valley Golf Club
Blackcomb Helicopters
