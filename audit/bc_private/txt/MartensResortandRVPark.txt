Martens Resort is a lakefront destination in Lac la Hache, BC, set along Timothy Lake in the heart of the Cariboo. We offer log cabins, RV sites, and a relaxed setting for families, couples, and groups looking to enjoy a true BC lake getaway.
​
Guests have direct access to boating, fishing, and swimming, along with essential amenities for a comfortable stay. Martens Resort is also a popular choice for intimate lakefront weddings, family reunions and corporate retreats, with accommodations that allow everyone to stay on-site.
