Welcome to Crazy Creek
A hot pools resort in the forest, made for sharing
Crazy Creek Hot Pools Resort sits in the forest where Crazy Creek meets the Eagle River in Malakwa, BC, on the Trans-Canada Highway between Revelstoke and Sicamous. Families, friend groups and couples come for the outdoor hot pools, stay in a forest cabin or bring the RV, and spend their evenings around the fire.
Our pools are filled with geothermally heated water. It isn't a natural hot spring, just clean, hot water with the mountains all around, every day of the year.

Questions guests ask before they come
Is Crazy Creek a natural hot spring?
No. Our pools are filled with geothermally heated water, not a natural hot spring. There are 40°C, 37°C and 32°C pools, plus an 8°C cold plunge.
Are the hot pools included with a cabin stay?
Yes. Every cabin and suite stay includes hot pool passes for all registered guests. Campground guests buy passes at the pool and save up to 55% on multi-night passes.
Do I need to book to visit the hot pools?
No booking is needed. Drop in any day from 9 am to 10 pm and buy a single dip or a day pass on site. From 9 to 10 pm the pools are adults only.
Is Crazy Creek open in winter?
Yes. The hot pools, cabins and campground are open every day of the year. In winter, two hot pools and the cold plunge are open, and select campsites are plowed. Check DriveBC for Highway 1 conditions before you travel.
How far is Crazy Creek from Revelstoke and Sicamous?
Crazy Creek is in Malakwa, BC, 30 minutes west of Revelstoke and about 20 minutes east of Sicamous on the Trans-Canada Highway. Kamloops and Kelowna are about 2 hours away, and Calgary and Vancouver about 5 hours.
Is Crazy Creek a good base for snowmobiling?
Yes. Eight riding zones are within 30 minutes, from Eagle Pass (3 minutes) to Boulder (30 minutes). There is trailer parking in our large lot, plus powered sled trailer parking beside the cabins for an extra charge. Our  snowmobiling basecamp guide  has staging, conditions and avalanche links for each zone.
Is Crazy Creek good for families?
Yes. Several cabins have bunk rooms, and kids love the playground, the pump track and the river.
Can we bring our dog?
Yes, dogs are welcome: up to two per cabin or campsite. A pet fee applies to cabin stays.
Can you host a family reunion or a large group?
Yes. Families and friends can mix cabins, RVs and tents on one property, and with the campground and the play field we can host groups of up to 500. Our meeting room seats 64 at tables or 100 for a presentation.
What time is check-in?
Check-in is at 4 pm for cabins and suites and 2 pm for campsites. Check-out is at 11 am for both.
Is there food on site?
A food truck runs on site from May long weekend to Thanksgiving, and the resort store sells firewood. Every cabin and suite has a kitchen. For a meal out, see  where we like to eat nearby .

Spring and fall camping in BC
How to spend a rainy day at Crazy Creek
Spring and fall bring cool, misty days to the Eagle River valley. Here at the campground, that's the good stuff. This is how a wet day goes, from first coffee to the last log on the fire.
Step 1 · Morning  Coffee under the awning
Let the rain drum on the awning while the coffee brews. Pull your chair under cover, breathe in the wet forest and take your time. There's nowhere you need to be.
Step 2 · Evening  A hot soak in the rain
When the pool lights come on, grab your towels and walk over. Sink into the hottest pool while cool raindrops land on your face, then dare each other into the cold plunge.
Step 3 · Night  Back to the fire
Head back to your site warm to the core, get the fire crackling and pull up a chair. Let the weather do whatever it wants.

Good to know
Camping questions, answered.
Updated  October 2026
Is Crazy Creek a good place for camping near Revelstoke?
Yes. Our RV park and campground has 97 RV and tent sites in the forest, three hot pools a short walk from your site, and it's open all year. We're 30 minutes west of Revelstoke on the Trans-Canada Highway and 20 minutes east of Sicamous and Shuswap Lake. Kelowna and Kamloops are about 2 hours away; Calgary and Vancouver are about 5.
What size RV will fit?
Our XL pull-through sites take the biggest motorhomes and fifth wheels. As a rough guide, sites run about 35 ft for Forest full hookup, 50 ft for Premium, 60 ft for XL pull-through, 25 ft for Budget power & water, and 20 ft for small RV and tent sites. Not sure? Call us and we'll help you pick the right site for your rig.
Which sites have 50 amp service?
Our Forest Full Hookup sites (20) and some XL Pull-Through sites (4) have 50 amp service, along with water and sewer. Look for "50" in the site name when you book.
Is this a hot springs campground?
Our hot pools are filled with geothermally heated water from a well on site, not a natural hot spring. You still get three steaming pools and a cold plunge a short walk from your campsite.
Are the hot pools included with a campsite?
Pool passes aren't included with campsites, but campground guests save up to 55% on passes for their whole stay, or 20% off a single dip. No booking needed.  See campground pool rates .
Can we camp near friends staying in a cabin?
Yes. RV sites are mixed in among the cabins, so families and groups can stay close together. See our  cabins , or plan a reunion or group trip on our  Family & Friends  page.
Can I bring a tent?
Yes. Tents are welcome on any site, and our forest sites are a favourite.
Is the campground open in winter?
Yes, camping is open year-round. We plow select sites through the winter, and they show as available when you book. Washrooms and showers stay open all winter, and water stays on in the forest sites. The sani-dump stays open too, but without water from Thanksgiving to May long weekend.
What are check-in and check-out times?
Campsite check-in is at 2 pm and check-out is at 11 am.
Can I bring my dog?
Yes, up to two dogs per site. Please keep them on a leash and don't leave them alone.
Can we have a campfire?
Yes. Every site has a fire pit, and campfires are allowed all year except during a regional fire ban. Firewood is sold at the resort store.
Can I run a generator?
Yes, from 9 to 11 am and from 6 to 8 pm.
Is there cell service?
Yes, cell service is strong at the campground, and Wi-Fi covers the property.
Is there room for a boat or an extra vehicle?
Yes. Extra vehicles and boats can park in the main parking lot.
Is it quiet?
Crazy Creek sits in the forest beside the Trans-Canada Highway and the rail line, so you'll hear some traffic and passing trains, day and night. Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am.
Is there food on site?
The food truck runs from May long weekend to Thanksgiving, and the resort store carries snacks, camp supplies and firewood.
What is your cancellation policy?
Our deposit and cancellation terms, including the terms for groups booking four or more sites, are on our  Resort Policies  page.

Is there WiFi?
Yes, every cabin and suite has WiFi.
What about food?
We recommend local caterers for group meals, and every cabin has a kitchen. A food truck is on site from May long weekend to Thanksgiving.
Are the hot pools included?
Yes, for everyone staying in a cabin or suite. Anyone camping in an RV gets  discounted rates on pool passes . The pools are open 9 am to 10 pm daily, and the last hour is adults only.
Can we have a campfire?
Yes, at the large group fire pit and at your cabin's own fire pit, subject to BC fire bans. Firewood is sold at the resort store.
What are the deposit and cancellation terms?
We'll go over them when we confirm your booking. Our current terms are on the  Resort Policies  page.
