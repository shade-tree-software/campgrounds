Take a break on your drive in Muncho Lake Provincial Park and stay at our lakeside RV park only 2.5 hours between Fort Nelson, BC and Watson Lake, Yukon.
Full washroom, coin shower, laundry facilities, boat dock, gazebos, fire pits and sewer dumping station are available for use by RV guests. Fill up at our gas station and enjoy 20 cents off per litre with your overnight stay.
Book a meal plan with your reservation and enjoy an all-inclusive Swiss-inspired dining experience in our restaurant.
