About Us
Looking for a break from the hustle and bustle of the big city? Enjoy some outdoor time at our RV park and campground outside Kamloops. Here you’ll find a relaxing oasis where you can recharge and enjoy the great outdoors. Nearby activities include hiking, fishing, horseback riding, boating, and more. Getting in touch with Mother Nature doesn’t have to mean hacking your way through miles and miles of untamed wilderness. Our campground and many amenities means you can customize your camping experience to a level of ruggedness in your comfort zone.
We are able to accommodate both tent camping and larger RVs. Our campground is equipped with water, 30 AMP electrical and sani-dump systems for RVs and offers large shaded areas along the S. Thompson River for tent campers. All guests have access to our washrooms and laundromats so you’re able to clean up after a long day of adventuring. Weekly rates are available for RV sites.
Call us today to book your stay and enjoy some time in nature.
Reservations are recommended but drop-ins welcome!

FAQs
How do I make a reservation?
​
Please call our office at   (250) 828-2077   between 9 AM and 8 PM PST, or email us at   info@silversagerv.ca  .
​
What are you  r rates?
Serviced sites (30 AMP power & water):
$49 per night
$289 per week
Unserviced sites:
$39 per night
Sewer service is not available at individual sites; however, a sani-dump station is available on-site for guest use at no additional charge.
Rates are based on two adults. Additional guests over the age of 9 are $5 per night.
All rates are subject to change.
​
Do you offer monthly or long-term rates?
No, we do not offer monthly or long-term rates.
​
Do you offer group rates?
Group rates may be available for unserviced (tenting) sites depending on availability. Please contact us directly to inquire.
​
What services do you offer?
We offer:
30 AMP power and water hook-ups at serviced sites
Free public toilets and showers
Sani-dump station
Wi-Fi
Coin laundry
What are your laundry facilities and hours?
Laundry is available on-site.
Wash: $5.00
Dry: $3.00
Laundry room hours: 8:30 AM – 8:00 PM
Last wash cycle starts at 6:30 PM
Prices and hours are subject to change.
​
What months are you open?
We are open from April 1 to October 15.
​
Can I make a reservation for next year?
We accept reservations for the current season and begin taking bookings for the upcoming season on March 1.
​
How do I get to your campground?
We are located off Highway 5 (Yellowhead Highway) at Mount Paul Way in Kamloops.
If coming from Highway 1, take Exit 374 toward Sun Peaks/Jasper and continue north on Highway 5.
Please avoid using directions through downtown Kamloops, as they may route you over a narrow bridge unsuitable for larger vehicles.
Turn onto Mount Paul Way and follow signage to the campground.
​
What is your refund policy?
All deposits are non-refundable.
Once payment is made upon check-in, there are no refunds.
If your plans are uncertain, we recommend booking closer to your arrival date.
