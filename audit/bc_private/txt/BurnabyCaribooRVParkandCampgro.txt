Why Choose Us?
Nestled in the heart of the Lower Mainland, Burnaby Cariboo RV Park offers the perfect blend of convenience, comfort, and adventure.
Located just minutes from Vancouver’s top attractions, premier shopping centres and a short walk to the SkyTrain, getting around the city has never been easier.
Proudly rated among the top 3% of RV parks in North America since 1986, we provide exceptional service and top-tier amenities. Enjoy full hook-up RV sites, modern facilities, and a variety of recreational opportunities, including an indoor heated pool, hot tub, fitness centre, and scenic outdoor spaces. Whether you’re here for a short getaway or an extended stay, experience the best in comfort, convenience, and hospitality at Burnaby Cariboo RV Park!

Frequently Asked Questions
What amenities are available at Burnaby Cariboo RV Park?
We offer  full hook-up sites, free Wi-Fi, an indoor pool and hot tub, laundry facilities, a fitness center, a games room, and a playground , ensuring a comfortable stay for all guests.
How close is the RV park to Vancouver attractions?
Our park is centrally located in Burnaby, just a 10-12 minute walk from the SkyTrain and a short drive to top attractions like Stanley Park, Granville Island, and Metrotown Mall.
Are reservations required, and how can I book a stay?
While drive-ins are welcome, we strongly recommend booking in advance, especially during peak seasons. Reservations can be made online or by phone.
Book Your Stay
Is the RV park pet-friendly?
Yes! We welcome pets and even have a   designated dog walk area  . We just ask that pets remain leashed and supervised at all times.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in time is 1:00 PM, and check-out is 12:00 PM. If you need a late checkout, please contact the office in advance to check availability.
