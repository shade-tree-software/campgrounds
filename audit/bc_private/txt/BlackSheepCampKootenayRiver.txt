Kootenay River
canal flats, British Columbia
roam. venture. stay.
Roam to Camp Kootenay River located in the scenic  Columbia Valley  with the world-class Kootenay River running alongside. Venture from the campground in your off-road vehicle to the mountains or mountain bike along the forestry service roads that track alongside the river or through wooded trails, meadows and mountain tops. Get wet and paddle the river, raft the river or set into Columbia Lake, just 10 minutes away from the campground. Hot springs and rivers through-out Columbia Valley offer incredible views while hiking, OHV’ing, and bicycling if you prefer to stay dry. Stay at a full-service or power site, a boondocking no service site, or a SoftTop tent with modern hand-made furniture, linens, a deck with chairs and power. Enjoy the boundless recreation and pristine wilderness that our location offers and then relax at the bonfire and stare at the stars. We look forward to welcoming you.

camp  location, recreation & amenities
The campground is located on the shores of the Kootenay River and minutes away from the shores Columbia Lake. Making the location even more spectacular is that we are in the valley between the Rocky Mountain and Purcell Mountain range. Every adventure you can dream of is accessible from Camp Kootenay River. OHV’s can rumble from your site out to the Forestry Service Road alongside the camp and up to the mountains or along the river. You can hike or bike from the campground, set-in your kayak or canoe or raft from the campground, or trailer to Columbia Lake or other nearby lakes, rivers – and hot springs.
The campground offers scenic camp sites, from full-service to power only to no service boondocking. We also provide fully furnished glamping tents with beds, bedding, furniture, deck & power for your convenience if you want to camp but don’t want to gear up. We have an office with supplies & sundries, sport equipment rental, propane, eV chargers, playground, volleyball court, park area, picnic tables, fire pits, with the river only steps away. The town of Canal Flats is minutes away with a gas station, an incredible coffee house, a pub open on weekends, and grocery store.

guidelines  for a perfect stay
Camp Guidelines:
Check-out time 11AM.
Check-in time 4PM -8PM.
Please notify the office if you plan to stay longer or leave sooner than planned.
Pets are welcome, but we insist that dogs are quiet and on a leash.
Visitors are welcome but must leave the campground by 9:30PM.
Quiet time is 10pm to 8am
Boondocking generator hours 9am-11am and 6pm-8pm
Charges are per sleeping unit maximum, not per site.
Please respect the one vehicle per site parking, visitors must park in overflow parking.
Campground speed limit is 10km/hr.
Please respect that there is no alcohol or smoking of any substance in public areas of campground.  Rude language and behavior, public intoxication, fighting or causing disruption to the health and wellbeing of all other guests and management will be cause for immediate eviction.
Please note that no area mats or rugs are permitted on the grass areas.
ATVs are not permitted for use in the campground but can idle in, idle out to the FSR is alongside with access to all recreation.  We hope you enjoy all the amazing trails in the area.
Black Sheep Ventures inc., its staff and contractors do not take any responsibility for any natural or unnatural events or weather.
We hope you enjoy your time at the river but please be aware it is at your own risk.
Black Sheep Ventures inc., its staff and contractors do not take any responsibility for any use and recreation in the Kootenay River.
What happens when I need to Cancel my reservation?
Reservations cancelled more than 1 week prior to the scheduled arrival date, are subject to a $15 cancellation fee, the remaining balance will be refunded.
Is there a limit for staying consecutive nights?
The maximum stay length is 10 consecutive nights. If you are wanting to stay longer please contact us before booking.
Currency
All pricing is in CAD

Full Hookup Campsites
If you’re looking for a campground near Invermere, BC, Black Sheep Camp Kootenay River offers excellent full hookup RV sites. Each site provides water, sewer, and 30 amp electrical connections, ensuring a comfortable and convenient stay. For those needing less, our power-only sites are perfect, offering electrical service without compromising on access to park amenities. Guests enjoy hot showers, laundry facilities, and our camp store, all within a stunning riverside setting. Whether for a weekend or a long-term stay, our RV sites make the perfect base for your Kootenay Rockies adventure. Best of all, our full hookup RV sites are available year-round, with winter-ready hookups so you can camp comfortably even in colder months.

Tips for RV Camping in BC
August 28, 2026
RV camping is a great way to explore the outdoors and enjoy the freedom of the open road. Whether you’re a seasoned RV camper or a first-timer, here are some tips to help you make the most of your RV camping experience. Black Sheep Camp Kootenay River has tons of great  RV spots available to book!
RV Camping in BC
Plan
Firstly, plan ahead. Research campgrounds and RV parks in the area you plan to visit and make reservations in advance. This will help ensure that you have a spot to park your RV and can avoid any last-minute stress.
Checklists
Secondly, make a checklist. Before you hit the road, make a list of everything you need to bring, from bedding and towels to cooking utensils and toiletries. This will help you stay organized and avoid forgetting any essential items.
Pack Light
Thirdly, pack light. While it’s tempting to bring everything you think you might need, RVs have limited storage space. Only bring what you need, and consider leaving non-essential items at home.
Practice
Fourthly,  practice safe driving . RVs are larger and heavier than most vehicles, so it’s important to take extra precautions when driving. Drive at a safe speed, use your mirrors to check blind spots, and be aware of low clearance bridges and overpasses.
Be Aware
Fifthly, conserve water and electricity. RVs have limited water and electricity supplies, so it’s important to use them wisely. Take shorter showers, turn off lights and appliances when not in use, and consider using solar panels or a generator to supplement your power supply. When you are parked with us, we have the water and power hookups, so no need to worry!
Respect Goes Both Ways
Sixthly, respect your neighbours. RV parks and campgrounds can be crowded, so it’s important to be mindful of your neighbours. Keep noise levels down, pick up after yourself, and follow campground rules and regulations.
Lastly, have fun! RV camping is all about enjoying the great outdoors and spending time with family and friends. Take advantage of the beautiful scenery, try new activities, and make memories that will last a lifetime.
RV camping can be a fun and rewarding experience if you plan ahead, pack wisely, and practice safe and respectful behaviour. By following these tips, you can make the most of your  RV camping  adventure and enjoy all that the great outdoors has to offer.

Summer Camping Safety: Tips for a Safe and Enjoyable Trip
July 29, 2026
Hello, fellow outdoor enthusiasts! It’s that time of year again when the flowers are blooming, the birds are singing, and the itch to go camping is just too strong to resist. But before you pack up your tent and hit the trails, it’s important to keep safety in mind to ensure a fun and enjoyable camping trip. As we dive into the Summer camping season of 2026, we’ve rounded up some tips and trendy products to help you stay safe and stylish on your outdoor adventure.
Summer Camping Saftey
Check the Weather:  While summer brings warmer temperatures, it can also be unpredictable with sudden rain showers and temperature drops. Be sure to check the weather forecast before you head out and pack accordingly. Don’t forget to bring extra layers, waterproof gear, and a quality tent that can withstand the elements.
Stay Hydrated:  As the temperatures rise, it’s essential to stay hydrated to avoid dehydration. Pack plenty of water bottles or invest in a portable water filtration system to ensure a safe and reliable source of drinking water. And hey, if you want to up your hydration game, why not try a trendy collapsible water bottle that’s not only functional but also eco-friendly? You’ll be the envy of the campsite with this stylish accessory.
Bring a First Aid Kit:  Accidents can happen, so it’s always wise to have  a first aid kit  on hand. Make sure it’s stocked with essentials like bandages, antiseptics, tweezers, and any medications you might need. And for a fun twist, why not get a quirky first aid kit with cute animal-shaped bandages? It’s a conversation starter and a practical safety item all in one.
Practice Fire Safety:  Campfires are a quintessential part of camping, but they can also be dangerous if not handled properly. Follow the rules and regulations of the campsite, and always have a fire extinguisher or a bucket of water nearby. And to add some flair to your campfire experience, why not invest in some trendy fire starters, like colorful firestarter sticks or scented firestarter cubes? You’ll not only light up the fire but also impress your camping buddies with your style.
Mind the Wildlife:  Summer is the season when wildlife comes out of hibernation, so it’s crucial to be mindful of their presence. Keep food and trash in sealed containers to prevent attracting animals to your campsite, and never approach or feed wildlife. Remember, they’re wild animals, not cute pets for photo ops. And to add a touch of wilderness chic to your camping gear, why not get a trendy wildlife-themed camping chair or a bear-shaped camping pillow? It’s the perfect Instagrammable accessory that’s also a reminder to respect nature.
Navigation and Communication:  Whether you’re an experienced hiker or a beginner camper, always bring a map and a compass or a  GPS device  to navigate your way through the wilderness. And if you’re going off the grid, invest in a satellite communication based device for emergencies. But hey, who says navigation and communication can’t be fashionable? Get yourself a trendy camping watch with a built-in compass and GPS, or a chic camping phone holder that doubles as a stylish accessory. You’ll not only stay safe but also look fab in the wilderness.
Stay Protected from the Sun:  With the warmer weather, it’s essential to protect yourself from the sun’s harmful rays. Pack sunscreen with a high SPF, wear a wide-brimmed hat, and bring sunglasses to shield your eyes. And for a touch of summer fashion, why not get a trendy sunhat with a floral pattern or a pair of stylish UV-blocking sunglasses that are both functional and fashionable? You’ll be the camping fashionista of the season!
Be Mindful of Leave No Trace Principles:  As outdoor enthusiasts, it’s our responsibility to protect and preserve nature. Follow the Leave No Trace principles, which include packing out all trash, minimizing campfire impact, respecting wildlife, and staying on designated trails. And to show off your eco-friendly style, why not invest in trendy reusable camping utensils or a cute reusable camping mug? You’ll not only reduce waste but also make a fashion statement for sustainability.
Camp with Friends or Share Your Location:  Camping is more enjoyable when done with others, so try to camp with friends or family members. It’s not only safer, but it’s also a great opportunity to bond and create lasting memories. And if you’re camping solo or going on a hike, always let someone know your plans, including your estimated time of return and your location. You can also share your location with them using a trendy camping app that allows you to track your adventures and stay connected with loved ones.
Have Fun and Embrace the Adventure:  Lastly, remember that camping is all about having fun and embracing the adventure. Be open to new experiences, try new activities, and take in the beauty of nature. Disconnect from the digital world and immerse yourself in the peacefulness of the wilderness. And to capture those memorable moments, why not get a trendy camping-themed instant camera or a stylish camping journal to document your outdoor escapades? You’ll not only have an amazing time, but you’ll also have stylish mementos to cherish for years to come.
Summer camping can be an exciting and fulfilling experience, but it’s important to prioritize safety to ensure a fun and enjoyable trip. From checking the weather to staying hydrated, practicing fire safety, being mindful of wildlife, and following Leave No Trace principles, there are various ways to stay safe while camping. And with trendy camping products for 2023, you can also add a touch of style and flair to your outdoor adventures. So, gear up, pack your camping essentials, and get ready for an unforgettable summer camping trip. Stay safe, stay stylish, and have a blast in the great outdoors!
Happy camping, fellow adventurers!
