Lakeside Cabins & Adventures in the Chilcotin
Cast a line, kick back, and make yourself at home
Woodlands Fishin Resort  is a rustic lakeside fishing resort located on beautiful Puntzi lake in the Chilcotin, British Columbia. Just 2 hours west of Williams Lake along Highway 20, it’s a peaceful place to enjoy cozy cabins, RV camping, and some of the best fishing in the region.
Nestled along the serene shores of  Puntzi Lake , a hidden gem where White Pelicans reside and fish abound, we believe in the power of  simplicity,    good food   , and the    great outdoors   to nourish the soul and soothe the mind.
Whether you’re here to cast a line, kick back, or simply enjoy the quiet beauty of the Chilcotin, Woodlands is a place to slow down, unwind, and feel at home.
Come and experience the quiet beauty of the Chilcotin for yourself.

FAQs
How to get to Woodlands Fishin’ Resort?
Woodlands Fishin’ Resort is located on the north shore of Puntzi Lake in the Chilcotin region of British Columbia. The most common route is via Highway 20 from Williams Lake, in the Cariboo. From Williams Lake, drive west on Highway 20 for approximately 210 km (130 miles), then turn onto the signed road to Puntzi Lake. Follow directional signs to the resort.
What types of accommodations are available at Woodlands Fishing Resort?
We offer cozy   lakefront fully-equipped cabins ,  RV sites    with fu  ll and partia l hook-ups, and   tent camping  options—per fect for families, couples, and solo adventurers alike. We also offer a fifth wheel rental for up to four people which includes the same amenities as our cabins.
Do I need a fishing license to fish at Puntzi Lake?
Yes, a valid British Columbia freshwater fishing license is required for all anglers. You can purchase one online through the BC Government’s website or at nearby license vendors.
Is Woodlands Fishin’ Resort family-friendly?
Absolutely! We welcome guests of all ages and offer a peaceful environment ideal for quality time in nature. There’s plenty of space for kids to play, explore, and enjoy the outdoors.
Are boats or fishing gear available for rent?
Yes, we have boat, canoes and kayak rentals available for guests and daily visitors. You’re welcome to bring your own gear or book rentals with us. Advanced booking is recommended, especially in high season and during the Annual Puntzi Lake Fishing Derby.
What amenities are offered at the resort?
Guests have access to   Puntzi Lake Bakery and Restaurant , a Gift Shop, showers, fire pits , picnic areas, fish cleaning station, kids play area and boat launch facilities. Whether you're here to fish, relax, or explore, we have what you need for a great stay.
What activities are there to do in the area besides fishing?
There's plenty to explore in the Chilcotin! Enjoy hiking and nature walks on scenic local trails, attend unique regional events like the Redstone Rodeo and the   Puntzi Lake Fishing Derby  , or take a drive to nearby sights and Indigenous cultural sites. For more inspiration, check out our   Things to Do   page.
Puntzi Lake – The Destination
Puntzi Lake is situated near the tranquil community of Chilanko Forks, approximately 175 km west of Williams Lake (a roughly 2-hour drive) via the Chilcotin-Bella Coola Highway (BC-20W). As one of the larger lakes in central Chilcotin, Puntzi Lake resides in the heart of the traditional territory of the Tsilhqot'in people and was once home to a sizable village.
Renowned for its abundant population of Rainbow Trout and Kokanee fish, along with its diverse birdlife, Puntzi Lake offers a picturesque and unspoiled natural setting unlike any other within a short distance from Cariboo’s central hub.

The Best RV Camping in the Cariboo Chilcotin
Jun 26
Written By  FMktg .
RV camping   doesn’t get much better than this. If you’re feeling like it’s time to unplug and just breathe for a bit, Woodlands Fishin Resort might be just what you’re looking for. Tucked away on the peaceful shores of Puntzi Lake in the Cariboo Chilcotin, this place has a way of slowing things down… in the best possible way.
If you travel by RV and love waking up to the sound of water lapping against the shore, you're going to feel right at home here. Whether you're casting a line early in the morning, sipping coffee with a view of the lake, or sharing stories around the fire at night, it’s the kind of place that reminds you why you hit the road in the first place.
Some guests come here for the fishing, hoping to reel in a big one. Others come just to spend time with family, breathe fresh mountain air, and reconnect with the outdoors. No pressure, no schedule. Just you, nature, and all the time you need.
We’ve got everything set up to make your stay easy and comfortable. And what really makes this place special is the people.
Lakeside RV Campground: Stay, Relax, Explore
Our little gem? A 12-site, all-grass RV campground right on the edge of beautiful Puntzi Lake in the Cariboo Chilcotin. It’s not just a place to park. It's where mornings start with colourful sunrises over the water and nights end under a sky full of stars. Simple, peaceful, and just the way we like it.
What really makes this spot shine is Puntzi Lake itself. The lake is clear, quiet, and full of fish. Perfect for anyone who loves to cast a line. You’re surrounded by untouched wilderness, so it’s easy to just slow down and soak it all in.
We’ve set up two types of RV sites so you can choose what works best for you:
6 lakefront RV  sites with water and power right by the water with unbeatable views
6 full hookup RV sites  with power, water, and sewer everything you need for a comfy stay
Grassy tent sites  if you’re more into classic camping
Everyone staying here gets free access to our boat launch and four docks, whether you're out fishing or just hanging out by the lake. There’s also a fish cleaning station, clean washrooms, picnic tables, and fire pits for those cozy nights around the fire.
Families will love the playground for the kids. And yes, we’re pet-friendly (just keep them on a leash). If you’ve got an ATV, you’re in luck: there’s easy backroad access to nearby trails. Need to stay connected? We’ve got Wi-Fi. Need to empty the tanks? We’ve got a sani dump too.
And if you’ve always wanted to try the RV life but don’t have one, check out our Fifth Wheel Rental. It’s fully stocked with bedding, towels, dishes: everything you need. It’s set up on a full-service site and has a queen bed, a convertible dinette, and space for a family of four.
RV Camping's Rates and Services
We think getting outside and enjoying nature shouldn’t break the bank, especially when you’re traveling with family or friends in the Cariboo Chilcotin. That’s why we’ve kept our   RV camping pricing   fair and simple. Our base rates include two adults (and kids under 5 stay free), with room to add more people if you’ve got a bigger crew - check them out here.
We’ve also got laundry, a sani-dump, and firewood available for a small extra charge to make things easier for you.
We get it, plans can change. If you need to cancel, we try to keep things fair. Cancel 14 or more days before your stay and there’s a $50 admin fee. If it’s within 14 days, we charge for two nights.
So if you’re dreaming of early mornings on the lake, campfire nights with the family, or just some peace and quiet in one of BC’s most beautiful spots, come stay with us at Woodlands Fishin’ Resort. Whether you roll up in your own RV or book our comfy fifth wheel rental, we’re ready to help you make some unforgettable lakeside memories.
To book with us, click    here   . See you soon!
