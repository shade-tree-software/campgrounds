Good resort
We stayed at this park for our first weekend with our new trailer. We enjoyed the park very much, it was nice and clean and quiet. Backing in our new trailer as easy thanks to the wide roads. The sites were very nice and a good size. The services were also very good. The facilities were excellent. There is an adult area that was perfect for us. The hot to was nice and clean. The washroom facilities were excellent as well. There is lots to offer with several pools, two restaurants, etc. It was our first experience at an RV park and we would go back. Older RV units may be allowed on a case by case basis, which will require a photo and in-person viewing by management prior to checking in.

Holiday Park Leaseholders Association (HPLA)
HPLA provides a channel of communications between RV and Condo sub-leaseholders and Holiday Park management on all matters affecting joint interests. Holiday Park Resort also represents all Timeshare and RV members within the HPLA on their behalf.
Please consider becoming a member of this dedicated group of volunteers, whose efforts in keeping our Resort running smoothly are so very much appreciated.   Please note that in order to have a vote at the Annual General Meeting, one must be a sub-leaseholder.
Click here for a the  current HPLA Executive Committees  and  Membership Application .  Please submit applications by mail to HPLA, drop off at Front Office reception, or email to  holidayparkleaseholders@gmail.com .  Membership applications are also available at the AGM.
Thank you, we appreciate your involvement in your community!

Water, sewer, hydro included.
WI-FI is available at the Recreational Centers
No open fires; BBQs and propane fueled contained fires may be acceptable but may be subject to approval.
Up to 6 people welcome on each site rental.
Use of resort facilities, amenities and 24-hour security included.

Holiday Park Leaseholders Association (HPLA)
HPLA provides a channel of communications between RV and Condo sub-leaseholders and Holiday Park management on all matters affecting joint interests. Holiday Park Resort also represents all Timeshare and RV members within the HPLA on their behalf.
Please consider becoming a member of this dedicated group of volunteers, whose efforts in keeping our Resort running smoothly are so very much appreciated.   Please note that in order to have a vote at the Annual General Meeting, one must be a sub-leaseholder.
Click here for a the  current HPLA Executive Committees  and  Membership Application .  Please submit applications by mail to HPLA, drop off at Front Office reception, or email to  holidayparkleaseholders@gmail.com .  Membership applications are also available at the AGM.
Thank you, we appreciate your involvement in your community!
