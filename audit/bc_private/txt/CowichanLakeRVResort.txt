Welcome to the Lake
Cowichan Lake Recreational Community (CLRC) is located in a spectacular setting on four legal parcels
nestled in Irving Bay on Cowichan Lake.  CLRC Shareholders have access to the private marina, recently constructed docks, designated swim dock and beach area, washroom and showers, laundry, designated parking areas, and on-site caretaker.
This   Site Plan   shows the general layout of our Park.
