History Of Delta Golf Course
At Delta Golf & Country Club, the dynamic relationship between golfer and environment are more closely knit than ever. Designed as nature allowed, our golf course is best experienced while walking and enjoying the magnificent west coast environment.
KNOW MORE
18 Hole Golf Course | 4,933 yards from the Blue Tees
The layout features seven par 3’s, nine par 4’s and two par 5’s
Newly Constructed Clubhouse features a full service restaurant and licensed lounge that overlooks the 9th green and lagoon.

Annual Pass Rules, Regulations & Restrictions
Full payment is due on date of purchase.
Passes are non- transferable and non- refundable.
Weekday annual pass is valid only Monday through Friday excluding holidays.
Rounds are restricted to 120 per a calendar year.
No shows and cancelations within 24 hours will not be refunded.
More than 2 No shows and cancelations within 24 hours will result in loss advanced booking privileges.
All pass holders shall register with the PROSHOP staff prior to using the facilities. Passes do not entitle pass holders unauthorized or unlimited access without the knowledge and consent of the proshop staff.
Only allowed to book 1 tee-time per day. Secondary tee-time on the same day is on stand-by basis.
No special discount for anyone. For example- No discount for seniors, previous pass holders, club- members etc.
Annual Pass is not valid for events or tournaments fees.
Pass holder agrees to pay for any damages to the facilities or grounds or for injuries to any person, caused by annual pass holder. Pass holder agrees to hold Delta Golf & Country Club and its employees, agents, and owners harmless and to defend them against all claims, and suits, for damages or injuries of any nature.
Outside alcohol is not allowed. Consumptions of outside alcohol on DG&CC property will result in immediate termination of Annual Pass
A breach of any terms and conditions of this contract will result in the cancellation of pass holder’s rights and privileges without any reimbursement of any kind.

You Can Join The Club

Tuesday Ladies’ Club
Tuesday Ladies are enjoying their weekly golf with friends alike on our lovely golf course perfect for anyone to join in on the fun!
The purpose of the League is to encourage sportsmanship and good fellowship among its members through participation in League activities, both golf and social.
In addition to weekly friendly competitive play, the League holds many activities, including:
Inter-Club competitions
Annual Pass Holder/Guest tournaments
League match play and other team competitions
Annual 2 day team event, patterned after the Solheim Cup Tournament
Fun mixers and theme tournaments
We strive to create an atmosphere of both high-spirited competition, fair play and game improvement.
To become a member of the ladies club you need to be a member at Delta Golf & Country Club
Delta Senior Wednesday Men’s Club
Wednesday mornings are reserved for our Wednesday Senior Men’s Golf Club.
With a great group of guys that like to challenge each other on the course, come join them!
The membership fee covers Membership in the Delta Wednesday Senior Mens Golf Club, prizes, mailings, hot dog and beer days and a year end luncheon. A variety of events occurs most Wednesdays.
Please go to “ deltawed.golfclub.net  “and click on “New member info”. Under this tab is an application form for joining our club. Once you have joined, further instructions will be emailed to you on how to sign up for weekly events and entering your scores for handicap purposes.
The Delta Golf & Country Club Proshop will be informed of all players who have signed up on a weekly basis.
Thursday Senior Men’s Club
“Whatever the weather the Senior Men get together.”
When the sun is shining, Thursday mornings our Senior Men are on our golf course enjoying a round of golf with their friends, and taking on each other for ‘bragging rights’.
Breakfast at the golf course is always a good start to their Thursday among friends and the chatter of stories.
We welcome anyone new to join their club, and will make you feel welcome.
Check out our website:  deltaseniormensgolf.com  and come join in on the fun for 18 holes!
