RV Park
Stay near the stunning Shuswap Lake at the Salmon Arm Elks RV Park.
Open from  April 1 to October 15 , our park provides a relaxing and well-equipped retreat.   The shoulder seasons are April 1   st    to June 10   th   , and Sept 16   th    to October 15 th .
Most sites are 25ft x 50ft and are at least partially shaded. Dry camping is available.
Group bookings for reunions and club gatherings are welcome!
Call Holly to reserve your site      250-833-4803
Located just 4 km from Canoe Beach, guests enjoy easy access to swimming, boating, hiking trails, and local attractions.

Amenities:
36 spacious RV sites    with full hookups (30-amp power, water, sewer, and Wi-Fi). 20ftx50ft lot sizes.
Family-friendly amenities   , including a playground and picnic areas.
Outdoor gazebo and covered meeting space    available for group gatherings.   Outdoor kitchen available .
Private washrooms and showers    for registered guest only.
New laundromat facility    with additional showers.
Pet-friendly policies    (leash required, owners must clean up after pets).
Quiet hours  from 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM to ensure a peaceful environment.
Close to Canoe Beach  (4 km away) for swimming, boating, and hiking opportunities. includes   and a public launching ramp.
Rules of conduct at the RV Park:
Pets are kept on a leash and owners pick up after them.
Excessive barking is frowned upon.
Quiet time between 11:00 pm and 8:00 am.
Respectful, civil behaviour and language in public at all times.
The management reserves the right to refuse to accommodate disrespectful guests.
No late check-ins after 9pm
