FORT LANGLEY
Fort Camping: Riverside Campground on Brae Island in Fort Langley
Fort Camping is operated by Pathfinder Camp Resorts under license granted by Metro Vancouver.
Fort Camping offers a unique blend of countryside serenity and city convenience in British Columbia’s Fraser Valley. Perfect for families, it provides access to outdoor activities and city attractions alike.
The resort accommodates all camping preferences with 156 RV sites, fire pits, a group tent area, and 5 tent cabins, making it ideal for a range of outdoor enthusiasts. From fishing on the Fraser River to golfing and exploring Brae Island, it’s all within reach.

Frequently Asked Questions
About your stay
What time is your check-in and check-out?
Check-in times throughout the year:
November 11th – April 13th: 1pm – 5pm
April 14th – May 15th: 1pm – 7pm
May 16th – June 25th: 1pm – 8pm
June 26th – September 2nd: 1pm – 9pm
September 3rd – Mid October: 1pm – 8pm
Check-out:  11 am
What is your payment and cancellation policy?
Payment Policy:  A payment of 100% is taken at time of booking.  Online payments are onlytaken by credit card and by providing a credit card you are authorizing  campspot.com —the third party booking provider—to bill your credit card for each payment amount as they come due, including any fees incurred while at the property, and any outstanding balance not paid at the time of departure.
Cancellation policy:  Cancellations are accepted by email only. Please email  info@pathfindercampresorts.com
Campsite reservations cancelled prior to 14 days before arrival are subject to a  $50 cancellation fee .  Campsite reservations cancelled within 14 days of arrival forfeit 100% payment.
30 days’ notice of cancellation is required for all tent cabins, groups of 10+ campsites and group site.   Deposit is forfeited if cancelled within 30 days of arrival.
Can I transfer my reservation?
Reservations are  not transferable . Reservation holder name(s) must be provided at the time of booking and cannot be added or changed later.  Provincial Government Issued ID will be required at check in .  Any reservation holders found to be transferring or selling their reservations to another party,  risk their reservation being cancelled without a refund.  If a customer can no longer use their reservation, they are encouraged to cancel it so that any unused camping fees that are not forfeited as a penalty, can be refunded to the credit card that was used to make the original reservation.
What time do your gate close?
Fort Camping is gated 24hrs. Upon check-in, campers are issued one gate entry card allowing them access to come and go as required. Gate entry cards are to be returned at check-out to the card box located at the back of the gate house. There is a $50 replacement fee for all non-returned or lost cards.
Gatehouse Hours
Mid April-May 16th: 10am-7pm
May 16-June 26th: 10am-8pm
June 27-Sept 2th: 9am-9pm
Sept 3-Mid Oct: 10am-8pm
Oct 14-Mid April: Closed
Store hours
Mid May – June 25th: Friday – Saturday 10am – 9pm
June 26th – End of season: open 7 days a week 10am – 9pm
How many vehicles can I park at my site?
ONE  vehicle only. Additional vehicles will be charged $10 per day and must park in the designated additional parking. NO DOUBLE PARKING and NO PARKING ON THE GRASS. We cannot accommodate boat trailers on campsites.
How many people can be one campsite?
Campsites have a maximum occupancy of 6 people per site combined children and adults. Site fees are based on 2 adults per site. Additional adults are $10 per night/per adult.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, in RV sites & tent cabins; maximum 2 pets per site. Pet Fee: $2 per pet/per day.
All pets, including cats, must be on a leash at all times including on your campsite. Pets are not to be left unattended at any time. Dog waste bags are provided throughout the campground, please ensure you clean up after your pet.
No off-leash area is available at Fort Camping.
Are there restrictions on the size or types of pets?
While we do not discriminate against size and type of pet, for the protection of all our guests any pet showing signs of aggression may lead to the termination of the permit to occupy.
Can I have more than one RV or tent on the site?
Fort Camping does not offer double sites. All sites are for single family camping only.
The Fort Camping policy states that each campsite can accommodate one sleeping unit per campsite (RV or tent). An additional small tent (max 8’x 8′) is permitted if it is for minor children in the same family group.
Can I camp in a tent on an RV-Full Hook-Up site?
Only   RV  ’s that are self-contained with   grey and black   sewer hook-ups   can reserve a FHU site
Can we have visitors?
Yes! Visitors are permitted to park at the day parking and may walk in to visit the sites. There is no visitor parking on site.
Can I book a specific campsite?
Yes, you can book a specific site, and you can “lock” it in for  $50 lock fee . If you choose not to lock your site in, you will risk being moved to an alternative site to optimize the sites in the campground.
What is a lock site fee?
You are able to lock in (guarantee) your site that you’ve chosen in our booking system for a site lock fee. You do not have to lock in your site, it is an option for you to guarantee your site. If you do not pay and lock in your site, you will risk losing it and being relocated to a different site in the camp resort.
We want to make sure families and friend groups of 5 sites or more can stay together hassle-free! If you book 5 sites or more, you’re guaranteed to be placed together without any extra  lock  site  fee . However, if your group falls below 5 sites, a  lock  site  fee  will apply at check-in.
If you’ve already booked and your group size is 5 sites or more, don’t worry! Just send us an email, and we’ll credit the  lock  site  fee  for you at the time of check-in. Please note, if your group falls below 5 sites, the  lock  site  fee  will still be applicable.
How long can I camp at Fort Camping?
Fort Camping is a recreational campground offering a maximum of 30 nights from April to October.
We offer a  recreational winter RV program  from October to April.
Please  visit this page  to learn more or contact us at info@fortcamping.com
Can I wash my RV at the campground?
Fort Camping does not offer space for RV washing. We recommend washing your RV before or after to your stay at one of the local car washes in the Walnut Grove/Langley area.
About Fort Camping
Are campfires permitted?
Yes, small fires are permitted in the fire pits provided,  subject to fire bans . Fires are only permitted from 8 am to 11 pm – for the safety of all this is strictly enforced. Please do not move fire pits. Propane fire pits are permitted and the same fire rules apply.
Do you have a camp store?
Yes! Fort Camping offers a variety of items including Ice cream, novelty items, batteries, canned goods, coffee, drinks, candy, RV supplies, firewood, and ice.
Peak Season:  10am – 9pm
Closed September – July
Do you offer propane services?
Yes! Fort Camping sells propane and offers filling options of both belly tanks and propane bottles. Propane is only filled by a certified attendant and during daylight hours.
Do you have restrooms and showers?
Yes! Fort Camping offers a facility with both restrooms & showers. No Family rooms.
Shower Fee:  $2 for 10 minutes of water
Do you have laundry facilities on site?
Yes! It is located in the same building as the Bathrooms & Showers.
Wash : $2.50  Dry : $2.50
Do you have a group site?
Yes! Fort Camping offers 1 group tenting area that can accommodate up to 65 campers. Please see the   group site page   on the website for more details. No RVs are permitted in the group site.
Do you offer RV storage?
Fort Camping does not offer RV storage or overflow.
Do you have a dump-station?
Fort Camping’s dump-station is open, weather permitting.  It is for RV waste only.  There is a $20 fee for non registered campers.
Do you have mosquitoes at the park?
Weather and bugs are a natural part of the camping experience. Because of high river levels we do have nuisance mosquitoes. However, the Township of Langley and Metro Vancouver are taking measures to control, monitor and treat the mosquito population, when it becomes an issue.
How can I reduce my risk of coming in contact with mosquitoes?
Mosquitoes are most active at dusk and dawn. When outdoors, cover up and use insect repellent. Repellent with DEET is most effective and safe when used as directed: The percentage of DEET should not exceed 30% for adults or 10% for children. DEET should not be used for infants under 6 months of age.
For more information on insect repellents please visit Health Canada’s web page.

Experience Glamping at Fort Camping Tent Cabins
May 7, 2024
Fort Langley
Embark on a unique glamping in BC, with all the comforts of home at   Fort Camping on Brae Island  . Our tent cabins offer a perfect blend of traditional camping and modern convenience, providing an ideal setting for families, couples, and solo adventurers looking to immerse themselves in nature without sacrificing comfort.
What is a Tent Cabin?
A tent cabin at Fort Camping combines the traditional elements of a tent with the sturdy structure of a cabin. This unique accommodation is designed to offer more protection from the elements than a conventional tent and includes additional amenities to enhance your camping experience. Each tent cabin features cozy beds, furniture, and even heating, ensuring your comfort no matter the weather.
Tent Cabin at Fort Camping Amenities
Glamping, or glamorous camping, allows you to enjoy the great outdoors without the hassle of setting up camp and dealing with uncomfortable sleeping conditions. Our tent cabins are equipped with:
Comfortable Beds:  Enjoy a restful night’s sleep in real beds. Please bring your own pillow and bedding.
Furniture:  Settle in comfortably with chairs and a table for your convenience.
Lighting and Heating:  Stay cozy with interior lighting and heating options to keep you warm during those cooler nights.
Air Conditioner : Relax with our updated air conditioner that lets you cool off.
Why Choose Fort Camping Tent Cabins?
Ideal Location : Nestled in the scenic Fraser Valley, Fort Camping offers a peaceful retreat with easy access to local attractions and natural beauty. Explore nearby trails, rivers, and cultural sites, or simply relax by the campfire and enjoy the stars.
Family and Pet-Friendly : Our tent cabins are perfect for families and are pet-friendly, so you don’t have to leave your furry friends behind. Enjoy spacious accommodations and safe play areas for children and pets alike.

Experience Winter RV Camping in BC: Fort Langley, Parksville & Agassiz-Harrison | Winter Program
July 14, 2026
Agassiz-Harrison ,  Fort Langley ,  Parksville
Border lineups, new entry fees, and a currency that never seems to cooperate. If driving south for the winter has started to feel like more hassle than it’s worth, you’re not alone. A growing number of Canadian snowbirds are choosing to stay closer to home instead, and BC’s winters make that an easy choice.
Pathfinder Camp Resorts’ Winter Camping Program runs October through April  at three locations across the province: Fort Camping in Fort Langley, Pathfinder Parksville on Vancouver Island, and Pathfinder Agassiz-Harrison in the Fraser Valley.
What is Pathfinder Winter Camping Program?
Here’s what makes it worth trading the border crossing for a winter camping in BC:
Full-Service RV Sites, So You Can Actually Relax
A long winter stay should feel like home, not like roughing it. Our full-service RV sites let you settle in comfortably for the season, hookups and all, so you can spend your time enjoying the winter instead of managing it.
Amenities That Make a Long Stay Easy
Free WiFi, a coin laundry room, and heated showers, the everyday comforts that make an extended stay simple rather than a chore. No need to hunt down a laundromat or ration your hot water on a cold morning.
Skip the Snow Shovel, Not the Season
BC has some of the mildest winters in Canada. That means you can enjoy the season without digging out your RV every morning. Trade harsh cold and snow shoveling for crisp air and mild days. It still feels like winter, just without the hassle.
A Community of Fellow Snowbirds
You won’t be wintering alone. You’ll meet fellow snowbirds who made the same call. Stay close to home, make new friends, and still make it a season to remember.
Everything You Need, Just a Short Drive Away
All three locations are close to grocery stores, local shops, cafes, bakeries, and restaurants. Downtown is just a short drive away too. Whether you’re stocking the fridge or hunting for a new coffee spot, you won’t have to go far.
Local Attractions and Festivals All Season Long
Winter in BC brings its own local charm. Enjoy holiday markets, light displays, and small-town festivals throughout the season.
Three BC Locations, Each With Its Own Winter Charm
Fort Camping, Fort Langley
Just an hour from Vancouver and walking distance to the historic Fort Langley village, this location puts you close to local shops, cafes, and many more. Fort Langley’s own Christmas in the Village market runs annually in December, a lovely way to spend an afternoon in town.
Sign up for winter camping at Fort
Pathfinder Parksville, Vancouver Island
Set on the Englishman River with some of the mildest winter weather in the province, Parksville is minutes from downtown shops, restaurants, and the annual Milner Gardens Christmas Magic light walk. Guests also get to enjoy the heated pool and FeelGood Sauna right on site, a nice way to warm up after a winter walk.
Sign up for winter camping at Parksville
Pathfinder Agassiz-Harrison, Fraser Valley
Just minutes from downtown Agassiz and nearby Chilliwack for groceries and dining, and a quick 7-minute drive from a soak at Harrison Hot Springs, this location offers mountain views to look around.
Sign up for winter camping at Agassiz

Halloween in August: A Family Camping Tradition at Fort Camping
August 10, 2026
Fort Langley
From August 5 to 9, 2026, Fort Camping turned into something a little spookier, and a whole lot more fun.
Halloween in August is one of our most loved themed weekends, and it only happens right here at Fort Camping. Every year, the FFBC community transforms our group site into a full haunted house, decked out from top to bottom, and shows it off to campers all weekend long.
It’s part tradition, part community project, and entirely something you have to see to believe. It’s also a perfect example of why Fort Camping continues to be one of the best family-friendly campgrounds near Vancouver.
Why Families Love Fort Camping
Themed weekends like Halloween in August are just one piece of what makes Fort Camping special. Families keep coming back because this isn’t just a place to park an RV or pitch a tent. It’s a community.
Kids grow up here. Neighbours become friends. Traditions get passed down year after year. That’s what happens when a campground puts family fun and community spirit at the centre.
Whether you’re looking for kid-friendly camping activities, a welcoming spot for your first family camping trip, or simply a Fort Langley campground where everyone knows your name, Fort Camping is built for exactly that.
A Yearly Tradition Built by the Community
What makes Halloween in August so special isn’t just the decorations. It’s the people behind it. Every year, FFBC pours their creativity, time, and Halloween spirit into building a haunted house experience that campers look forward to all year.

A Fairytale Autumn at Fort Camping: Deb’s Winter Stay Through Her Lens
November 6, 2025
Fort Langley
If you’ve ever wondered what camping in Vancouver feels like in the fall, one look at Deb’s photos will tell you everything — magical, peaceful, and straight out of a storybook. 🍂
Deb, one of our wonderful campers joining our Winter Camping Program at Fort Camping, has an incredible eye for photography. Along with her husband and their two adorable cats, she’s spending six cozy months with us as part of our snowbird winter program in Vancouver — and her photos capture exactly why Fort Langley is such a special place to call “home” for the season.
Life at Fort Camping in the Autumn
The town of  Fort Langley  already has that charming, small-town feel — with its historic streets, local cafés, and riverside trails — but in autumn, something magical happens here. The trees along the Fraser River turn brilliant shades of gold and amber, the crisp air smells faintly of campfire, and every corner looks like it’s been painted from a fairytale scene.
For Deb and her husband, that means morning walks under golden leaves, peaceful afternoons by the river, and evenings spent cozy inside their home-on-wheels — with their two cats enjoying the view from the window, of course. 🐾
Fort Langley: A Fairytale Escape in the Lower Mainland
There’s no denying it, Fort Langley is one of the most picturesque towns in the Lower Mainland, especially in autumn. From misty mornings by the Fraser River to cozy strolls through the village, every moment feels a little magical.
And when you stay at Fort Camping, you’re right in the heart of it all. You can explore boutique shops, enjoy brunch at local cafés, or simply take in the fall colors from your RV or cabin porch. It’s no wonder many of our winter campers say it’s the best campground near Vancouver for year-round stays.
The Beauty of the Winter Camping Program at Fort Camping
Our Winter Camping Program at Fort Camping gives campers like Deb the chance to stay long-term — enjoying the peaceful off-season while still having access to all the comforts of home.
Whether you’re a full-time RVer, a remote worker looking for a quiet space, or a couple wanting to embrace a slower pace of life, our snowbird winter program in Vancouver is perfect for you.
You’ll get:
✅ Full-service RV sites
✅ Heated washrooms and laundry
✅ Free Wi-Fi
✅ A friendly community of fellow snowbirds
✅ Easy access to Fort Langley’s local shops, cafés, and trails
Deb’s photos remind us why we love what we do — seeing campers truly  live  the experience, capturing the beauty of nature, and finding joy in the simple things. Her snapshots of fog rolling over the river, amber leaves glistening in morning light, and cozy campsite scenes perfectly show what makes  camping in autumn  so special.
We’re so happy to have Deb and her family (cats included!) with us this winter. Their stay is a beautiful reminder that every season at Fort Camping holds a little magic.
If Deb’s photos inspire you to slow down and enjoy the beauty of the season, we’d love to welcome you too.
👉 Learn more about our   Winter Camping Program at Fort Camping   — one of Vancouver’s best campgrounds for family camping, snowbird stays and fun glamping.
Book your stay at Fort Camping   and experience autumn’s fairytale camping for yourself!

Local Markets and Grocery Stores Near Fort Camping
November 28, 2024
Fort Langley
At   Fort Camping  , you can enjoy a peaceful stay surrounded by the natural beauty of Langley, BC, while still having easy access to all the essentials. Whether you’re looking for fresh produce, local meats, or gourmet treats, there are several grocery stores and farm markets near Fort Camping.
Here are some top spots within 10 minutes from Fort Camping:
Lee’s Market
Address:  23320 Mavis Ave, Langley, BC
Phone:  (604) 888-5622
Lee’s Market is a family-owned gem offering fresh and local products. This market prides itself on supporting the local community, with a variety of items such as deli meats, baked goods, fresh produce, and even gluten-free products. It’s a fantastic place to grab something quick and delicious during your camping stay.
Fort Langley Village Farmers’ Market
Address:  St. Andrew’s Historic Church, 9025 Glover Rd, Langley, BC
Hours:  Open seasonally on Saturdays, 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM (check for seasonal dates)
For a true taste of Langley’s local farming scene, head to the Fort Langley Village Farmers’ Market. The market offers an array of fresh, seasonal produce, local meats, cheeses, and artisanal items. Open on Saturdays, this is an ideal stop for campers who want to enjoy local, farm-fresh products during their stay.
Walnut Grove Save-On-Foods
Address:  8840 210 St, Langley, BC
Phone:  (604) 888-0333
If you’re looking for a large, full-service grocery store, Walnut Grove Save-On-Foods is a great choice. Open every day from early morning to late evening, you’ll find everything from fresh produce to bakery items, meats, and even a pharmacy. This store is perfect for stocking up on all the essentials for your camping trip.
B&J Grocery
Address:  9110 Glover Rd, Langley, BC
Phone:  (604) 888-8882
B&J Grocery is a convenient store for picking up everyday essentials. Located just a short drive from Fort Camping, it offers a variety of groceries for campers in need of quick and easy supplies.
These grocery stores near Fort Camping shows that you’re never far from what you need. Whether you’re shopping for fresh ingredients, local specialties, or everyday essentials, you’ll have no trouble finding everything within a short drive.
So come enjoy your stay at Fort Camping, where convenience meets nature!

Family Camping Trip Itinerary Ideas at Fort Camping
November 6, 2024
Fort Langley
Looking for a fun family camping trip itinerary in   Fort Langley  ? Use this guide inspired by  Rory  and family’s recent stay at Fort Camping. Enjoy a weekend filled with great food, outdoor fun, and quality time, with the added bonus of convenience if you don’t feel like cooking. A classic 3 days 2 night camping trip that all family will love.
Day 1
Arrival and Setup at noon
Arrive at Fort Camping in the noon to get settled into your RV site (tent-cabin check in is at 3PM). Set up your space, explore the campground amenities, and get ready for a great weekend.
Afternoon Stroll in Fort Langley
Take a stroll through the charming streets of Fort Langley. Explore local boutiques, historic sites, and the vibrant community atmosphere.
Dinner at Rail & River Bistro
In the evening, treat your family to dinner at Rail & River Bistro. Their stacked onion rings and flavorful tacos are a must-try and provide the perfect end to your first night.
Family Quality Time
Get cozy in your tent, RV, or tent-cabin and enjoy some quality family time. After the trip to the campsite, it’s the perfect time to kick back and relax. A cup of hot chocolate sounds like a great idea too! There’s something about that first night at camp that makes it extra special—it’s the excitement for the next day!
Day 2
Morning Paddleboarding on the River
Start your second day with a fun paddleboarding session on the river next to Fort Camping. It’s a great way for the whole family to enjoy nature and see the beautiful scenery from the water.
Picnic Lunch by the River
Pack a picnic lunch and enjoy it on the riverbank. It’s a simple, yet special way to make the most of your outdoor adventure.
Afternoon Snack
Beat the afternoon heat with a stop at Crosswalk Ice Cream. With a variety of delicious flavors, this treat is sure to please everyone.
Explore Fort Langley National Historic Site
Spend the afternoon exploring the Fort Langley National Historic Site to learn about the rich history of the area. This immersive experience is both fun and educational for all ages.
Dinner Cookout by the Campsite
For dinner, cook up a meal at your campsite. This family cookout experience will add to the camping vibes and create more bonding time.
Campfire and Marshmallow Toasting
End the night with a little sweet treat! Toast marshmallows, share stories, and enjoy the starry sky—a perfect way to wind down your day.
Day 3
Brunch at Blacksmith Bakery
Start the day with a brunch at the nearby Blacksmith Bakery. Enjoy their renowned pastries, hearty sandwiches, and excellent coffee to fuel up for the day.
Another Walk Around the Town
Take a final walk around Fort Langley, visit any spots you may have missed, or return to your favorites for one last look. Tips: find the vintage thrift stores and you may find unique treasures!
Pack Up and Departure
Pack up your campsite, ensuring you leave everything tidy. Say goodbye to your camping home and start planning your next visit!
Plan Your Family Adventure
With Fort Camping’s location just a short walk from the heart of  Fort Langley , your family camping trip itinerary can be as relaxed as you like. Whether you choose to dine out or bring your own meals, this 3-day itinerary balances adventure, relaxation, and convenience. Book your stay today and enjoy a stress-free, memorable family camping experience!
