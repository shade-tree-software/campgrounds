Camping at its finest
Night Sky  is located on beautiful Boundary Creek with direct access to the KVR trail where you can bike, quad, or hike. The Park is close to a number of Boundary Country’s vibrant rural communities including Greenwood, Christina Lake and Grand Forks. Farmers markets, restaurants serving food sourced from local producers, as well as fresh roasted coffee and handmade ice cream are just a few of the choices.
The area has countless lakes, rivers, roads and trails to explore.
All sites have 30amp power, water and sewer.  Bookings are available from late April to late October.   With only 11 sites in the whole park, early reservations are highly recommended.
