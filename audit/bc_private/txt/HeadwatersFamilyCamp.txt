Welcome to Headwaters
We are located at 21 km on Peachland Main, less than 30 minutes above Peachland.
We have 10 Rustic cabins equipped with dishes, silverware, pots and pans for cooking. Remember to bring your own bedding and drinking water.  Our cabins are cozy, clean, well kept and welcoming.
For all the Campers we have 14 campsites, we can accommodate any size motorhomes or trailer.
From fishing, boating, canoeing, swimming, hiking, quading in the summer - to a quiet weekend getaway - all at affordable rates -  we invite you to leave the hectic pace of a busy life and come enjoy our  tranquil setting and everything Headwaters has to offer.

Tent and Camper Camping
If you are looking to be a bit more traditional and want to camp in a tent at Headwaters - we have 14 great spots for you to choose from. Your even welcome to bring your camper if that's your style! We can accommodate any size motorhomes or trailer.
Shower and clean outdoor toilets are centrally located, and just a stones throw away.
