Qualicum First Nation
A popular campground with waterfront campsites, cabins and full R.V. hook-ups on Vancouver Island.
Our campgrounds
The Qualicum First Nation Waterfront Campground, Cabins and RV Park is a very popular vacation getaway, centrally located a short 14 minute drive from Qualicum Beach on beautiful Vancouver Island, offering a great camping experience with beach access for all our campers.
Explore our campgrounds
RESERVATIONS
North Campground
Located on the north side of the Big Qualicum River, and backing on to the Strait of Georgia. Providing basic camping, and both, serviced and non-serviced waterfront and off water RV recreational vehicle sites.
Explore the North Campground
South Campground
Located on the south side of the Big Qualicum River and backing on to the Strait of Georgia. Complimenting the North Campground, providing great recreational sites for “Seasonal Quests Only”. This campground is not available to ‘weekend campers’ – other than seasonal guests.
Explore the South Campground
Qualicum First Nation Band Office
The Qualicum First Nation is a First Nations band government located in Qualicum Bay at the mouth of the Big Qualicum River, 15 minutes north of Qualicum Beach on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, Canada.
