A Place to Enjoy Mother Nature
Come and relax at the West Kettle River in a Natural park-like setting.   Enjoy the Little Dipper Hideaway while camping in a log cabin, RV or tent.
Enjoy hiking and biking on the Trans-Canada Trail! The KVR trail runs through./along our property....
You will find a beautiful mixed forest of deciduous and coniferous trees, mixtures of shrubs and other flora.
Our property is  along the beautiful West Kettle River, creating some interesting places to explore and enjoy!
For bike enthusiasts, the Little Dipper Hideaway makes a great stop over; either coming from or going to Midway via the Trans-Canada Trail.  Take advantage of a good night's rest in the clear mountain air..
Daily sites available..For Seasonal sites- please call 250 446 2213 to inquire. Our season is open from May 1 to October 31..

The Little Dipper Hideaway OPENED in 1992.; Built by George and Frauke Delisle. It has always been a  POPULAR DESTINATION HOLIDAY RESORT. Ideal for young families as well as retired people!
In the SUMMER TIME, the swimming hole and extensive sandy beaches make for a pleasant afternoon..The trees provide the shade:)
Our grounds are clean, and quiet...the way Nature intended it to be.
Our campsites are well spaced out providing  privacy. We provide overnight camping as well as seasonal sites.
