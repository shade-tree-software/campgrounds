Welcome to the Lakeside Resort
Situated on Tuc-el-Nuit Lake, a spring-fed oasis amidst the splendor of Canada’s only true desert, our location offers peaceful views from our park-like 5-acre grounds with a 500-foot private beach. You will love our beautiful lakefront log gazebo and patio garden, which becomes a spa during 'adult season.' Tuc-el-Nuit is perfect for families, as it is safe, shallow, warm, clean, and power boats are not permitted.
NO PETS - JULY & AUGUST

Lakeside Resort Policies
Deposits and Cancellations
Nightly Bookings
We require a credit card number at time of booking. If you cancel less than 24 hours before arrival we will charge your card for one night.
Summer Weekly Bookings
We require a deposit at the time of booking.
We will refund your deposit, less 15%, if you cancel over 30 days
before arrival. There will be no refund if you cancel within 30 days
of arrival. If, however, we are able to re-rent your booking we will
refund your deposit less the 15% cancellation fee.
Deposits/week:
One bedroom $750
Two bedroom $950
Three Bedroom $1200
Standard $500
Cabana $350
Campsite $250
Winter Monthly Bookings
$100 deposit required for each month booked. We will refund your deposit, less 15%, if you cancel over 30 days
before arrival. There will be no refund if you cancel within 30 days of arrival. If, however, we are able to re-rent your booking we will refund your deposit less the 15% cancellation fee.
Parking
One vehicle per accommodation, park in designated space only or off site. No extra parking/vehicles on site.
We have limited storage space for boats, utility trailers, etc. There will be a $20/day charge. First come, first serve.
General Policies
Quiet time from  11pm – 7:00 a.m.
Games Area Available from  9:00am to 9:00pm
No wood burning campfires. Propane is permitted.
No more than one family per site (Each site includes/allows: 2 adults plus kids)
PLEASE ENJOY OUR SMOKE-FREE ENVIRONMENT:
Cigarette & Vape use in designated areas only. Please ask at the front desk. No marijuana use on property.
Smoking is not permitted inside at any time. There will be a $250 charge for cleaning if you choose to do so. We provide ashtrays outside of each unit for mid and low season guests
No Riding in the resort between July 4th and Labor Day,  No bikes, scooters, skateboards, etc. (If it has wheels it must be taken off site)
NO MATS on grassy sites.
PETS
We  DO NOT  allow pets in our suites at any time. We allow pets in the campground/cabana area from Labor Day until July 4th, 2026 but not during the summer high season.
Dogs must be under owner’s control at all times. Leashed, not left unattended and never on the beach!
PARKING
One vehicle per accommodation, park in designated space only or off site. No extra parking/vehicles on site.
We have limited storage space for boats, utility trailers, etc. There will be a $20/day charge. First come, first serve.
