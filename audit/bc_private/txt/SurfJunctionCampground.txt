Surf Junction Campground
is a welcoming forested campground located between Ucluelet and Long Beach on Vancouver Island’s wild west coast. There are roughly 90 campsites nestled on 74 acres of land. Just a few minutes drive from Pacific Rim National Park Reserve and some of Canada’s best surf beaches. It offers private wooded campsites, full-service RV sites, and a relaxed, surf-inspired atmosphere—perfect for campers, surfers, families, and outdoor adventurers looking to explore the area and unwind in nature. Everyone is welcome!

Reservations
Please book  Online  or call our toll free number at
1 (877) 922-6722
Strongly recommended May through September.
We require a credit card to stay.  The card holder must be staying at the campground.
2 night minimum stay on weekends
3 night minimum on long weekends.
$7 non-refundable reservation fee & full payment is taken when the reservation is made.  This fee is applied for each campsite reserved.
Extra people and parking will sometimes be charged at check-in.
14 night maximum stay per person/group per year.
No-Shows:  We will hold your site for two nights. If you have not shown up and checked in after two nights, we will remove your reservation from our system without a refund.
Cancellations
Campsites are refundable as long as 7 days notice prior to arrival date is given.
We charge a $12 Cancellation Service Fee for ALL Cancellations. You also lose the $7 reservation fee paid at the time of booking. $19.95 after tax in total is forfeit. No exceptions.
If you cancel within 7 days of your arrival date, you will NOT get a refund.
Refunds can only be given on a credit card.
We are NOT waiving cancellation and reservation fees due to travel restrictions or bans.
We are NOT waiving cancellation and reservation fees due to acts of God. Ex: road closures or delays due to forest fires.
Changing Reservations
We do not charge for changing your reservation UNLESS you are removing one or more nights from your stay and you are doing so before 7 days of your arrival. A single charge of $5 will be applied if you’re removing nights from your reservation if you give us more than 7 days notice.
If you are dropping nights within 7 days prior to your arrival date, you will not get a refund for the nights dropped or changed.
You will NOT get a refund if you change your dates 7 days prior to your arrival. You will be making a new reservation with new fees. (ex: you want to change your reservation to the next week or month.)
If you are adding people, changing campsites, or adding nights to your stay, you will NOT be charged this fee.
Please call or email us if you need to change your dates or campsites. Do NOT create a whole new reservation online. This will save you from being charged the cancellation fees.
Late Arrivals
If you will be arriving after our office closes (9:00pm low season / 10:00pm high season) please proceed to the front office door. Here there will be a map with your name on it. Please find your site and set up quietly. Be extra aware of not leaving any bear attractants (items that have a scent in any way) outside of your vehicles.
Please make sure to come and check-in / sign-in as early as possible the next morning.
We do not have any kind of gate at night.
The bathroom codes will be on your late arrival map.
Group Sites:
Price reflects the first 8 people and 2 vehicles.
*Tents or Camper Vans only**
Early Checkouts
No refunds for early checkouts.  Unless it’s a family or medical emergency.
No refunds because of the weather…it’s a rainforest- it tends to rain here :)
Rules & Regulations
In order to make your stay and that of others around you a happy experience, we would like to call your attention to a few rules.
Around 15,000 campers will use the campground this year – please do not cut or damage any tree, bush or flower and only walk on designated pathways and roads.
Check-in is between 1PM and 10PM (9PM in the shoulder season),  unless specific arrangements have been made.
You will receive parking passes for your vehicles at check-in.  Please make sure they are displayed in your vehicles at all times.
Check out time is 11am. There is a $20/hr fee for every hour you stay in your campsite. After 1pm you will be charged the cost of the campsite and still won’t be allowed to camp in it.   If we have another camper coming to check into your campsite, we will move all of your belongings out of the campsite and store them at the office. So please, don’t go surfing when it’s time to check-out. If something comes up, please just call the office so we can make adjustments.
Quiet time is 11pm-9am, excessive noise or loud music will not be tolerated at any time.
Dangerous or disorderly conduct will not be tolerated and will result in an automatic eviction of the entire party. There are no refunds if you are evicted.
A major credit card and/or valid ID will be required of all groups.
Any additional fees (Cleaning fee, extra guests, damages, etc.) will be charged to the credit card on file.
No refunds for early checkouts. So please know exactly how many nights you’re going to be staying before checking in.
No swimming or entering into the campground’s pond or marsh area at anytime. This includes boats, paddle boards, or any other floating device.
Bear and Wildlife Attractant
Do not leave any animal attractants (anything with a scent) unattended in your site, when you leave or go to bed.
Pack everything into your vehicle or lock in storage lockers. You can also bring things to the office and we will put them in back.
Wildlife attractants include, but aren’t limited to: food, garbage, coolers, EMPTY COOLERS, bins, stoves, dishes, cans, empty cans or bottles, soap, and toiletries.
We take this VERY seriously. Dirty campsites will be cleaned immediately by our staff and will result in a $25 cleaning fee that will be charged to the credit card on file. If it happens again, you will be evicted without a refund.
We want to keep this area and these animals wild. Bears that become accustom to humans, and their food, are terminated on Vancouver Island.
Visitors
All visitors must register at the office upon arrival and get a parking pass.
Our amenities are for paying guests only. Visitors cannot use the showers or hot tub.
Visitors must leave the campground by 10pm.
Please remember that you are our guests and if you have visitors, you are responsible for their behaviour. If your visitor is breaking the rules, the entire booking will be evicted. There are no refunds for evictions.
Pet Rules
Dogs must be on leash at all times. There’s a $2 pet fee for dogs.
Cats must remain in vehicles or leashed.
Dogs may not be left unattended at any time.
Excessive barking will not be tolerated.
Animal stools must be picked up, wrapped and placed in garbage cans.
Animals are not allowed in washrooms, office or hot tub area (except guide dogs.)
Dogs cannot go swimming in the pond area. It’s a little protected marsh for lots of wildlife. Sorry, doggos!
Hot Tub Rules
Guests must shower before using hot tub, failure to shower will result in loss of hot tub privileges.
Hot tub and sauna are for registered guests only.
Guests must wear a bathing suit – no tee shirts or cut offs.
No food, drink or smoking permitted in the hot tub.
Alcohol and smoking will not be tolerated in the hot tub or sauna area.
No glass in or around the hot tube area.
No jumping or diving.
The hot tub is open from 9am to 10:30pm.
The sauna is open from 9am-10:30pm.
Children under 8 are not allowed in the hot tub, Children 9-12 must be accompanied by an adult.
Do not dry clothes in the sauna (or wetsuits)- that smells!
Washrooms
The campground uses a combination of flush toilets which go to a septic field, and composting toilets. It is extremely important that no chemicals, plastics or foreign materials are put in either system. (For information on composting toilets see  www.compostingtoilet.com )
We pride ourselves on providing clean washroom facilities, please help us by reporting any problems to staff immediately.
Washrooms and showers are open 24 hours a day.
Showers are now FREE! They are on a push-timer, which is set to 2 minutes. We are on a well, so showers still need to be short and sweet.
Laundry
Laundry is available around the corner from the office (by site 37)
You must come to the office and buy a token to run a load.
1 token = 1 wash, 1 token = 1 load in the dryer.
You must use natural laundry detergent. Otherwise you will ruin our septic system.
You cannot wash sleeping bags in the washer or any large items. You will break our machines. You can put ONE sleeping bag in the dryer.
You must empty out the lint before drying.
Do not leave your clothes in the dryer after the load is done.
Please be kind to our machines.
Children and Youth
Please drive with extreme care, especially when backing up. We have lots of small children running around.
Please make your children aware of the rules, most of the rules are designed to protect them.
Children should not climb or slide on banks or trees.
Washrooms are not playgrounds.
Parents are responsible for the behaviour and supervision of the children and youth at all times; and must be in their campsites by 11pm.
Surfing Lessons and Rentals
We have a full surf school and lots of surf rental gear at the campground’s office
Group surf lessons go out daily at 9:15am and 1:15 pm.
We also offer private lessons and private family lessons in the high season.
We have a great surf rental bundle available. This includes everything you need for surfing, including a roof rack.
We sell surf wax, fins, leashes, sunscreen, combs, and other surf gear at the office.
Lessons are always recommended to beginners.
Store
For your convenience our store carries some basic groceries: Canned food, pasta, snacks, ice, ice cream, cold drinks, coffee and espresso drinks, camping supplies and firewood.
We lend out sporting equipment : soccer, volleyball, badminton, football, bocce ball and croquet.
Open 9am-10pm in the summer and 9am-9pm in the low season.
Recycling and Waste Management
Our recycle centres are located by the composting toilets and by the showers. All returnable bottles are donated to the local food bank.
Sewage and wastewater: If you are not in a full-service site and need to dump, we charge a minimal fee of $10.00. We are on a septic system and pay to pump what you put in.
Dumping portable toilet waste into the composting toilets will damage these very expensive systems. Anyone found dumping into these toilets will be charged with to the full extent of the law and will be held responsible for any loss of income due to the closing of the toilet facilities. PLEASE USE THE SANI DUMP!
This property is privately owned and operated. We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone. We are not responsible for accidents or injuries to our guests. We are not responsible for loss of equipment or valuables of any kind.
