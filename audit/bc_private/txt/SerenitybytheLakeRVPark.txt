​
Serenity by the Lake RV Park opened in 2020 with a very successful season, as an RV park and tent campground located on beautiful White Lake BC.  Our guests views expressed a very enjoyable and fun-filled visit with us.  We are situated in the stunning Shuswap Lake recreational area! We are only 7 km from Hwy 1, 27 km north of Salmon Arm, and 92 km east of Kamloops.
​
Our RV park is on the north shore of White Lake, settled between two arms of Shuswap Lake. With stunning views of one of the top trout fishing lakes in BC, we are excited to provide 7 RV camping sites with 30 amp power, non-potable water and quality wifi.
​
White Lake is renowned for its fly fishing with crystal clear waters; perfect for trolling, water skiing, paddle boarding or kayaking. White marl shoals follow the entire circumference of the lake providing habitat for aquatic bugs, and more importantly, lots of food for trout to quickly grow to trophy size.
​
We border White Lake municipal park where there is a boat launch and ample parking for boat trailers and vehicles. Boat rentals are available nearby and with our fabulous children/teen park, John Evdokimoff Community BMX park, with hiking trails, slides, and a basketball court you will have a good variety of activities.
​
"There are no strangers here, just friends you haven't met yet"
​
We look forward to having you visit us at Serenity by the Lake!

Rules:      ​
Check-in time 12:00 p.m.- 8:00 p.m. Check-out time 11:00 a.m. for RV sites,  check in is 1:00 for RV rental and check out is 10:00.
Note:  If a longer stay is required in an RV site (please inquire with management), the rate is $10.00 per hour.  Late arrivals will be charged additional $10.00 after 8:00 p.m.
Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.
No abusive language - not tolerated in public shared areas
Dogs:
Well behaved dogs with a responsible owner are permitted (subject to breed restrictions - Please check)
Excessive noise, not picking up after your dog, or allowing your dog to roam off leash could result in you being asked to leave the park without a refund
Propane fire pits are permitted, wood burning fires are permitted in designated areas only if there is no FIRE BAN
No BBQ's on picnic tables unless raised to protect the table
In addition to the RV itself, the Guest is allowed to park one vehicle
No parking on the grass
No oil changing or major vehicle repairs allowed in park
Serenity RV Park reserves the right to move a guest’s reservation to a different site within the park, if we find such a move is necessary
Serenity RV Park reserves the right to refuse entry without refund of deposit for any reservation that is made involving false claims when booking e.g. Number of guests and unit type
Guests will be held liable for any loss or damage of property while moving into or out of a site
Generators are not allowed without prior approval from management
Garbage should be enclosed in plastic bags which are tied, prior to placing in garbage cans
Absolutely no laundry hanging from RV's or trees
No visitor parking - all visitors to park any vehicle at the community centre parking lot nearby unless approved
