“ENJOY THE SPECTACULAR COUNTRYSIDE”
The Buntzen Lake located at the entrance of Anmore Camp & RV is a 45 minute drive east of Vancouver, is a nature lover’s paradise.
Buntzen Lake is flanked by Eagle Ridge/Mountain (1,240 m) to the east and Buntzen Ridge (560 m) to the west. Indian Arm Provincial Park and Belcarra Regional Park make up a large portion of the surrounding hiking trails, which are suited for all levels of experience and physical abilities.
