FAQ most popular questions
Frequently asked questions
What is the cancellation policy? Can I cancel a booking I just made?
Cancellation Policy
- NO SHOW POLICY or cancelling the SAME DAY of ARRIVAL - cancellation.
The total amount of your stay will be charged.
- WEEK of ARRIVAL - Cancelling less than 7 days before arrival, the 25% deposit is
non-refundable.
- CANCELLING UP TO THE WEEK BEFORE ARRIVAL.
The DEPOSIT refund will be done less a service charge (up to $25 MAX)
- Cancellations made AFTER arrival will have a refund less 50%
of total amount paid for the remainder of your stay. Or 1 night non-refundable, whichever is more.
What are the cancellation - booking policies?
NO SHOW POLICY or cancelling the SAME DAY of ARRIVAL - cancellation. The total amount of your stay will be charged.
WEEK of ARRIVAL - Cancelling less than 7 days before arrival, the 25% deposit is non-refundable.
CANCELLING UP TO THE WEEK BEFORE ARRIVAL. The DEPOSIT refund will be done less a service charge (up to $25 MAX)
Cancellations made AFTER arrival will have a refund less 50% of total amount paid for the remaining days of your stay, not including the day of cancellation.
BY RESERVATION ONLY. NO DROP INS.
At the time of booking your reservation, a 25% deposit will be charged to the card used to reserve with the remaining amount paid to same card on the day of arrival.
Non-contact registration and non-contact full payment is made day of arrival using the same credit card on file for reservation.
IF YOU ARE USING DIFFERENT PAYMENT METHOD*, OR NEW CARD*,
(*compromised card, new expiry date, etc.)
LET US KNOW BEFORE ARRIVAL.
Visa, Visa Debit, MasterCard, American Express, Discover accepted for NON-Contact payment
When do you close the campground?
The campground is open from May Long Weekend to the end of September.
Campground is closed from October to April.
What RV services do you have?
All the campsites have power, water and sewer. Most are 30 amp.
There is one site which has 30/50 amp power.
Free wifi is included.
Washrooms and shower for registered guests. Doors have a code.
Coin laundromat - 3 washers and 3 dryers
Most back-in sites have a fire pit.
Pull-through sites do not have a fire pit.
How do I book a Cabin or RV site online and what information is required?
Go to any of the links for Book Online. It will take you to our online booking site     https://app.littlehotelier.com/properties/glacierviewcabinsrvpark
Names of all adult guests staying.
Ages of children and how many.
Any pets and description.
Size of RV and type.
Home address
Email address
Contact number for text preferred
How many beds needed for cabin.
A credit card or visa debit for 25% deposit and final payment taken day of arrival.
Do you allow pets? Which cabins are NO PETS allowed?
Yes we do allow pets - cats or dogs.
Most of the cabins allow pets, except for Cabin 10.
The pet charge varies depending on the size of your dog.
Pets are allowed in the RV sites. Daily charges apply.
Must be under control on a lead at the site or cabin, and on a leash when walking.
The area in front of the main building by the BIG highway sign and the outhouses can be used a spot to take your dog.
And always pick up poop afterwards.
Thanks
Do you have a laundromat? Are there coins available on site to get in exchange for Canadian dollar bills?
Yes, a coin laundromat is available to use.
A scheduled time and day is set up for using.
Cost is $4-$5 loonied per wash (one is $4 and two are $5 - larger)
Dryers are quarters - US or Canadian coins
Cost is $2-$4 depending on how big a load to dry and the temperature.
Can I book a cabin or site for today? When can I book for next year?
BY RESERVATION ONLY. NO DROP INS.
Bookings are accepted for next day arrival.
Send us a message by text, email or leave a message on the phone.
As cabins and sites come available to book, they can be booked online a month in advance over the season and through the winter.
RV sites will be available online starting in April. Check the availability from the Online Booking link     https://app.littlehotelier.com/properties/glacierviewcabinsrvpark
What information is needed to book online?
The names of all adult guests.
How many beds for a cabin.
Ages of children and how many.
Any pets and what kind.
Your home address, email address, contact number for texting
Credit card for 25% deposit and to be used for final payment that will be taken day of arrival.
What do the cabins have inside? Are they heated?
All the cabins are self-contained with running water, electricity and furnished.
Kitchen or kitchenette with pots & pans, dishes, cutlery, glasses, etc.
Queen bed with twin bunks or King bed with twin bed. 1 bed studio or with bedroom.
Bedding and linens are provided. Hand soap, toilet paper, dish soap.
Coffee maker, coffee or tea, powdered cream, sugar.
Microwave and toaster oven or stove/oven.
Couch or love seat, vintage kitchen table with chrome chairs
Bathroom with sink, toilet and shower.
Covered deck with chairs. Fire pit or Outdoor fireplace.

Site descriptions
Large pull-through for EXTRA LONG large RVs - a slight slope down in front - easy out.
This site has an approach with a direct right turn after driving into the property.
Please make sure to STOP at the Stop sign located before the Office Registration building.
Mature trees on all sides. No view except out a driver's side window.
The passenger side faces the back of site 13.
NO FIRE PIT IN PULL THROUGH

SITES 15,16,17,18
Pull-Throughs 30-amp, water and sewer
SELF-CONTAINED UNITS ONLY - NO TENTING or TENT TRAILERS
Site Nightly Rate $42.00 for 2 people (plus 5% GST - No PST - Will be adjusted)
Week Rate $273.00 plus GST (7 days at $39)

Book online RV Sites
Site descriptions
These sites have different approaches to getting into your site. Please STOP on the SERVICE ROAD and check your approach before entering driveway property.
Site 15 - service post at the front of the site. Good south exposure from the front of the site for sat dishes.
Site 16 - service post towards the middle of the site. Some view of the glacier from the driver's windows. Sunshine from the east setting sun.
Site 17 - service post towards the back. Mature trees in front of the site. Good view of the glacier and mountains from the front of the site.
Site 18 - service post at the back of the site. Pine tree towards the back of the site. Garden on the driver's side.
Long pull-through sites for vehicle towing.
Some grass, open view to the glacier - some visibility, some mature trees - NO FIREPITS. Can use firepits available at sites across from each pull-through.
Pets are welcome and must be cleaned up after. Keep on a leash and under control.
There is a charge for pets. Please check your site before you leave.
