Welcome to Pacific Palms RV Resort
in the heart of Parksville beachfront!
Our spectacular RV resort is located along side Parksville Beach. We opened in the summer of 2022 and offer 84 full-service 50amp sites. Our prime location in the heart of Parksville gives you many options for actives and nearby events. Enjoy amazing panoramic views of the Georgia Strait and distant mountains. Within walking distant you will find numerous restaurants, shopping, and the Parksville Community Park.
We look forward to hosting you and your family!
We recommend to book online through our booking system by clicking "Book Now", if you need any further assistance, please feel free to contact us.
Full payment including taxes are required at the time of booking.
For Extended Stays, please email or call us.

Sites
Parksville's newest beachfront RV resort!
Enjoy our spectacular location right on the shores of Parksville Bay within steps of the popular Parksville Community Park. Take in the panoramic views of the Strait of Georgia, fabulous sunsets, beachcombing adventures, warm water, and the best sandy beach on this side of Vancouver Island.
﻿ We're your perfect vacation choice for the entire family.
Our full hook-up sites can accommodate a full-size RV with 14 ft wide clearance and up to 45 ft, except sites 69 to 84, which are for rigs 40 ft or under.

Drive In Sites

Platinum Pull-in sites just a few steps from the beach with an amazing oceanfront view. Closest to the beach front board walk.   No Trailer or 5th Wheel.
