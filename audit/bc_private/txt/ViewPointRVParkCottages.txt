ViewPoint RV Park & Cottages offers full hookup RV Sites and self contained cottages. Situated just outside of Salmon Arm in the sunny Shuswap, we operate from about the beginning of April to about mid October, depending on weather.
Viewpoint RV Park & Cottages provides a quaint setting for your family retreat. We provide cottages, campground, RV washrooms and laundry facility for your use. With clean accommodations and reasonable rates, we are here to ensure your stay is enjoyable and relaxing.
YOUR HOSTS AT VIEWPOINT RV
Scott and Liz are the proud owners of Viewpoint RV Park & Cottages. We took over as the new campground owners in 2021 and can say we are really enjoying the experience. We get to meet and interact with so many wonderful people from all over the world. Each day brings with it new and exciting opportunities to visit with and serve you, our guests.
ViewPoint RV Park & Cottages is our dream and we are excited to share it with you! Feel free to email, call, or use the booking form. We look forward to hosting you and having the opportunity to share our paradise!
