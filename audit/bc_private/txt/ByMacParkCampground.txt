Trails
Parks
Steelhead Park
Situated along Highway 16, Steelhead Park is truly a community landmark. Home to the iconic ‘World’s Largest Fly Rod’, this beautifully maintained park features walking trails, a new playground installed in 2026, dog run, picnic areas, and colourful flower gardens. It’s a favourite gathering place for residents and a must-stop for visitors passing through.
Location
Jamie Baxter Memorial Park
Running along West 14th Street near the Houston Health Centre, Jamie Baxter Park is a hub of activity. The park includes a skateboard park, BMX track, ball diamond, playground, and picnic area. The park’s amphitheatre hosts community events, such as Houston’s Canada Day celebration and movies in the park.
Location
Irrigation Lake
Located 14 km west Houston, Irrigation Lake is a popular summer destination for swimming, paddling, relaxing, and family outings. Visitors can enjoy picnic areas, fire pits, a sandy beach, and a wheelchair accessible dock installed in 2025. The lake is regularly stocked with rainbow trout, making it a popular fishing destination in the Houston area.
Location
Duck Pond Walking Trails
Accessible from Hungerford Drive, Hagman Crescent, or Nadina Way, the Duck Pond is a peaceful natural retreat. Visitors can enjoy walking trails, benches, and a viewing platform perfect for feeding ducks. It’s a popular spot for quiet walks, wildlife viewing, and taking in the scenery.
Location
Four Seasons Park
Found at the far end of Mountain View Drive, Four Seasons Park is a large, multi-use recreational area. The park features four ball diamonds, soccer fields, a large fenced dog park, rodeo grounds, and a riding arena. It’s a key venue for community and sporting events.
Location
Avalon Park
Tucked away just off Baggerman Crescent in the Avalon Subdivision, Avalon Park is a cozy neighbourhood playground perfect for younger children.
Location
Northside Park
Located at the corner of 3rd Street and Copeland, Northside Park offers something for everyone. Enjoy a game at the ball diamond, let the kids explore the playground, or relax in the picnic area. With walking trails nearby, it’s a great spot for casual outdoor recreation.
Location
Hagman Park
Located in the Mountain View Subdivision at the entrance to the Duck Pond area, Hagman Park is a welcoming playground space designed for young children. It’s convenient location near walking trails makes it a great starting point for a day of exploration.
Location
Nadina Park
Just off Nadina Place, Nadina Park is a small neighbourhood park with swings and a picnic area. It’s an inviting spot for a quick play break of a relaxed afternoon outdoors.
Location
Ruiter Heights Park
Located at the end of Lahti Drive in the Ruiter Heights Subdivision, this park features a well-loved playground ideal for young children.
Location
By-Mac Park
Located approximately 4 km west of Houston via Highway 16 and Morice River Road, By-Mac Park offers an outdoor experience along the Morice River. The park features nine unserviced campsites, a boat launch, and picnic areas, making it a great destination for camping, fishing, or a day by the river. *Campground redevelopment, including serviced campsites, washrooms, and shower facilities coming in Summer 2026.
Location
Northwood Picnic Site
Approximately 5 km west of downtown Houston along Highway 16, the Northwood Picnic Site is a convenient stop for travellers and locals alike. With picnic tables and basic facilities, it’s a comfortable place to take a break and enjoy the outdoors.
