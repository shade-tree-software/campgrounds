Welcome to the Will-O-Bend RV Park.  With the rising price of gas, people are looking for a place to park their RV and to enjoy the many activities.
Will-O-Bend is unique because it can cater to many different types of travelers.  We offer ownership opportunities, yearly rentals and overnight visits to truly see what this beautiful area has to offer. The Kootenays offers an assortment of lakes, golf courses, parks, rivers and many other outdoor activities.  Located in Jaffray, BC, Will-O-Bend is perfectly located to take advantage of these wonderful family activities.  Will-O-Bend RV park provides a unique sense of community with its mountain setting, sweeping views and peaceful environment.  With a host of local attractions, Will-O-Bend is the perfect recreational destination.
Each site is approximately 2,750 square feet and comes with 50 amp power (purchase sites), irrigation spigot, drinking water, sewer, laundry mat, shower house and swimming pond (Cable and internet are available at an additional monthly charge),  Will-O-Bend also comes fully equipped with community fire pits, event gazebo, walking trails and is beautifully landscaped throughout.  If you are looking for a place to park your trailer long term Will-O-Bend has you covered.
