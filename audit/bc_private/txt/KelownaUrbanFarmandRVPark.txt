Propane Fire Pits and Tanks
Due to forest fire risks during the summers, Kelowna bans open wood fires. However, every site at the Central location is outfitted with a propane fire pit which is completely legal! Bring your own tank.

River trail
Just outside your door is the beautiful Mission Creek Greenway. It is the well maintained walking and cycling path that follows the biggest river in the city. Follow it west to the lake, or east to Mission Creek Park and parts of the river that have more character. Bridges, hills, and sometimes rapids. An amazing place to walk the dog, get some exercise, or just enjoy nature. This access is unlike any other RV park in the city.

We Have chickens!
Kelowna Urban Farm and RV park is an active farm. We sell free-range eggs and chicken, seasonal fruits and vegetables, flowers, and sometimes honey! You can make the chickens very happy by feeding them your leftover table scraps.

Reservation Policies
Reservations of one week or less must be paid in full at the time of booking. Any reservations longer than a week require a minimum payment of one week at the time of booking with the remaining balance due at check in. Cancellations with more than a week of notice will be refunded everything but the first night. To receive any refund at least 7 days notice must be given. Any payment for a night with less than 7 days notice will be forfeit. (Eg. If a cancellation is made on a Friday, dates after and including the next Friday can be refunded)
*The first night of any stay is non-refundable upon making the deposit. There are no exceptions to this.*
For reservations of a month or more, the non-refundable amount increases to $200/month.
$10 nightly surcharge for all long weekends and holidays.
Early departures will be charged the full cost of the initial booked reservation.
Extensions made after arrival will be considered independently regarding weekly or monthly rates.
Check in is between 2-6pm. Check out is 11. If you need an early or late check in please let us know and we will do what we can to accommodate you.
Only one unit per site.
