WELCOME TO KLAHANIE
Get Direction
Walking distance, to Shannon Falls Provincial Park, Sea-To-Sky Gondola, Stawamus Chief featuring hiking trails, rock climbing, and many other recreational activities.
We offer year-round camping for RVs, truck campers, tent trailers, and tents. We feature semi-serviced and dry sites.  Sites are thoughtfully spaced,  treed, and equipped with a picnic table and fire pit. The park offers a variety of amenities including internet access, private shower facilities, flush toilets, and washing stations.
Location
5 km south of Squamish (watch for the Hwy signs). You’ll find us at the traffic lights directly across the street from Shannon Falls.  Quietly nestled off the  Sea to Sky Highway, Klahanie is a convenient 45 min drive north of Vancouver and 45 min drive south of Whistler.
Klahanie Campground is family-owned and operated, serving local, and international campers for over 55 years.  Located on Hwy 99 midway between Vancouver and Whistler.  The park offers scenic views of Howe Sound, Stawamus Chief, and Shannon Falls. We are walking distance to the Sea to Sky Gondola, numerous hikes, and rock climbing routes.
