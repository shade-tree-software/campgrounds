About Us
The small, lively community of Radium Hot Springs is set against the Canadian Rockies, overlooking the Purcell Mountains. Mountain Springs Motel is located on Radium Boulevard, a half block west of Highway 93/95. Our quiet, two-story structure offers 14 units ranging from single and double standard rooms, to one and two bedroom fully-equipped kitchen suites. All our rooms are non-smoking.
﻿ The Mountain Springs motel is within walking distance of dining, shopping, mini golf, splash park, playground, trailheads and, of course, the world famous Radium Hot Springs of Kootenay National Park, which is a short two-kilometre drive or stroll from our front door.

Our Accommodations
Retreat to our thoughtfully appointed suites in Radium Hot Springs, where mountain charm meets the soothing calm of the Columbia Valley. Each space features cozy comforts, fully equipped kitchenettes, and easy access to the area's natural beauty. Designed for deep relaxation after a day of soaking in the hot springs, hiking scenic trails, or exploring Kootenay National Park, our retreat offers the perfect escape for travelers seeking peace, comfort, and a true mountain getaway.
