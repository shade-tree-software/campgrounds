What Madera Ranch
HAS TO OFFER you
LARGE TREED LOTS
You’ve never seen lots like these! Our spacious, treed, and year-round RV lots are fully serviced and all come equipped with an amazing view. Our private estate lots take it one step further, with architectural controls that ensure your investment will always be surrounded by quiet beauty.
GORGEOUS SETTING
The pristine beauty of the lake and the surrounding Rocky Mountain wilderness is on hand to greet you every morning.
Nature’s gift is something we can never overemphasize – one visit and you’ll want to come back again and again.
LAKE
ACCESS
Stop staring at the lake and jump in! Madera Ranch offers full lake access, and our properties are located just a short distance from the beach. Whether you prefer to be on it, in it, or beside it, you’ll never tire of spending your days surrounded by the shimmering magnificence of Lake Koocanusa.
CONNECTION
Madera Ranch offers the chance to get away from it all while growing closer to those who you most cherish. Invite family and friends out for the weekend at our gated resort, or spend your visit meeting – or reconnecting with – your new neighbors. We’re all family here at Lake Koocanusa.
CONVENIENCE
You’re surrounded by untouched forest – but our fully-serviced lots guarantee that you’ve got all the comforts of home at your fingertips. We’ve got on-site laundry, year-round boat storage, golf carts, and even a playground for the little ones. Rough it in style at Madera Ranch.
ADVENTURE
Hike, bike, quad, boat, ski, or swim – Madera Ranch is on the doorstep of year-round adventure. Set out for a day of thrilling excitement, then come home to Madera Ranch for a quiet rest, evening by the campfire, or cup of warm cocoa by the window on a crisp winter day.
