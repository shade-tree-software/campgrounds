WELCOME TO CEDAR BEACH RESORT
Cedar Beach Resort is located on beautiful St. Mary Lake on Salt Spring Island, British Columbia.
At our 5 acre resort, we have 17 cabins and suites as well as 24 fully serviced RV and camping sites. We do not offer designated tent camping although there is the option to set up single tent off of some of the RV sites.
We have our own private, sandy beach right on the lake. The adjoining Beach House offers indoor and outdoor recreation space for our guests’ enjoyment. An outdoor barbecue/ bonfire and lakefront picnic tables are very popular features. The Resort offers an exceptional venue for family reunions and friends gatherings.
All of our cabins and suites are fully furnished, with cooking facilities. Some units offer such up to date amenities as televisions with cable, wifi, or DVD players and propane BBQ’s while others retain the feel of a rustic cabin in the forest without all the urban elements.
We have a men’s and a ladies’ washrooms in the RV / campground area, and a fully equipped accessible washroom adjoins the main washrooms.
All of our sites have water, sewer, and power (30 amp service).
As a family oriented resort, you can enjoy the frivolity during the day and the peacefulness of the night.
We are OPEN year round……………. come and enjoy the Island during the off seasons.
