Welcome to Pachena Bay Campground on Vancouver Island’s West Coast
Enjoy unparalleled wilderness adventures at Pachena Bay Campground, around the corner from the picturesque town of Bamfield, BC and at the trail head to the world famous West Coast Trail. Go beach combing on pristine shores, hike through old-growth rain forests and watch soaring eagles and other local wildlife along the way. Enjoy some of the world’s best sports fishing, kayaking, diving and eco-tours and cultural adventures.

About Pachena Bay Campground
Pachena Bay Campground is nestled in an old-growth rainforest next to an expansive, sandy beach that directly faces the open Pacific Ocean. The campground has been smartly upgraded to welcome RVs and campers as well as tenting.
The campground is at the mouth of the Pachena River next to the  Huu-ay-aht First Nations community  of Anacla. Pachena Bay is at the head of the world-famous  West Coast Trail  and is 5 kms from Bamfield, BC on Vancouver Island’s unspoiled west coast.

Campground Rules & Regulations
C HECK IN TIME IS 3:00 PM
• Guests who arrive after the office is CLOSED, please use the Camp Spot guest portal to check in.
• All guests need to check in with office staff for camping information and safety information.
CHECK OUT TIME IS 11:00 AM
• Please advise the office if you intend to depart later than 11 am.
CANCELLATION POLICY – ALL CANCELLATIONS ARE SUBJECT TO A $25 ADMIN FEE
0-7 days in advance – no refund
8-21 days in advance – charged first night’s stay + $25 admin fee
22 + days in advance – full refund less $25 admin fee
No refunds for early departures, no shows or evictions.
SITE OCCUPANCY – EACH SITE PERMITS ONE CAMPING UNIT (TENT/RV) WITH 4 ADULTS
• Additional Tents/RV may be permitted, depending on the site will be an extra charge.
• Additional Occupants may be permitted and will be an extra charge.
QUIET TIME IS 11 PM- 8:00 AM. – NO EXCESSIVE NOISE AND/OR PARTYING
• Management has the right to evict you and your party from the campground.
•Generators are permitted for 2 hours in the Mornings and Evenings (9 AM-11 AM & 5 PM-7 PM)
CAMPFIRES – CAMPFIRES ARE PERMITTED UNLESS A FIRE BAN IS IN EFFECT.
• All campfires must be supervised by an adult and contained within the fire rings.
• Please fully extinguish your campfire before you leave the site.
TABLES/FIREPITS  – Keep table and fire pits in designated campsite, do not move.
GARBAGE  – Campsites must be clean and tidy (free of attractants to wildlife)
• Limited resources on site, please “Pack In, Pack Out “.
RECYCLABLE/RETURNABLES
• “Pack In, Pack Out “. Whatever you bring in please bring it back home with you.
• Limited capacity on site, available recycling options at the Bamfield Transfer Station
ALCOHOL CONSUMPTION  – A person may not consume liquor in a public place, or a public beach.  RCMP patrols this campground regularly.
SECURITY  – Pachena Bay Campground is not responsible for vehicles or belongings brought into campsite.
PRIVACY  – Please respect the privacy of fellow campers and their desire for quiet enjoyment.
BEACH USE  – Please do not leave garbage on the beach, no building large structures or digging holes on the beach as it poses a risk to other campers.
SPEED LIMIT IS 10 KM/HR . – Drivers must obey posted speed limits and be aware of pedestrians, especially children at all times.
FOREST SIDE CAMPERS  – Please use designated public beach accesses only.
PET RULES  – Dogs must be kept on a leash; dogs must not be left unattended at any time.
• Excessive barking will not be tolerated.
• Animal stools must be picked up, wrapped, and placed in the garbage bins.
This is a condensed list of the Rules and Regulations.
For a full copy of the Rules and Regulations; these are presented upon confirmation/completion of your reservation in the Term & Conditions through Campspot.

West Coast Trail
The West Coast Trail is a 75-km long hike between the Vancouver Island communities of Bamfield and Port Renfrew, BC. The world renown multi-day backpacking trail is a challenge for even the most seasoned hikers and brings over 7,500 backpackers from around the world to the beautiful traditional territory our people, the Huu-ay-aht as well as the Ditidaht and Pacheedaht Nations.
West Coast Trail History
Our people have lived on the west side of Vancouver Island for over 10,000 years. Our ancestors created ancient paths and paddling routes that were used for trade and travel. Camps and villages existed along the coast before the arrival of Europeans.
As more ships travelled the west coast of Vancouver Island during the 1900’s, they navigated over reef and rocks during storms and big swells. Shipwrecks were quite common and stranded sailors ended up on the desolate coast. A trail was created that improved and linked the ancient pathways of our peoples.  A telegraph line and six emergency shelters with provisions were built and was called the Dominion Life Saving Trail.
In 1970, the Canadian Government created a new Park called Pacific Rim National Park Reserve and three years the life-saving trail was brought under the management of the Park board.
Hiking the West Coast Trail
This is one of the most challenging hikes in North America. But with the challenges come spectacular beauty.
The hike wanders along the coastline through old growth forests, sandy beaches, rocky headlands, slippery, root laden sections and navigates up and down many ladders. Doing all of this while carrying a week’s worth of food and your gear on your back, makes for a rewarding experience.
This hiking trail is not for the inexperienced. Being fit before the start of the trip is key to truly enjoying every minute of the day.
Most people take 5-7 days to complete the trail and there are designated campsites along the trail which ensure everyone has a place to lay their head at the end of each day. It is recommended by Parks Canada that you leave each campsite at least 5 hours before sunset to have time to make it to the next West Coast Trail campsite.

Trail Reservations
Being one of the most popular trails in Canada for hikers from around the world, bookings should be made well in advance of your travels. The reservations are necessary as there are a limited number of hikers allowed to start on the trail on any given day of the hiking season.
Parks Canada Online Reservation System  handles all bookings as this trail is located within  Pacific Rim National Park .
When making your booking you will need to have the following information ready as well as a means of payment
Preferred start dates
Number of people in your group
Names and emergency contacts
Point of departure – Pachena Bay, Gordon River or Nitinaht Village
