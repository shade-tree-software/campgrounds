CAMPGROUND & RV – STAY JUST MINUTES FROM THE HOT SPRINGS
Riverside camping for RVs and tents is available at Nakusp Hot Springs Campground above the soothing sound of the beautiful Kuskanax Creek. We have power services for RVs and are steps away from our natural mineral soaking pools. There is no sani-dump on site.  A   sani-dump is located 15 minutes away in Nakusp.
We offer thirty RV power sites (15 or 30 amp), eight tenting sites  (no   direct vehicle access ), three non-powered overflow sites. There are also accessible sites with power and water that are closer to the Hot Springs Building for guests with mobility issues (only 15amp power). We have clean, centralized washroom facilities with hot showers that are open 24/7 during the camping season.
All sites in the Campground proper have fire pits and fires are permitted except when fire bans are in place. Firewood is available for your convenience.
Book early — the sites are popular all summer and fall. The campground is open from May 1st (weather permitting)  to mid October, weather permitting. ONE SLEEPING UNIT PER SITE. Charges may apply to extra units.
Please note — campers need to be self-contained throughout the winter months as the water is turned off. Powered Sites A & D are available.

Cedar Chalets
If you like the amenities of cottage life, you will love our cozy chalets; full of charm and only a few feet from the hot springs. Perfect as a basecamp for all of your outdoor adventures; but be sure to book in advance as they are popular with our return customers, skiers in the winter, and hikers in the summer.
ACCOMMODATION FEE INCLUDES SOAKING PASSES FOR TWO PEOPLE. ADDITIONAL GUESTS ARE CHARGED A FEE OF $25 PER PERSON, WHICH INCLUDES ONE SOAKING PASS PER ADDITIONAL GUEST.
Each of our Chalets consist of a larger front suite and an adjoining smaller back suite. They can be rented separately, or combined for larger parties. Each chalet is equipped with satellite T.V. for your convenience. All of our Chalets are non-smoking. A shared BBQ is available for all guests.
All of our front suites are based on double occupancy (4 persons maximum). The Back Suites in each Chalet can accommodate a maximum of 2 persons. Each of our Chalets are pet-friendly, EXCEPT for the front suite of Chalet 1.
Check-in is 4:00pm – 8:00pm
Check-out is 10:30am (late checkouts may be subject to a $50 extra charge).

Payment of the balance of the reservation is due seven (7) days prior to the first day of the Guest’s stay and will be charged automatically by the reservation system.
Cancellation Policy :
A non-refundable $25 deposit will be charged at the time of booking for all reservations and will be deducted from the balance owing when the payment of the remaining balance is due.
In the event a reservation is canceled by a Guest, refunds will be issued as follows:
Notice received 8 (eight) or more days prior to the arrival date will not result in the outstanding balance of the reservation applied to the Guest’s credit card. The Nakusp Hot Springs will retain the $25.00 deposit charged at the time of booking.
Notice received between seven (7) and four (4) days prior to the arrival date entitles the Guest to receive a 50% refund.
Notice received three (3) days or less of the arrival date is not entitled to a refund of any kind; this includes when a guest does not show up for their reservation.
In the event of adverse weather conditions negatively impacting a Guests’ stay, impacts a Guests’ ability to reach the Nakusp Hot Springs, or results in a power outages, the above cancelation policy conditions apply. It is the guest’s responsibility to acquire trip or travel cancelation insurance through their chosen provider.

We offer a 10% discount for seniors aged 60+ who are paying for a chalet. Please contact us at the time of your booking to have this discount applied to your chalet reservation.
Chalet 1
Chalet 1 is our largest and most spacious suite, comfortably accommodating up to 8 guests. The suite features a primary bedroom with a queen bed, two additional bedrooms with one double bed and one queen bed, and a double fold-down futon. All bedrooms are in the upstairs section of the chalet.
Guests have access to a three-piece bathroom with a tub/shower, toilet, and sink, as well as a convenient two-piece bathroom with a toilet and sink.
The fully equipped kitchen includes a full-sized refrigerator, four-burner range and oven, sink, ample cupboard space, and cooking utensils – everything you need to prepare meals during your stay.
The base rate includes accommodations for up to 4 guests. An additional fee of $25 per person applies for each guest over four, and includes one soaking pass per additional guest.
Pets are NOT permitted in the Front Suite of Chalet 1.
