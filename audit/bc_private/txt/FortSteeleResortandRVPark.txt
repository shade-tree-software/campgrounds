Welcome to Fort Steele !
BOOK NOW
Fort Steele Resort and RV park is the perfect location for your next British Columbia getaway. Located in the heart of the Kootenay Rockies, enjoy unlimited recreational activities and more sunny days than any other place in British Columbia. Wake up next to the Rocky Mountains with a magnificent view of Fisher Peak.
​
Open year round and centrally located, there are lots of things to see and do in and around the resort. Directly across from the resort, Fort Steele Heritage Town will transport you back in time with the sights and sounds of frontier life. Our campground borders fields with a variety of animals to discover - horses, donkeys and emus. The area is bursting with outdoor activities of all kinds, from great hiking trails to superb lakes to amazing golf courses. Fort Steele Resort is the perfect destination for a family vacation, the long-term guest or the explorer.
Camping
At Fort Steele Resort & RV Park we offer camping for all preferences.
Whether you own an RV or prefer sleeping in a tent, we have sites for everyone.     If you prefer a solid roof over your head, we have a cabin to keep you cozy. If you like to spend your holidays by the pool, we have that too!
​

Our RV resort is a fantastic place to stay whether you're a travelling explorer, on a family vacation, or looking for a place to stay long-term. We offer over 110 serviced sites (30 or 50 amp/water & sewer) plus a dozen Unserviced sites. The majority of our Full Hook Up sites are pull-throughs for your convenience.
Our RV sites are wheelchair accessible and pet friendly. Each site has a picnic table and there are community fire pits located throughout the resort.
​
All sites have access to the outdoor swimming pool,  playground, sports field and basketball hoop. There are free hot showers for registered guests only and a coin operated Laundromat with single and triple front load washing machines. See all the     amenities     and     activities     the resort has to offer.
​
If you are looking to store an RV/trailer/boat any time of year, we offer monthly and yearly rates. We have uncovered outdoor storage sites available for monthly or yearly rental.
Please note that storage sites do not include access to campground amenities.

We offer over 60 tenting sites that are mostly located in a heavily treed area with water taps for everyone to share. Each site has a picnic table and some have their own fire pit. Because of the heavy trees not every site has its own fire pit, but we do have community fire pits for everyone's enjoyment as well as a covered shelter to share and available portable fire pits for rent.
​
All sites have access to the outdoor swimming pool,  playground, sports field and basketball hoop. There are free hot showers for registered guests only and a coin operated Laundromat with single and triple front load washing machines.
