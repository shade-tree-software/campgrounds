About Us
A Highway Tradition Since 1949
New Ownership, Timeless Service
Located at the junction of the Coal and Liard Rivers in the beautiful Northern Rockies, BC — 30 minutes north of Liard Hot Springs and 1.5 hours south of Watson Lake, Yukon.
Originally built in 1949, Coal River Services has served countless travelers along the Alaska Highway. Under new ownership by Kelly Clark, we've modernized while preserving the historic character that makes this stop special.
We're here for weary travelers, commercial operators, and adventure seekers — your northern highway oasis.
