Where “I’m Bored” Doesn’t Stand a Chance
Kids entertained, adults relaxed, and everyone just a little more outdoorsy than when they arrived.
At  Revelstoke RV & Cabin Resort , adventure starts steps from your site, and somehow gets better by the hour.
No traffic. No over-planning. Just real mountain time.
Book Your Revelstoke Stay
Why  ‍  You’ll Love it here
Basecamp to the Mountains -  Minutes from world-class parks, trails, and rivers, your next adventure starts before you even unpack.
Something for Everyone -  Pool days, playground takeovers, movie nights, sauna sessions… and yes, time to do absolutely nothing.
Comfort Without the Chaos -  Full-service RV sites, clean facilities, WiFi when you need it  (and a break when you don’t) , plus a store stocked with the essentials… including ice cream. Priorities.

Camping & Nightly Stays
