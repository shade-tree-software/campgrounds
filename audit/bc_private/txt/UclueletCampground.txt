Welcome
Located on the beautiful west coast of Vancouver Island in Ucluelet BC, expect 125 unique sites spread over 15 acres that range from harbour and mountain views to semi-private forested sites.
Are you looking for an RV Site this Winter?
Ucluelet Campground has seasonal sites from October – April.
Full-Service Sites at great Monthly Rates!
To inquire please email us  camp@uclueletcampground.com
We look forward to hearing from you!
Water Front Sites
These sites offer views of the Ucluelet inlet, the harbour and boat basin, and mountain views.  These view sites are available for both tents and RVs.
Full RV Hookups
Our full service RV sites have electric, water and sewer connections.  Depending on the site, we can accommodate RVs between 19ft and 42ft.
Forest Sites
These unique, semi-private campsites are located beside the trees.

Campground Rates
Rates
Our rates vary depending on the site and season.  For the latest availability and most up to date rates, please follow the link below to our booking page. Click on the map to view a larger image of our map and welcome brochure. A campground and site map is also available by following our booking link.
Rates are based on one vehicle with up to 4 occupants per site.  Additional fees apply for extra people. ($6/night)
Children aged five years and under are free.
Pets are welcome, and free of charge as of June 2026!
Reservations are recommended, and especially during the peak months of July and August.
Campground Map
Book Online
2026 Campsite Rates
Please Note:
All rates are before tax and reservation fees
Rates are based on a one vehicle and up to 4 people per single campsite.
The charge for extra people is $6 per person per night. (6 people per site max)
Site price allows 2 tents.  A third Tent is $7 per unit per night. (3 Tents maximum per site)
Children five years and under are free of charge.
Pets are welcome! And Free of charge starting June 2026!
OPEN YEAR ROUND!
Off Season Reservations must be made online.  Office is closed October 1st and Re-opens in Spring!
See  cancellation policy  on our website.
Valid Credit Card full prepayment is necessary for all reservations.
Thank you!
