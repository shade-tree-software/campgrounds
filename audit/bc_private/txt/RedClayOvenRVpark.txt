Premier RV Park
Safe and Accessible Camping Site
Escape the hustle and bustle of city life at Red Clay Oven RV Park. We are a premier, locally-owned residential RV park offering a secure, accessible, and well-maintained camping site in Powell River, BC.
Whether traveling with family and friends or going solo, our RV park can accommodate all your needs. With spacious sites equipped with full hookups, water, electricity, sewer, and fast Wi-Fi, our park offers comfort and convenience. Additionally, our park provides clean washroom facilities, showers, and laundry rooms, making your stay comfortable.
