Get Away From It All

Relax in the Quietness of the Chilcotin

Barney’s Lakeside Resort is here because of a pure passion for people. Our goals, ambitions and improvements have come together to form a truly amazing place for all our guests to enjoy. 
 
Get in touch with our region, if you are into outdoor activities, relaxing by the fire or have a passion for fishing, we have it all.

LOYALLY SERVING OUR CUSTOMERS SINCE 2016

Come enjoy Puntzi Lake!

Barney’s is a 12-acre lakeside resort located on beautiful Puntzi Lake. The lake is approximately 11km long and 5km wide with over 500’ of lake frontage, RV, and campground. Cabins range from 1 to 7 bedrooms.  

​

Go fishing for wild rainbow trout and kokanee (salmon)! We are a bird watcher’s (view our resident nesting pelicans!) and wild life viewing paradise. You can also enjoy hiking, mountain biking, ATV, water skiing, swimming, jet skiing, ice fishing, and snowmobiling!

We are located 2 hours west of Williams Lake and 4 hours east of Bella Coola.
Make Barney’s your home base as you experience the Chilcotin.

The Details

Beautiful lakeside camping for the whole family, 9 extra large full hook up RV sites, pull through big rig friendly, 6 additional campsite (2 partial hook ups with water and power / 4 dry), 1 campsite with travel trailer rental, and lakeside and private camping spots.

Full men’s and women’s showers, flush toilets. Sani dump available ($20 fee).

Cabins include beds, sofa beds, towels and bedding.

Full kitchens: All kitchens include utensils, pots, pans, microwave, fridge, stove, oven, coffee, tea, sugar, and whitener. 

Our NEW luxury lakeside cabins are open year round.

Our 7 bedroom Cabin is open all year round for weddings, reunions, corporate events, music festivals, and large fishing or hunting groups. 

Boat rentals: 14' or 16’ aluminum fishing gas motorboats available ($80/full day). Two double kayaks ($20/2 hours or $40/4 hours). Canoe ($20/2 hours or $40/4 hours). Two paddleboards ($30/2 hours or $60/4 hours). Please ask about half day rates.

Our property boasts a boat house, fish cleaning house, boat launch and two 40’ docks.

No day use for the public. This is a private resort.

Visitors (and their pets) of registered guests must check in at the office.
