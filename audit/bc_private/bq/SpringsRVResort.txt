Welcome to Springs RV Resort, where we are excited to have you join us for a night, a season, or as an owner of your own piece of BC paradise. Nestled amidst the mountains in the charming Village of Harrison Hot Springs, our Resort offers breathtaking views and easy access to the largest lake in the Fraser Valley, the stunning Harrison Lake.

Situated 90 minutes or less from most anywhere in the Lower Mainland, we provide a convenient escape from the hectic pace of daily life. Picture yourself enjoying our refreshing pools, soaking up the sun, and sharing quality time with loved ones. With Phase 3 now complete, we are ready to welcome you this summer!

We are confident that you will agree: life is truly better at Springs RV Resort.

DAILY RENTALS

Experience the ultimate RV getaway at Springs RV Resort! Discover a timber and cedar clubhouse, three heated pools, and hot tubs. Enjoy our games room, playground, and outdoor fireplaces. Bring your pets along and walk the forested trails. Join in the many social activities; movie nights, dances, BBQ’s, kids crafts on Saturday morning. Unwind by the family pool or find tranquility at the adult pool.
Your ideal RV adventure awaits!

FAMILY RENTAL

Located near the Family Pool and Hot Tub, beautiful spa-like bathrooms with heated floors, showers, and laundry room. Close to the gardens and playground. Family lots have 30 amp service, with full hook-ups, a variety of amenities – wood decks, or stamped concrete, some have gazebos and a picnic table.

PREMIUM RENTAL

Spacious 50-amp sites with full hook-ups and access to all resort amenities, including three pools, hot tubs, sauna, clubhouse, playground, and dog wash. Many sites feature stamped concrete patios, grass areas, and upgraded outdoor living spaces. Perfect for guests looking for extra space and access to our newest amenities.

 Thank you for choosing Springs RV Resort as your vacation destination.
There are some important details we want you to know before you make your booking.
We do not accept tent trailers, tents. RVs – motorhomes, 5th wheels, trailers, travel vans and truck campers only please.
Check-in is between 1:00 pm and 8:00 p.m.
If you expect to arrive later than 8:00 p.m., please call the office at 1-604-796-9767 in advance. Late check-ins may be subject to a $35 fee. 
Cancellation Policy and Reservation Change Policy:
Cancellations made within 14 days of your scheduled arrival are non-refundable.
Cancellations made more than 14 days in advance are subject to a $ 30. cancellation fee + $15 booking fee ($45). The remaining balance will be refunded to the credit card on file.
No changes to your reservation within 14 days of your scheduled arrival.
Changes to your reservations more than 14 days in advance may be subject to a $15. change fee.
No-shows and early departures are charged the full reservation amount.
 To make a reservation, please check the box to confirm that you have read and understood the criteria above.
