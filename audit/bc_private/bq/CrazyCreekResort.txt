WELCOME TO CRAZY CREEK

A hot pools resort in the forest, made for sharing

Crazy Creek Hot Pools Resort sits in the forest where Crazy Creek meets the Eagle River in Malakwa, BC, on the Trans-Canada Highway between Revelstoke and Sicamous. Families, friend groups and couples come for the outdoor hot pools, stay in a forest cabin or bring the RV, and spend their evenings around the fire.

Our pools are filled with geothermally heated water. It isn't a natural hot spring, just clean, hot water with the mountains all around, every day of the year.

SPRING AND FALL CAMPING IN BC

How to spend a rainy day at Crazy Creek

Spring and fall bring cool, misty days to the Eagle River valley. Here at the campground, that's the good stuff. This is how a wet day goes, from first coffee to the last log on the fire.

Step 1 · Morning
Coffee under the awning

Let the rain drum on the awning while the coffee brews. Pull your chair under cover, breathe in the wet forest and take your time. There's nowhere you need to be.

Step 2 · Evening
A hot soak in the rain

When the pool lights come on, grab your towels and walk over. Sink into the hottest pool while cool raindrops land on your face, then dare each other into the cold plunge.

Step 3 · Night
Back to the fire

Head back to your site warm to the core, get the fire crackling and pull up a chair. Let the weather do whatever it wants.

Your Sledding Basecamp

Find your riding zone, check the latest from the people who know it, then settle into a forest cabin and make time for the hot pools. Malakwa, BC, 30 minutes west of Revelstoke.

Check cabin availability
Eight riding zones. 
One basecamp.

From club-managed trails to places that call for more independent planning, each zone has its own character. Staging references below come from club, provincial and tourism sources checked in September 2026; six have a parking pin and three unmanaged areas have roadside references only. A pin is never proof that a road is open today. Confirm with the club before you go.

Owlhead

Sicamous side · Sled Sicamous managed. A varied area with a club cabin and both groomed and ungroomed routes. Respect the mapped Kingfisher Ecological Reserve closure.

Staging: Owlhead Parking Area at the start of Owlhead FSR, off Maclean/MacPherson Road. 

Map pin  · Owlhead Webcam · Grooming report · Club area page

Queest

Sicamous side · Sled Sicamous managed. The club's oldest area: a meadow by the cabin, views across the Eagle Valley on the access trail, and steeper, more varied ground beyond. Check the club map for ungroomed routes and cliff warnings.

Staging:  1800 FSR in Sicamous

Map pin · Queest cabin camera · Grooming report · Club area page

Blue Lake

Sicamous side · Sled Sicamous managed. Wide open alpine and subalpine terrain with a cabin and a novice training hill nearby. Groomed access leads to ungroomed riding, so treat the wider area as backcountry.

Staging: Blue Lake Parking Area at the end of Yard Creek Loop Road, where Yard Creek FSR begins. 

Map pin · Blue Lake Camera · Provincial trail record · Grooming report · Club area page

Eagle Pass

Sicamous side · Sled Sicamous managed · access needs confirming. Big, steep alpine terrain above 2,000 metres; the provincial record says it is not recommended for novice riders. Sled Sicamous posted an East Perry FSR landslide closure in March 2025 and we have not confirmed a reopening. Check with the club before planning around this zone.

Staging: Eagle Pass Parking Area via Bowolin Road and the East Perry River FSR, east of Sicamous. 

Map pin · Eagle Pass Cam · Grooming report · Club area page

Boulder Mountain

Revelstoke side · Revelstoke Snowmobile Club managed. A well used club trail system with groomed access, Boulder Cabin, bowls and technical trees, and advanced ungroomed terrain farther in. Trail fees and dates are set by the club.

Staging: the RSC Welcome Centre at 4177 Westside Road, Revelstoke. The club says West Boulder Trailhead has no parking. 

Map pin · Provincial trail record · Grooming reports · Club area page

Griffin

Revelstoke side · unmanaged. Low angle terrain, spaced trees, meadows and rolling hills, with no groomed club trail or cabin. Motor vehicles are prohibited in the adjacent Mount Griffin Ecological Reserve.

Staging: Wap Lake FSR roadside only; no exact lot is verified. 

Road search (not a parking pin) · BC Parks reserve · Club area page

Mt Begbie

Revelstoke side · unmanaged. The club describes a smaller, technical area with expansive views, reached from Wap Lake FSR. Do not confuse it with the Highway 23 Mount Begbie hiking trailhead, and check the Mount Macpherson seasonal motor vehicle closure boundaries.

Staging: Wap Lake FSR roadside only; no exact lot is verified. 

Road search (not a parking pin) · Club area page

Joss

Revelstoke side · unmanaged. Huge, wide open alpine after a long approach up Wap Lake FSR through burn and forest to rolling alpine and meadows. English Lake Provincial Park next door is non-motorized.

Staging: Wap Lake FSR roadside only; no exact lot is verified, and the provincial Joss Pass hiking record is not a sled staging point. 

Road search (not a parking pin) · CKMP terrain notes · Club area page

Sled Sicamous

Revelstoke Snowmobile Club
