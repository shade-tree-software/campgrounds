FORT LANGLEY
Fort Camping: Riverside Campground on Brae Island in Fort Langley

Fort Camping is operated by Pathfinder Camp Resorts under license granted by Metro Vancouver.

Fort Camping offers a unique blend of countryside serenity and city convenience in British Columbia’s Fraser Valley. Perfect for families, it provides access to outdoor activities and city attractions alike.

The resort accommodates all camping preferences with 156 RV sites, fire pits, a group tent area, and 5 tent cabins, making it ideal for a range of outdoor enthusiasts. From fishing on the Fraser River to golfing and exploring Brae Island, it’s all within reach.

Address
9451 Glover Rd, Langley Twp, BC V1M 2R9

Gatehouse Hours
Mid April-May 16th: 10am-7pm
May 16-June 26th: 10am-8pm
June 27-Sept 2nd: 9am-9pm
Sept 3-Mid Oct: 10am-8pm

RV SITES

Each of our RV and tent sites offers 30 amp, water, and electricity. We also offer 30 & 50 amp full hookups, catering to any needs. Big or small, there’s a perfect site for you and your family.

Can I have more than one RV or tent on the site?

Fort Camping does not offer double sites. All sites are for single family camping only.
The Fort Camping policy states that each campsite can accommodate one sleeping unit per campsite (RV or tent). An additional small tent (max 8’x 8′) is permitted if it is for minor children in the same family group.

About Fort Camping
Are campfires permitted?

Yes, small fires are permitted in the fire pits provided, subject to fire bans. Fires are only permitted from 8 am to 11 pm – for the safety of all this is strictly enforced. Please do not move fire pits. Propane fire pits are permitted and the same fire rules apply.

AGASSIZ
RV Park, Tenting and Cabins in Agassiz, 7 Minutes from Harrison Hot Springs

Nestled amidst rolling green fields and backed by stunning blue mountains, Pathfinder Agassiz is your slice of old-country charm. If you’re searching for a hotel in Agassiz or a motel stay, our resort offers a warm alternative with 8 freshly renovated cabins — perfect for a comfortable family getaway with or without an RV. 

Just a short drive from Harrison Hot Springs and seconds from downtown Agassiz, we also feature 32 full hookup sites and 31 campsites for every type of traveler.

 

Address
6110 Lougheed Hwy., Agassiz, BC V0M 1A0

WAYS TO STAY
RV SITES

Each one of our RV sites are 30/50 amp full hookup sites. We can accommodate any guest’s need: big or small!
