Camping at its finest

Night Sky is located on beautiful Boundary Creek with direct access to the KVR trail where you can bike, quad, or hike. The Park is close to a number of Boundary Country’s vibrant rural communities including Greenwood, Christina Lake and Grand Forks. Farmers markets, restaurants serving food sourced from local producers, as well as fresh roasted coffee and handmade ice cream are just a few of the choices. The area has countless lakes, rivers, roads and trails to explore. All sites have 30amp power, water and sewer. Bookings are available from late April to late October. With only 11 sites in the whole park, early reservations are highly recommended.

Welcome to Night Sky!

We are delighted to have you here. To ensure an absolutely wonderful experience for everyone, please read the following guidelines:
Park Policies
All RVers must be fully self contained. Wash up station & pit toilet are for tenters only.
Plan accordingly: There are no garbage or recycling facilities at the campsite – learn to be a minimalist, do not make garbage. The landfill is open Tue, Fri, Sat for recycling and garbage, if you must.
Rinsing your refundable drink containers will keep the wasps from an all out invasion. Lots of organizations will take those if you care to donate and will appreciate that they are clean.
Please reduce your energy requirements. Air conditioners are definitely not the  answer to this changing climate. Our ability to stay cool outside, at, or in the creek is absolutely vital to living in harmony with nature. Please do not leave  air conditioners or pets unattended.
Dogs must be leashed at all times. Lots of wildlife passes through here..
No unattended exterior lighting. I have been so impressed with everyone following  this long standing policy. The nights here are truly magical, darkness is 50% of the earths ecology and a vital aspect of our health. Thank you so much, spread the word about responsible lighting!
Absolutely no camp mats, other than park approved. It is both  the grass and the soil we need to protect. Please do not drive on it other than when it is absolutely necessary. Grey water watering is appreciated.
No canned music. If you play a musical instrument we would love to hear it.
