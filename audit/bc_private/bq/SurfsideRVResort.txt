Surfside RV Resort
Spectacular oceanfront RV membership resort with clubhouse, pool and hot tub. Members enjoy the security and privacy of a gated community surrounded by natural beauty within walking distance to Parksville´s commercial centre, restaurants as well as a wide variety of leisure activities.

Resort Policies
add
Reservations

Prepayment of entire balance is required at the time of booking. No exceptions.

Registration at the Main Office is required prior to occupying your designated site.

Site type and amenities are guaranteed; however, our automated optimization system may change your site location unless secured with a $30 location lock fee.

add
Cancellations

Cancellations will only be accepted by email to reservations@surfsiderv.ca

Cancellations will not be accepted by phone.

Any cancellation received within 14 days of the scheduled arrival date will result in a forfeiture of your entire payment. Cancellation received before this 14-day period are subject to a $56 cancellation fee, the remainder will be refunded to the credit card on file.

In the case of any no-shows or early departures, guests will still be responsible for the full amount of their reservation.

add
Occupancy Rules

Prices are based on two adults per site. Additional adults or children over six (6) years of age is $5 per person per day. The maximum occupancy on a campsite is six (6) people.

add
Deposits

Prepayment of entire balance is required at the time of booking. No exemptions (this does not include Winter Rentals for 28 days or more).

add
Security Access Cards

A $75.00 charge to your credit card will be added for each Security Access Card not returned, lost, or damaged. A maximum of two (2) Security Access Cards will be issued per site.

add
Check-In/Check-Out

Check in time is 1:00pm.

Check out time is 11:00am.

add
Sites

All sites are to be kept neat and clean for the duration of your stay.

The total number of guests in any space cannot exceed the maximum capacity of six (6) people total at a site at any given time.

Storage is not allowed under or around your RV or picnic table.

add
Vehicles

Speed limit is 20 km/hr in the RV Park. Any warnings may result in you, or your guests being asked to leave.

Only one (1) RV per space and a maximum of two (2) other vehicle allowed per site unless stated otherwise.

Please do not park your vehicle or trailer on an adjacent site. Use of more than one site will result in additional charges.

Maintenance of RV’s or vehicles on the property is not allowed. This includes fluid changes.

Washing/rinsing of RVs or vehicles on the property is not allowed except in designated areas.

We reserve the right to refuse access to any guests whose vehicle is deemed to be unacceptable.

add
RV Requirements

RV’s must be 15 years old or newer (or reviewed by Management). The RV must be fully self-contained, well kept, and in good condition. Exceptions are made at the discretion of management; however, we reserve the right to deny entrance to any RVs that do not conform to the resort specifications. Sorry, no tents, tent trailers, campers or pop-up vans.

add
Quiet Hours/Guest Conduct

Guests are expected to exhibit good conduct while on the premises. Please be courteous and do not disturb other guests.

Quiet hours are from 10:00 pm to 8:00 am.

Loud music or objectionable noise is not allowed at any time. Obnoxious behavior of ANY kind is prohibited.

Profanity, threats or violence towards other guests or Park staff will result in immediate eviction from the park. Use of illegal drugs is prohibited on the property and will result in an immediate request to vacate the property.

Vandalism of any kind by anyone in your group (regardless of age) may result in eviction from the park.

add
Children

Children refers to all persons 14 years of age and under. Children must always be accompanied by an adult while using any of the facilities.

add
Pets

There is a two-pet (2) limit per RV site.

Dogs and/or other pets must be on lead (leash) and under your control when outside your RV. Please use the dog walk area and clean up after your pet at both your site and common areas.

Four Pet Stations are provided for your convenience.

Excessive barking disturbs neighbours and may result in you being asked to leave without a refund.

Guests should carry proof of their pet’s current vaccinations.

Surfside RV Resort is not responsible for and will hold accountable pet owner(s) for any injury/damage caused by the pet(s).

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone whose pet is not considered suitable for the serenity/safety of our guests or the Park.

add
Open Fires

Open fires are not allowed in the park. Propane fire rings are permitted.

add
Fireworks

Fireworks of any kind are prohibited.

add
Trash Disposal

Dumpsters are available inside of the park. Please tie trash bags completely closed and put trash completely in the trash container. If one container is full, please use the other.

add
Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited in all common areas of Surfside RV Resort, both indoors and outdoors, to ensure a safe and comfortable environment for all guests.

Management reserves the right to make changes deemed necessary in these regulations. We reserve the right to refuse services to current and returning guests that have violated our rules and regulations and or have vacated the park without paying all fees.

Guests violating rules that result in eviction notice will not receive any refunds for their stay.

Getting Here
Parksville is centrally located on Vancouver Island's east coast. For travellers from the mainland, there are four main ferry terminals.
add
From South Vancouver Island(Victoria & Nanaimo)

Once on Vancouver Island, take Highway 1, which becomes Highway 19, north from Victoria or Nanaimo to Parksville and take Exit 46 for Parksville onto Highway 19A. Drive into town and turn right at the traffic light onto Corfield Street which is adjacent to Parksville´s Community Park and Curling Rink.

add
From North Vancouver Island(Campbell River & Comox)

Travelling down island from Campbell River and the Comox Valley, take Highway 19 to Exit #51 for Parksville and follow Highway 4 into Parksville. When you come to the junction of Highway 19A (you´ll see the ocean ahead of you), turn right moving as soon as possible into the left lane for a left turn at the Corfield Street traffic light.

add

Once you arrive at Surfside in Parksville come to the office for registration. You can download a full-size sitemap to help get a feel for the layout of the resort.
