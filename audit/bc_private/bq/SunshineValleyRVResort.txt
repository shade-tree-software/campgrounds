Embrace the Great Outdoors in Sunshine Valley

Located just 15 minutes east of Hope, BC, Sunshine Valley RV Resort & Cabins is your ultimate year-round destination for outdoor fun and relaxation. Surrounded by the majestic Cascade Mountains, our resort offers 110 large, 50-amp serviced sites and ten cozy, fully-equipped cabins, ensuring a comfortable stay no matter the season.

Adventure Awaits Every Season

No matter the season, Sunshine Valley brings the outback to your front door! Whether you’re looking to hit the slopes or explore the trails, you’ll find it here:

WINTER WONDERLAND

Enjoy skiing, snowboarding, and snowshoeing at nearby Manning Park. Our trails also transform into perfect routes for snowmobiling.

SUMMER EXPLORATIONS

Hike through miles of scenic trails, ride ATVs or dirt bikes, or take a leisurely boat ride on nearby waterways. In the colder months, these waters become a picturesque ice skating haven.

Resort Amenities for Ultimate Comfort and Fun

Our resort is equipped with all the amenities to enhance your stay:

POOLS AND HOT TUBS

Dive into our indoor and outdoor heated pools or relax in the hot tubs available year-round.

RECREATION

Enjoy access to fire pits, a fitness centre, and a recreation hall with a wood-burning fireplace.

CONVENIENCE ON SITE

Visit our General Store for necessities including beer, wine, and liquor, or utilize our propane filling station.

COZY CABIN GETAWAYS

For those who prefer a more enclosed setting, choose from our 10 mountain-style furnished cabins

Sunshine Valley Cabins: Your Home in the Wild

For those who prefer a more enclosed setting, choose from our 10 mountain-style cabins.

FULLY FURNISHED COMFORT

STAY CONNECTED

Free WIFI is available in each cabin for up to two devices, ensuring you stay connected while enjoying the wilderness.

New Surf Park opening 2025 - Bridal Falls, BC
The first phase of a new attraction bringing a surf park and Nordic spa to Bridal Falls Waterpark is set to open in 2025.
       
'The Reef Club at Bridal Falls' will bolster the recreational infrastructure in the region, with investment by Bridal Falls Waterpark and North Vancouver-based Ka’ana Wave Company.
               
They plan "to install Canada’s first surf park and Nordic spa" at the waterpark with patented wave-creating technology that they developed, according to Jamie Watson, founder and CEO of Ka’ana Wave Co.
                  
The mashup of water sports and winter spa is what will make it a four-season destination, and the first of its kind in Canada.

Escape to Bridal Falls, Where Adventure and Relaxation Meet

Nestled at the base of majestic Mt. Cheam in the picturesque Fraser Valley, Camperland RV Resort & Cabins offers a serene getaway just a stone’s throw from the hustle and bustle of city life. Located conveniently off the Trans Canada Highway and easily accessible from Vancouver, Chilliwack, Abbotsford, and Hope, our resort is your perfect base for an unforgettable escape.

Your Home Away from Home

Whether you’re planning a weekend break or a longer stay, Camperland has everything you need:

RV SITES

Explore our range of 280 RV sites, designed to accommodate any camper’s needs. Whether you choose a site with full hookups for ultimate convenience or prefer the simplicity of a partial hookup, our options—including several pull-through sites—ensure easy access and a hassle-free setup for your RV.

Long-term stays are available from October 15 to May 15.
CABINS AND FURNISHED RV RENTALS

For a more contained camping experience, consider staying in one of our 10 cozy camping cabins or opt for one of our 11 furnished RV rentals. Each offers a unique blend of comfort and the great outdoors, providing all the amenities of home in a scenic, natural setting. Whether you’re looking for a rustic cabin vibe or a more modern RV lifestyle, we have the perfect spot for your getaway.

RESORT HALL

The Camperland Hall can be rented for weddings, parties, dances, celebrations of life and other events. It is also available for one-time or recurring meetings of clubs or other groups.

General Policy
HTR Members; your site is assigned by the registration office.

Members who cancel outside 72 hours prior to their arrival date will not be charged a cancellation fee. Cancellations made within 72 hours of your reservation will be charged one night’s fee.


*Your member card and matching ID is required at check in.


Failure to show up on reservation day (no-show) will result in forfeiture of the entire
reservation and the member will be billed for up to three nights.

Non Members:

All reservations (excluding long weekends) carry a 7 day cancellation policy. Credit cards will be processed at time of booking. Photo ID (and matching credit card used for payment if checking into a cabin) will be required by guest at time of check in. This MUST match reservation. Reservations are Non-Transferable. Minimum age is 19.

A Reservation fee of $7 + tax will be applied to all bookings at time of reservation, and is non refundable.
