Experience the perfect blend of nature and convenience at Burnaby Cariboo RV Park. Whether you’re traveling in a fully-equipped RV, pitching a tent under the stars, or seeking a comfortable stay close to Vancouver, we offer premium accommodations to suit every adventurer.

RV Sites

Settle into our spacious full hook-up RV sites, complete with 30 amp power, water, sewer, and complimentary Wi-Fi. Enjoy paved pads with a picnic table, privacy hedge, and all the comforts you need for a stress-free stay.

Tent Sites

Immerse yourself in nature at our dedicated tenting area, where you can enjoy a peaceful outdoor experience with convenient access to modern washrooms, picnic areas, and park amenities. Please note that pets are not permitted in this area, and stays are limited to a maximum of 7 nights per calendar year.
