WHAT'S NEW

September 2, 2026

Good news, campers: the campfire ban has been lifted effective Thursday, September 3 at 12pm. Campfires are permitted again, but we ask everyone to use caution and follow all fire safety regulations. Fires must be kept to the regulated size of 50 cm x 50 cm, and water should always be kept nearby. Never leave a fire unattended, and before leaving your campsite or going to bed, make sure the fire is completely out and cool to the touch. Thank you for helping keep the campground safe for everyone this weekend.

August 17, 2026

Campers travelling to or from Caycuse Campground should be aware of a temporary road access change scheduled for August 28 through September 2, 2026.

Mosaic Forest Management will be replacing a bridge on South Shore Main at approximately the 10 km mark. During the bridge replacement, traffic travelling beyond the 10 km point will need to access the area via North Shore Road. Guests travelling to or from Caycuse Campground during the construction period will need to use the North Shore route rather than continuing along South Shore Main beyond the 10 km point.

The bridge replacement is currently scheduled to take place from August 28 through September 2, 2026. Access arrangements may change depending on construction progress and road conditions.

We apologize for any inconvenience this temporary closure may cause and appreciate everyone’s patience while this necessary infrastructure work is completed.

Please refer to this MAP for the recommended route to Caycuse Campground during the construction period.

August 17, 2026

Road maintenance and danger tree work will be taking place on the North Shore Main and South Shore Main over the coming weeks.

North Shore Main – August 18–21

Starting Tuesday, August 18, an excavator and flaggers will be working along North Shore Main through the remainder of the week to complete road repairs and maintenance. Minimal delays are expected, but drivers should slow down, follow flagger instructions, and use extra caution when travelling through active work areas.

South Shore Main – Following Week

Danger tree falling will also be taking place along the South Shore Main the following week. Drivers should be prepared for active work crews and temporary traffic interruptions in these areas. Please plan accordingly and drive cautiously around all roadwork and forestry operations.

We appreciate your patience and cooperation while this work is completed.

CAMPSITE MAP

Situated approximately 60 km west of Duncan on the end of Cowichan Lake forest service road. Before KM19up on North Shore Road, turn left on South Shore Road, until KM25down. If coming from Honeymoon Bay, it is located around KM24up on South Shore Road. There are 48 drive-in sites, and 15 walk-in sites that are directly on the beach. Non-potable tap water is available onsite (please note, tap water may be unavailable during drought season). The campsite can accommodate motor homes, trailers, and fifth wheels. Onsite you will find a beautiful gravel beach, and a trail up Heather Mountain. 15 beachfront tent sites are available and there is a public area with a boat launch for boats no longer than 14 feet. Firewood may be available for sale when campfires are permitted. Basic services can be found in Youbou (30 min drive) such as general store (including liquor and ATM), café, garage (including gas and propane), and pub. A little further is the town of Lake Cowichan, with extensive services such as grocery store, liquor store, hardware store, restaurants, sani-dump, water fill-up, laundromat, and public showers.
