Lakeside Cabins & Adventures in the Chilcotin
Cast a line, kick back, and make yourself at home

Woodlands Fishin Resort is a rustic lakeside fishing resort located on beautiful Puntzi lake in the Chilcotin, British Columbia. Just 2 hours west of Williams Lake along Highway 20, it’s a peaceful place to enjoy cozy cabins, RV camping, and some of the best fishing in the region.

Nestled along the serene shores of Puntzi Lake, a hidden gem where White Pelicans reside and fish abound, we believe in the power of simplicity, good food, and the great outdoors to nourish the soul and soothe the mind. 

Whether you’re here to cast a line, kick back, or simply enjoy the quiet beauty of the Chilcotin, Woodlands is a place to slow down, unwind, and feel at home.

Come and experience the quiet beauty of the Chilcotin for yourself.

Puntzi Lake – The Destination

Puntzi Lake is situated near the tranquil community of Chilanko Forks, approximately 175 km west of Williams Lake (a roughly 2-hour drive) via the Chilcotin-Bella Coola Highway (BC-20W). As one of the larger lakes in central Chilcotin, Puntzi Lake resides in the heart of the traditional territory of the Tsilhqot'in people and was once home to a sizable village.

Renowned for its abundant population of Rainbow Trout and Kokanee fish, along with its diverse birdlife, Puntzi Lake offers a picturesque and unspoiled natural setting unlike any other within a short distance from Cariboo’s central hub.

Lakeside RV Campground
Come Camp With Us

Give a man a fish and you will feed him for a day. Teach a man to fish, and you will get rid of him for the weekend!

Escape the daily grind and reel in some relaxation at our Lakeside RV Campground at Puntzi Lake, Chilcotin, BC. Our 12-site, all-grass RV campground is the perfect catch for those traveling with a mobile home, offering a tranquil and beautiful spot by the lake. Tenting spaces are also available!

Wake up to stunning sunrises and sleep under the stars, surrounded by the sounds of nature. We provide our Tent and RV Campground guests a free boat launch and 4 docks for your fishing adventures, plus a fish cleaning station, ensuring you have all the gear you need to reel in the big one

Book your stay with us and dive into the ultimate lakeside getaway in the Chilcotin. Woodlands Fishin’ Resort RV Campground offers:

6 RV sites with partial hookups along the lakefront.

6 RV sites with full hookups, offering power, water, and sewer.

Quiet, flat grass tenting sites available.

Prices include access to a washroom, fish cleaning station, and  water.

Pets are welcome but must be kept on a leash.

ATVs are welcome with back road access to nearby trails.

Playground area for kids. Fire pits and picnic tables provided.

Sani dump available at a fee.

Wi-Fi available for guests.

Reserve Your Site

All rentals and campsites are based on a family of two adults and children under the age of 5. We allow up to two additional adults/children for an extra fee. Please note that four adults is the maximum occupancy for all sites and rentals.

Extra Guests & Services

Each additional person is charged CAD$5 to 10. 

Children aged 5 and under stay free of charge.

Children over 5 should be included in the total number of adults for booking purposes.

Additional services such as laundry facilities, Sani-dump, and firewood are available for extra fees.

Cancellation

Cancellations made 14 or more days prior to the arrival date, excluding the arrival date itself, are subject to a $50 administrative fee.

Cancellations made less than 14 days prior to the arrival date, excluding the arrival date itself, are subject to a cancellation fee equal to two nights' stay, totaling $60.00.

Lakeside Cabins at Puntzi lake, BC

 Cozy lakeside cabins at Puntzi Lake in the Chilcotin, British Columbia, just 2 hours west of Williams Lake along Highway 20. Our cabins are perfect for relaxing, fishing, and enjoying the quiet beauty of the region.

Get ready to cast your worries away and dive into comfort at Woodlands Fishin Resort's rustic cabins! With breathtaking views of Puntzi Lake, our cabin rentals are perfect for reeling in relaxation and adventure. Each of our newly built cabins features a spacious deck, fully equipped kitchen, cozy bedding, fresh towels, and high-speed internet. Get ready to cast your worries away! Hook, Line, and Sinker into comfort!

Our rates include complimentary use of our laundry room and fish cleaning station, taking the hassle out of your holiday. For added convenience, guests can enjoy delicious meals at Puntzi Lake Bakery and Restaurant and access firewood and ice upon request at a nominal rate. Book a cabin rental today and let us make your stay unforgettable, just like landing the big one!
