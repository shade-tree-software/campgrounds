ABOUT US

We’re a small, thoughtfully operated campground in Squamish, BC, designed for outdoor explorers who want clean, calm, and convenient access to world-class adventure.

Our location backs directly onto Squamish’s extensive trail network, offering ride-in, ride-out and hike-in, hike-out access to some of the best trails in British Columbia. Whether you’re here to bike, climb, hike, paddle, or simply spend time in nature, MTN Fun Basecamp is your launchpad.

This isn’t a campground for parties, large groups, or campground-style entertainment. You won’t find playgrounds or loud events here. Instead, we’ve created a peaceful space where guests can rest well, rise early, and get outside.

Our campground is run by a small on-site team and designed for guests who value quiet nights, clean amenities, and a respectful environment. We welcome solo travelers, couples, families, and international visitors who are here to enjoy the Sea to Sky’s natural beauty — not make noise.

If that sounds like your kind of place, we’d be happy to host you.

Stay With Us

Whether you're road-tripping in an RV, pitching a tent under the stars, or looking for a low-key basecamp with a real bed, MTN Fun offers a range of outdoor-friendly options for every kind of adventurer. Browse our accommodations below to find the best fit for your trip.

Campsites
Unserviced Tent & Dry Camping Sites

Our unserviced sites are ideal for tent campers and dry camping setups looking for a more traditional campground experience.

We offer:

Smaller sites suited for 1–4 people

Larger family sites for 4–8 people

Designated group tent sites (by request only) for up to 12 people

Most sites are shaded and private, and select sites offer upgraded Wi-Fi coverage.

Each campsite includes:

Picnic table (group sites include two)

Fire pit (subject to fire bans)

Access to the main lodge with hot showers and shared washroom facilities

Many of our larger tent and group sites can accommodate dry camping setups, including vans, trailers, or RVs up to 22 feet, provided no hookups are required.

Please note:

Tent and dry camping sites do not include power, water, or sewer

Group sites are not bookable online and must be approved in advance

All sites are intended for single, self-contained travel groups

Serviced RV Sites

Our serviced RV sites are located in quieter areas of the campground, near the creek or at the base of DeBeck’s Hill. These sites are reserved exclusively for self-contained RV setups using full hookups.

Each serviced RV site includes:

30-amp power, water, and sewer
 (Green 8 includes power and water only)

Fire pit and picnic table

Access to upgraded high-speed Wi-Fi at most sites

Access to lodge washrooms and hot showers

Important notes:

RV sites are not available for tent-only bookings

Sprinter vans and self-contained overlanding vehicles are welcome

RVs and trailers must be 10 years old or newer or have clear records of maintence

A single small tent for children or guests is permitted only when accompanying an RV or trailer

Roof-top tents are not permitted

RV site occupancy limits apply and are strictly enforced

Rooms & Cabins
Hotel Rooms

Our cabin-style hotel rooms offer a comfortable indoor option while keeping you close to the outdoors. These rooms are well suited for couples or small families who want privacy without a resort feel.

Each room includes:

Queen or King bed, plus futon or sleep nook
 (sleeps up to 2 adults and 1 child)

Kitchenette with fridge, microwave, Keurig, and basic cookware

Private bathroom

Heating and air conditioning

Wi-Fi access

Hotel rooms are listed and booked through Airbnb, and Airbnb’s policies apply.

Tiny Cabins

Our tiny cabins are rustic, glamping-style accommodations popular with climbers, bikers, and hikers who want a simple, affordable place to sleep.

Cabin layouts:

One cabin sleeps 2 adults plus 1 child (child in top bunk)

Two cabins sleep 2 adults only

Each cabin includes:

Mini fridge and Keurig coffee maker

Heater or fan (seasonal)

Outdoor seating

Access to lodge washrooms and hot showers

Additional details:

No kitchens or private bathrooms

Guests may use the communal propane BBQ area

Propane fire pits are available to rent, or you may bring your own

Wood fires are not permitted near the tiny cabins

Tiny cabins are intentionally simple — cozy, practical, and designed for guests who plan to spend their days outside.
