Lodging & Accommodations

Bella Coola Valley has something for everyone!

Lodges, Hotels & Motels
Tweedsmuir Park Lodge Guided Tours
Tweedsmuir Park Lodge offers guided tours for guests to explore the stunning natural beauty of British Columbia's Bella Coola Valley. Experienced guides lead small groups on wildlife viewing excursions, fly fishing trips, and hiking adventures. Guests can also enjoy cultural experiences and relaxation at the lodge, surrounded by the majestic coastal mountains.
Learn More
Eagle’s Nest Resort
Eagle's Nest is located on peaceful Anahim Lake in the heart of BC's spectacular Chilcotin plateau. Choose a cozy lakeside cabin or comfortable room. Enjoy delicious meals in our licensed lakeside dining room, rent a canoe to explore the lake, hike in nearby Tweedsmuir Park, relax in our Guest Garden to enjoy the view, or watch the many species of birds. Open year round to serve you
Learn More
Bella Coola Valley Inn
Bella Coola Valley Inn is located in the heart of Bella Coola, British Columbia. The inn features comfortable rooms and suites with modern amenities, a restaurant serving delicious local cuisine. Guests can explore the beautiful Bella Coola Valley or take a guided tour to see grizzly bears and other wildlife.
Learn More
Retreat Wilderness Inn
Retreat Wilderness Inn is an All-Season destination, offering intimate, four star suites. With over 40 years experience operating this property, your hosts, Donn and Lee will assist in making your vacation one to remember. Our picturesque, landscaped, lakefront grounds provide an amazing setting for relaxing on the patio or watching the sunset on the waterfront deck as the loons sing their soulful call.
Learn More
Bella Coola Grizzly Tours
Bella Coola Grizzly Tours and Adventure resort is a wilderness lodge nestled in the Bella Coola Valley, offering guests comfortable accommodations and guided grizzly bear viewing tours in the surrounding area.
Learn More
Suntree Guest Cottages
Escape to a natural getaway on the Bella Coola River with deluxe guest cottages in a serene wilderness setting. Nestled on 40 acres surrounded by mountains, it's a perfect retreat with all amenities and complete privacy.
Learn More
Eagle Bear Lodge (Tatla Lake)
Eagle Bear Lodge is a wilderness resort located in Tatla Lake, British Columbia, Canada. The lodge is situated on 10 acres of forested land and offers a variety of activities such as wildlife viewing, fishing, hiking, and canoeing. The lodge features comfortable accommodations, a restaurant, and a gift shop.
Learn More
Woodlands Fishin Resort
Woodlands Fishing Resort is a serene retreat located on Puntzi Lake. With breathtaking natural beauty, it offers an ideal setting for fishing enthusiasts. The resort provides well-equipped cabins camping, RV sites and services to enhance the fishing experience. Guests can relax, unwind, and immerse themselves in the tranquil surroundings while indulging in their favorite sport.
Learn More
Nuk Tesli Lodge and Guided Tours
Nuk Tesli Lodge is a wilderness retreat in BC's Chilcotin region, situated in a peaceful valley with stunning views of the Coast Mountains. The lodge offers comfortable accommodations in cozy cabins, guided hiking and horseback riding tours, and opportunities to spot wildlife such as bears, moose, and eagles.
Learn More
Great Bear Chalet
Great Bear Chalet is a rustic lodge in Tweedsmuir Provincial Park, BC. With 2 private suites , each featuring a private bathroom and stunning views of the park, guests can unwind by the fireplace. The lodge offers guided nature tours, fishing trips, and wildlife watching. It's the ideal place to disconnect and escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life. We don't 'tour' it, we coexist here.
Learn More
Bella Coola Mountain Lodge
Bella Coola Mountain Lodge is a rustic mountain lodge in the Bella Coola Valley, offering guests comfortable accommodations, delicious cuisine, and access to outdoor activities such as hiking and fishing.
Learn More
Tweedsmuir Park Lodge
Tweedsmuir Park Lodge is a luxury wilderness resort located in the Bella Coola Valley, offering guests comfortable accommodations, gourmet dining, and guided tours for outdoor activities and wildlife viewing.
Learn More
Red Cariboo Resort
Red Cariboo Resort is located near Anahim Lake on a remote property at Highway 20. Modern apartments with fully equipped kitchens or cozy cabins for up to 6 persons, very comfortably furnished are directly heading to the waterfront and offer a fantastic view. The Resort offers various activities year-round like horse-riding, hiking, canoeing, ice-/fishing as well as gear rentals for private excursions.
Learn More
Firvale Wilderness Resort
Firvale Wilderness Lodge is a secluded retreat in the heart of the Great Bear Rainforest, offering guests comfortable accommodations and guided tours for grizzly bear viewing, fishing, hiking, and kayaking.
Learn More
Bella Coola Eagle Lodge
Bella Coola Eagle Lodge is a luxury wilderness retreat located in the heart of the Great Bear Rainforest, offering guests comfortable accommodations and unparalleled access to outdoor activities and wildlife viewing.
Learn More
Bella Coola Heli Sports
Bella Coola Heli Sport offers helicopter skiing and snowboarding adventures in the rugged mountains surrounding the Bella Coola Valley. With over two million acres of terrain to explore, experienced guides, and comfortable accommodations, it's an unforgettable experience for advanced skiers and snowboarders seeking an adrenaline-filled winter vacation.
