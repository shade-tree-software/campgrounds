The forest is calling.....
Cedar Falls Campground is your backcountry escape just 12 minutes from Vernon on the way to Silver star ski hill and mountain bike park. Family friendly campground with a wide variety of activities and games, nestled in a beautiful forest surrounded by cedar trees in BX area / Vernon. We are right across the road from the BX waterfalls. Vernon RV Camping Tenting sites Cabins available.

Our unique cabin
Our lovely little cabin is 11’ x 11’ and it sleeps 4. It has a custom made murphy Queen size bed and two single beds up in the loft. The loft is simply a ladder up to a small sleeping area with a very short wall and is not recommended for young children.
The cabin has a partial dry kitchen with fridge, microwave, toaster oven, coffee maker, tea kettle and toaster and a TV. We offer an outside BBQ kitchen, as well as a fire pit and picnic table inside a partially fenced area. There is an air conditioner and an electric wall heater.
 The cabin does not have plumbing, but it is in close proximity to our beautiful washroom facilities. You will need to bring all your own bedding and towels. While pets are permitted to camp here they are not permitted in the cabin.
The cost starts at $85.00 per night plus GST,PST and MRDT tax. This includes two adults and two children. Extra adults are $10/person/night, up to a maximum occupancy of 4 people. We do require a three night minimum stay. Vernon long term rv seasonal sites available.
