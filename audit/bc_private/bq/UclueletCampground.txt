Welcome

Located on the beautiful west coast of Vancouver Island in Ucluelet BC, expect 125 unique sites spread over 15 acres that range from harbour and mountain views to semi-private forested sites.

 
 

Are you looking for an RV Site this Winter?

Ucluelet Campground has seasonal sites from October – April.
Full-Service Sites at great Monthly Rates!
To inquire please email us camp@uclueletcampground.com

We look forward to hearing from you!

Water Front Sites

These sites offer views of the Ucluelet inlet, the harbour and boat basin, and mountain views.  These view sites are available for both tents and RVs.

Full RV Hookups

Our full service RV sites have electric, water and sewer connections.  Depending on the site, we can accommodate RVs between 19ft and 42ft.

Forest Sites

These unique, semi-private campsites are located beside the trees.

Campground Rules
Please read all the information on this page to ensure you have a safe and enjoyable stay
Campground Rules
Check- in Time: After 2:00 p.m.  Guests who have booked online may check in after office has closed.  Please be mindful of noise curfew
Check-Out Time: before 11:00 a.m. No refunds for early checkouts.
Quiet Time: 10:30 p.m. – 7:00 a.m., excessive noise or loud music will not be tolerated at any time.
One registered vehicle per site, (all other vehicles must park in the street)
Dangerous or disorderly conduct will not be tolerated and will result in an automatic eviction of the entire party. There are no refunds if you are evicted.
A major credit card and valid ID will be required of all reservations. Any additional fees will be charged to the credit card on file.
No boats or boat trailers are permitted on-site – Boat Trailer storage available nearby – contact the Campground Office
SEAPLANE BASE Sites may park a boat on their site if space allows, for a $15 fee, per stay
Bear & Wildlife Attractant
Do not leave any animal attractants (anything with a scent) unattended on your site/ in your tent when you leave or go to bed. Pack everything into your vehicle or our storage shed.
Wildlife attractants include, but aren’t limited to: food, garbage, coolers, EMPTY COOLERS, BBQ’s, bins, dirty stoves, dirty dishes, cans, empty cans or bottles, soap, and toiletries.
Dirty campsites will be cleaned immediately by our staff and will result in a $25 cleaning fee that will be charged to the credit card on file. If it happens again, you will be evicted without a refund.
For more info visit WildSafe BC website
Visitors
The registered person, whose name the reservation is made under, assumes all responsibility for all adults, children or wards in the registered parties group. This reservation holder must agree to all campground rules.
If a guest is breaking the rules, the entire booking will be evicted. There are no refunds for evictions.
Pets
Dogs must always be on leash; Cats must remain in vehicles.
Dogs may not be left unattended at any time; Excessive barking will not be tolerated.
Animal stools must be picked up and placed in garbage cans.
Animals are not allowed in washrooms, or office (except guide dogs).
Generator Use
Generator use is only permitted between the hours of 9 a.m. – 10:30 a.m. and 5 p.m. – 7 p.m. daily, in order to provide campers with a quiet and enjoyable experience.
Campfires
Be informed of any current fire BANS or restrictions.
Fire pits must not be moved.
Fire Size – fires must be contained within the firepit provided. It is illegal to have a fire outside of a designated location.
Wood – Use wood that is provided within the campground.
Do not burn garbage or collect deadfall from the surrounding forest for burning.
Times – Campfires are not permitted during quiet hours (10:30 pm to 7am).
Fires must always be attended.
During Fire Bans CSA approved propane and charcoal appliances are permitted.
Thank you and enjoy your stay!

This property is privately owned and operated.  We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone.  We are not responsible for accidents or injuries to our guests.  We are not responsible for loss of equipment or valuables of any kind.
