THIS IS BARKERVILLE

Located in Nature’s Heartland, Barkerville was born from Billy Barker’s legendary gold strike on Williams Creek; an event that triggered a multi-billion-dollar industrial revolution. Today, this National Historic Site brings the Gold Rush era to life along a preserved streetscape of more than 125 heritage buildings.

Explore historic homes and shops, step inside working displays and museums, connect with costumed interpreters, and experience the stories, sights, and flavours of a 19th-century town set amid the forests and mountains of the Cariboo.

FOREST ROSE CAMPGROUND

OPEN MAY 14 – SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

This forested campground offers a sunny creek-side setting for campers of all kinds, with more trees and privacy on the north end in the first come, first served section, and sunnier pull-through sites on the south end in the reservable section.

Forest Rose Campground has 56 campsites, 26 of which can be booked in advance. It features a playground, and a washroom building with flush toilets, potable water, and coin-operated showers.  Bear-proof garbage and recycling bins are offered in central locations throughout the campground.

All campsites are equipped with a wheelchair-accessible picnic table and campfire ring. Campsites are an average of 55 feet long and 13 feet wide and can accommodate most trailers and RVs. Standard cabins are available in the Lowhee Campground.

Forest Rose Campground is located off of the Bowron Lake Road, along the east side of Williams Creek and a stone’s throw from the Barkerville Highway. It’s 2 km north of the Barkerville Welcome Centre. Guests can travel back and forth from the Historic Zone to Forest Rose via the highway, or cross the highway on foot to access a well-groomed walking path. Forest Rose Campground is part of the 1,100 hectare property that forms Barkerville Historic Town & Park and is managed by Barkerville Heritage Trust.

LOWHEE CAMPGROUND

OPEN JUNE 18 – SEPTEMBER 28, 2026

This well-treed campground offers a private and peaceful setting for campers of all kinds, with a deep forest on the south end in the first-come, first-served section, transitioning to a wetland on the north end in the reservable section.

Lowhee Campground has 80 campsites, 47 of which can be booked in advance. It features a playground, three washroom buildings with flush toilets and potable water, as well as a coin-operated shower house. Bear-proof garbage and recycling bins are offered in central locations throughout the campground.

All campsites are equipped with a wheelchair-accessible picnic table and campfire ring. Campsites are an average of 50 feet long and 14 feet wide and can accommodate most trailers and RVs when backed into the sites. Pull-through sites are available in the Forest Rose Campground. 

Lowhee Campground is located along the west side of the Barkerville Highway, 2 km north of the Barkerville Welcome Centre. Guests can travel back and forth from the Historic Zone to Lowhee along the highway, or via a well-groomed walking path alongside the highway. Lowhee Campground is part of the 1,100-hectare property that forms Barkerville Historic Town & Park and is managed by Barkerville Heritage Trust.

THE BARKERVILLE COTTAGES

Fully equipped modern vacation rentals at Barkerville’s doorstep!

$429.00 per night, two night minimum stay

The Barkerville Cottages offer wheelchair-accessible, fully furnished retreats with queen bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, dining, and living rooms. They come with bed linens, towels, kitchen equipment, and Wi-Fi. The cottages also have a porch and mudroom for storage and outdoor enjoyment.

THE HISTORIC GUEST HOUSES

A quiet getaway in historic Barkerville!

$459.00 per night, two night minimum stay

Luxury Heritage accommodations right in the center of town. Located within the heritage site in a restored Barkerville home. Guests will be surrounded by period décor that will transport you back to the Cariboo’s great gold rush era. The Historic King and Kelly Guest Houses are pedestrian access only.

Please note that a refundable damage deposit of $250.00 will be charged on each accommodation. This must be paid prior to check in.
This deposit will be refunded after you check out, provided the accommodation is left in satisfactory condition.

Each accommodation sleeps up to six people.
