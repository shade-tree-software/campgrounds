CHARMING RV PARK

With over 6 acres in beautiful Vernon, British Columbia, Lake Front RV Park is nestled against the shorelines of the wildlife-rich Swan Lake. We are centrally located across Highway 97 from The Squires Four Pub, 10 minute drive from both Okanagan and Kalamalka Lake beaches. We are a short 7-min drive to Splashdown Vernon Waterslides and 25-min drive to SilverStar Mountain Resort. Quick access to grocery stores, restaurants, gas stations and more! Come experience a premier Okanagan RV Site. 

We are a full service campground with 47 full-service sites with water, power (50amp) and sewer. 6 full-service sites with water, power (30amp) and sewer and 28 half-service with water and power (30amp). The majority of the sites are along the shoreline. 

Welcome to Lake Front RV Park where your Okanagan adventure awaits!

Property Information


With over 6 acres in beautiful Vernon, British Columbia, Lake Front RV Park is nestled against the shorelines of the wildlife-rich Swan Lake. We are centrally located across Highway 97 from The Squires Four Pub, 10 minute drive from both Okanagan and Kalamalka Lake beaches. We are a short 7-min drive to Splashdown Vernon Waterslides and 25-min drive to SilverStar Mountain Resort. Quick access to grocery stores, restaurants, gas stations and more! Come experience a premier Okanagan RV Site. 

We are a full service campground with 47 full-service sites with water, power (50amp) and sewer. 6 full-service sites with water, power (30amp) and sewer and 28 half-service with water and power (30amp). The majority of the sites are along the shoreline.  

Welcome to Lake Front RV Park where your Okanagan adventure awaits!

Property and Cancellation Policies
Property and Cancellation PolicyAt Lake Front RV Park, we have implemented a clear, consistent, and firm cancellation policy to support operational planning and guest transparency. Please review the following terms carefully, as all bookings are subject to these non-negotiable conditions:
Cancellations Made 15 Days or More in Advance
Cancellations received 15 days or more prior to the scheduled arrival date are eligible for a full refund, less a non-refundable $15 administrative fee.
Cancellations Made 14 Days or Less in Advance
No refunds will be issued for cancellations made within 14 days of the scheduled arrival date. This includes changes to the reservation or shortening of the stay.
Early Departures
Guests who arrive and elect to depart prior to the completion of their reserved stay will not be eligible for any refund of unused nights.
No-Shows
If a guest fails to arrive on their scheduled date without prior written notice of cancellation, the full amount of the reservation will be charged. The reservation is deemed forfeited after the first missed night.How to Cancel
All cancellations must be submitted in writing to lakefrontrvpark.bc@gmail.com. The cancellation request must reference the reservation number and full name on the booking. A cancellation is only confirmed once the guest receives written acknowledgment from Lake Front RV Park.Force Majeure (Acts of God)
Refunds will only be considered if a formal government-issued evacuation order is placed on the campground property located at 6310 Stickle Road, Vernon, BC. General weather events, smoke, or travel disruptions do not qualify for refunds under this clause.We thank you for your cooperation and understanding. These policies allow us to maintain fairness for all guests and ensure a high level of service continuity.
