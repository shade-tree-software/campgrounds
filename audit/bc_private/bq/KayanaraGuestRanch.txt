Why stay at Kayanara?
Read More

We all deserve to dream.

Eco-Friendly Farm Stay Cabins in British Columbia. Nestled in the heart of the beautiful South Cariboo, Kayanara is a 220-acre eco-friendly guest ranch offering an unforgettable farm stay experience. Surrounded by towering trees and bordered by the tranquil Eagle Creek, our accommodations provide the perfect blend of comfort and sustainability. Whether you’re visiting for a family vacation or a relaxing retreat, our farm stay cabins near Canim Lake offer a peaceful escape into nature.

Sustainable & Farm-Fresh Experience. Kayanara is more than just a place to stay—it's a chance to connect with nature in a meaningful way. We are committed to sustainability and tending to this land consciously. With a focus on eco-friendly practices and living every day full of joy, we offer pet-friendly vacation rentals in British Columbia that embrace the beauty of the land while minimizing our environmental impact.

Our newly built greenhouses and a 33-foot geodesic dome extend our growing season, allowing us to offer farm-fresh produce to our guests when available. Our on-site farm store features farm-raised eggs, naturally grown meats, and a selection of artisan offerings, ensuring your stay supports both local agriculture and sustainability.

Ideal for Nature Lovers & Eco-Conscious Travellers. Our multi-use trails offer breathtaking views and direct access to the expansive backcountry, perfect for hiking, biking, or wildlife watching. Experience the tranquillity of Kayanara, where you can immerse yourself in nature’s beauty, all while enjoying eco-friendly accommodations in Cariboo. We invite you to discover pristine lakes, winding rivers, and abundant wildlife, making Kayanara the ideal destination for those seeking an intentional, sustainable retreat.

This gorgeous cabin offers a cozy retreat. Its inviting layout comfortably sleeps four guests in one queen-sized bed and two single beds, with room for up to six using the two sofa beds. The bright, open interior is accentuated by stunning blue-tinted wood, a unique and beautiful mark left by the Blue Pine Beetle that once passed through the area. Handcrafted furniture, created from wood harvested right here on the property, adds a touch of rustic elegance throughout the space.

At the heart of the cabin is a charming wood stove, creating a warm and cozy ambiance on chilly nights—the perfect spot to relax after a day of exploring.

Included amenities:

2 inviting bedrooms & 1 full bathroom
Fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances
Dining area and a spacious, comfortable living room
High-speed wireless Internet and Bluetooth speaker for entertainment
Private deck with seating, a picnic table, and a barbecue 
Laundry facilities with a washer and dryer available in separate building

This peaceful, secluded cabin blends luxury with tranquility, making it an ideal setting for a relaxing getaway, whether you’re looking to unwind or reconnect with nature.
