Aloha,

We are a Family Run Campground that offers Seasonal Camping and Venue Rentals for Retreats, Weddings & Private Events. Located in Celista, BC, The HeArt of The Shuswap.

​

Our Campground offers, Cabin Rentals, Full Service RV Sites, Partial Service RV Sites, Trailer Rentals & Tenting alongside the Creek. All Guests have access to our beautiful Bathhouse, including Coin Laundry & an outdoor Dish Cleaning Area. 

 

Only a Block away from our Public Beach on Shuswap Lake, a Boat Launch & Convenience Store. ​

​​

A perfect place for an Intimate Wedding in Nature, Camping alongside the Creek and a Lovely Home-away-from-Home experience!

 

​Much Love,

Belle & Taylor Campbell
