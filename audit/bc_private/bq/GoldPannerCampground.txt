EXPLORE LAND WITH A RICH HISTORY

Old growth forests, rushing creeks, and rainforest air scented with cedar & moss - the landscape around Gold Panner Campground has been drawing explorers into the Monashees for generations.

A FAMILY-FRIENDLY DESTINATION FOR ADVENTURERS AND TRANQUILITY-SEEKERS ALIKE

Whether you're looking to explore the creeks and mountains of the Monashees, try your hand at gold panning, or curl up and relax in nature with a good book, Gold Panner Campground has something for you.

Fall Bonfire Nights at Gold Panner Campground
October 23 - October 24

Pull up a stump, grab a roasting stick, and warm up by the fire. 🔥 Join us for two cozy fall evenings at Gold Panner Campground in Cherryville, the weekend before Halloween. Roast hot dogs over the bonfire, sip hot chocolate or warm apple cider, and enjoy a night under the Monashee sky with neighbours, friends, and family. All proceeds from admission and food sales go to Cherryville Community Food & Resources. When: Friday, October 23rd and Saturday, October 24th 4:30 - 9:00pm What's on: - Community bonfire - Hot dogs available for purchase for you to roast over the fire or BBQ (BYO roasting sticks encouraged!) - Hot chocolate and hot apple cider - Family-friendly, all ages welcome - A draw to enter to win a weekend of camping during the 2027 season! Admission: By donation. All proceeds support Cherryville Community Food & Resources. Bringing a non-perishable food item for the food bank is encouraged too! Good to know: - Is it family-friendly? Yes! Kids are welcome, with adult supervision around the fire. - Do I need to book? No, just drop in! Come one night or both. - Do I need to bring my own roasting sticks? It is highly encouraged if you have them, as we will have some available but our supply is limited! - What if the weather turns? Watch this event page for updates.

RATES:

Our rates use a dynamic pricing system, meaning prices may vary depending on season, demand, length of stay, and how far in advance you book. Some dates—such as long weekends—may carry premium pricing, while quieter periods or early bookings may see reduced rates (often even cheaper than previous management!) Extended stays may also qualify for automatic discounts. The most accurate rates will always appear on our booking platform here. For occasional sneak-peek offers and limited promotions, be sure to follow along on our social media channels!
