RV Sites
RV Sites

All our RV sites offer full hookups with the option of having 30 or 50 amp. Enjoy free passes to our pools, waterslides, and mini golf. The on-site playground is available for all ages. Most sites are private and offer plenty of space for up to 40 ft rigs.

Check In

Check in time is 2:00 pm Check out time is 11:00 am. After hour check-ins are to be arranged with the office during regular hours. Weekdays 8:00 am to 9:00 pm. Weekends 9:00 am to 9:00 pm.

FREE WIFI

Each site comes with free WiFi Internet access.

LAUNDRY

Our resort offers coin laundry facilities for our guests. Open during regular office hours.

BATHROOM

We have a building with public washrooms central to the park and coin-operated showers with hot water.

OFFICE HOURS

Regular office hours are: Weekdays 8:00 am to 9:00 pm. Weekends 9:00 am to 9:00 pm.

FREE PARKING

There is free parking for the guests of our resort on their site. Extra parking is available on request.

AMENITIES

All sites come with their own fire ring and picnic table. Firewood is sold at the store.

PETS

Pets are allowed on our property as long as they are leashed.

THE ACCOMMODATIONS
Extended Stay Units – Winter Season

Planning to stay for 30 days or longer this winter? Our resort offers comfortable extended stay accommodations available from September 1st to June 15th, perfect for enjoying a long-term winter getaway. Rates vary depending on the type of unit and the season, and all rates include taxes, water, hydro, Wi-Fi, and parking.

Our units are self-sustainable and fully equipped with a full kitchen (or kitchenettes in studio suites) and a full bathroom, giving you the convenience and comfort of home.

Additional features for extended stays:

Bedding and kitchen supplies are not included, but can be provided upon request.
Pet-friendly units are available for an extra monthly fee (service dogs excluded).

Whether you’re looking to work remotely, enjoy a winter escape, or simply relax in a cozy space, our extended stay units offer the perfect home-away-from-home experience.

To book or inquire about availability, please contact us directly. We’d be happy to help secure your winter stay!

RV SITES
Extended Stay RV Sites – Monthly or Year-Round

Looking for a place to call home? Our RV sites are available for extended stays,whether for a month or all year round. Each site is full hook-up, including water, power, and sewer, giving you the convenience and comfort you need for long-term living.

Join our friendly and welcoming community and enjoy access to resort amenities while having your own private space. Our park is perfect for those who love the freedom of RV life.
