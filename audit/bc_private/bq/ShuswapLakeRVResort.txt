WELCOME TO SHUSWAP LAKE RV RESORT

​

Shuswap Lake RV Resort is located in Celista, BC. The property is 29 acres of  forest with towering trees and 300  feet of pebble beach on Shuswap Lake  for owners, renters and guests. The RV sites are available for year-round use and built in amongst the beautiful trees.

Head down to the beach, hang out on the dock and swim in the beautiful clear waters of Shuswap Lake. There is a brand new boat launch across the road!

SHUSWAP LAKE

Shuswap Lake is located in the southern interior of British Columbia. Our resort is on the sunny North Shore of Shuswap Arm in the small community of Celista.

EXPLORE THE AREA

The area surrounding Celista is perfect for all your outdoor adventure needs such as swimming, boating, mountain biking, golfing, hiking, cross-country skiing, snowmobiling, and much, much more!
