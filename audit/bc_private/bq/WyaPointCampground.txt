Experience the rugged, natural beauty of the west coast.

 

Wya Point Resort rests on pristine, private beaches surrounded by 600 acres of old-growth forest on Ucluelet First Nation’s traditional territory, welcoming visitors with luxurious lodge accommodations, waterfront yurts and beachfront camping.

 

This natural, west coast adventure lodging is located only a few kilometres outside Ucluelet and minutes from the world-famous Pacific Rim National Park.

 

Outstanding Accommodation Award by Aboriginal Tourism BC in 2014.

Oceanside Campsites

WYA Point offers the only beachfront camping south of the junction!

With secluded sandy coves, clear water and hidden rock pools, the beaches at Wya Point provide hours of recreational fun for all the family.

Camp just steps from a private beach and enjoy the crashing waves as you sleep.

AMENITIES
Secluded pitches
Fire pit
Picnic table
Communal Washroom – with fully serviced flush toilets & showers
Communal Dishwashing station
Fire Wood (addt’l charge)
PET FRIENDLY

Don’t leave Fido at home.  Four legged family members are welcome!

The beaches are a perfect place to run and there are kilometers of nearby trails for you and your dog to explore.

Please let us know at the time of reservation that you are bringing your pet.   There is a pet fee. We can accommodate up to 2 pets (dogs or cats) per unit.  Sorry, no other pets can be accommodated.
