A mix of forest and waterfront sites are found here, along with a boat launch for boats no longer than 14 feet, a picnic area, and a nice beach. The site permit tents, motor homes, trailers, and fifth wheels. Firewood may be available for sale when campfires are permitted, please check with host.

WHAT'S NEW

September 2, 2026

Good news, campers: the campfire ban has been lifted effective Thursday, September 3 at 12pm. Campfires are permitted again, but we ask everyone to use caution and follow all fire safety regulations. Fires must be kept to the regulated size of 50 cm x 50 cm, and water should always be kept nearby. Never leave a fire unattended, and before leaving your campsite or going to bed, make sure the fire is completely out and cool to the touch. Thank you for helping keep the campground safe for everyone this weekend.

August 17, 2026

Campers travelling to or from Caycuse Campground should be aware of a temporary road access change scheduled for August 28 through September 2, 2026.

Mosaic Forest Management will be replacing a bridge on South Shore Main at approximately the 10 km mark. During the bridge replacement, traffic travelling beyond the 10 km point will need to access the area via North Shore Road. Guests travelling to or from Caycuse Campground during the construction period will need to use the North Shore route rather than continuing along South Shore Main beyond the 10 km point.

The bridge replacement is currently scheduled to take place from August 28 through September 2, 2026. Access arrangements may change depending on construction progress and road conditions.

We apologize for any inconvenience this temporary closure may cause and appreciate everyone’s patience while this necessary infrastructure work is completed.

Please refer to this MAP for the recommended route to Caycuse Campground during the construction period.

August 17, 2026

Road maintenance and danger tree work will be taking place on the North Shore Main and South Shore Main over the coming weeks.

North Shore Main – August 18–21

Starting Tuesday, August 18, an excavator and flaggers will be working along North Shore Main through the remainder of the week to complete road repairs and maintenance. Minimal delays are expected, but drivers should slow down, follow flagger instructions, and use extra caution when travelling through active work areas.

South Shore Main – Following Week

Danger tree falling will also be taking place along the South Shore Main the following week. Drivers should be prepared for active work crews and temporary traffic interruptions in these areas. Please plan accordingly and drive cautiously around all roadwork and forestry operations.

We appreciate your patience and cooperation while this work is completed.

August 6, 2026

Road Work Notice Beginning August 17 2026 road improvement work will be taking place on Nanaimo Lakes Main Road (0 km to 4.5 km). Traffic control will be in place with periodic delays of up to 30 minutes while culvert replacements and drainage improvements are completed. Crews will begin work at 5:00 a.m. to minimize impacts on campground traffic. Please allow extra travel time and follow all traffic control personnel.

July 14, 2026

Effective 12:00 p.m. (noon) on Thursday, July 16, a campfire ban will be in effect at all Mosaic campgrounds. Visitors are reminded to follow all posted restrictions and use caution while enjoying the outdoors. For full details and updates, please visit: BC Wildfire Service

May 28, 2026

Good news, campers: the campfire ban has been lifted effective immediately Wednesday, May 27 at 4pm. Campfires are permitted again, but we ask everyone to use caution and follow all fire safety regulations. Fires must be kept to the regulated size of 50 cm x 50 cm, and water should always be kept nearby. Never leave a fire unattended, and before leaving your campsite or going to bed, make sure the fire is completely out and cool to the touch. Thank you for helping keep the campground safe for everyone this weekend.

May 18, 2026

Please note that Mosaic Forest Management, as private landowners, have decided to keep the campfire ban in place on their lands. Campfires are not permitted at this time at any of their campgrounds. Propane fire pits are still allowed in approved areas as long as the flame height does not exceed 15 cm. Please use caution and help reduce wildfire risk while enjoying the outdoors.

May 6, 2026

A campfire ban will take effect at 12:00 PM (noon) on Thursday, May 7. All wood burning campfire will be prohibited as per BC Wildfire Service regulations. Propane fire pits are still permitted as long as the flame hight is under 15 cm and they are used in approved areas only; they are not allowed on the beach. These measures are in place to help reduce the risk of wildfires. For full details and updates, please visit: BC Wildfire Service

South Vancouver Island

Situated approximately 40 km west of Duncan, BC, the campsites are located on the shores of Cowichan Lake.

Heather, Caycuse, Little Shaw and Kissinger Lake

Central Vancouver Island

Nanaimo Lakes campsites are located on the shores of First Lake, 35 km southwest of Nanaimo, BC.

Old Mill, Windy Point, Riverside and Deadwood.

Macktush - Port Alberni
30-km drive from Port Alberni along Canal Main is the only motor route where you can access the north shore of the Inlet, which also takes you to Arden Creek, Nahmint Lake, Gracie Lake and the back side of Sproat Lake.
