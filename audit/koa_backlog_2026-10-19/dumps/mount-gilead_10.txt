PRESIDENTIAL SITE: There's only ONE! The Presidential Site is secluded in its own private section on the back end of the grounds situated on a site that measures 175 ft wide and 150 ft deep overlooking 50 acres of meadow. This elite site blends all the deluxe camping upgrades with extra luxuries such as a 16 x 24 furnished shelter with vaulted ceiling & chandelier to your very own golf cart. Here's the luxurious details.......... PULL RIGHT ON IN: The pull thru RV pad is 120 ft long with 41 ft of it being solid level concrete where the RV is parked. Step out onto an oversized concrete patio that houses an outdoor dining table with chairs & umbrella with a gorgeous shelter just ahead. RELAX IN THE SHELTER: The shelter beyond the patio is open on all sides and boasts a giant outdoor candle chandelier, concrete & tile bar, mini fridge (3.2 cu. ft.), trash receptacle cabinet, kitchen sink w/ hot water tank, standing ice chest with wheels, bar stools, furniture and Smart TV with remote. GATHER OUTSIDE: Just outside the shelter, you will enjoy a fire pit surrounded by giant stone boulders & 4 Adirondack chairs. Two (2) Weber gas grills side by side with BBQ utensils for cooking. Your pet will get plenty of exercise in the oversized paw pen close by. ACTIVITIES ON SITE: Farther out on the site is your personal putting green (balls/putters provided) and Just beyond, is your own private walking path leading to a pond (don't forget your fishing rods!). Not into golfing or fishing? This site has it's own heavy duty cornhole board set. GETTING AROUND THE GROUNDS: If all of that isn't special enough, then this surely will make an impression.....this site comes with a 4 passenger golf cart! That's right, you have use of the presidential site golf cart for each day you are registered on this site! SITE DETAILS: Nightly price includes up to five (5) people. City Water, City Sewage, Double Drop Sewage, 20/30/50 amp Electric. In addition to the one (1) RV unit, this site is allowed to have one (1) tent added at no additional cost.
