The Back In sites are on the northern perimeter of the campground, perfect for smaller RVs. Book early for the few longer Back In sites (or reserve a Pull Thru at a comparable cost!). The Back In sites are full hookup, with 50/30 amp electric, water, and sewer. Every site is a buddy site, gravel, has a picnic table, and is large enough for one RV and one vehicle. Nearly all are pet friendly, some have a shade tree, and a few have a built-in firepit (you may bring your own if it has legs). There is plenty of free overflow parking for cargo trailers and additional vehicles.

Cancellation Guideline
The Pull Thru sites are in the center of the campground, mostly north of the main drive. These buddy sites are full hookup, with 50/30 amp electric, water, and sewer. All have a picnic table, several are close to the bathhouse and pool, some have a shade tree, and a few have a built-in firepit (you may bring your own if it has legs). All sites are gravel, pet friendly, and large enough for one RV and one vehicle. There is plenty of free overflow parking for cargo trailers and additional vehicles. There are big-rig friendly options, with a max pull-thru length of 50’. Please be aware that we require ALL towed vehicles and trailers to be disconnected from the Motorhome prior to moving to a Pull Thru site. To keep your truck connected to your trailer overnight, book early for availability and provide the combined length of your tow vehicle and trailer.

The Premium Pull Thru Patio sites are in the center two rows of the southern side of the campground with excellent views of Red Cliffs Recreation Area.
