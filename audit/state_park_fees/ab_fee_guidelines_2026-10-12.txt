The range for fees in Alberta's provincial parks is set through directive by the Department head.
Each campground's fees are based on market demand and the services available at that site (within the approved range).
Fees are inclusive of the federal Goods and Services Tax (GST) unless otherwise stated.
There are no fees for day use in provincial parks, however a vehicle parking pass is required for vehicles parked at provincial parks and public land sites in Kananaskis Country and the Bow Valley Corridor.
The Camping Fee Discount for Alberta Seniors is applicable at select individual front country campgrounds.

Damage Deposits
Damage deposits may be required for some comfort camping units, group camping areas and/or equipment rentals (e.g. a canoe). Damage deposits allow Alberta Parks to collect for any loss, damage, substantial cleaning or additional human resources costs associated with rental of a facility or rental of equipment. The damage deposit will be held until the facility or equipment has been inspected and cleaned (where applicable). Once the facility or equipment has been deemed to have no extra cost associated with said rental, the damage deposit hold will be released. If there is loss, damage or additional human resource costs for extra cleaning, only the actual amount of the loss, damage or extra cleaning will be taken from the damage deposit hold.
