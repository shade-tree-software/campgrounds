CAMPGROUND SERVICE RATES 2026
Summary
The following Rates apply to all of the Provincial Park Campgrounds. HST (15%) and Transaction Fees are applicable to all reservations.
Camping Fees
Unserviced |
$38.00 |
Two-way Service |
$45.00 |
Three-way Service |
$50.00 |
Camp Cabins (4 person) |
$75.00 |
Transaction Fees (non-refundable)
Transactions fees for reservations are as follows:
$6.00 + HST reservation
$2.75 + HST change to reservation
$2.75 + HST cancellation reservation
Site Service(s)
Unserviced sites will not have any services such as Electricity, Water etc.
Two-way service sites will have Electricity and Water.
Three-way service sites will have Electricity, Water and Sewer.
Camp Cabin sites will have Basic wooden structure cabin (sleeps 4)
Discounts
Senior Citizen Rates
Senior Citizens 60 and over may receive a 10% discount on the daily rate.
Disabled/handicapped
Disabled/Handicapped persons may receive a 10% discount on the daily rate.
Applying Discount
Visitors are only permitted to one (1) camping discount to their reservation.
