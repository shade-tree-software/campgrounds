General Rules and Regulations
Deposits
All SRA Parks require a 1-night, non-refundable deposit for all overnight reservations at the time of reserving the facility.
Payment is due from the customer at the time of check-in for the remaining balance owed for the duration of the stay.
Refunds will not be made for customers wishing to shorten their stay after check-in.
Reservation Policies at Cypress Bend Park
Reservations for Easter, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, and Major Events cannot be made until January 2nd of the year they occur.
No reservation can be made in excess of 15 days unless authorized by the Administrative Office. Any authorized reservation will require a 50% deposit. Reservations made in sequence (same pad for same family) will be considered as one reservation and charged accordingly.
Blocks for tournaments and/or family reunions will be handled via contract.
Overnight Reservations
Overnight reservations are available at Cypress Bend Park, Oak Ridge, Pleasure Point, and San Miguel Park for all cabins and RV spurs.
Reservations can be made by calling each individual park or the Toledo Bend Tourist Center at
(800) 259-LAKE (5253) or (318) 256-4112 .
All reservations must be secured by a credit card.
There is a 2-night minimum stay for reservations made from March through September on weekends, and a 3-night minimum stay for Easter, Memorial Day, 4th of July, Labor Day, and Major Events.
Cancellation Policy
Any overnight accommodations canceled prior to 14 days of arrival will result in the loss of the deposit.
Cancellation within 14 days or less of the arrival date will result in being charged for the entire reservation.
Cancellations for designated holidays and major events will result in the loss of the 2-night deposit required at the time of booking.
Monthly campers are subject to a separate cancellation policy.
Annual Passes
An Annual Pass entitles the holder and one guest (over the age of 12) to enter any SRA Recreational Facility for day use.
Annual Passes are for individual and/or family use only.
Discounts
Senior Citizens (62+) and Disabled Veterans may receive a 20% discount on all overnight rental fees with proper verification.
To qualify:
Senior Citizens must show proof of age (e.g., Driver’s License).
Disabled Veterans must present a Disabled Veterans State Park ID card.
Discount applies only if the qualifying person stays overnight in the facility.
Applies to RV Spurs, RV Rentals, Cabins, and Shelter rentals of 10+ nights.
