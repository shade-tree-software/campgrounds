Park fees 
Day-use parking 
Day-use parking requires a Discover Pass which is $10 for the day or $45 for an annual pass. You don't have to pay this if you have already paid for camping in the park you staying in. 
Watercraft launch fee 
$7 for the day, with a Discover Pass. You don't have to pay this if you have already paid for camping in the park you staying in. 
Trailer dump fee
$5 per use, with a Discover Pass. You don't have to pay this if you have already paid for camping in the park you staying in. 
Overnight unattended vehicle fee
$10 per night. Allows you to leave your vehicle parked over night at a long distance trailhead or watercraft launch site.

Campsite descriptions
Hiker/biker sites are
 open to campers who arrive by foot or bicycle. Amenities may not be close by.
Water trail sites are
 open to campers who arrive by non-motorized watercraft. Amenities may not be close by.
Primitive sites are
n't near flush restroom. Primitive campsites may be walk-in without nearby parking.
Standard sites
 have nearby drinking water, sink waste, garbage disposal and flush restrooms.
Partial hookup sites are
 standard sites with electric hookup. Many also have water hookup. They don't include a sewer hookup.
Full hookup sites are 
standard sites with the addition of electric, water and sewer hookup. Due to freezing temperatures, water may not be available during the winter months.
Extra vehicle fee
$10 per night. Campsites, roofed shelters and vacation homes include parking for one vehicle. Extra vehicles must register at check-in and park in designated spaces.  
Vehicles towed into the park by an RV will not be charged the extra vehicle fee. The RV must remain at the site for the entire length of stay. If both the RV and towed vehicle are moved from the site, the towed vehicle will be charged the extra vehicle fee.
Campers are encouraged to leave extra vehicles at home, as not all sites can accommodate extra vehicles.

Moorage site 
descriptions
Docks and linear moorage are available first come, first served.  
Buoys are available first come, first served.
Marina sites at Twenty-Five Mile Creek, Lake Chelan, Sun Lakes, Lincoln Rock have reservable slips used to store your boat during your camping stay.
Phone in buoy registration fee
There is a $4.50 convenience fee to register by phone for a buoy. 
Dockside electric hookup fee
Some parks offer electric hookups on the dock. Each plug is $6.   
Reservation fees
A non-refundable reservation fee is charged for each reservation, $8 if made online and $10 if made by phone - (888) CAMPOUT or (888) 226-7688.
We also charge a cancelation fee. Learn more about our cancellation policy for details.
Non-Resident Reservation Fee
Customers who do not reside in Washington state will be charged a $15 fee per reservation. This fee is in addition to the regular reservation fee.
Change and Cancelation Fees
$8 to change or cancel online. $10 to change or cancel by phone - (888) CAMPOUT or (888) 226-7688.
Reservation dates may be extended without paying a change fee.  Learn more on the Changing a Reservation page.
