Camping Reservations Visitors may make reservations from 3 days to 11 months in advance on reservenevada.com. Inventory opens up at 8 a.m. Pacific Standard Time and is on a rolling 11 month booking window. All reservable campsites not reserved may be paid for on a first come, first served basis for 1 night at a time. First come, first served visitors may extend their stay by reaching out to park staff. Campground and cabin check in is at 2:00 pm and check out is at 11:00 am. Group Use Areas vary by check in and check out times but are listed on their Unit Details. There is no minimum stay requirement, but each park varies for their maximum stay from 7 to 14 days. This information can be found on each park’s webpage. Payment is due at the time of booking your reservation. To ensure fair access to cabin reservations, modifications and cancellations will be restricted for a period of one week. This policy is designed to prevent last-minute adjustments by those booking on the edge of the reservation window, to allow others the opportunity to reserve available dates before an existing reservation is extended or adjusted. -Reservation Fees Visitors pay a non-refundable $5.00 transaction fee per reservation. -Modifications Modifications can be made up to 3 days before the day of arrival. Visitors will be charged $5.00 for making a change further than one month out from their upcoming stay. Visitors will be charged $10.00 for making a change less than one month out from their upcoming stay. -Cancellations Visitors can cancel their reservation up to 3 days before the day of arrival. Cancellations occurring more than a month out from the arrival date will not be charged for canceling their reservation, with the exception of the $5.00 nonrefundable transaction fee. Cancellations occurring less than a month out, but more than 3 days before the day of arrival, will have a $10.00 fee assessed. Cancellations that occur within 3 days of the reservation arrival date will have a $10.00 fee assessed plus the cost of a one-night stay. - Refunds will not be given for: Events, circumstances, or decisions that are beyond the control of the Agencies. Including bad weather and other weather conditions (smoke), early departure not due to an emergency, drought, bugs, fire restrictions or bans, water conditions, trail closures, algae, traffic/city noise, equipment failure, customer error and Wi-Fi/cellular service availability. Early departures or cancellations after arrival date. -No Shows Park staff will hold reserved campsites or cabins until the applicable check-in time, one day after the scheduled arrival date. If contact is made with the park, the reservation will be honored. If the visitor does not make an effort to contact the park then the reservation will be cancelled and nonrefundable.
