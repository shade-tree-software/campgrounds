Introduction
This schedule lists fees established by the Minister pursuant to authorities in the Park Act.

The schedule represents recreation user fees regulated under the BC Parks Recreation User
Fees Regulation. This regulation does not include park use permits or administrative penalties.

This fee schedule does not include Service Plus fees. Service Plus fees are charged by park
operators for enhanced services. They are established by agreement between the Ministry
and park operators. Service Plus fees related to camping are identified in the costs listed in BC
Parks reservation service at camping.bcparks.ca.

This schedule also sets out fee exemptions under the authority of Section 29.2(4) of the Park
Act.

The tables below list recreation user fees by protected area for services found in Section 3,
Table 1 of the BC Parks Recreation User Fees Regulation, long-stay camping as per Section
39(1) of the Park, Conservancy and Recreation Area Regulation, application of a $20 non-
resident fee charged to visitors from outside of British Columbia for select services, and notes
on the recreation user fee schedules, including specific fees in effect in select locations from
January 30, 2026 until May 14, 2026.

Non-resident fee
Effective May 15, 2026, a $20 flat rate non-resident fee will be charged to visitors
from outside of B.C. The non-resident fee applies to frontcountry and backcountry
camping, cabin rentals, and the use of mooring buoys and docks. Non-resident
fees do not apply to children or youth.


The non-resident fee will not be implemented until 2027 for:
   •   The Berg Lake Trail in Mount Robson Park
   •   The Bowron Lake Canoe Circuit in Bowron Lake Park
   •   Backcountry cabins in Mount Assiniboine Park
   •   Groupsites
   •   Picnic shelters
