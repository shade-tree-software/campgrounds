AZTEC CAMPING QUEBEC
Cottage and RV Resort Camping

Located midway between Sherbrooke and Montreal, Aztec Camping is a great vacation destination for families who enjoy good company and entertainment.

Cycle the path that runs through the resort, followed by a relaxing swim in our 200ft wide lake, or play a game of soccer on our recently upgraded field. Enjoy the warm summer days when you're relaxing by the pool, or watch the kids have fun on the splash pad. Take the family to our full-service restaurant located on-site or stop by the convenience store for an evening snack.

Aztec Camping has become a premier vacation destination for families looking to spend a summer in great company, having great fun.
