THE #1 FAMILY CAMPSITE IN MONTÉRÉGIE
Welcome to campers and their families!

This summer, discover the Domaine International de Rouville located at the foot of the Mont Saint-Hilaire biosphere reserve. Since 1960, this popular international tourist destination has been offering an aquatic component, camping stays, magnificent golf courses and a brand new performance hall. We distinguish ourselves by our two magnificent fine sandy beaches, with a large number of activities and our superb seasonal and traveler grounds. The attention paid to safety, cleanliness, good taste in the facilities and the large number of activities also make us famous. For a day, for your holidays or for the whole summer, come have fun and enjoy a lively, dynamic and friendly atmosphere. OUR STORY

A rich history

For 65 years now, the Domaine International de Rouville has been an iconic place for camping and recreation in Quebec. Founded in 1960 by architect Maurice Robillard and his wife Gertrude Fontaine, the Domaine was born from a simple idea: to create a large, accessible and vibrant outdoor space at the foot of Mont-Saint-Hilaire, supplied with exceptionally high-quality water.

Visionaries for their time, the founders launched Quebec's first true campground and one of the first public golf courses, establishing from the outset a culture of innovation and close relationships with visitors. This tradition of hospitality and quality continues today, as the site welcomes over 200,000 visitors annually, including thousands of loyal seasonal campers.

Choosing a seasonal plot at the Domaine also means being part of a family history, a close-knit community and a place that evolves with the seasons, while preserving the soul of its origins.

Important Information
The prices shown are before taxes.Prices are valid for 2 adults and 4 children.Please note that the showers are free to use.The minimum age to rent a campsite is 18. An adult aged 18 or older must accompany minor campers on the rented campsite at all times.Please note that no dogs are allowed in the lakes or the swimming pool. Dogs are also not allowed on the sand. Your dog must be kept on a leash (1.85 m) and must be under the constant supervision of an adult within one meter.

HOURLY


Arrival time is 3pm . Departure time from the rented land and site is 1pm.

BOOKING POLICY

Number of nights:

Please note that no minimum number of nights is required for travellers except on the following occasions where a minimum of two nights is required: Saint-Jean Baptiste (June 23-26), Construction Holidays (July 23-August 5), Canada Day (June 30-July 3) and Labour Day (September 1-4).

When making a reservation, you must have the following information on hand for each site booked:

NamesFull addressPhone numberE-mail addressEquipment specificationsA credit card (Visa or Mastercard)

For all reservations, the total amount will be charged to your credit card. Additional charges may apply upon arrival for extra people and/or vehicles. By providing their credit card details, the customer agrees to the cancellation policy outlined below.

CANCELLATION POLICY

➤ Cancellation within 24 hours of order creation: a full refund of the order will be issued.

➤ Cancellation up to 7 days before the arrival date: the order amount will be refunded at 50%, except for the ticketing system service fees which are non-refundable.

➤ Cancellation between 0 and 6 days before the arrival date: no refund will be issued.

* Certain specific conditions may apply in the event of any change to your booking beyond the allotted 24 hours (cancellation of part of the stay, change of booking dates, etc.)
