Welcome to Camping St-Polycarpe

Located near highway 20, exit 6, 30 minutes from Montreal. Camping St-Polycarpe offers a pleasant and relaxing environment in the heart of nature. We are a family campground, which offers a variety of activities for the whole family. A large playground with several play modules, 2 swimming pools, a small lake with a superb beach, a convenience store, and restaurant. A multitude of activities are organized by the recreation committee for everyone’s pleasure.

We offer a variety of weekender and seasonal lots as well as cabin and RV rentals.

Visit the “Reservations“ tab and submit a booking request for your stay with us.

Our Services

At Camping St-Polycarpe we are proud to offer you a variety of services and amenities.
Each campsite includes a picnic table, fire pit, and free WiFi.
Each campsite includes a picnic table and an outdoor fire pit.
We have a large heated family pool as well as an adults-only (18+) pool.
The playground includes multiple play structures, swings, jungle gym.
We have a large dedicated horseshoe and lawn bowling area that hosts multiple tournaments throughout the season.
A new area has been designated for a basketball court with non-slip tiles and new basketball hoops.
Two pickleball courts have been built for everyone who enjoys practicing this new sport.
We have a restaurant offering dine-in meals, a patio area, and take-out options, as well as a newly developed bar and terrace section.
You can find a variety of items in our convenience store, such as ice, beer, wine, and anything you might have forgotten.
Our small lake is surrounded by a beautiful beach with plenty of room for the entire family to enjoy.
Our community hall and pavilion offer large spaces for events, gatherings, and live music every Saturday night.
The social club works hard to organize various events and activities for both young kids and adults to enjoy.
Propane refills, firewood, a pump-out station, and a laundromat are also available.
Public washrooms and showers are available throughout the campground.
