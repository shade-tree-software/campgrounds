Camping Le Domaine du Bel-Âge
Welcome to the closest campground to Montreal
Campground in an urban setting. 10 minutes from the Mercier Bridge, 2 minutes from Highway 30. Less than a kilometer, IGA grocery store, pharmacy, restaurant and SAQ and many others.
Within 5 kilometers, hiking trails, bingo, shopping centers, hospital and much more! Public transport to Montreal passing in front of the campground.
For people who seek tranquility and proximity to all services.
