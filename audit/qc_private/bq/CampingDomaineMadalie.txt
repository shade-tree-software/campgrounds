What is Envato?

Envato is your all-in-one creative asset toolkit. With one simple subscription, you’ll have access to all of our AI Tools plus unlimited downloads of over 28+ million creative assets and templates across the widest range of asset categories, with new assets added daily.

Our growing suite of AI Tools is powered by leading models and supports a wide range of creative needs across image, video, music, sound, voiceover, & graphics, to generate. Generate, edit, and customize content with advanced controls that bring your creative vision to life.

Our stock catalog covers everything you need, including stock photos, video footage and motion graphics, royalty-free music & stems, sound effects, graphics & illustrations, fonts, design templates & mockups, video templates & transitions, LUTs, 3D models, Lottie animations, presentations, & more.

Do any limits apply to downloads?

Stock Catalog: Subject to our Fair Use Policy, no limits apply to downloads of stock assets! Your Envato subscription allows you to download any asset you want, as many times as you like.

AI Tools: For Individual, Team and Student plan customers, the following limits apply:

Core Plan: Individual, Team and Student plan customers on the Core plan will receive 20 AI credits a month.
Plus Plan: Individual and Team plan customers on the Plus plan will receive 200 AI credits a month.
Ultimate Plan: Individual and Team plan customers on the Ultimate plan will be able to choose 500, 1000 or 2000 AI credits to suit your needs.
For Enterprise customers, access to AI and applicable credit limits will depend on your specific account plan and terms. Reach out to your Account Manager for more information.

As our stock assets and AI Tools are subject to our User Terms, and Fair Use Policy, please remember:

The subscription is for an individual (or a specific number of people with our Team or Enterprise accounts).
You cannot use scripts or bots to mass download assets or mass generate content, or engage in activities that suggests inappropriate, excessive, or unusual use patterns (including sharing of accounts, or engaging in excessively high downloads in a short period of time).
How does licensing work?

Our licensing is designed to make it easy for you to do the right thing and gives you the rights to use an item in a broad variety of projects, including those of a commercial nature.

Highlights of the license for creative stock assets:

All stock items available through our subscription have the same simple license terms for commercial use, including client work & monetization.
Each time you download an item, you get an ongoing license for a single, specific project. An item can be easily used again by simply creating a new license for each separate project.
Some common-sense limitations do apply. You can’t re-sell, re-distribute, or merchandise items without adding value.
Future proof licensing: If you unsubscribe, while you can’t continue to use items for any new projects, any existing licensed uses are still fully covered forever. Now that’s peace of mind!

Still have more questions about the License? Check the full list of FAQs from our support team.

Highlights of the license for generative AI assets:

Broad commercial license: For AI assets generated with our AI Tools, you’re granted a commercial license to use your AI asset as part of an End Product.
If you use an Elements stock Item as input (for example, editing an Elements photo), the use of the item is subject to the Envato Elements License as well as the AI Product Terms.
AI Music has extra restrictions: AI Music assets also come with a commercial license, but it comes with additional limitations (notably around using it as standalone music or in audio streaming services).

Still have more questions about AI asset licensing? Check the AI Product Terms for the full details.

When can I cancel or upgrade my subscription?

You can cancel or upgrade your Envato subscription at any time.

Canceling
If you cancel your Envato subscription, you will have access to Envato until the day your next subscription payment is due to be billed. For example, if you cancel your monthly subscription 23 days before the next payment, you will still have access for another 23 days. If you cancel your annual subscription three months before your payment is due, you will still get access for another three months. We do not refund canceled subscriptions.

However, as part of Envato’s worry-free licensing agreement, even after your Envato subscription has ended you will be able to see the list of items you have previously licensed and continue using them for the completed projects they were licensed for. Learn more here.

Upgrading
If you wish to upgrade from a monthly to annual subscription for even more savings, the remaining days in your monthly subscription will be discounted from your annual subscription – so you don’t get charged twice. The total amount will be shown in your invoice.

If you wish to upgrade to a higher plan level, you will be upgraded at a pro-rata rate for your current billing period, and you will be able to start using your upgraded plan immediately.

Downgrading
If you wish to downgrade your plan to a lower plan level, your plan will be downgraded at the end of your current billing period (monthly or annual).

Can I get support for specific items?

The Envato subscription does not provide direct item support – all the assets listed are created by our community of global artists. Due to this and the nature of our unlimited stock asset subscription, we cannot provide direct technical support or advice on the use of items. We however encourage you to check the zip file you downloaded for any support documentation or resources which the author may have included.

Can you use an Envato subscription to download assets from Envato Market?

No, an Envato subscription cannot be used to download or purchase assets from Envato Market.

Although they offer similar content, some items are exclusive to either platform. So, some assets are on both, some are only available via the Envato subscription, others are only available on Envato Market.

If you are looking for a specific item, please be sure that the item is available with the Envato subscription before subscribing.

Are AI Tools available for Enterprise?

Our AI Tools are available on selected plans for Enterprise customers. Existing Enterprise customers can reach out to their Account Manager for more information. For new enterprise enquiries, visit the Enterprise page.

How is AI usage counted?

AI credit usage varies depending on the type of generation and the complexity of the task, but you’ll always see the exact amount of credits you need before generating.

Most AI actions like image generation and editing, graphic generation, or voice and sound generation start at 1 credit, while more intensive actions such as video generation may start at multiple credits.

As our AI tools and technologies evolve, we may update our AI credit structure from time to time. This may include changing the credit usage for particular actions, introducing new features or tools, or offering promotional or limited time features or capabilities.

Learn more here.

What AI models are available?

We use a mix of state of the art models such as Nano Banana, Google Gemini Omni, Kling, Runway, ElevenLabs, and more to match the right models with specific use cases and styles under the hood. We constantly partner with leading providers as new models launch and update our integrations as existing models improve and add capabilities. To keep things fast and flexible here, model-specific availability is always evolving and subject to change at Envato’s discretion without notice.

What are parallel generations?

Parallel generations are the number of AI actions you can run at the same time to speed up your workflow.

Actions draw from your AI credits, so you can run parallel generations across different AI Tools. For example, if your plan includes 10 parallel generations, you could run 4 video generations, 3 image edits, 1 video upscale, and 2 sound generations simultaneously — as long as the total adds up to 10 or fewer.

Which plan should I choose?

All of our plans are perfect for designers, editors, and creators looking to fuel their creative momentum, with unlimited stock asset downloads across the broadest stock catalog.

Each plan has a different level of AI usage to suit different creative needs:

Core plan is for creatives who rely on stock assets to move fast, and want to try out AI Tools.
Plus plan is for those who want to make AI a part of their creative process, and level up their workflow.
Ultimate plan is for the most demanding creative workflows, offering multiple levels of AI credits.

What is Envato?

Envato is your all-in-one creative asset toolkit. With one simple subscription, you’ll have access to all of our AI Tools plus unlimited downloads of over 28+ million creative assets and templates across the widest range of asset categories, with new assets added daily.

Our growing suite of AI Tools is powered by leading models and supports a wide range of creative needs across image, video, music, sound, voiceover, & graphics, to generate. Generate, edit, and customize content with advanced controls that bring your creative vision to life.

Our stock catalog covers everything you need, including stock photos, video footage and motion graphics, royalty-free music & stems, sound effects, graphics & illustrations, fonts, design templates & mockups, video templates & transitions, LUTs, 3D models, Lottie animations, presentations, & more.

Do any limits apply to downloads?

Stock Catalog: Subject to our Fair Use Policy, no limits apply to downloads of stock assets! Your Envato subscription allows you to download any asset you want, as many times as you like.

AI Tools: For Individual, Team and Student plan customers, the following limits apply:

Core Plan: Individual, Team and Student plan customers on the Core plan will receive 20 AI credits a month.
Plus Plan: Individual and Team plan customers on the Plus plan will receive 200 AI credits a month.
Ultimate Plan: Individual and Team plan customers on the Ultimate plan will be able to choose 500, 1000 or 2000 AI credits to suit your needs.
For Enterprise customers, access to AI and applicable credit limits will depend on your specific account plan and terms. Reach out to your Account Manager for more information.

As our stock assets and AI Tools are subject to our User Terms, and Fair Use Policy, please remember:

The subscription is for an individual (or a specific number of people with our Team or Enterprise accounts).
You cannot use scripts or bots to mass download assets or mass generate content, or engage in activities that suggests inappropriate, excessive, or unusual use patterns (including sharing of accounts, or engaging in excessively high downloads in a short period of time).
How does licensing work?

Our licensing is designed to make it easy for you to do the right thing and gives you the rights to use an item in a broad variety of projects, including those of a commercial nature.

Highlights of the license for creative stock assets:

All stock items available through our subscription have the same simple license terms for commercial use, including client work & monetization.
Each time you download an item, you get an ongoing license for a single, specific project. An item can be easily used again by simply creating a new license for each separate project.
Some common-sense limitations do apply. You can’t re-sell, re-distribute, or merchandise items without adding value.
Future proof licensing: If you unsubscribe, while you can’t continue to use items for any new projects, any existing licensed uses are still fully covered forever. Now that’s peace of mind!

Still have more questions about the License? Check the full list of FAQs from our support team.

Highlights of the license for generative AI assets:

Broad commercial license: For AI assets generated with our AI Tools, you’re granted a commercial license to use your AI asset as part of an End Product.
If you use an Elements stock Item as input (for example, editing an Elements photo), the use of the item is subject to the Envato Elements License as well as the AI Product Terms.
AI Music has extra restrictions: AI Music assets also come with a commercial license, but it comes with additional limitations (notably around using it as standalone music or in audio streaming services).

Still have more questions about AI asset licensing? Check the AI Product Terms for the full details.

When can I cancel or upgrade my subscription?

You can cancel or upgrade your Envato subscription at any time.

Canceling
If you cancel your Envato subscription, you will have access to Envato until the day your next subscription payment is due to be billed. For example, if you cancel your monthly subscription 23 days before the next payment, you will still have access for another 23 days. If you cancel your annual subscription three months before your payment is due, you will still get access for another three months. We do not refund canceled subscriptions.

However, as part of Envato’s worry-free licensing agreement, even after your Envato subscription has ended you will be able to see the list of items you have previously licensed and continue using them for the completed projects they were licensed for. Learn more here.

Upgrading
If you wish to upgrade from a monthly to annual subscription for even more savings, the remaining days in your monthly subscription will be discounted from your annual subscription – so you don’t get charged twice. The total amount will be shown in your invoice.

If you wish to upgrade to a higher plan level, you will be upgraded at a pro-rata rate for your current billing period, and you will be able to start using your upgraded plan immediately.

Downgrading
If you wish to downgrade your plan to a lower plan level, your plan will be downgraded at the end of your current billing period (monthly or annual).

Can I get support for specific items?

The Envato subscription does not provide direct item support – all the assets listed are created by our community of global artists. Due to this and the nature of our unlimited stock asset subscription, we cannot provide direct technical support or advice on the use of items. We however encourage you to check the zip file you downloaded for any support documentation or resources which the author may have included.

Can you use an Envato subscription to download assets from Envato Market?

No, an Envato subscription cannot be used to download or purchase assets from Envato Market.

Although they offer similar content, some items are exclusive to either platform. So, some assets are on both, some are only available via the Envato subscription, others are only available on Envato Market.

If you are looking for a specific item, please be sure that the item is available with the Envato subscription before subscribing.

Are AI Tools available for Enterprise?

Our AI Tools are available on selected plans for Enterprise customers. Existing Enterprise customers can reach out to their Account Manager for more information. For new enterprise enquiries, visit the Enterprise page.

How is AI usage counted?

AI credit usage varies depending on the type of generation and the complexity of the task, but you’ll always see the exact amount of credits you need before generating.

Most AI actions like image generation and editing, graphic generation, or voice and sound generation start at 1 credit, while more intensive actions such as video generation may start at multiple credits.

As our AI tools and technologies evolve, we may update our AI credit structure from time to time. This may include changing the credit usage for particular actions, introducing new features or tools, or offering promotional or limited time features or capabilities.

Learn more here.

What AI models are available?

We use a mix of state of the art models such as Nano Banana, Google Gemini Omni, Kling, Runway, ElevenLabs, and more to match the right models with specific use cases and styles under the hood. We constantly partner with leading providers as new models launch and update our integrations as existing models improve and add capabilities. To keep things fast and flexible here, model-specific availability is always evolving and subject to change at Envato’s discretion without notice.

What are parallel generations?

Parallel generations are the number of AI actions you can run at the same time to speed up your workflow.

Actions draw from your AI credits, so you can run parallel generations across different AI Tools. For example, if your plan includes 10 parallel generations, you could run 4 video generations, 3 image edits, 1 video upscale, and 2 sound generations simultaneously — as long as the total adds up to 10 or fewer.

Which plan should I choose?

Each plan has a different level of AI usage to suit different creative needs:

Core plan is for creatives who rely on stock assets to move fast, and want to try out AI Tools.
Plus plan is for those who want to make AI a part of their creative process, and level up their workflow.
Ultimate plan is for the most demanding creative workflows, offering multiple levels of AI credits.
