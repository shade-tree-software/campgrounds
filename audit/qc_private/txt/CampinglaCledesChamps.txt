Welcome to La Clé des Champs RV Resort
La Clé des Champs RV Resort is a beautifully appointed 201-site campground, surrounded by nature for peace and quiet.
Experience the life of a superb RV resort in the Montérégie region immediately south of Montréal; a mere 20 miles from the US / Canada border (Saint-Bernard-de-Lacolle / Champlain).
Open from early April to the end of October.
Visit our camping virtually :  virtual visit .

This what RV resorts should be like - nice roads - gentle speed bumps - very professional and courteous staff and awesomely equipped sites - sadly only stayed there one night - but on our list of "go to" next time into the area. Pool was refreshing (they break at 5:30 for dinner) walking around the area was very clean / safe / and an experience as there are so many folks there who really dress up their sites. Did not check out the facilities - but a very beautiful area - Gilles was very pleasant and showed us around to see available sites.
-David B
We have traveled a lot in our RV and this is one of the nicest spots we've stayed at. The showers/restrooms and laundry room were spotless. The staff were so helpful-they told us where to drive and park to catch a bus (20 minute ride) into Montreal. From there we took the Metro or walked everywhere. Beautiful pool, great workout room and even a splash pool and playground for kids. I would highly recommend.
-Nanber2

Our Mission
La Clé des Champs RV Resort is a  co-ownership  corporation whose mission is to provide all campers with an exceptional environment, equipment, infrastructures, and service .  We are committed to provide our residents and guests with a best in class RV Resort lifestyle amenities, with human values such as respect, fairness and transparency in the forefront.
Experience the life of a superb RV resort in the Montérégie region immediately south of Montréal; a mere 20 miles from the US / Canada border (Saint-Bernard-de-Lacolle / Champlain). It’s a great opportunity for Americans to visit parts of Canada, save on fuel, and take advantage of the strong US currency. We are opened from early April until the end of October, a great place to stop before or after crossing the border to your next destination. We do not compromise on comfort and offer all the most  modern installations . All of our clients receive high-class service.
All lots reserved for travellers, vacationers and snowbirds are equipped with wifi, full patio furniture, a fire pit and a stone paved terrace. There is also an electronic gate at the entrance to ensure security for all.
La Clé des Champs RV Resort, more than just a campground…a world of opportunities for all!
A 5-star camping with all the amenities
For Camping Clé des Champs Complex VR, the classification "5 stars", means a quality above average at the level of nature and the quality of its infrastructure as well as its service offer!
In terms of our hosting infrastructure has two passenger reception desks and a convenience store for your last-minute needs;
A large community hall with a complete kitchen accessible to all;
Sanitary blocks with toilets and showers including all necessary amenities;
Recreational areas include a library, game room, playground, petanque, irons, water games, heated pool and spas;
Passenger sites, feature a cobblestone patio, table and chairs, swing and fireplace. All in a high-end setting!
All our roads are paved
All of quality and impeccable cleanliness!
