Camping at the summit
Spend the night at 840m altitude
From May 30 2026 to October 18 2026
Enjoy a great hike to reach directly our  camping located at the top of Mont SUTTON’s ski trails .  Surround yourself with nature and spend the night in one of our shelters or in your tent on one of our campsites. Wake up with a striking view on the beauty of the Eastern Townships landscape!
Campsites and shelters are at the summit alt. 840 m, at about 1 hour and a half of uphill walking distance from the main chalet (alt. 400 m), 45 minutes from the summit of chairlift II if you enjoy the  chairlift ride  (take note of the cost and schedule), and about 1 hour of uphill walking distance from Chalet Alt. 520m on the « Miracle » ski trail, or again through the hiking trails of the  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton ’s which the online reservation for access are necessary.
*** The sites are only accessible by foot (no car or mtb access). Also take note that from August 3 to September 18, access will only be available from the main chalet at Mont SUTTON due to construction on Chemin Réal.***
SUMMIT CAMPGROUND MAP
CAMPSITES
Twenty-one campground sites are available close to Mont SUTTON’s highest chalet (alt. 840 m). The sites are all at the summit. Sites can accommodate 1 to 3 tents (the price presented is for 2 people per site, there is an extra fee for each additional person – up to a maximum of 6 people in total and 1 to 3 tents depending on the sites). Tent(s) not included, bring your own tent(s). The sites are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
RATES :
$59   /site /night for 2 persons
+ $10 per extra person aged 6 years old and over  (maximum of 4 to 6 people, depending on the campsite selected)
Sites 3, 7, 11, 15 to 21 : 1 tent maximum (up to 4 people)
Sites 12 and 13 : 1 to 2 tents (up to 6 people)
Sites 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 14 : 1 to 3 tents (up to 6 people)

SHELTERS (Lean-to)
No need to set the tent! Two shelters are available for you if you don’t want to bring your tent all the way to the top.  They can accommodate up to 6 people for the night , in each shelters. The shelters, built on a wooden platform, have three closed sides. The price for the shelter is the same whether you are 1, 2 or 6 people; in short, the shelter is private! One shelter is close to the Exil ski trail and the other one is close to the chalet. The shelters are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
A mattress for each person is provided in the shelter!  They are cleaned and disinfected between each camper.
RATE:
$99 /shelter/night  (capacity of 6 people)
Shelter 1: at 5 min from Chalet Alt. 840 m
Shelter 2: closest to the chalet

FOOD DELIVERY AT THE SUMMIT
We are now offering nothing less than a camping menu that you can cook on the grill of our collective fire pits! Not just any menu, but a menu that will please the most discerning palate.  It is necessary to reserve your food a minimum of 48 hours prior to your stay  to guarantee its delivery to the summit.

We also have coffee, beer, wine, premixed cocktails, Kombu, Guru, and snacks available for purchase at the summit.
IMPORTANT INFORMATION
RULES –    PLEASE READ CAREFULLY
By making a reservation for the rustic campground Alt. 840 m, you agree to abide by all the rules and regulations in force at the campground.
You can  arrive  from 12 noon or later and we ask you to  leave  by 11 a.m. at the latest. The campground keeper welcomes you
directly at the summit, starting form 4 pm.
Minors  (under 18 years old) have to be under the supervision of at least one responsible adult.
A  curfew  is in place from 11 p.m until 7h am. Thank you for respecting other camper’s right to tranquility.
Ecological dry toilets  are available at Chalet alt. 840 m at all times by all users.
Drinkable water  is carried up each week by chairlift. It is accessible to all in containers stored at the entrance of the chalet. Thank you for not wasting it. It is limited and it requires effort to bring it to the summit. Note that there are  no showers .
The designated  campfire sites  located near the chalet at the summit are the  (only places where fires are permitted.)  Campers are responsible for making and maintaining fires with the campground attendant’s permission, and fires are to be shared among all campers. The campground attendant is responsible for the firewood supply, and the permission he grants is based on the fire danger index  SOPFEU .
Leashed dogs  that are calm and do not bark are allowed on campsites and in the shelters.
Thank you for not feeding wild animals, for their well-being and your safety!
A fee of $150 will be charged for any onsite  damage .
Please note that, access to the infrastructures of the Chalet Alt. 840 m are not authorized for the camping season. Only access to washrooms is possible.
If you have questions, don’t hesitate to ask the campground keeper. Have a nice stay!
Reservation required.   Book online now.
For any question, you can contact us at 1 866 538-2545, ext 0 or by email at  sutton@montsutton.com .
HIKING & ACCESS TO THE CAMPGROUND
Is this your first visit? We recommend that you arrive before 4:45 pm to make your ascent in daylight, or if you choose to enjoy the  chairlift ride  ($), and to get recommendations or directions if necessary.
To get to the camping at the top of the mountain, here are 4 hiking options:
From the main chalet of Mont SUTTON at altitude 400 m  (parking P2C, P3A ou P3B), start in front of the Boutique Expérience SUTTON and follow the signs to the campground. Plan around 1 hour and 30 minutes of walk to get to the campsite. See  Mont SUTTON’s trails map here .
With your ticket for the  chairlift ride  ($) : the day of your visit, park at altitude 400m (parking P2C, P3A or P3B), get your ticket for the ride with your reservation at the ticket office before 3:45 p.m. Board the chairlift II and once at the top of chairlift II, climb up the stairs behind the chalet Alt. 680m to the observatory point and follow your ascension on the Alleghanys trail for about 45 minutes all the way to the Chalet Alt. 840m where the camping guide is.
Starting from Chalet Alt. 520m (Parking 5), take “Miracle” ski trail all the way to the summit of the ski trails at Chalet Alt.840m. Allow about one hour of hiking in ascent to get to the top of the ski trails.
From the Parc d’environnement naturle de Sutton’s booth at 900 Chemin Réal, follow signs to Lac Spruce all the way to Lac Spruce, then follow signs towards Round Top until junction 775, then follow signs towards Mohawk until the next junction (830) and then make the final climb in the ski trail to the campground. You can  reserve your tickets online  or on site for the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton!
*But, please note that Parking Lot P5 is temporarily closed while the City of Sutton replaces a culvert, through September 23.
Hiking is free on the Mont SUTTON ski trails. Take the opportunity to admire the summer flora on the ski trails, the sculptures of local artists that adorn some of our trails and the magnificent viewpoints on the valley.
It is also possible to buy a ticket access to Lac Mohawk at Mont SUTTON’s ticket office and at the camping at the summit.
See here  Mont SUTTON’s trail guide  and  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton’s trail map .
CANCELLATION AND DATE CHANGE POLICIES
It is the customer’s responsibility to contact the Reservation Center at 1-866-538-2545 option 0 to reschedule or change a reservation (within the time frames outlined in the policies below). Voice messages on the Reservation Center’s answering machine will be accepted.
Cancellation policies : A 50% penalty is applicable for a cancellation made within within 4 to 14 days of your reservation date. Three days or less before your reservation date, there will be no refund.
Date change policy : A change of date is possible without charge more than 3 days before your reservation. For example, for a reservation on Saturday, you can change your reservation date up to and including Wednesday. Three days or less before the date of your reservation, no change is possible. Only one (1) date change is allowed per reservation, once a change is made a 50% penalty is applicable for any subsequent modification.

Camping au sommet
Passez la nuit à 840m d'altitude
Du 30 mai 2026 au 18 octobre 2026
Profitez d’une belle randonnée pédestre pour vous rendre directement à notre  camping situé au sommet du Mont SUTTON ! Entourez-vous de nature pour la nuit en campant avec votre tente sur l’un de nos sites de camping ou dans l’un de nos abris rustiques. Puis, réveillez-vous en plein air devant la saisissante beauté des paysages des Cantons-de-l’Est!
Les sites de camping et les abris sont situés au sommet des pistes de ski à l’altitude 840 mètres, soit à environ 1 h 30 de marche en ascension à partir du chalet principal du Mont SUTTON (Alt. 400 m), 45 minutes à partir du sommet du télésiège II si vous profitez de la  balade en télésiège  (prenez connaissance de l’horaire et des tarifs), à environ une heure de marche en ascension à partir du chalet Alt 520m par la piste de ski « Miracle » ou encore via les  sentiers du Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton  pour lesquels il faut avoir réservé votre billet en ligne.
*** Les sites sont uniquement accessibles à pied (aucun accès en voiture ni en vélo). Prendre note aussi que du 3 août au 18 septembre, seul l’accès à partir du chalet principal du Mont SUTTON sera disponible en raison du chantier sur le chemin Réal.***
Plan du camping au sommet
SITES DE CAMPING
Vingt et un sites de camping vous attendent autour du plus haut chalet du Mont SUTTON (altitude 840 m). Les sites sont tous au sommet.  Les sites peuvent accueillir de 1 à 3 tentes (le prix présenté est pour 2 personnes par site, il y a un extra pour chaque personne supplémentaire – jusqu’à un maximum 4 ou 6 personnes et de 1 à 3 tente selon les sites). Tente(s) non incluse(s), vous devez apporter votre(vos) propre(s) tente(s). Les sites sont uniquement accessibles à pied (de 1 heure à 1 h 30 de marche en ascension selon le point de départ).
TARIFS :
59 $ /site /nuit pour 2 personnes
+ 10 $ par personne supplémentaire de 6 ans et plus  (max. 4 ou 6 personnes, selon le site choisi.)
Sites 3, 7, 11, 15 à 21 : 1 tente maximum (jusqu’à 4 personnes)
Sites 12 et 13 : 1 à 2 tentes (jusqu’à 6 personnes)
Sites 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 et 14 : 1 à 3 tentes (jusqu’à 6 personnes)

ABRIS RUSTIQUES
Pas de besoin de monter la tente (ni de la monter au sommet à la sueur de votre front)! Deux abris rustiques sont mis à votre disposition en alternative au camping.  Jusqu’à six personnes  peuvent dormir dans chacun de ces abris, assis sur une plate-forme de bois, fermés sur trois côtés. Le prix pour l’abri est le même que vous soyez 1, 2 ou 6 personnes; bref l’abri est privé! Il y a un abri situé en haut de la piste l’Exil et un autre situé plus près du refuge Alt. 840 m. Les abris sont uniquement accessibles à pied (de 1 heure à 1 h 30 de marche en ascension selon le point de départ).
Un matelas pour chaque personne est fourni dans l’abri!  Ils sont nettoyés et désinfectés entre chaque campeur.
TARIFS :
99$ /abri /nuit  (capacité de 6 personnes)
Abri 1 : situé à 5 min du refuge Alt. 840 m
Abri 2 : plus près du refuge Alt. 840 m

OPTION BOUFFE AU SOMMET
Nous vous proposons rien de moins qu’un menu camping livré au sommet que vous pourrez cuisiner vous-même sur nos ronds de feu collectifs. Pas n’importe quel menu mais bien un menu qui saura plaire aux palais les plus fins. Il est  nécessaire de réserver un minimum de 48 heures avant votre séjour  pour assurer la disponibilité au sommet.
Voyez le menu et les détails pour réserver ici .
Café, bière, vin, cocktails prémix, Kombu, Guru et collations aussi disponibles pour achat au sommet.
AUTRES INFORMATIONS IMPORTANTES
RÈGLEMENTS –    VEUILLEZ LIRE ATTENTIVEMENT
En procédant à une réservation pour le camping au sommet, vous vous engagez à respecter tous les réglements en vigueur au camping.
Vous pouvez  arriver  à partir de 12h et nous vous demandons de  quitter  votre site au plus tard à 11h. Le gardien du camping vous accueille directement au sommet, à compté de 16h.
Les  mineurs  de moins de 18 ans doivent être supervisé par un adulte responsable en tout temps.
Couvre-feu  en vigueur à partir de 23 h jusqu’à 7h du matin. Merci de respecter le droit à la tranquillité des autres visiteurs.
Des  toilettes  sèches au Chalet Alt. 840 m sont accessibles en tout temps à tous les utilisateurs du site.
De l’ eau potable  est montée chaque semaine par télésiège. Elle est accessible en tout temps dans des bidons entreposés à l’entrée du chalet. Nous vous remercions de ne pas la gaspiller, étant donné qu’elle est limitée et que efforts doivent être consacrés à son transport. A noter aussi qu’il n’y a pas de douche.
Les  ronds de feu communautaires  situés à proximité du chalet sont les  (seuls endroits où il est autorisé de faire un feu.)  Les campeurs sont responsables de faire et entretenir le feu avec l’autorisation du gardien et les feux sont à partager avec l’ensemble des campeurs. Le gardien du camping est responsable de la réserve de bois et l’autorisation qu’il donne respecte l’indice  SOPFEU .
Les  chiens  en laisse qui sont calmes et n’aboient pas sont autorisés sur les sites de camping et dans les abris.
Merci de ne pas nourrir les animaux sauvages, pour leur bien et votre sécurité! Aussi, il est recommandé de garder votre nourriture avec vous.
Un frais de 150 $ sera appliqué pour tout  dommage  causé sur le site.
Prenez note que l’accès aux infrastructures du Chalet Alt. 840 m n’est pas autorisé pour la saison de camping. Seul l’accès aux toilettes est possible.
Si vous avez des questions sur place, n’hésitez pas de demander au gardien. Bon séjour!
Réservation requise.   Réservez en ligne dès maintenant.
Pour toute autre demande, vous pouvez communiquer avec nous au 1 866 538-2545, poste 0 ou par courriel à  sutton@montsutton.com .
RANDONNÉE PÉDESTRE ET ACCÈS AU SITE DE CAMPING
C’est votre première visite? Nous vous recommandons d’arriver avant 16h45 pour faire votre ascension à la clarté du jour et pour, si tel est votre choix, profiter de la  balade en télésiège  ($) et, au besoin, avoir des recommandations ou indications.
Voici 4 options pour vous rendre au camping au sommet :
À partir du chalet principal du Mont SUTTON à l’altitude 400 m (stationnement P2C, P3A ou P3B), rendez-vous devant la Boutique Expérience SUTTON et suivez les pastilles de signalisation vers le camping. Il faut prévoir 1h30 de marche en ascension à partir du chalet principal. Voir la  carte du Mont SUTTON ici .
Avec un billet pour la balade en télésiège réservé en ligne ($), la journée de votre visite, stationnez-vous à l’altitude 400m (stationnement P2C, P3A ou P3B), récupérez votre billet pour la balade avec votre réservation à la billetterie avant 15h45. Montez à bord du télésiège II et une fois en haut, montez les marches derrière le Chalet Alt. 680 m jusqu’à l’observatoire et poursuivez ensuite votre ascension sur la piste Alleghanys pour environ 45 minutes jusqu’au Chalet Alt. 840 m où se trouve l’accueil du camping.
À partir du Chalet Alt. 520m (stationnement P5), prendre la piste de ski Miracle jusqu’au sommet des pistes de ski au Chalet Alt. 840 m. Il faut prévoir environ une heure de marche en ascension à partir du Chalet Alt. 520 m.
À partir de l’accueil du Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton au 900 Chemin Réal, suivez les indications vers le Lac Spruce jusqu’au Lac Spruce, puis vers le Round Top jusqu’au carrefour 775, puis vers Mohawk jusqu’au carrefour suivant (830) pour finir par la piste de ski jusqu’au camping. Veuillez noter que les réservations sont obligatoires pour l’accès aux sentiers pédestres du Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton, rendez-vous sur leur site pour visualiser la carte et pour  réservez vos billets d’accès en ligne !
*Prendre note par contre que le stationnement P5 est exceptionnellement fermé pendant les travaux de remplacement d’un ponceau par la Ville de Sutton jusqu’au 23 septembre.
La randonnée pédestre est gratuite sur les pistes de ski du Mont SUTTON. Profitez-en pour admirer la flore estivale dans les pistes de ski, les sculptures des artistes locaux qui arborent quelques-unes de nos pistes et les magnifiques points de vue sur la vallée.
Il est aussi possible de se procurer un billet vers le Lac Mohawk à la billetterie du Mont SUTTON et au camping aussi.
Pour les sentiers pédestres du Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton, vous pouvez aussi  réserver vos billets d’accès en ligne !
Voir la  carte des pistes du Mont SUTTON  et la  carte des sentiers de randonnée du Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton .
POLITIQUE D’ANNULATION ET DE CHANGEMENT DE DATE
Il est de la responsabilité du client de contacter le Centre de réservation au 1 866 538-2545 option 0 pour reporter ou modifier une réservation (en respectant les délais mentionnés dans les politiques ci-dessous). Les messages vocaux sur le répondeur du Centre de réservation seront acceptés.
Politique d’annulation  : Une pénalité de 50% est applicable pour toute annulation faite entre 4 et 14 jours avant la date de votre réservation. Trois jours et moins avant la date de votre réservation, aucun remboursement.
Politique de changement de date:  Un changement de date est possible sans frais plus de 3 jours avant votre réservation. À titre d’exemple, pour une réservation le samedi, vous pouvez modifier votre date de réservation jusqu’au mercredi inclusivement. Trois jours et moins avant la date de votre réservation, aucune modification n’est possible. Un seul changement de date est alloué par réservation, une fois un changement fait, une pénalité de 50% est applicable pour toute modification subséquente.

Camping at the summit
Spend the night at 840m altitude
From May 30 2026 to October 18 2026
Enjoy a great hike to reach directly our  camping located at the top of Mont SUTTON’s ski trails .  Surround yourself with nature and spend the night in one of our shelters or in your tent on one of our campsites. Wake up with a striking view on the beauty of the Eastern Townships landscape!
Campsites and shelters are at the summit alt. 840 m, at about 1 hour and a half of uphill walking distance from the main chalet (alt. 400 m), 45 minutes from the summit of chairlift II if you enjoy the  chairlift ride  (take note of the cost and schedule), and about 1 hour of uphill walking distance from Chalet Alt. 520m on the « Miracle » ski trail, or again through the hiking trails of the  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton ’s which the online reservation for access are necessary.
*** The sites are only accessible by foot (no car or mtb access). Also take note that from August 3 to September 18, access will only be available from the main chalet at Mont SUTTON due to construction on Chemin Réal.***
SUMMIT CAMPGROUND MAP
CAMPSITES
Twenty-one campground sites are available close to Mont SUTTON’s highest chalet (alt. 840 m). The sites are all at the summit. Sites can accommodate 1 to 3 tents (the price presented is for 2 people per site, there is an extra fee for each additional person – up to a maximum of 6 people in total and 1 to 3 tents depending on the sites). Tent(s) not included, bring your own tent(s). The sites are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
RATES :
$59   /site /night for 2 persons
+ $10 per extra person aged 6 years old and over  (maximum of 4 to 6 people, depending on the campsite selected)
Sites 3, 7, 11, 15 to 21 : 1 tent maximum (up to 4 people)
Sites 12 and 13 : 1 to 2 tents (up to 6 people)
Sites 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 14 : 1 to 3 tents (up to 6 people)

SHELTERS (Lean-to)
No need to set the tent! Two shelters are available for you if you don’t want to bring your tent all the way to the top.  They can accommodate up to 6 people for the night , in each shelters. The shelters, built on a wooden platform, have three closed sides. The price for the shelter is the same whether you are 1, 2 or 6 people; in short, the shelter is private! One shelter is close to the Exil ski trail and the other one is close to the chalet. The shelters are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
A mattress for each person is provided in the shelter!  They are cleaned and disinfected between each camper.
RATE:
$99 /shelter/night  (capacity of 6 people)
Shelter 1: at 5 min from Chalet Alt. 840 m
Shelter 2: closest to the chalet

IMPORTANT INFORMATION
RULES –    PLEASE READ CAREFULLY
By making a reservation for the rustic campground Alt. 840 m, you agree to abide by all the rules and regulations in force at the campground.
You can  arrive  from 12 noon or later and we ask you to  leave  by 11 a.m. at the latest. The campground keeper welcomes you
directly at the summit, starting form 4 pm.
Minors  (under 18 years old) have to be under the supervision of at least one responsible adult.
A  curfew  is in place from 11 p.m until 7h am. Thank you for respecting other camper’s right to tranquility.
Ecological dry toilets  are available at Chalet alt. 840 m at all times by all users.
Drinkable water  is carried up each week by chairlift. It is accessible to all in containers stored at the entrance of the chalet. Thank you for not wasting it. It is limited and it requires effort to bring it to the summit. Note that there are  no showers .
The designated  campfire sites  located near the chalet at the summit are the  (only places where fires are permitted.)  Campers are responsible for making and maintaining fires with the campground attendant’s permission, and fires are to be shared among all campers. The campground attendant is responsible for the firewood supply, and the permission he grants is based on the fire danger index  SOPFEU .
Leashed dogs  that are calm and do not bark are allowed on campsites and in the shelters.
Thank you for not feeding wild animals, for their well-being and your safety!
A fee of $150 will be charged for any onsite  damage .
Please note that, access to the infrastructures of the Chalet Alt. 840 m are not authorized for the camping season. Only access to washrooms is possible.
If you have questions, don’t hesitate to ask the campground keeper. Have a nice stay!
Reservation required.   Book online now.
For any question, you can contact us at 1 866 538-2545, ext 0 or by email at  sutton@montsutton.com .
HIKING & ACCESS TO THE CAMPGROUND
Is this your first visit? We recommend that you arrive before 4:45 pm to make your ascent in daylight, or if you choose to enjoy the  chairlift ride  ($), and to get recommendations or directions if necessary.
To get to the camping at the top of the mountain, here are 4 hiking options:
From the main chalet of Mont SUTTON at altitude 400 m  (parking P2C, P3A ou P3B), start in front of the Boutique Expérience SUTTON and follow the signs to the campground. Plan around 1 hour and 30 minutes of walk to get to the campsite. See  Mont SUTTON’s trails map here .
With your ticket for the  chairlift ride  ($) : the day of your visit, park at altitude 400m (parking P2C, P3A or P3B), get your ticket for the ride with your reservation at the ticket office before 3:45 p.m. Board the chairlift II and once at the top of chairlift II, climb up the stairs behind the chalet Alt. 680m to the observatory point and follow your ascension on the Alleghanys trail for about 45 minutes all the way to the Chalet Alt. 840m where the camping guide is.
Starting from Chalet Alt. 520m (Parking 5), take “Miracle” ski trail all the way to the summit of the ski trails at Chalet Alt.840m. Allow about one hour of hiking in ascent to get to the top of the ski trails.
From the Parc d’environnement naturle de Sutton’s booth at 900 Chemin Réal, follow signs to Lac Spruce all the way to Lac Spruce, then follow signs towards Round Top until junction 775, then follow signs towards Mohawk until the next junction (830) and then make the final climb in the ski trail to the campground. You can  reserve your tickets online  or on site for the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton!
*But, please note that Parking Lot P5 is temporarily closed while the City of Sutton replaces a culvert, through September 23.
Hiking is free on the Mont SUTTON ski trails. Take the opportunity to admire the summer flora on the ski trails, the sculptures of local artists that adorn some of our trails and the magnificent viewpoints on the valley.
It is also possible to buy a ticket access to Lac Mohawk at Mont SUTTON’s ticket office and at the camping at the summit.
See here  Mont SUTTON’s trail guide  and  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton’s trail map .
CANCELLATION AND DATE CHANGE POLICIES
It is the customer’s responsibility to contact the Reservation Center at 1-866-538-2545 option 0 to reschedule or change a reservation (within the time frames outlined in the policies below). Voice messages on the Reservation Center’s answering machine will be accepted.
Cancellation policies : A 50% penalty is applicable for a cancellation made within within 4 to 14 days of your reservation date. Three days or less before your reservation date, there will be no refund.
Date change policy : A change of date is possible without charge more than 3 days before your reservation. For example, for a reservation on Saturday, you can change your reservation date up to and including Wednesday. Three days or less before the date of your reservation, no change is possible. Only one (1) date change is allowed per reservation, once a change is made a 50% penalty is applicable for any subsequent modification.

VAN and RV Overnight Stay
Parking lots where Van and RVs can be parked for a night stay:
☀️ In summer/fall season :  P2C, P3B, P5C et P5D  *
*Please note that Parking Lot P5 is temporarily closed while the City of Sutton replaces a culvert, through September 23.
❄️ In winter season:  P3B, P5C et P5D  *
Exclusive to users of  recreational activities of Mont SUTTON  as required by municipal rules.
During your stay, take the opportunity to discover  all our activities  and  our restaurant . Please note that the restaurant has a liquor license, so no outside beverages are permitted at the foot of the slopes.
No services:  No water, electricity, toilet, shower or picnic table… you are outdoors and must be fully self-sufficient.
Fees: $20 / per night, per vehicle – to be paid  online  or at the shop.
The costs to be covered allow to ensure the good cohabitation and the sustainability of this permission in the long term. Thank you..
Rules to follow to guarantee the sustainability of this great opportunity:
Although somewhat distant from residential areas, we ask that you be mindful. Sound carries easily in the mountains and other campers might not appreciate the disturbance. Obviously, mutual respect is paramount between users, the mountain staff and the community.
There is no tent camping allowed at the base of the mountain and fires are not permitted.  Rustic camping for tents  is located at the top of the mountain and by reservation only (available during the summer and fall seasons only).
We ask that you park in such a way as to minimize the space occupied in the parking lots and perpendicular to the edge of the parking, so that a maximum number of users can be accommodated by day and night.  All types of recreational vehicles without generator up to a maximum of 18 feet are accepted in P2C, P5C and P5D and up to a maximum of 30 feet in P3B. You must be self-sufficient and pack out everything you bring. There are no garbage or recycling containers for your waste. Please make sure to keep the area clean, hence maintaining the right of access to the grounds.
Dogs are allowed but they must be under the control of their owner at all times and not disturb. For each activity, the rules are different: they must be kept on a leash near the main chalet, are tolerated on mtb trails but must not interfere with other mountain bikers and are not allowed on many of the hiking trails of the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton.
If you have any questions, please write us at  sutton@montsutton.com .
Details of activities and schedule of services here.
* Only van and small RV under 18 feet, without a generator, are permitted in parking lots P2C, P5C and P5D. Motorized recreational vehicles under 30 feet, without a generator, are welcome in parking lot P3B. No tents allowed anywhere other than the summit campground.
**Please note that during freeze/thaw periods when the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton hiking trails  Parking lot P5  are closed.
Rates in Canadian dollars and taxes not included. Rates and rules subject to change without notice.

Seasonal lockers
No need to lug your equipment from home to the mountain!
Mont SUTTON offers you the possibility to rent seasonal lockers directly at the foot of the slopes. The rental is for the whole ski season! Different types and sizes of lockers are available.
AT PARKING LEVEL
This renovated space is located on the lower level of the Chalet Alt. 400 m (parking level). The locker room is accessible directly from the P2B parking lot.
New spacious lockers with integrated boot dryer
These new state-of-the-art lockers offer enough space to store skis, boots, helmets, gloves/mittens… for 2 or 4 people depending on the option chosen. You can even be sure that your equipment will be dry on your next outing, since boot dryers are integrated into the lockers.

Camping at the summit
Spend the night at 840m altitude
From May 30 2026 to October 18 2026
Enjoy a great hike to reach directly our  camping located at the top of Mont SUTTON’s ski trails .  Surround yourself with nature and spend the night in one of our shelters or in your tent on one of our campsites. Wake up with a striking view on the beauty of the Eastern Townships landscape!
Campsites and shelters are at the summit alt. 840 m, at about 1 hour and a half of uphill walking distance from the main chalet (alt. 400 m), 45 minutes from the summit of chairlift II if you enjoy the  chairlift ride  (take note of the cost and schedule), and about 1 hour of uphill walking distance from Chalet Alt. 520m on the « Miracle » ski trail, or again through the hiking trails of the  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton ’s which the online reservation for access are necessary.
*** The sites are only accessible by foot (no car or mtb access). Also take note that from August 3 to September 18, access will only be available from the main chalet at Mont SUTTON due to construction on Chemin Réal.***
SUMMIT CAMPGROUND MAP
CAMPSITES
Twenty-one campground sites are available close to Mont SUTTON’s highest chalet (alt. 840 m). The sites are all at the summit. Sites can accommodate 1 to 3 tents (the price presented is for 2 people per site, there is an extra fee for each additional person – up to a maximum of 6 people in total and 1 to 3 tents depending on the sites). Tent(s) not included, bring your own tent(s). The sites are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
RATES :
$59   /site /night for 2 persons
+ $10 per extra person aged 6 years old and over  (maximum of 4 to 6 people, depending on the campsite selected)
Sites 3, 7, 11, 15 to 21 : 1 tent maximum (up to 4 people)
Sites 12 and 13 : 1 to 2 tents (up to 6 people)
Sites 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 14 : 1 to 3 tents (up to 6 people)

*Taxes not included. Hiking is free on the Mont SUTTON ski trails but access rights to the trails of the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton are extra and reservations are recommended.
Bring your own tent and gear
Shared fire pit

SHELTERS (Lean-to)
No need to set the tent! Two shelters are available for you if you don’t want to bring your tent all the way to the top.  They can accommodate up to 6 people for the night , in each shelters. The shelters, built on a wooden platform, have three closed sides. The price for the shelter is the same whether you are 1, 2 or 6 people; in short, the shelter is private! One shelter is close to the Exil ski trail and the other one is close to the chalet. The shelters are only accessible by foot (1 to 1.5 hour of uphill walking distance).
A mattress for each person is provided in the shelter!  They are cleaned and disinfected between each camper.
RATE:
$99 /shelter/night  (capacity of 6 people)
Shelter 1: at 5 min from Chalet Alt. 840 m
Shelter 2: closest to the chalet

FOOD DELIVERY AT THE SUMMIT
We are now offering nothing less than a camping menu that you can cook on the grill of our collective fire pits! Not just any menu, but a menu that will please the most discerning palate.  It is necessary to reserve your food a minimum of 48 hours prior to your stay  to guarantee its delivery to the summit.
Click here to view the menu and the details to reserve .
We also have coffee, beer, wine, premixed cocktails, Kombu, Guru, and snacks available for purchase at the summit.
IMPORTANT INFORMATION
RULES –    PLEASE READ CAREFULLY
By making a reservation for the rustic campground Alt. 840 m, you agree to abide by all the rules and regulations in force at the campground.
You can  arrive  from 12 noon or later and we ask you to  leave  by 11 a.m. at the latest. The campground keeper welcomes you
directly at the summit, starting form 4 pm.
Minors  (under 18 years old) have to be under the supervision of at least one responsible adult.
A  curfew  is in place from 11 p.m until 7h am. Thank you for respecting other camper’s right to tranquility.
Ecological dry toilets  are available at Chalet alt. 840 m at all times by all users.
Drinkable water  is carried up each week by chairlift. It is accessible to all in containers stored at the entrance of the chalet. Thank you for not wasting it. It is limited and it requires effort to bring it to the summit. Note that there are  no showers .
The designated  campfire sites  located near the chalet at the summit are the  (only places where fires are permitted.)  Campers are responsible for making and maintaining fires with the campground attendant’s permission, and fires are to be shared among all campers. The campground attendant is responsible for the firewood supply, and the permission he grants is based on the fire danger index  SOPFEU .
Leashed dogs  that are calm and do not bark are allowed on campsites and in the shelters.
Thank you for not feeding wild animals, for their well-being and your safety!
A fee of $150 will be charged for any onsite  damage .
Please note that, access to the infrastructures of the Chalet Alt. 840 m are not authorized for the camping season. Only access to washrooms is possible.
If you have questions, don’t hesitate to ask the campground keeper. Have a nice stay!
Reservation required.   Book online now.
For any question, you can contact us at 1 866 538-2545, ext 0 or by email at  sutton@montsutton.com .
HIKING & ACCESS TO THE CAMPGROUND
Is this your first visit? We recommend that you arrive before 4:45 pm to make your ascent in daylight, or if you choose to enjoy the  chairlift ride  ($), and to get recommendations or directions if necessary.
To get to the camping at the top of the mountain, here are 4 hiking options:
From the main chalet of Mont SUTTON at altitude 400 m  (parking P2C, P3A ou P3B), start in front of the Boutique Expérience SUTTON and follow the signs to the campground. Plan around 1 hour and 30 minutes of walk to get to the campsite. See  Mont SUTTON’s trails map here .
With your ticket for the  chairlift ride  ($) : the day of your visit, park at altitude 400m (parking P2C, P3A or P3B), get your ticket for the ride with your reservation at the ticket office before 3:45 p.m. Board the chairlift II and once at the top of chairlift II, climb up the stairs behind the chalet Alt. 680m to the observatory point and follow your ascension on the Alleghanys trail for about 45 minutes all the way to the Chalet Alt. 840m where the camping guide is.
Starting from Chalet Alt. 520m (Parking 5), take “Miracle” ski trail all the way to the summit of the ski trails at Chalet Alt.840m. Allow about one hour of hiking in ascent to get to the top of the ski trails.
From the Parc d’environnement naturle de Sutton’s booth at 900 Chemin Réal, follow signs to Lac Spruce all the way to Lac Spruce, then follow signs towards Round Top until junction 775, then follow signs towards Mohawk until the next junction (830) and then make the final climb in the ski trail to the campground. You can  reserve your tickets online  or on site for the Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton!
*But, please note that Parking Lot P5 is temporarily closed while the City of Sutton replaces a culvert, through September 23.
Hiking is free on the Mont SUTTON ski trails. Take the opportunity to admire the summer flora on the ski trails, the sculptures of local artists that adorn some of our trails and the magnificent viewpoints on the valley.
It is also possible to buy a ticket access to Lac Mohawk at Mont SUTTON’s ticket office and at the camping at the summit.
See here  Mont SUTTON’s trail guide  and  Parc d’environnement naturel de Sutton’s trail map .
CANCELLATION AND DATE CHANGE POLICIES
It is the customer’s responsibility to contact the Reservation Center at 1-866-538-2545 option 0 to reschedule or change a reservation (within the time frames outlined in the policies below). Voice messages on the Reservation Center’s answering machine will be accepted.
Cancellation policies : A 50% penalty is applicable for a cancellation made within within 4 to 14 days of your reservation date. Three days or less before your reservation date, there will be no refund.
Date change policy : A change of date is possible without charge more than 3 days before your reservation. For example, for a reservation on Saturday, you can change your reservation date up to and including Wednesday. Three days or less before the date of your reservation, no change is possible. Only one (1) date change is allowed per reservation, once a change is made a 50% penalty is applicable for any subsequent modification.
