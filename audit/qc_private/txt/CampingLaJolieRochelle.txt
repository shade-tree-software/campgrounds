Experience nature at its best in the heart of Bellechasse!
La Jolie Rochelle facilitates easy access without sacrificing the high-quality requirements in the development of its infrastructure. Located on the edge of the magnificent Rivière-du-Sud, forty minutes from Québec City, the site offers an atmosphere of relaxation and tranquility without compromise.
