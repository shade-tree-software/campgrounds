The Elms on Grand RV Campground
Contact this Campground
541 Third Avenue, Young, Saskatchewan, Canada
 Website
Upcoming Seasons & Booking Dates

Nestled in the heart of Young, Saskatchewan, The Elms On Grand RV Campground and Residential Park offers a true prairie vacation experience. Whether you’re planning a weekend RVing getaway or a seasonal stay in one our year round sites, we promise to offer spacious RV campsites amongst a beautiful setting! Enjoy full hookups, sewer access, and convenient amenities in a peaceful, family-friendly setting. Just steps away from the community’s outdoor pool and children’s playground, our RV campground is perfect for families — and yes, we’re dog-friendly too! There’s plenty of open space in our RV campsites for you to stretch your legs and relax after a long journey. Come for the quiet charm, stay for the endless Saskatchewan sunsets. UPDATE There will NO fires at your site as it poses a Fire Hazard on the grass, if you own a propane fire ring you are welcome to use it at your site. Looking to settle in for a while? Our Residential Park features long-term pad rentals for mobile homeowners, complete with full utility hookups and access to on-site amenities.

Enjoy full-service RV camping, long-term stays, and endless sunsets in Young, Saskatchewan

Experience prairie life at The Elms On Grand—where peaceful escapes meet family comfort.

Book your stay easily through our Let’s Camp booking website—fast, simple, and secure. Relax at The Elms RV Campground — Prairie Comfort Awaits.

Have questions or prefer to chat? Call us to book your stay today — we’re happy to help!

Refund Rules

**Cancellation Policy**
If you cancel your reservation of 28 days or more with 14+ days notice, you'll receive a 100% refund but have to pay a $50.00 cancellation fee.

A change fee of $20.00 will be applied when a camper changes their site or moves their booking dates.

If you cancel your reservation of 28 days or more with 7-13 days notice, you'll receive a 50% refund but have to pay a $50.00 cancellation fee.

If you cancel your reservation of 28 days or more with less than 7 nights notice will receive no refund with a $50.00 cancellation fee.

If you cancel your reservation of less than 28 days with 2 days notice or more, you'll receive a 100% refund but excluding 1 nights fees.

Let’s Camp’s Refund Policy

Let’s Camp reserves the right to refund reservations that were canceled due to extraneous circumstances, such as public health orders or government mandates.

Aside from these circumstances, Let’s Camp’s individual campgrounds have final authority over all cancellations and refunds, and are not required to give refunds for inclement weather, wildlife, air quality, fire bans, or other inconveniences.

Modification of Terms

Let’s Camp and its individual campgrounds reserve the right to update their refund policies at any time in accordance with this provision.
