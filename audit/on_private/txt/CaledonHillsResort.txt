Something for everyone
Your central hub for all things fun!
Caledon Hills offers a perfect blend of tranquility and excitement, making it an ideal destination for those seeking a peaceful getaway within reach of the Greater Toronto Area. Surrounded by lush greenery and picturesque landscapes, this charming region invites visitors to unwind and explore its natural beauty. From scenic hiking trails to serene lakes, there are endless opportunities to connect with nature and rejuvenate the mind and body.
For those looking to indulge in leisurely activities, Caledon boasts numerous top-notch golf courses that cater to players of all skill levels. After a round of golf, immerse yourself in the local culture by dining at one of the many charming restaurants that offer delicious cuisine made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients.
Whether you’re a food enthusiast, nature lover, or avid shopper, Caledon Hills has something for everyone to enjoy. So pack your bags, embark on an unforgettable journey, and let the magic of Caledon Hills captivate your heart and soul.

Whether you’re looking for a secluded spot in the woods or close to the action, we’ve got you covered.
Exclusive Extended Season:  Our exclusive extended season is designed to provide our campers with even more opportunities to enjoy the great outdoors and create lasting memories. With this extended season, campers can take advantage of additional time to enjoy outdoor activities, bond with friends and family, and immerse themselves in nature. Whether it’s enjoying the beauty of the changing seasons, trying new outdoor adventures, or simply relaxing by the campfire, our extended season offers something for everyone. We are committed to making sure that our campers have the best possible experience, and our extended season is just one way we strive to exceed expectations.
