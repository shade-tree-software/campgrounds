Whispering  Pines
Family Campground
For over 50 years,  Whispering Pines Campground  in Hepworth, Ontario, has been the go-to destination for affordable, family-friendly camping. Tucked amid towering pines and scenic landscapes, we offer a welcoming retreat where you can unwind, explore, and make new memories. With plenty of on-site activities and easy access to nearby adventures, there’s always something exciting to discover. We invite you to experience why countless families have called Whispering Pines there go to camping destination.
Proudly Owned and Operated by the Kiwanis Club of Owen Sound.
