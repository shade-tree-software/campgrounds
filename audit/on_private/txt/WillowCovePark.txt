Unwind in the County
Seasonal living at Willow Cove means you can do absolutely nothing – read a book, enjoy a glass of wine by the bonfire and sit back to revel in the rich hues of the evening sky. You can swim, fish, play volleyball, paddleboard, kayak or go boating in the calm waters of the bay.
Or enjoy the many festivals, savour the internationally recognized food and wine, experience the galleries and artisans, and explore the simplicity of rural life in the tiny hamlets and villages of The County. They are all within minutes of Willow Cove. In The County, you’re always welcome to come as you are. And you can leave the dress code behind. It’s all about keeping it real.
