FAQs in full Bloom.
What accommodation options do you offer at your resorts?
We offer seasonal sites and, at select locations, overnight RV sites. You can purchase a brand new or pre-owned lakefront retreat or wooded hideaway, or bring your own RV for the season or for shorter stays. To learn more and get your next adventure started, please visit our  Ways to Stay .
What months can I visit and stay at Bloom Resorts?
Most of our properties operate seasonally, typically from May 1 to October 31. Please check with your specific resort location for exact dates and off-season access options. For more information on each location, please visit  Our Resorts  page.
What is the minimum and maximum amount of days I can book overnight stays at a participating Bloom Resort?
For our Bloom Resorts with overnight stay offerings, guests are required to book a minimum 2 night stay, and a minimum 3 night stay on long weekends and holidays. Guests can also book up to 30-night stays at participating resorts.
