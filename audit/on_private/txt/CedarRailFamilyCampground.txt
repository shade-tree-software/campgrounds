Family Fun Starts Here
Cedar Rail offers over 300 campsites and three distinct cottages available for rent, ensuring a perfect site to meet your family’s unique needs.
Nestled along the picturesque Saugeen River and surrounded by lush, natural landscapes, Cedar Rail provides an ideal setting for relaxation, recreation, and quality time with loved ones. Whether you seek tranquil riverside moments or engaging family adventures, there is something for everyone to enjoy.
With a wide range of amenities, boredom is simply not an option. Enjoy our mini golf course, pickleball court, dog park, swimming pool, pond with inflatables, game room, and extensive bike trails. Additionally, we offer canoe, kayak, and paddleboard rentals to enjoy on the Saugeen River-perfect for unwinding while taking in a breathtaking sunset.

Cedar Rail Campground Reservation Information
Reservation Methods:
By Phone:  Call us at (519) 363-3387
By Email:  Email us at  camp@cedarrailcamp.com
Online:  Please use the online booking system
Check-In and Check-Out Times:
Campsites:  Check-in at 1:00 PM
Cottages:  Check-in at 3:00 PM
Check-out for All:  11:00 AM
Site Regulations:
Each site is permitted  one trailer . In addition, a  dining tent  and a  small child’s tent  are allowed, if desired.
No additional tents  are permitted beyond these allowances.
Group sites and sites 151-155 must be booked through the office.
Deposit Requirements:
A deposit equal to one night’s stay, plus the booking fee and HST, is required at the time of booking for all phone and email reservations.
Payment Options:
Credit Card:  Call the office directly to provide your credit card details. (Please do not leave credit card information on voicemail or in email.)
All reservations (including group sites) must be fully paid, including deposit, at least  30 days in advance  to avoid cancellation.
For  online bookings , payment is required in full at the time of reservation.
Check-In Procedures:
Check-in must be completed during office hours.
If you plan to arrive after office hours, please contact the office  prior to check-in  to make alternative arrangements.
Liability Waivers:
All adults and visitors must sign a  liability waiver  upon entry. This waiver also covers minors.
If you are bringing a  golf cart , a separate  golf cart waiver  must be signed.
Cancellation Policy:
More than 14 days prior to check-in:  Cancel without penalty. Any deposits paid,
except a non-refundable reservation fee + HST will be refunded in full.
Within 14 days of check-in:  A cancellation fee will apply equal to one night’s stay, plus the reservation fee and applicable HST .
Within 48 hours of check-in : No refunds will be issued.
Most sites are reservable online starting March 3rd, 2026, at noon.  Please note there is a $10.00 Reservation fee and fees are paid upon booking.
Please let us know if you need any further clarification or assistance. We look forward to hosting you at Cedar Rail Campground!
