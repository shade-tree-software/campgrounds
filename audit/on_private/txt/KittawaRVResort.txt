Lots of fun happenings for the whole family to enjoy!
Conveniently located just 5 minutes from Calypso Waterpark
30 minutes east of Ottawa, Kittawa RV Resort offers the ultimate family-friendly getaway in Eastern Ontario. Open from May 1 through October 31 (weather permitting), this lively seasonal resort welcomes both transient visitors and seasonal RV and Resort Cottage guests looking for fun, comfort, and unforgettable summer memories.
With 310 spacious, tree-lined sites, Kittawa is designed for guests who want more from their camping experience. From the man-made lake and swimming pool to the relaxing hot tubs, exciting Aquapark, ballfield, and sport court, the resort is packed with amenities that make every stay feel like a true holiday.
Whether you’re seeking adventure, family fun, or a place to return to year after year, Kittawa RV Resort has it all – a vibrant setting, outstanding amenities, and a convenient location near Ottawa that makes every summer escape easy.
