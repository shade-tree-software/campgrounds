Welcome To Head Lake RV Resort
A small, clean & very quiet family park located directly opposite Head Lake, known very well for the great fishing it offers.

2026 Seasonal Rates
Season is from May 8 to October 12, 2026
Prices do not include taxes (HST), unless noted otherwise
Inground pool open from June 5 to September 14, 2025
Overnight & Weekly Camping
$68.00 per night
Based on 2 Adults & children under the age of 10  AND  1 vehicle per site
Add-Ons
Additional adults/children over 10, to pay   $13.00   per night per adult/child
Non overnight visitors   $8.00   per visitor
Overnight visitors   $13.00   per visitor per night
Boat slip   $30.00   per night
Boat trailer storage $10.00 per night
Cargo trailer storage $20.00 per night
Above prices include HST.
Refundable deposit of  $50.00  for 1 gate entry access card
2026 Seasonal Lot Fees
From $2500 to $4500
Above prices DO NOT include HST.
Based on 2 adults, children under 19 & 1 vehicle per site
Add-Ons
Additional adults pay   $200.00   for the season
Gate entry access card   $100.00   per card (for 1 vehicle only)
Metered electric charges are extra
Extras
Boat slip   $600.00   ($650 for pontoon)
Boat trailer storage   $200.00   ($225 for pontoon)
Visitors Non-overnight   $8.00   per person
Visitors Overnight   $13.00   per night per person
Visitors’ Children under 10 No charge
Above prices include HST
