Welcome to Castleton Hills RV Park
Castleton Hills RV Park has been a family-run campground for over 40 years.  Located in beautiful Castleton Ontario, just 10 minutes north of the 401, this campground is rich in nature. Nestled into a hill, surrounded by trees, we offer many “pockets of paradise” for campers who prefer a more rustic and secluded accommodation.
OPEN: May to October .
Why stay  with us
Whether you are 2 or 92 there is something for everyone at Castleton Hills. Sit back and relax on the beach while your children build sandcastles and swim. Enjoy a game of horseshoes or volleyball. Go for a quiet paddle on the pond or venture up the hills for an invigorating hike. Join us for children’s crafts, or curl up in our library with a good book. Be sure to check out our events page to see what exciting activities are happening each weekend, all summer long!

Farm Experience
At Castleton Hills RV Park, we invite you to step into a world where the crackling of dawn is announced by a rooster’s crow, where the air is as fresh as the produce on your plate, and where every day brings you closer to the earth that feeds us. Our Farm Experience is more than just a retreat from the city—it’s a return to the roots of living.
