A family place…
As a full service, family park, we can accommodate tents, tent trailers, travel trailers, motorhomes and park models. And yes! ...We are pet friendly!
We are taking overnight reservations by telephone at 705 324-9317 after 10am.

Seasonal Sites
Welcome to the Double "M" Family!
Book a Tour
Interested in Seasonal Camping for the summer?
We would be very happy to show you the available sites in our campground, and to also learn a little more about you so that we may best suit your vacation needs.
Please let us know when you would like to visit so that we may plan to make the most of your time!
Contact Us
Come stay with us.
Our seasonal sites are serviced with 4-way hookups... water, 30amp hydro service, sewer, and bell telephone ready; many sites have mature trees on them to provide you with both sun and shade. These sites are of various sizes, many generous enough to easily accommodate larger trailers that are up to 40' in length.
