Explore the serene beauty of Big Valley Resort
Nestled in the heart of St. Catharines, Ontario, Big Valley spans 26 acres of lush green land and has been a cherished family-run business since 1976. Whether you’re looking to stay for the season or just a short getaway, Big Valley offers the perfect retreat. Choose from tent sites, RV sites, or cozy cabin rentals, all designed to provide comfort and convenience. With hot showers and bathrooms on-site, your stay will be both relaxing and enjoyable.
At Big Valley, endless activities await to keep you entertained, and our prime location means you’re just minutes away from major attractions. Picture yourself enjoying picnics, swimming in our pristine waters, and gathering around campfires under the stars. Come to Big Valley and create unforgettable memories in a beautiful, serene setting. We can’t wait to welcome you!

Your central hub for all things fun!
Located in Lincoln, Ontario, just minutes from many exciting attractions, Big Valley Campground offers the perfect blend of peace, relaxation, and outdoor adventure. Whether you’re here for a short visit or an entire season, our campground provides a variety of activities to keep you entertained. Enjoy tennis, volleyball, swimming in the pool, soaking in the hot tub, playing horseshoes, and much more.
Surrounded by the beauty of nature, you’ll find the perfect spot to unwind and connect with the great outdoors. Big Valley Campground offers a serene environment where you can relax, enjoy picnics, and gather around campfires under the stars. Our lush green landscapes and well-maintained facilities ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay for all guests.
Ideally situated near the region’s top attractions, Big Valley Campground offers easy access to Niagara Falls, local wineries, Marineland, and shopping—all just a short drive away. With endless outdoor activities and nearby adventures, you’ll never run out of things to do. Embrace the great outdoors and make unforgettable memories at Big Valley Campground.
