“OUR SEASON: May 1st - Oct. 31st”
longest camping season in the area
Nith River Campground is the hidden jewel of Southern Ontario.
It sits on the banks of the Nith River
surrounded by lush, green forest.
The park is secluded from traffic noise by the heavy forest and the
fact that the start of our sites is about 2,000 ft from the nearest highway.
In less than 15 minutes you
can drive from Stratford, Kitchener-Waterloo, 25 minutes to Cambridge or Woodstock, or in 5 minutes
you can drive to the picturesque town of New Hamburg.

Nature Trails/Fishing
Fish on the Nith River or in our Catch and Release Pond.
Walk our extensive trail system through ecologically protected woodlands
Fully Serviced Sites
30 and 50 amp hydro, water and sewer hookups, fire pits and picnic tables on all of our 132 sites.
Community Centre
Our Community Centre will accommodate 100 people indoors and another 40 on the patio. It is complete with a kitchen, BBQ and a fireplace for those cooler days.
Controlled Entrance
For the safety and security of our guests, access to the park is controlled by security cards and remote access. All guests to the park must register at the main office.
