IT ALL HAPPENS  HERE
Rochester Place is about Escaping without going far.
It was built as a local destination on Lake St. Clair where people from Windsor–Essex and the surrounding communities can slow down, get outside, and spend real time together. Play a round of golf in the morning, dining on our patio at sunset, kids burning off energy throughout the resort, and weekends that feel like a mini vacation. For more than just special occasions, it’s a place you come back to, season after season, for the course, the water, the food, and the atmosphere.
At its core, Rochester Place is a lake-accessible golf-and-resort community, a scenic setting, a relaxed vibe, and the kind of views and outdoor moments people remember.
PLAY
The premier southern Ontario golf experience. A parkland course built for fun, challenge and replay value.
Rochester Place Golf Club is a parkland style design that’s enjoyable for all calibers of players. The course has been completely renovated, winding through mature trees and sculpted berms. Ponds and waterways create multiple risk-reward holes that keep every round interesting. Course conditions are described as rivaling top parkland courses across the province, and the greens have been called “The Best Putting surfaces in Windsor.” To book online non-members register once, and the tee-time portal opens at 6AM.

STAY
Experience non-stop recreation and uninterrupted relaxation on a secluded 167-acre resort property.
Whether you want pool time, a patio cocktail, or a quiet reset, you can do as much or as little as you want here. Lounge by the pool, let the kids hit the playground, and make Parkside Grille part of the routine. Spend your days on the water with Lake St. Clair access for boating, fishing, and paddling. Rochester Place is a seasonal resort (May 1 – Oct. 31), so it’s built for spring-to-fall stays and repeat weekends. Check-in is after 2pm, check-out is before 11am, and booking requires payment in full. Choose your fit, then book online!

DINE
Welcome to Parkside Grille Patio dining with fire features and serene golf-course views.
Enjoy a 3,000 square foot outdoor patio with a fountain pool, fire features, and a spacious dining room lounge area. Settle in with chill music, relaxed energy, and spectacular sunsets over the course. Open daily for lunch and dinner 11AM - 9PM, and later until 10PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Reserve ahead through Tock for the smoothest experience. Menu-first? Jump straight to the Food Menu. Find us at 981 County Rd 2, Belle River or call (519) 728-2361.

HOST
Your event destination. Weddings, corporate, and celebrations hosted at Rochester Place & Parkside Grille.
From weddings and corporate events to showers, fundraisers, and gatherings, the team can help you host it here. Parkside Grille’s 3,000 square foot patio lounge seats up to 120, with a fireplace, water feature, and golf-course views. Inside reception space accommodates up to 175 for dining and dancing. For a bigger open-air feel, The Rendezvous offers 5,000 square feet of covered outdoor seating for up to 250 guests. Cuisine and bar service are handled through Parkside Grille, with group menu options and accommodations for allergies and health concerns. Choosing your event type, then request information, or download the Events Guide for a quick overview.
