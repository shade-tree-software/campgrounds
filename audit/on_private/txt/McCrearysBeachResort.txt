Waterfront Cottage Resort Just Minutes from Historic Perth
A Short Drive to Ottawa!
Located on the shores of Mississippi Lake near Perth, Ontario, McCreary’s Beach Resort offers resort cottage ownership in one of Eastern Ontario’s most scenic waterfront destinations. Just 45 minutes from Ottawa and minutes from the historic Town of Perth, this family-friendly resort community provides the perfect balance of lakeside relaxation, outdoor recreation, and small-town charm.
With direct access to Mississippi Lake, owners and guests can spend their days boating, fishing, swimming, and exploring the many islands that make this beautiful waterway a favourite destination for outdoor enthusiasts. Back at the resort, families can enjoy a variety of amenities, planned activities, and spectacular sunsets overlooking the lake.
Whether you’re looking for an affordable alternative to traditional cottage ownership, a seasonal family retreat, or a waterfront escape close to Ottawa, McCreary’s Beach Resort offers an exceptional opportunity to experience Ontario cottage living. From local festivals and cultural events in Perth to endless days spent on the water, every visit brings new opportunities to create lasting memories with family and friends.

Home of the “Mammoth Cheese”
Discover the legendary Mammoth Cheese displayed at Tay Basin, near the Crystal Palace. Crafted in 1893 for the World’s Fair in Chicago, this colossal 22,000-pound cheese is now a historical landmark and a must-see! Made from milk donated by 10,000 cows and showcased at the Fair, a portion of this historic cheese is now housed at Perth Museum, along with the bronze medal it earned.
Visit Website
Perth Wildlife Reserve Conservation Area

Perth Farmers’ Market
Experience the charm of Perth Farmers’ Market, established in 1992 and thriving ever since. Set against the picturesque Tay Basin, with the iconic Crystal Palace as its backdrop, the market offers a delightful array of crafts, special events, and local music. Open rain or shine every Saturday morning from 8AM-1PM, starting Mother’s Day weekend until Thanksgiving.

Stewart Park
