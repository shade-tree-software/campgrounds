Hope Bay Campground is located on Georgian Bay and the Bruce Trail in the South Bruce Peninsula.
It is a quiet family campground with Cabins, Full Service RV sites and Tent Camping.

Over night Camping
Please start the booking process to find out availably and rates.
​Rates are based on 2 Adult and 2 children under the age of 18.
​Additional people are $17 per night.  All reservations require payment in full.
There are no water view sites.
All sites come with a fire pit and picnic table.
​Additional vehicle Fee:  Due to the shortage of parking, each site is limited to one vehicle.  Any additional vehicles will be required to pay $12 a day to park in designated parking areas.
Cancelation:   14 day or more notice         Minimum of ONE night  NON REFUNDABLE on o vernight Camping sites or cabin/bunkies
8-14 days notice                 50% refund
7 days or less notice           No refund
Reservation Changes   Changes to your reservation may be made after time of booking however if your reservation is shortened, the full amount of the original booking will be charged. Reservations may be transferred to a future date within the same calendar year as the original booking without forfeiting your deposit.
