Serene Sleeping
We have 30 acres of open lots. In addition to our seasonal lots, we have Standard Tent Sites, Transient Standard Back-In 30 Amp, and Transient Standard Back-In 50 Amp.
Plus: We offer two incredible cottage rentals—our Brown Cottage and our Green Cottage. Each one can accommodate 8 to 10 guests and are carefully appointed for a family or friends’ getaway with all the amenities you need: air conditioning, wi-fi, cable, BBQ, private fire pit and more. Both have large back decks—perfect for watching the big ships go by on the river. Also: Pet-friendly!
Cottage Rentals
Cameron’s Point Cottages offer a beautifully upgraded retreat in a peaceful setting, perfect for reconnecting with the people who matter most. Ideal for large family gatherings or getaways with friends, these spacious cottages give you full access to all on-site amenities, including the sandy beach and inviting pool, so everyone can relax, play, and make memories together.
