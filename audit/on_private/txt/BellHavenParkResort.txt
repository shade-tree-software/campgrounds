WELCOME
Bell Haven Park is a quiet family-friendly resort offering vacation rentals, along with seasonal & overnight camping.  Our Pigeon Lake waterfront provides a naturally beautiful setting while being very close to a historical city of      Bobcaygeon     .  We have overnight sites with hydro and water, and a few waterfront un-serviced tent sites.  Our amenities include washroom and shower facilities, heated swimming pool (open from July long weekend to September long weekend), sandy beach, small convenience store, coin laundry, recreation hall, pool table, tennis table, playground, horseshoe pits, baseball field, basketball pad, volleyball etc. We also offer a variety of organized activities for both adults and children.  An electronic security gate controls traffic in the park.
Pigeon Lake is part of the    Trent-Severn Waterway    located in the heart of the Kawarthas.  While boaters appreciate the convenience of the locks to access the many other lakes and rivers of the system, they also provide a fascinating spot to watch the engineering marvel, Lock 32 in action. You can find renowned restaurants for any cuisine, and exclusive shops in downtown Bobcaygeon.  Don't forget to check out Bobcaygeon    EVENTS    site . The    Kawartha Lakes    region is renowned for golf courses, fishing, boating, hiking, biking, and many other recreational and cultural activities.
