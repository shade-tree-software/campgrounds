About Us
WHO ARE WE
McAlpine Beach is a first-class 96-acre campground nestled on the shores of Bright Lake, offering our guests 2,000 feet of water frontage. We are surrounded by sandy beaches, diverse wildlife, abundant wooded forests, and scenic trails for walking and ATV riding. It provides breathtaking scenery, excellent lodging, and numerous campsites for a perfect outdoor experience.
We are conveniently situated just outside of Iron Bridge, Ontario, 1 KM off of Highway 17.
An ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike!

AMENITIES
Guests will have a fantastic experience with access to a wide range of amenities, including a shower house, bathroom facilities, a spacious docking area, boat launch, fish cleaning station, recreational equipment, playground, and firewood. Each lot also offers a private fire pit and picnic table, making it perfect for outdoor gatherings. Plus, being animal-friendly ensures everyone, including pets, can enjoy their stay!
Bright Lake offers some of the best fishing spots in the area, including pickerel, pike, bass, and musky, making it a paradise for anglers. The facility also provides a variety of recreational activities and amenities suitable for the whole family, such as pontoon and fishing boat rentals, kayaking, paddle boating, canoeing, hiking trails, volleyball, and horseshoes. Additionally, nearby attractions include horseback riding, golfing, and much more, ensuring there's something for everyone to enjoy.
