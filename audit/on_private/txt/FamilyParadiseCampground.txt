Welcome To Camping Oceanic
Located in Shediac, New Brunswick
At Camping Oceanic, our goal is simple. Relaxed camping, clean surroundings, and friendly service.
Guests enjoy a heated pool, playgrounds, sports courts, a bouncy pillow, and a full schedule of weekend activities. The campground sits just minutes from the shore of Parlee Beach and close to shops and town attractions in Shediac. Settle in and enjoy long summer days, laughter around the campfire, and easy nights with friends and family.
