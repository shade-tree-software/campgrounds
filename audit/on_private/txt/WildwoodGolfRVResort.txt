Wildwood Golf & RV Resort in Essex
The relaxing atmosphere, the peaceful scenery, and the location of Wildwood Golf & RV Resort in Essex, ON are just a few reasons why our visitors and seasonal residents keep coming back.
Surrounded by a fantastic wine route, great shopping and dining, as well as area attractions, what more could you ask for? There is so much to see and do while you are here. Whether you stay with us for a day, a month, or a season, we invite you to discover Windsor and Essex County.

Play, Relax, and Enjoy
From charming boutiques to big malls, the Windsor and surrounding areas have it all. With hundreds of restaurants to choose from you can start right here at Twisted Tees Bar & Banquet.
Looking for a place to golf? Look no further as our beautifully manicured 18-hole course will have you using every club in the bag. Players can warm up on the practice green or chipping green.
Explore Epic Wine Country where there are approximately 1000 acres of grapes in the Lake Erie North Shore and Pelee Island Designated Viticultural Areas. For craft beer enthusiasts check out the Craft Breweries also emerging in Windsor-Essex County.
Foreign guests can enter Windsor from the U.S. via the Windsor-Detroit Tunnel or the Ambassador Bridge. Our golf course and resort is located approximately 25 minutes from the border. Signs are posted along the way, which will direct you right to our resort.
We look forward to your visit and hope you enjoy what our resort has to offer.
