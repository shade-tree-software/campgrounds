Welcome to Sauble Falls Tent & Trailer Park
Set in the peaceful town of Sauble Beach, Ontario, our family campground sits right across from the famous Sauble Falls and just a 5-minute drive from one of Canada's longest freshwater beaches. Whether you're pitching a tent, rolling in with an RV, staying in one of our rustic cabins, or treating yourself to our fully-equipped house trailer rental — we have a spot for you.
Why Families Love It Here
🌳 The Setting
Mature trees, dappled sunlight, shaded and sunny sites — our campground has a magical atmosphere that brings families back year after year.
📍 The Location
Right across from Sauble Falls Provincial Park and just 5 minutes to one of Canada's longest freshwater beaches — you couldn't ask for a better base camp.
🤝 The Community
Family run since 1987 with a tight-knit community feel. Kids Day Festival, live bands, karaoke, dances — there's always something going on.
