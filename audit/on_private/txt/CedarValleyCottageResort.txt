Cedar Valley Offers Something for Everyone on the Scugog River
Nestled along the scenic Scugog River in Kawartha Lakes, Cedar Valley Cottage Resort offers the perfect blend of waterfront relaxation and active family fun. Open from May 1 to October 31, our seasonal resort is ideal for those looking to enjoy cottage living without the upkeep of traditional ownership.
Since 2020, Cedar Valley has been thoughtfully enhanced with a stunning waterfront redevelopment, premium docks, new cottage sites, and a multi-sports court featuring road hockey, pickleball, tennis and basketball  – creating a welcoming space for families of all ages to connect and unwind.
Enjoy peaceful river views with convenient boating access, then explore the nearby town of Lindsay, just minutes away, offering shopping, dining and local events. You’re also perfectly positioned to discover the natural beauty, lakes, parks and locks throughout the Kawartha Lakes region.
Looking for your own seasonal escape? Discover our  resort cottage ownership opportunities  and start making memories at Cedar Valley year after year.
