Our Facilities
Located a short walk from a unique seven mile stretch of natural sand beach on the clean blue waters of Lake Huron. Sauble Beach is famous for excellent swimming and spectacular sunsets.
To ensure your enjoyment, Woodland Park stresses a clean campground and enforces quiet time from 11 p.m. to 8:30 a.m.   No golf carts / 4 wheelers.
Check-in time is after 12 noon but before 8:00 p.m.
Check-in time for Rental Trailers after 3 p.m.
Check-out time is 10 a.m.
Our Location
Woodland Park is located in Sauble Beach, Ontario.
Whether you like to swim, shop, golf or just relax in the sun, there's something for every taste here.
