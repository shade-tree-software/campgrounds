WELCOME TO
Ahmic Lake Resort
Located north of Muskoka in Ontario’s beautiful Almaguin Highlands,
Ahmic Lake Resort is a year-round destination for cottage getaways, camping, dining and outdoor adventure.    Our family- and pet-friendly resort offers comfortable cottage rentals, camping, watercraft rentals and the Swiss Country House Restaurant.
Set on
70 acres with 2,400 feet of waterfront  , the resort is surrounded by the natural beauty of Ahmic Lake, with a river, rapids and dam right on the property.
Experience Ahmic Lake
Get out on the water and explore
31 miles of navigable waterways    from Ahmic Harbour to Burk’s Falls. Spend the day boating, paddling or fishing for pike, bass and pickerel, or relax and swim at our protected beach.
On land, enjoy peaceful walks, wildlife and bird watching, spectacular fall colours and plenty of space to simply unwind by the lake.
Explore the Almaguin Highlands
There’s even more to discover beyond the resort. Explore the communities, attractions and outdoor experiences of the Almaguin Highlands and surrounding Parry Sound region, then return to Ahmic Lake to relax and enjoy cottage-country life.
Stay. Camp. Dine. Explore. Celebrate.

Comfortable Cottages on Ahmic Lake
Choose from our
1, 2 and 3-bedroom cottages, spacious 5-bedroom double cottages, or 5-bedroom Lakeview House  , surrounded by trees with beautiful lakefront or lake views.

The Restaurant
The Swiss Country House Restaurant & The BAR"N"
Enjoy great food and warm hospitality at the
Swiss Country House Restaurant at Ahmic Lake Resort   . Located in Ahmic Harbour in Ontario’s Almaguin Highlands, our licensed restaurant is known for our
classic pork schnitzel  , hearty favourites and relaxed cottage-country atmosphere.
Join us for breakfast on Sunday, or dinner with seasonal specials and
live music and entertainment throughout the year   .
Whether you’re staying at the resort, visiting the area or joining us from the local community, everyone is welcome.
Come for the schnitzel. Stay for the music, atmosphere and Ahmic Lake hospitality.

Camping on Ahmic Lake
Experience Ontario cottage country from your own campsite at
Ahmic Lake Resort in the Almaguin Highlands   .
Surrounded by forest and the natural beauty of Ahmic Lake, our campground offers options for
seasonal RV camping and short-term camping stays  .
Spend your days swimming, boating, fishing and exploring the lake, then return to your site to relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.
Whether you’re joining us for a getaway or looking for a seasonal home away from home, Ahmic Lake Resort is a great place to enjoy the outdoors and make the most of camping season.

Marina
Marina and Boating at Ahmic Lake Resort
Boating on Ahmic Lake
Get out on the water and experience beautiful Ahmic Lake and its 31 miles of navigable waterways. The marina at Ahmic Lake Resort provides convenient access for resort guests, campers, seasonal residents and visitors looking to enjoy a day on the lake.
Marina & Docking
Our marina offers docking and boating services with easy access to Ahmic Lake. Whether you’re staying at the resort, camping with us or visiting for the day, contact our team for current dock availability, rates and services.
Boat Launch
Bringing your own boat? Our
boat launch provides convenient access to Ahmic Lake    so you can spend more time on the water fishing, cruising and exploring.
Boat launch fees apply. Please contact us for current rates and information.
Boat & Watercraft Rentals
No boat? No problem. Ask us about our available
boat and non-motorized watercraft rentals    and explore Ahmic Lake at your own pace.
Rental availability and rates may vary throughout the season.
Marine Fuel
Marine fuel is available on-site for added convenience during your day on the water. Please contact us for current availability and service hours.
Explore Ahmic Lake
From boating and paddling to fishing and swimming, the lake is at the heart of the Ahmic Lake Resort experience. Explore the waterways, discover quiet stretches of shoreline or simply spend the day enjoying life on the lake.
Marina Information
Looking for
docking, boat launch, watercraft rentals or marina services on Ahmic Lake?    Contact Ahmic Lake Resort for current availability, rates and information.
