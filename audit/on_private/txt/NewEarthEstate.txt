A PEACEFUL LAND FOR ALL GREEN SOULS
Nature Lovers retreat and Event Venue
Need to take a break from your day to day routine?
Need to unwind and shed your stress?
Let's get you back on the right track!
Life is a three dimensional canvas. Let us help you experiment with Eco-friendly living adventure and getting back to the natural form of life. Depending on the event you are participating in this Eco-Retreat, find your inner peace by experiencing meditation, mindfulness, forest bathing, awakening ceremonies, free form dace, soulful music, alternative medicine, holistic health care, art projects and more.
New Earth Estate is a year-round retreat and event centre in the heart of Grey County just outside of the village of Markdale, founded in 2016, which conducts soulful programs and events throughout the year. Also as an event venue, on a 50 acre piece of nature, with pond, river, forest and hills, New Earth Estate welcomes, entrepreneurs, event and wedding planners, institutes, or individuals to have their programs, occasion, or special events held at its beautiful outdoor and indoor spaces.
Our event venue is used for events we announce in our   event page   or could be booked for YOUR events. For overnight accommodation we have chalet unit and other close by options that could be arranged.

Things To Do Around
Other than   activities inside the property,   you can do many activities and excursions around here depending on your interest, ability, time, and preference.
PADDLING
Kayaking, Canoeing, Paddle board tours for their relaxing effect, and visiting beautiful nature of the water way of grey county, as well as a fun summer exercise which will keep you active. If you have your canoe or kayak bring them with you, if not you can   rent a canoe here  .
ECO-ADVENTURE
Team building through experiencing Tree Tracks, Zip Lining, Suspension Bridge, Caving, Rock Climbing, and much more.   Read more ...
TRAIL HIKING
Enjoy hiking in wonderful endless trails and visit amazing water falls of Grey County;    Hoggs Falls  ,   Inglis Falls  ,   Eugenia Falls  ,   Walter's Falls  , and   Indian Falls  .  This is another team building skills opportunity for your group.
ENJOY THE PRISTINE SAUBLE BEACH
Sauble Beach is the  second longest freshwater beach in the world ! A phenomenon of sandbar deposits building out along the Lake Huron shoreline keeps the water at Sauble very shallow and warm.
EQUIN THERAPY
Equine Assisted Therapy aids in the healing of many psychological conditions such as grief & Loss, disordered eating, depression, relationship issues, anxiety, anger and more.   Read more ...
GOLFING
Golfing in one of the beautiful 18 hole golf courses of   The Ferns Golf Resort  ,   Townsend Lake Golf Course,   or 9 hole course of   Markdale Golf and Curling Club  . Each have their own beauty and charm.
SCANDINAVE SPA
Ultimate relaxation in a zen environment in the heart of nature. Enjoy different saunas and beautiful outdoor hot and cold streams and more.   Read more ...
WINE, BEER, AND CIDER TASTING
Wine, Beer, and Cider Tasting tours, which can be combined with another activity depending on the season. Such as Paddle & Wine.   Find out more ...
EXPLORE TOBERMORY
World-famous hiking in a rugged landscape, home to orchids, ferns, and black bears. The wonderful Grotto, this geological marvel, the Flowerpot Island, dramatic caves and limestone cliffs overlooking Georgian Bay, the shipwreck Sweepstakes, all around Tobermory, Ontario which is Bruce County’s most popular attraction. Hidden away along the rugged shores of the Bruce Peninsula.
WINTER ADVENTURE
Snow Shoeing, Cross Country Skiing, Down Hill Skiing, Snowmobiling, Dog Sledding are some of the winter fun that we have here for you to learn and experience.   Let us know what you are looking for and find out more ...
