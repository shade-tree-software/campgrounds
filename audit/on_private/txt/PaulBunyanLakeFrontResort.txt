On the shore of Lake Huron, at the south edge of the beautiful village of Bayfield, is situated one of  Ontario's most outstanding lake front RV parks , Paul Bunyan Lake Front Resort. We offer all the beauty of Lake Huron together with the most up-to-date   amenities  . Our resort is a family-oriented campground, with majority being seasonal guests, also welcoming some   overnight camping  , and now   cabin rentals!
​   ​
Family owned and operated by the Boere & Vandenbussche families since 2000 and it is our goal to make your stay at Paul Bunyan a relaxing and enjoyable one.

Cabin Rentals
​      Our cabins are all equipped with AC/furnace unit, a kitchenette and full washroom on the inside. On the outside, enjoy a covered deck with picnic table, a fire pit with chairs and BBQ. Sites located close to main building, pool, playground, beach.
​
Cabana  Cabins (sites 239 and 240): sleeps 4 (twin bunk room, 1 queen)
Chalet  Cabin (site 241): sleeps 6 (twin bunk room, twin bunk room, queen room)
​
Wi-Fi access included.
We provide a fridge (Cabana models are mini fridge - no freezer), microwave, dishes & cutlery, toaster, Keurig machine (please provide your own favourite k-cups), electric hot plate, toilet paper, dish soap, garbage bags and basic cleaning supplies.
​
We ask that you please provide your own linens (beds, towels, pillows), hand soap, personal items and food.
*OPTIONAL:  you can add a "linen package" to your reservation. Linen package includes all bed sheets, blankets, pillows, bath towels, hand/face towels. Please bring your own beach/pool towels.

Cancellation Policy
Full payment is required at time of booking.
All cancellations and shortened stays are subject to a $20 cancellation fee.
Cancellations that are made more than 14 days in advance of your arrival date will receive a full refund less the $20 cancellation fee.
If your cancellation occurs within 14 days of your arrival date your payment will be refunded except for the first night's camping fee plus the $20 Cancellation fee.
If you cancel same day as check in, do not show up, or need an early departure, there is no refund of your payment.
