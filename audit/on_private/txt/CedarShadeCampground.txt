Additional Information
Prices do not include HST
Number of people permitted per lot, 2 adults and 4 children less than 18 years old.
Maximum one tent per site.
Must be 25yrs or older to camp.
Check-out time is 1pm or 10$ extra until 6pm.
Charges will apply to golf carts, extra cars, boats, jet skies, trailers, pets, etc...
Daily Visits
Swimming is from 9:00am to 9:00pm (or until dark).
No animals permitted when spending the day, they are not allowed in public areas.
No drugs or alcohol permitted in public area.
Containers must be plastic when picnicking.
Children that are under 12 must be accompanied by a parent when swimming.
Pool and spas are reserved for campers only, we encourage you to come camping for a weekend and you will be able to use these facilities.
