Helping Families Create Lifetime Memories,
One Campfire at a Time
Willow Lake Campground & RV Park is a fun, safe, and family-friendly getaway where families can relax, explore, and make lasting memories together. Whether you’re roasting marshmallows by the campfire, splashing in the pool, or enjoying outdoor games, there’s something for everyone to enjoy. Our peaceful, well-maintained campground provides a welcoming atmosphere for campers of all ages, with spacious sites, modern amenities, and plenty of activities to keep the whole family entertained. At Willow Lake, we believe in bringing people together to enjoy the great outdoors in a safe and enjoyable environment. Come stay with us and experience the perfect blend of adventure and relaxation!

WILLOW LAKE'S OPTIONS FOR HAPPY CAMPERS
OVERNIGHT SITES
We have great overnight sites to choose from!
30A Full Service EWS (electric, water, septic) Pull Throughs
30A Full Service EWS Back Ins, (plus one solitary 30A EW Back In!)
50A Full Service EWS Pull Throughs
Premium 50A Full Service EWS Pull Throughs, extra long
Sites are grass and flat, and never a far walk from the washrooms, GamesRoom, Recreation Hall, or Campstore! No additional tents on site with an RV.
Tenting available on select RV sites.  One sleeping tent per site.
RENTAL CABINS
Situated at the top of the campground there are four cabins, the Purple, Blue, Red and Orange cabins. Think of them as a wooden tent, except that unlike a normal tent, they keep you dry and warm, have real beds and electric lights.
Great for couples and families who don’t’ have an RV but want the true camping feel!
CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO AND PICTURES!
RETRO COTTAGE
A fun and family friendly cottage that can accommodate 6 people. Styled just like grandma’s cottage, retro furnishings, wood paneling, it’s truly a blast from the past!
2 bedrooms, each with bunkbeds, double on bottom, single on top. Full small bathroom with sink, toilet, and shower. Kitchen with sink, fridge, microwave, kettle, toaster, dishes and cutlery.
BBQ outside for cooking. Picnic table and firepit.
