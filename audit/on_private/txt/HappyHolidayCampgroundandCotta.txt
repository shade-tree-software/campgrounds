HAPPY HOLIDAY CAMPGROUND & COTTAGES
About Us! -  We are expanding the campground and are looking for seasonal campers to COME STAY WITH US!
(Seasonal Spots Still Available For 2023)
Welcome to Happy Holiday Campground & Cottages. We are a family campground with lakeside cottages for rent on Duncan Lake, which is just 10 minutes north of Temagami. Activities & Services include:
Seasonal, Weekly & Daily Camping for Tents, RVs and Trailers & Lakeside Cottage Rentals!
We welcome off road vehicles / ATVs. Many Fish chain of lakes attached to Duncan Lake.
We have many other lakes in the area including Lake Temagami, Net Lake, Marten River, Cassells Lake and Kanichi Lake for Walleye, Pike, Lake & Brook Trout, Smallmouth & Largemouth Bass.
Canoeing, Hiking, ATVing, Swimming, Badminton, Basketball, Horseshoes, Biking, Children's Playground and bi-weekly events in July and August.
Local attractions such as museums, Temagami Fire-tower, boat tours and much more.
10% Senior Citizen Discount: on Nightly Campsite Rates Only in May and September. Cannot be combined with any other discount offers.
Happy Holiday Campground & Cottages is a clean and comfortable place to stay while enjoying all that Temagami has to offer.
