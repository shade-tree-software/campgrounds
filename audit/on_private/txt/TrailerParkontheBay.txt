Trailer Park on the Bay         & Cottages
Welcome to all new campers and to those
planning on returning to our family park this year! At        Trailer
Park on the Bay         &
Cottages       ,       you will have the opportunity to enjoy our many recreational activities and brand new facilities
including a children's playground, recreational hall, public washrooms, sandy beach with swimming area and our newly renovated two bedroom cottages. We have also updated our hydro offering with
new  30 amp electricity available and can now offer        Wi-Fi        internet access throughout the
campground.
We are located on Shadow Lake between
Coboconk and Norland east of Hwy 35. Shadow and Silver Lake combined have many bays with a total shore line of 41 km. These largely protected landlocked lakes provide calm waters and create splendid
boating condition for canoeing, kayaking, sailing and fishing enthusiasts. The flow and water level of the gull river, Shadow Lake system is controlled through dam operated by Parks Canada. At
Trailer Park on the Bay     & Cottages    we can help you plan
even find places to stay, suggest travel arrangements and much more. Your comfort and enjoyment is our first priority. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us, we would be happy to
hear from you and do not forget to check out some new pictures of our beautiful campground below and    Photo
Gallery   .
Have any questions? Contact your hosts Reza and
Afsaneh:
Phone     : 705 454-8176
or 416 721-5172
