RV Campground & Cottages
located 30 km north of Huntsville Ontario
We have over 40 acres of woodlands overlooking Doe Lake, a predominately sandy bottom warm water lake and a private sand beach with a separate Dog Beach.  There are over 4km of hiking trails through our hardwood forest.  We have 2 washroom complexes with a coin operated laundry facility located at washroom complex #1.                                                                                        Absolutely NO OFF-PREMISES FIREWOOD permitted at our resort. All firewood must be purchased in our office.                         While we are pet friendly, no aggressive behavior (breeds) or excessive barking of dogs are allowed. Please clean up after your pet, leashes are required at all times. Doe Lake CR Seasonal Sites
Doe Lake Campground Rizzort is mainly a seasonal campground. We currently have 95 seasonal sites. Water and sewage are included in the site fee along with free winter storage.
Each site has its own hydro meter and each camper pays for their own individual usage. Check our   RATES      for more info.
Doe Lake CR is not a field with trailers lined up in rows. The Park is situated on a hillside and the roads meander through a fully treed setting. A tour is the best way to appreciate the layout of the Park.
Beginning in 2025, all seasonal sites have fibre optic high speed internet available offering 1Gps to your RV!
