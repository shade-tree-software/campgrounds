Welcome to West Pines Park Resort — located on the sandy shores of Lake St. Peter
Experience West Pines Park Resort and all that Lake St. Peter and Hastings Highlands has to offer!
Located along 1000-feet of sandy shoreline, West Pines is a family-friendly campground featuring both seasonal and overnight campsites, waterfront cottages, and trailer rentals. We pride ourselves in offering well maintained sites and clean facilities, in a relaxing and kid-friendly environment.
Spend your holiday enjoying the many amenities at West Pines, or simply relax by the lake and cozy up around your campfire.
Looking to explore? Algonquin Park is just a short, scenic drive from our main entrance. Nearby small towns offer unique art galleries, antique and gift shops, homemade local fare, and weekly farmers’ markets.
Whether you bring bikes and beach toys or a good book and a lounge chair, the lake is just steps away— ready for adventure or relaxation.

SEASONAL CAMPING
Seasonal camping is an excellent way to enjoy the great outdoors and create family memories year after year. Featuring 90 seasonal sites in a family-oriented and relaxing atmosphere, West Pines offers an authentic camping experience along the sandy shores of Lake St. Peter. If you’re looking for a seasonal site to enjoy at your leisure, please reach out to book a park tour - we’d be happy to show you around!
LEARN MORE
OVERNIGHT CAMPING
Whether you’re camping with us for the night or here for a few weeks, our overnight sites invite you to make yourself at home amongst the tall pines and sandy shores. We have a variety of unique overnight sites located all throughout the park, including prime waterfront and beachfront camping experiences. Each site is located within close proximity to all park amenities and includes a campfire pit, picnic table, 2 Muskoka chairs and complimentary use of canoes, kayaks and paddle boards.
LEARN MORE
COTTAGE RENTALS
Our rustic lakefront cottages are located just steps from the water’s edge and are a favourite amongst families who have been vacationing at West Pines for generations! The perfect destination for a couples’ getaway or a family vacation. You can spend your days on the water or along the sandy shores – you’ll have endless fun boating, fishing, swimming, or playing in the sand. At night, enjoy a lakeside campfire under the stars or grill up a feast on one of our new BBQs. Our cottages feature all of your basic necessities and some additional cottaging comforts. Outside, each cottage comes with a campfire pit, picnic table and 2 Muskoka chairs. Each cottage comes with its own dock – you supply the watercraft!
LEARN MORE
TRAILER RENTALS
A great (and easier) way for you to enjoy the camping experience! Our trailer rentals come completely set up and ready for you to enjoy. The best part, no need to apologize to your spouse or camping buddies for the things that you said while trying to park the trailer! Our trailer rentals come equipped with of all the essential kitchen appliances, BBQ, 3-piece bathroom, covered deck and outdoor furniture – just to highlight a few amenities.

Overnight Sites
All of our transient sites feature a campfire pit and picnic table. Each transient site is unique and located in a different section throughout the park. Please read the individual site descriptions to choose which site best suits your needs. Some of our overnight sites accommodate two or more units – please call if you have any inquiries regarding group bookings, or if you’d like more information regarding these specific site set-ups.

Camping Rates
Overnight camping rates are for 2 adults and dependent children under 18.
All reservations are charged a one-time, non-refundable $20 + HST reservation fee.
Premium Waterfront & Beachfront Sites: $71/night + HST
Classic Hook-Up Sites: $60/night + HST
The Pines Sites: $60/night + HST
Additional Campers: $20/night + HST or $80/week + HST
Cottage & Trailer Rental Rates (Weekly)
Cottage and trailer rental rates are for 2 adults and dependent children under 18, up to 4 guests.
Cottage #1: $1009/week + HST
Cottage #2: $1009/week + HST
Cottage #3: $1309/week + HST
Puma by the Lake: $799/week + HST
Puma on the Hill: $1189/week + HST
Hillside Retreat: $1189/week + HST

Check-In & Check-Out
Camping
Check-In Time: 2pm
Check-Out Time: 12 noon
Cottage & Rental Trailers
Check-In Time: 4:00pm
Keys/Passcodes are to be picked up at the main office. All campers/cottagers are required to check-in at the main office and sign a waiver form prior to entering the premises.
Check-Out Time: 10:00am
Keys are to be returned to the main office on your departure day. A key dropbox is located by the main door of the office, should you be departing outside of office hours.
Early Check-In and Late Check-Out can be requested, but is not guaranteed and is subject to availability and additional fees.
Deposits
For all camping and accommodation rentals, a 50% deposit is due at the time of booking. The balance owing is due upon arrival and prior to entering the park.
Deposits can be paid by E-transfer to:   hello@westpineslsp.com
Upon arrival, the remainder of your balance can be paid by cash, debit or E-transfer.
(Sorry, we do not accept credit cards.)
Cancellation Policy
We sincerely hope that you won’t have to cancel your reservation, however, we do understand that unforeseen circumstances and family emergencies present themselves. If possible, we ask that you transfer your reservation deposit to a future date, subject to availability.
All cancellations are subject to a  $20+HST reservation fee for each cancelled site.
Cancellations made 30 days prior  to your arrival date qualify for a full refund minus the $20+HST reservation fee.
Cancellations made between 29 days – 8 days  prior to your arrival date qualify for a transfer credit in the amount of your deposit, minus 1 overnight rental fee + $20+HST reservation fee. Transfer credits are to be used within one-year of original reservation date.
Cancellations made LESS than 7 days  prior to arrival – No Refund/Non-transferable.
Cancelling Days/Shortening Stays Upon Arrival:  Please understand that shortening your stay upon your arrival (or within 7 days prior to your arrival) does not qualify for a discount or refund, as we’ve reserved your requested dates for you and turned other guests away.
