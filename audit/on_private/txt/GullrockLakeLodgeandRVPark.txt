Gullrock Lake Lodge : Nestled along the serene South Bay shoreline of  Gullrock Lake  in  Northwestern Ontario, Canada,  our lodge offers an idyllic escape for nature enthusiasts and fishing aficionados.
Lake Connections  :  Gullrock Lake  seamlessly connects to four other pristine lakes:  Red Lake, Keg Lake, Ranger Lake, and Two Islands . These interconnected waterways are renowned for their abundant walleye and pike fisheries, promising unforgettable angling experiences.
Wilderness Ambiance :  Our lodge is strategically situated to provide that coveted remote wilderness feel. Surrounded by untouched natural beauty, guests can immerse themselves in the tranquility of the Canadian outdoors. Yet, remarkably, we’re just 10 kilometers away from the vibrant town of Red Lake, striking a harmonious balance between seclusion and convenience.
Customized Packages  : Unlike most Northwestern Ontario resorts, we empower our guests to curate their own vacation experiences. At Gullrock Lake Lodge, you’re not confined to rigid per-person packages. Instead, tailor your stay to your preferences—whether it’s a fishing-focused adventure, a family getaway, or a serene retreat. We’re committed to working closely with our guests to ensure the best value for their dream vacation.
Discover the allure of our  lakeview cabins , the convenience of our  full-service RV sites , and the thrill of world-class fishing—all set against the backdrop of our stunning  9-acre wilderness property . Welcome to Gullrock Lake Lodge, where memories are made and nature beckons!
