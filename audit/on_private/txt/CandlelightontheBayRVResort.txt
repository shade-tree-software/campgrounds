Welcome to our family campground
Nestled on the shores of Lake Manitouwabing, Candlelight on the Bay provides a natural escape for families looking to relax and unwind.
As a seasonal camper, you are welcome to come and go on your schedule.  We encourage you to take advantage of all your vacation days and come to the park to rejuvenate under the stars.
Our guests enjoy gorgeous shorelines, colourful sunsets, fishing from the docks, and regular family activities.
Our seasonal park offers guest getaways from May – October each year.

Seasonal Camping
Relax and Rejuvenate all season long!
Seasonal camping allows you to camp on your schedule without the hassle of setting up and tearing down your site with each visit.  Spend your time with friends and family, enjoying traditional camping activities and getting away from your everyday rather than worrying about check out time.  Many of our guests have developed lifelong friendships with their camping neighbours, creating a community they look forward to retreating to with each visit.
Start creating your camping oasis today!
