What makes Widgawa different
New team, new focus, new experience…
Killarney Park is the crown jewel of the Ontario Provincial Parks and the closest Wilderness Park to the GTA.
The Widgawa Lodge (west side of Killarney) is only 33 minutes farther than Killarney Park east (George Lake)!
We have upgraded our facilities to make a smooth launching point to the Killarney area.
We have amazing day trips and recommendations for the guests who are not able or wanting to camp in Killarney.
After our first year as new owners along with our great staff, our Google reviews show you our commitment to making your visit and stay a wonderful experience!

Camping
Camping at widgawa
Embark on a rugged wilderness adventure at Widgawa Lodge’s camping site, offering close proximity to the untamed beauty of Killarney Provincial Park. Nestled in the heart of nature, our camping experience is a gateway to the wild, where every sunrise promises new discoveries. Immerse yourself in the untouched landscapes, surrounded by the rugged beauty that defines the region. As you embrace the thrill of camping, rest assured that modern comforts are within reach, with convenient access to bathrooms and a public shower. Explore the wilderness by day and unwind under the starlit sky at night – Widgawa Lodge camping is your passport to an adventurous retreat in the heart of nature.
Book online now!
Hammocks & Tents in Camping Area
Ancient Forest - Widgawa Lodge
Ancient forest solo Camping
Discover serenity in the heart of Widgawa Lodge’s Ancient Forest camping experience, a rare gem of rugged, untouched beauty. Nestled among ancient trees, this limited and exclusive camping opportunity invites guests to embrace tranquility and reflect on what truly matters. With a commitment to preserving the peaceful essence of the forest, the limited availability ensures an intimate and restful retreat. Bask in the rejuvenating embrace of enormous trees, providing a natural sanctuary for quiet contemplation and blissful relaxation. Opening its doors in the summer of 2024, Widgawa Lodge’s Ancient Forest camping promises a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in nature’s timeless beauty and find solace in the stillness of the ancient woodland.
Email us to reserve a space for you in 2024!
