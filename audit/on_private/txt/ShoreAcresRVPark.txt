Welcome to Shore Acres Park!
Located in Port Dover, Ontario, Shore Acres Park is a family-owned and operated private RV and Trailer Park and has been in operation since 1962.
We are proud to say that we are the best South Coast vacation park in Norfolk County!
Our park sits on 25 acres of beautifully landscaped land with mature trees and boasts a private beach on the shores of Lake Erie.
We offer fully-serviced trailer sites (30 AMP) with municipal water and sewer. We have 191 seasonal sites and 23 overnight sites.
We are a pet-free park.
Park Information
Shore Acres is a beautiful , scenic , quiet park that has a well maintained beautiful private sandy beach , that is run by a very friendly attentive staff . It's proximity to downtown port Dover means you can easily walk or bike to Dover's great restaurants and attractions . We are enjoying Shore Acres now (4 years) as our parents have done for the past 25 years. Love it.

What can we say about this beautiful park. We first started camping at Shore Acres two seasons ago. Our first summer we stayed for a couple of weekends, then last year decided to become full time seasonal campers and we just loved it. Where else can you camp and get a million dollar view of Lake Erie in your backyard.  The beach is private and well maintained and the grounds are very clean with grass under your feet.  All of the facilities are clean and the staff and fellow campers are very friendly.  You are just a few kilometres from downtown Port Dover where there are many restaurants, stores and shops. You are also minutes away from the Lynn Valley Trail and lots of water sports.  Did I mention the famous Lake Erie perch? A must have. So when we're asked where we camp, we say we Summer in Port Dover on the Radical Road.
Ron and Bev, Simcoe Ontario
