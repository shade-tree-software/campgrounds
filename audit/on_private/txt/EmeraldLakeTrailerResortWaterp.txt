Welcome To Emerald Lake
Emerald Lake is a stunning RV, waterpark, and camping destination that is also great for day trips. You'll find our park and campground fully equipped to meet your needs.
Prices for Waterpark & Lake area
Live this summer to the fullest
The sun is shining, and the weather is sweet. The happiest season of the year invites you to take a dip at our water park... It’s okay! ...even we are excited!
Click Here
How to Get Splashing:
1. Complete a Waiver
All players on the Emerald Lake must have a completed and signed waiver.
Link to the Waiver
2. Arrive Early
You'll need to purchase a park pass for the Emerald Lake, which is not included in your ticket price.
3. Head to the Lake
Check in at the Emerald Lake.
4. Learn the Safety Rules
We'll tell you what's allowed on the Emerald Lake and get you equipped with a life jacket.
5. Jump In!
Go ahead and get wet and have lots of fun!

Great place for a splish splash on a hot summer day. It has a kids pool, a splash pad, cabin, change rooms, rental shop, and etc. There is a lot of things for you to do here, with family or friends or kids. You can rent a life vest in the rental shop. So much fun! My kid surely loved it. A friend of ours hosted a party here and it was a wonderful event. ❤ Check out their site.
What a great place for a family outing. Lots to do for the kids. I was attending a birthday party. Clean washrooms. Lots of parking. Even had life jackets to rent. Many picnic tables around the park. Will definitely return with my family next summer!?
Adventurous and active son loves this place. We visited for the day and there are several floating stations such as slides, trampolines, and platforms. Located in a former quarry so water is very deep. Life jackets are required in some areas, rentals available. Something for all ages. A water lover’s paradise.
Went to Emerald Lake for the first time today. I had 3 boys with me ages, 13, 12, and 8. They loved it and we had the nicest day. Make a day out of it by bringing a beach umbrella or shelter, even a bbq. Even though it was a busy long weekend, the lines were quick and the facilities/washrooms were clean. Camping at
Emerald Lake
Seasonal Sites
Family Camping for RV’s and Tents
Fantastic Features
30 & 50 Amp Hydro Hook-ups
Minimum length of trailers for seasonal is 28’
Security Gates
Laundry Facilities
We take Trailers that are 10 years of age or less (*prerequisites required)
Seasonal Stay Memberships are entitled to premium inclusions and access to other privileges* not afforded to our short-term overnight or day visiting guests. Please inquire at the office for more information on these special inclusions and privileges. *some privileges are available at an additional cost. Please inquire for more info*
Seasonal Residents Only (May 1 – Oct 15) Includes full use of the waterpark + indoor pool (hot tub-adults-only. Full gym on-premises access will be an additional fee.
Creating Memories! Great Fun For Kids!
The water park is included with the Site fees for all campers registered to each site.
Enjoy camping close to nature
Emerald Lake offers you a great choice of activities to choose from
Modern, clean washrooms, with hot metered showers & laundry facilities
Easy access to Hwy 6 and Hwy 401
Family Camping only for RV’s and Tent Trailers, tents, buses & Conversion Vans etc.
Weekend entertainment during the summer season.
Our quoted prices always include HST added unless stated otherwise.
We accept all sizes of motor homes, travel trailers, hybrids, 5th wheels, tent trailers or pop-ups, and tents.
Camping & RV sites are for 2 adults and up to 2 children.
Extra adults mean  – Your children are 18+ years of age or your parents, staying in the same unit or tent with you. (Max 4 adults per site & a total of 6 people per site).
Only 1 RV or tent per site. A small pup tent will be allowed for the children. All vehicles must display their car passes on the dashboard at all times. Maximum of 2 vehicles per single site, space permitting.
Camping & RV Cancellation Policy.
There is a non-refundable reservation fee of $10 added to each reservation.
Payment is due in full at the time of booking.
Reservation payments may be made with cash, debit or credit card.
If we receive more than 21 days notice of cancellation, before the arrival date, the cancellation fee is $25.00 + tax for RV & tent camping ($50.00 + tax for cabins)
8-20 days; cancellation fee is $50.00 + tax  for RV & tent camping.
7 days or less – NO REFUNDS. NO EXCEPTIONS

5 Acre, Pollution Free, Spring Fed Lake
5 acre spring fed quarry (15-40 ft. deep), with high & low diving area. We have WATERTOYS in the quarry consisting of: 2 bubbles, rock-climbing wall, jungle joe, obstacle course and many more.
Over 200 Ft. Waterslide which empties into its own 4 ft. pool. All waterslide riders must be at least 4 ft. tall and able to swim to ladder unassisted. One single rider on the slide at a time; NO TANDEMS.
Large 2 ft. deep kiddie pool
3400 square ft. splash pad with rubber top and water-guns & a large mushroom
Kiddie Playground
18 regulation sand volleyball courts throughout the waterpark
Basketball Courts
Sandy beach area
Lots of picnic areas with picnic tables
Pavilions available to rent for your special functions
We do have lifeguards on duty however it is your responsibility as parents to watch your children. No one under the age of 12 is permitted to go to the waterpark without adult supervision.
