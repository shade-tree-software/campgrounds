Your stay, Your Way
From seasonal sites to monthly stays and winter camping, Rock Glen offers flexible options to fit your lifestyle. Enjoy great value, a welcoming community, and access to resort amenities all year long.
Book A Tour
Monthly Camping Options
Looking for flexibility? Our month-to-month sites are perfect for those who want to enjoy Rock Glen without committing to a full season.
Stay for a month at a time and experience everything the resort has to offer—whether it’s a spring getaway, summer escape, or fall retreat.
Rates:
May, June, September & October:   $1,200/month + tax
July & August:   $1,800/month + tax
Bookings are  one month at a time , giving you the freedom to plan your stay your way.
✨  Member Perk:  Members receive  50% off monthly rates , making it even more affordable to enjoy extended stays.
Escape to Rock Glen Family Resort RV Park
6-Month Summer Seasonal
Make the most of your summer at Rock Glen Family Resort! Enjoy your own private site all season long, with full access to our amenities, activities, and family-friendly atmosphere.
Whether it’s weekend getaways or extended stays, you’ll have your home away from home ready and waiting. Water service is available from May 1 to October 31, weather permitting.
Seasonal sites range from  $4,500-$5,000 , depending on location within the park, plus applicable taxes and fees.
9- Month seasonal
Looking for even more time at the resort? Our 10-month sites give you extended access to enjoy Rock Glen for most of the year—perfect for those who want to fully embrace the camping lifestyle.
We  recommend a park model trailer  for these sites to maximize comfort and make the most of your extended stay.
✨  Limited Availability:  We currently have  2 extended stay sites available —don’t miss your chance to secure your spot!
Extended stay sites range from  $8,000 – $9,000 , depending on location within the park, plus applicable taxes and fees.
❄️ Winter Camping (November 1 – April 30)
Enjoy a quieter, more relaxed side of Rock Glen during the winter season. It’s the perfect way to stay connected to the resort while avoiding the busy summer crowds.
Package Price:   $1,995 + tax & hydro
Water is not included on standard winter sites. If you require water service,  limited winter water sites may be available at an additional cost —please check with the resort for availability.
Your winter package includes access to our  indoor pool, play area, and resort amenities , so there’s still plenty to enjoy year-round.
⚠️ Please note: Winter camping is for  recreational use only  and is limited to  up to 4 days per week . Full-time living is not permitted under the winter seasonal package.
