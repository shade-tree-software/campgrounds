Enjoy the Excitement & serenity of Duck Lake
Discover seasonal getaway vacation fun at Duck Lake RV Resort, nestled on the south side of Duck Lake in the heart of the Kawarthas. This scenic, spring-fed lake, uniquely shaped like a duck, sets the stage for a memorable resort experience filled with family fun and outdoor adventure. With 500 seasonal sites for your RV or resort cottage, treed lots, a beach, and amenities including a water playground, sports court, playgrounds, and boat launch, there’s something for everyone to enjoy. Open from May 1 to October 31, weather permitting.
New for 2026:  Heated family pool, event pavilion, and beach volleyball.
