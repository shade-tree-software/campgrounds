Why Glamping Properties Are Canada's Hidden Real Estate Gold Mine.
Glamping properties are revolutionizing Canada's real estate landscape, with the industry projected to  grow at an impressive 14.7% annually  over the next five years. In fact, some property owners are achieving remarkable success, with case studies showing first-year profits of up to $345,000 from a single glamping operation.
We've seen firsthand how this unique investment opportunity has gained momentum since the pandemic as travellers increasingly seek distinctive outdoor experiences. Specifically, with  entry costs starting from just $25,000  for smaller geodesic domes, glamping offers an accessible path to real estate investment that combines high-quality accommodations with strategic business opportunities.
In this comprehensive guide, we'll show you why glamping properties represent Canada's hidden real estate gold mine and how you can position yourself to capitalize on this growing market.
The Booming Canadian Glamping Market  The Canadian glamping market shows remarkable potential, with projections indicating a   11.5% growth rate from 2025 to 2030    Additionally, the market demonstrates exceptional vigor in specific regions, with Canada experiencing a notable  20.3% CAGR during 2021-2027 .
Market research reveals diverse target demographics driving this growth, primarily including:    Millennials seeking luxury outdoor experiences
Baby boomers wanting comfortable nature encounters
Young families desiring turnkey camping solutions
Corporate groups looking for unique retreat venues  [3]
Meanwhile, seasonal occupancy rates paint an impressive picture, with yurts at federal and provincial campgrounds achieving   82% occupancy  , while ready-to-camp experiences reach  89% occupancy . Notably, the user base is expected to expand to  2.57 million by 2029 , with an average revenue per user reaching  CAD 211.37 .
The market's strength stems from shifting travel preferences and growing environmental awareness. Provincial parks and recreation areas across Canada offer diverse amenities, from basic washrooms to full-service sites with electricity and water . Furthermore, the post-COVID era has sparked increased interest in sustainable tourism, with adventure seekers actively searching for experiences that align with environmental consciousness.
The market remains notably underserved in Canada, particularly when compared to standard vacation rentals. This gap presents a unique opportunity for investors, especially considering that online sales are projected to generate  65% of total revenue by 2029 .
Investment Requirements and Setup  Starting a glamping business requires strategic planning around three core elements: land acquisition, structure selection, and proper permits. Initially, land represents the largest investment variable, with options ranging from outright purchase to creative arrangements like land sharing or renting.
For structure selection, costs vary significantly based on accommodation type. Here's a breakdown of investment ranges:
Safari tents: Starting from CAD 56,966
Luxury lodge tents: Starting from CAD 85,990
Geodesic domes: CAD 58,360 - CAD 80,867
Luxury RV (Destination Model) from CAD 120,000
Consequently, permit requirements demand careful attention. Most rural properties fall under municipal jurisdiction, requiring formal site plans and drawings before construction. Moreover, zoning regulations vary between counties, making local research essential. A conditional use permit might be necessary if the land isn't zoned for glamping.
The setup process primarily focuses on practical considerations. Properties need proper sewage systems, flat surfaces for tent placement, and appropriate utility connections  [7] . Furthermore, eco-conscious choices in materials and sustainable practices can enhance appeal while minimizing environmental impact.
For those seeking an entry point, bell tents offer the lowest initial investment, making them ideal for testing the market. Alternatively, more substantial structures like domes provide higher returns on investment, though they may face additional zoning challenges.
Maximizing Revenue Through Strategic Operations  Successful glamping operations demand strategic pricing and revenue management. Properties achieving  60% occupancy  with rates around  CAD 278.67 per night  can recoup their investment within three years  [9] . Rather than relying solely on accommodation fees, successful operators diversify their income through multiple revenue streams:
Luxury amenities and experiences
Seasonal event packages
Corporate retreat offerings
Eco-friendly activity programs
Accordingly, the target demographic spans across age groups, with  18-32-year-olds accounting for 43% of revenue , and the  33-50 age group growing at 11% annually  . Altogether, families comprise  46% of glamping guests , primarily seeking memorable experiences with modern comforts.
​
Strategic seasonal operations generally yield the highest returns. Properties operating from May to October, with weekend availability during off-peak months, achieve  51% first-year occupancy , increasing to  56% by year three . Hence, a well-managed 10-unit operation can attract  over 2,000 guests annually .
Technology integration undeniably enhances operational efficiency. Smart property management systems automate bookings and maintenance schedules. Soon, contactless technologies and virtual concierge services will become standard offerings, ensuring seamless guest experiences while optimizing staff resources.
Pricing strategies should certainly reflect market positioning. With luxury tents commanding  CAD 151.88 to CAD 416.61 per night , operators must balance competitive rates with value-added services. Otherwise, consider implementing dynamic pricing models that adjust for peak seasons and special events.
Conclusion  Glamping properties represent a remarkable investment opportunity in Canada's evolving real estate market. Above all, the projected 14.7% annual growth rate, combined with first-year profits reaching $345,000, demonstrates the substantial potential for savvy investors.
The accessibility of this market stands out, with entry costs starting at just $50,000 for quality structures like geodesic domes. As a result, both seasoned real estate investors and newcomers can participate in this growing sector while maintaining full control over their investment scale and pace.
Altogether, glamping offers a unique combination of high-end accommodations and strategic business opportunities. The numbers tell a compelling story - with 60% occupancy rates at $278.67 per night, investors can recover their initial investment within three years while building a sustainable, future-proof business model.
​
Canadian glamping properties blend luxury comfort with natural experiences, creating an unmatchable investment proposition in today's market. Whether you're considering a small-scale bell tent operation or a full-service luxury dome and RV resort, the glamping sector presents an excellent opportunity to be part of Canada's next big real estate

Features and Amenities of Luxury Safari Tents:
​
Luxury safari tents are designed with the utmost attention to detail, providing guests with an exceptional level of comfort and style. Each tent typically features spacious interiors that are thoughtfully decorated to enhance the natural beauty surrounding it. High-quality furnishings, plush bedding, and elegant décor create an inviting atmosphere where guests can unwind after a day of adventure.
In addition to aesthetic appeal, these tents often come equipped with modern amenities that cater to the needs of discerning travelers. En-suite bathrooms are a common feature, allowing guests to enjoy privacy and convenience without sacrificing the outdoor experience. Many tents also include private decks or patios, perfect for enjoying meals or simply soaking in the stunning views of the wilderness.
Beyond the tent itself, eco resorts often provide a range of communal amenities that enhance the guest experience. pool overlooking the landscape, gourmet dining options featuring locally sourced ingredients, and wellness activities such as yoga or spa treatments are just a few examples of the indulgences available. This combination of luxury and immersion in nature ensures that your stay is both relaxing and invigorating.
