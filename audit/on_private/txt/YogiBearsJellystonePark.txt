About Jellystone Park™ Niagara Falls
Yogi Bear's Jellystone Park™   Niagara Falls  is the closest campground to Niagara Falls.    (just 3.5 miles away from the Falls).  There is private   shuttle bus service     to the Falls close to all of the fun, plus Jellystone Park™ Niagara offers tour   packages to attractions like Niagara City Cruises, Journey Behind   the Falls and more! Spend the day on Clifton Hill, hiking along the gorge,   biking the trails to the some of Niagara’s finest Wineries. Explore all that   Niagara Falls has to offer!
But there’s so much to do at the Camp-Resort, you just may not want to ever leave.

About The Park
When you visit  Jellystone Park ™  Niagara Falls  you, your family and friends can enjoy all of the onsite amenities that include the Water Zone, a heated swimming pool, a giant water slide, playground jumping pillow and more!

All Inclusive Water Zone
The Water Zone is currently closed for the 2026 season. Our heated pools is still open until September 20th,2026
Jellystone Park™ Niagara Falls  is home of the “world’s tallest inflatable Waterslide". It’s all part of the all-inclusive Water Zone, a perfect wet and wild way to cool off plus experience the ride of your lifetime. At 34 feet tall, this slide is something you don’t want to miss while you are here at the park! The Water Zone also includes a large heated swimming pool, and the new Aqua Playground with 4 slides, spray features and dumping bucket. All included FREE with every registered campsite guest.   It's lots of fun for tall ages!

CLOSED  7pm
Jellystone Park™ Niagara Falls Shuttle Bus
Our private shuttle bus will take your directly from the campground   to Table Rock Centre  (right at the brink of the falls) .The Table Rock   complex is the perfect spot to start your sightseeing. It is the location   of the welcome centre where you can set up your VIP timed ticketing   for the Falls Pass, and is also the location of Journey Behind   the Falls! The WEGO bus will also pick you up   at this location and take you north along the river to the White Water   Walk, Niagara City Cruises, Butterfly Conservatory and more!
The Shuttle Bus is no longer running for the 2026 Season
