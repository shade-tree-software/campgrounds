WELCOME TO DESERT LAKE FAMILY RESORT
Desert Lake Family Resort is a family run campground for families just like yours. In addition to our beautiful and spacious campsites, we offer accommodations and special packages for group events such as family reunions, weddings, and corporate getaways.
Desert Lake Family Resort is a modern vacation facility located in the Land O’Lakes Region on the south shore of Desert Lake. A swimming beach, boat launch, docks and a host of other amenities to are available to make your vacation fun-filled and worry free
​
It’s a short drive to historic Kingston, the 1000 Islands and many other pristine lakes. A 10 minute drive takes you to Frontenac Provincial Park. Hiking, canoeing, cross-country ski routes and unspoiled scenery await you. Rivendell Golf Club offers 18 holes just 15 minutes away.

CAMPING
We offer seasonal RV site,shorter stay tenting and RV sites with water and electric service.  All campsites have a fire pit and picnic table and are near our clean and well maintained washroom facilities.
​    ​
CAMPSITE FEATURES
All camping sites have full access to all park amenities:
Picnic Table and fire pit
Regular scheduled theme weekend events
Shallow sandy swimming area
2 well equipped play areas
Horseshoes and shuffleboard
Boat launch and free lake map provided
Our store includes snack bar, groceries, souvenirs and supplies
Gas can be purchased in Verona or Sydenham
One family per site policy applies
HST applies to camping rates
Free Dump Station or midweek mobile pump out for a fee
Pets welcome provided you follow our pet policy
Desert Lake is a FAMILY oriented campgrounds
Quiet hours after 11 pm weeknights and midnight on weekends with on-site security

RESERVATIONs
Here is some general reservation information that you will need to know, including check-in and check-out times and additional requirements for our accommodations.
​
CHECK IN TIME (Accommodations): 3:00 pm
​
CHECK OUT TIME (Accommodations): 11:00 am
​
CHECK IN/CHECK OUT TIME (Camping): 2:00 pm
​
​
HST applies on accommodations and camping.
Reservations accepted but specific suites/sites are not guaranteed.
Handicap Accessible suites available upon request.
Linens are not included with the room rate. We do have laundry facilities on site if you wish to bring your own.
Desert Lake Resort is a FAMILY campground. We have quiet hours after 11 pm, midnight on weekends, and we have roaming security on-site.
Pets allowed only in select units  .
