Cottages
Book a Cottage Today!
At Sunny Hill you have access to everything you need to ensure a great and memorable vacation as well as amenities to keep you and your family comfortable.
​
All of our cottages have a water view and most are located right on the shoreline of Bark Lake.  Cottages are fully furnished for self-catering with a BBQ and propane supplied.  Each cottage has its own fire pit with chairs and is stocked daily with firewood for campfires.
​
To reserve or to check availability, visit our
Online Reservation System, or give us a call at 613-756-2640.

Camping
Reserve your Spot!
We are located on the east shore of Bark Lake and have a large, treed camping site area.  Campers have full access to Bark Lake, our beach, our docks, and the spectacular views with amazing sunsets.
All sites have a fire pit and picnic table.  A restroom with hot showers and flush toilets is open to all campers camping at Sunny Hill Resort.
Camping sites are 30 amp full-service and campers have access to our indoor saltwater pool, hot tub, and rec hall.

Camping at Sunny Hill
Located on the east shore of Bark Lake, Sunny Hill Resort has a large full-service camping site area.
All sites are full-service and you are welcome to bring your
Motor-home, Trailer, RV, or Camper Van.
NOTE - we do not accommodate tents.
​
Camping sites are reserved for 2 adults and any minor children living at home with full access to Bark Lake, our beach, our docks, and the spectacular views and sunsets.
Book Now
Seasonal Full Service Camp Sites
Open from the second week of May until Thanksgiving weekend in Oct!
For many, Sunny Hill Resort is a home away from home for the summer months in Ontario.  Becoming one of our seasonal campers here at Sunny Hill Resort allows you to get away from the city and enjoy the country and lakeside without the high costs and maintenance of your own waterfront cottage.  We offer full-service seasonal camp sites with 30 amp electrical, water, and sewer services.  We have lake view lots as well as some water front lots.  Our seasonal campers also have the option to rent their own private dock on Bark Lake along with full access to Sunny Hill Resorts boat launch.
To inquire about our seasonal camp site availability, please call 613-756-2640, or email us at   info@sunnyhillresort.com  .
