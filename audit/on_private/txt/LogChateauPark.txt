Welcome to Log Chateau Park.
Family-owned and operated since 1967, Log Chateau Park spans 320 acres in the scenic Kawartha Lakes. We offer 240 seasonal and 80 transient campsites with hydro, water, and sewer options, plus 30/50 amp trailer hookups. Amenities include ATV trails, a boat launch, swimming pool, hot tub, sauna, outdoor fitness center, and a jumping pillow. Enjoy 8 cabins in Basic, Deluxe, or Premium styles, plus a barn store with an arcade, a food truck, and our new 80x100 indoor pavilion with pickleball and basketball courts. Proudly run by Lucas and Stephanie Lowell, we welcome families to relax, play, and make lasting memories.
Accommodations & Amenities
Enjoy your stay in one of the many choices Log Chateau has to offer!
Camping Cabins
We now offer 8 different camping cabins in three different styles. Enjoy your stay with ease and comfort.
Trailer Camping
We have pull through trailer sites as well as 3 full hook up sites. We offer 30 to 50 amp hydro services with water. There is an onsite dump station as well.
Tent Sites
We offer treed tent sites for the outdoor enthusiasts. We have 4 comfort stations available as well as laundry facilities for your convenience.
Banquet Barn
We have a large Banquet Barn which has a store, dance hall with dances for both kids and adults, pool table and arcade games. Any events that need to take place indoors when it rains. Crafts for the kids. Card games for the adults.
Bring your ATV
Make sure you bring your ATV as our Park is right on the trail that will connect you to miles of beautiful scenery to explore! Click on  Victoria Rail Trail  for more information and maps on the trail that runs through the park.
Fish & Boat
If you enjoy fishing or boating we offer a boat launch and dock space if you wish to leave your boat in the water.  The river offers full access to Cameron Lake  and the Trent-Severn Waterway.  The area is known for bass, walleye and muskie fishing.
Swimming
We have a pool, hot tub and sauna. The pool area is also a free wifi hotspot. Prefer to swim in the river? Check out our small sandy beach on the Burnt River.
Outdoor Activities
For our sports enthusiasts there's an Outdoor Fitness Centre, jumping pillow, beach volleyball, basketball, tennis court and horseshoe pits.
& So Much More…
We have a food truck and new 80×10 enclosed pavillion with indoor pickleball and basketball court. In case it rains or when you need shade on those hot days we have a covered picnic area.  There's also multiple playgrounds to keep the kids occupied.
