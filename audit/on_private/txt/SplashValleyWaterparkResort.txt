at Splash Valley Waterpark & Resort
Your go-to family vacation spot nestled on the pristine shores of Astrolabe Lake in Eastern Ontario! Just 1.5 hours from Ottawa and conveniently located east of Cobden along Highway 17, our resort offers on-site accommodations and a ton of exciting activities for the whole family to enjoy.
Get ready for an amazing adventure that begins with the ultimate splash! Our resort is a haven for water enthusiasts, boasting a variety of thrilling summer activities that will make your family vacation truly unforgettable.
Waterpark Extravaganza
Get ready to MAKE A SPLASH and dive into the excitement with our thrilling water slides, paddleboats and splash pad – where there’s always a new adventure waiting to happen.
Learn More
Floating Water Playground
At Splash Valley, we take fun to the next level with a wide array of exhilarating features designed to delight thrill-seekers of all ages on our floating water playground.
Learn More
Other Things to Do
Soak up the sun, build a sandcastle or enjoy a lively game of volleyball with new friends, all on our newly expanded beachfront offering 450 feet of shoreline  boasting large umbrellas and lounge chairs so you can relax and enjoy.
Learn More
Golf with a Scenic Backdrop
The Oaks of Cobden Golf Club (located right next door to the resort) provides a par 71 golf course with stunning views of the surrounding landscape. A picturesque backdrop for golf enthusiasts seeking a perfect swing.
Learn More
Accommodations that Wow
Choose from a range of accommodations that suit your family’s needs, from RV sites and rentals to cedar cabins, 1 bedroom suites, and luxurious 2-bedroom villas. Prime RV sites, cedar cabins, and villas all offer breathtaking views of the lake, ensuring your stay is as picturesque as it is comfortable.

Stay n’ Play with us
Stay at Splash Valley Waterpark & Resort and play a round at the scenic Oaks of Cobden, located just minutes away.

RV Park & Campground
Create Memories – Go Camping!
Our RV Park & Campground is a popular destination for families looking to spend some quality time together. Most RV sites offer 30amp service, but there are also 9 50 amp sites available. RV sites are suitable for travel trailers, 5th wheels & Motorhomes.  Sites are also available for tent trailers. RV sites include a fire pit and picnic table. Many days were fully booked last season and we strongly recommend booking early to avoid disappointment.
There are a variety of tent sites available at the park. Available tent sites include sites that overlook our beautiful spring-fed lake along with sites a little more secluded in a mature hardwood forest. All tent sites are suitable for 2 or 3 smaller tents or 1 larger tent. Each site includes a fire pit and picnic table.
Pets are welcome on our tent and RV campsites.

Campground Policies
RATES & OCCUPANCY
Rates reflect a maximum depending on site or accommodation type. Additional fees apply if you exceed the maximum occupancy.
Overnight guests are entitled to a discount at our Waterpark. Please let the Admissions staff know you are an overnight guest when purchasing passes. Free Wifi hotspots available in RV Park & Waterpark.
DAY GUESTS
Visiting day guests pay general admission rates upon arrival at the ADMISSIONS BUILDING. Visitors must leave by 11 pm.
Visitors must park in main parking lot.
TENT SITES
Maximum of 2 sleeping tents, 1 dining tent, and 1 vehicle.
PETS
Pets must be contained or leashed on your site.
Pets are not permitted in the Waterpark, in the lake, or in any buildings at any time.
You are responsible for your pet’s behaviour. Please keep pets quiet to be considerate of other guests.
Clean up after pets.
Unruly pets must be removed from the property upon request.
CONDUCT
Guests are expected to behave responsibly with regard to other guests, staff, animals and property.
Firearms, explosives, weapons and fireworks are strictly prohibited. Anywhere. Anytime.
Recreational Drug use prohibited. Anywhere. Anytime!
Alcoholic beverages are not to be consumed in common areas such as roadways, play areas, beach areas, trails, and public shelters/buildings. Guests who appear to be intoxicated are subject to immediate removal from the property without refund.
Smoking is prohibited in buildings, Waterpark, beach and eating areas.
Foul language will not be tolerated.
Leave vegetation intact. Do not debark, damage, or remove trees and flowers.
Leave wildlife other than fish in tact. Please use a catch and release approach when fishing. Do not poach endangered species, specifically turtles. Carry current fishing licence.
MOTORIZED VEHICLE OPERATION
Operate only licenced motor vehicles on roads and in parking areas. Dirt bikes, ATV’s, mopeds, kid’s electric jeeps, etc. are not permitted.
Park speed limit is  9km/hr.
Motorized boats are not permitted on our beautiful spring fed lake.
WATERPARK AND LAKE
Lifejackets are required on watercraft.
We do not permit the following at the waterfront:
Motor boats
Pets
Glass containers
Washing clothing or dishes
Alcoholic beverages
CAMPFIRES
Obey fire ban when posted.
Contain campfires within the designated fire pit.
Attend your fire at all times and extinguish before retiring.
Firewood is available at the ADMISSIONS BUILDING. Outside firewood is not permitted. Please visit Canada Food Inspection Agency website at inspection.ca for further information regarding moving firewood and associated penalties.
GARBAGE
Place garbage and recycling in the disposal on the main road before exiting the park.
Place plastic bottles and metal cans in the large recycling bins.
$25 will be charged to your credit/debit card for garbage left on your site after your departure.
SURVEILLANCE
We employ video surveillance in some public areas to detect, deter, and assist in investigating criminal activity.
Quiet hours are from 11pm until 7am
Check-in Time: After 4PM & Check-Out Time: Before 11AM
For a $20 fee, late check-out is offered for Campsites & RV sites provided your site is not booked. Please register for late check-out before 10am.

Book a Seasonal RV Site
Setting up your trailer on a seasonal site at Splash Valley is the perfect way to enjoy summer with the family. All of our seasonal sites have 30 amp electrical service, along with water and septic hook-up. Unlike many campgrounds, our seasonal fee includes hydro.
We have seasonal sites available.  All new seasonal campers must have a newer RV, that is no more than 10 years old.
Our camping season runs from Friday of the Victoria Day long weekend until Monday of the Canadian Thanksgiving weekend.
