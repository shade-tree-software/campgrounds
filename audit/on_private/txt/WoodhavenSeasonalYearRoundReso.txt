Welcome to Woodhaven!
Located in Lambton Shores, Ontario
At Woodhaven our goal is simple. Relaxed camping, clean facilities, and friendly service.                                 Guests enjoy a large outdoor pool, mini-golf, playgrounds, sports courts, shuffleboard, horseshoes, a bouncy-pillow vibe of fun, and a full schedule of family-friendly events. The resort sits just minutes from sandy Ipperwash Beach on Lake Huron, and stays close to shops, nearby towns, and summer attractions. Settle in and enjoy time with friends and family, long beach days, easy evenings, and warm weekends together.
