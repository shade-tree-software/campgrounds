Pack up the Tent, Trailer or Boat and Come and Enjoy the Finest in Ontario Camping Vacations!
Sturgeon Woods truly is ‘Ontario Camping at its Best’… an Ontario campground in the most southern part of Canada’s mainland with only a short boat or ferry ride from Pelee Island Ontario, the most southern point in Canada.
FAMILY OWNED and OPERATED for OVER 25 YEARS!
Ontario Camping & Marina  – Sturgeon Woods    Campground and Marina    is located in Southwestern Ontario on Lake Erie, minutes from the town of Kingsville and Wheatley. Our campground is situated on the Point Pelee peninsula just 1 km from Point Pelee National Park, 5 minutes from Leamington and only 45 minutes from Windsor and Detroit Casinos. Sturgeon Woods… the perfect Ontario    camping location    for the whole family!
The  Point Pelee National Park  in Ontario, is world famous for bird and butterfly watching, Ontario camping, excellent walleye and bass fishing. The Pelee Passage in Lake Erie is just minutes from our marina and has become a Mecca for Ontario camping, scuba diving and famous for its many sunken ships. Close to golfing, shopping, swimming, go-carting and more!

Campground & Marina
Fresh air, family fun, boating and the great outdoors, all in one location. Spend the night or stay for the season!
Tents and trailers…  Sturgeon Woods has over 400 campsites on 57 acres of oak and maple forest. Grassy, treed areas for tents; camping cabins; and full hook-up for big trailers. All of the sites include a picnic table and fire pit. Enjoy the great amenities the park has to offer with lots of scheduled activities for the whole family; venture out and explore the area attractions nearby; or just sit back and relax. Whatever you decide to do, you’ll have a wonderful and memorable vacation!
Cabins…  Try a cozy cabin and enjoy the fun of camping out, with the security & comfort of sleeping indoors. Equipped with bunk beds, fridge, air conditioning, table, chairs, fire ring and picnic table. NO plumbing, but washrooms and showers are near. Sleeps 2 adults and 3 children. Two nights minimum required.
Full Service Cottages…  for those who prefer the comforts of home, choose from two or three bedroom fully serviced cottages, that are complete with kitchen, family room, bathroom and even air conditioning. Also included are a fire ring and picnic table. Some cooking utensils and supplies are provided, but be sure to bring your own bedding and linens as they are not supplied. Comfortable accommodations that are well maintained, clean, private and secure. One week minimum required. Sorry, but there are no pets allowed in the rental units.
$100 refundable security deposit for all cabin, trailer and cottage rentals required upon arrival.
Marina…  If you’re planning to bring along your boat, Sturgeon Woods provides ample docking facilities complete with a boat launch. The Pelee Passage in Lake Erie is just minutes from the marina and has become a Mecca for Ontario camping, scuba diving and famous for its many sunken ships. Enjoy the close proximity of the many area boating attractions, as well as all the recreation facilities and activities the campground has to offer.
