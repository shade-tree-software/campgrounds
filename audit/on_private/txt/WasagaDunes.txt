Wasaga Dunes
Tiny, Ontario
​​​​​​​Surrounded by trees, sand dunes and wide open spaces, Wasaga Dunes’ spacious full-service seasonal sites and singular Overnight RV Site are perfect for those looking to get in touch with nature.  Wasaga Dunes RV Resort is located just a few minutes outside of Wasaga Beach, within the heart of Ontario’s prime cottage country. Nestled in a mature forest setting, Wasaga Dunes is a resort equipped to meet your modern convenience needs for dedicated RVers. Wasaga Dunes’ spacious full-service seasonal sites and sigular Overnight RV Site are perfect for those looking to get in touch with nature.
