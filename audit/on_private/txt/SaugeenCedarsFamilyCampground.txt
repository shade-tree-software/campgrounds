NATURAL BEAUTY ALONG THE SAUGEEN RIVER!
Welcome to Saugeen Cedars Family Campground!
Experience a relaxed environment where you can unwind and enjoy the beauty of nature. Our spacious sites cater to your preferences, whether you desire treed, full sun, or riverfront locations.
Stay Connected:
FREE WIFI  Hotspots available, so you can work while you're away.
For the Kids:
Water Slide
Playground
Plenty of spaces to ride their bikes
Facilities:
Clean, NEWLY RENOVATED Main Washrooms:  Equipped with FREE hot showers, new washer, and dryer.
Souvenir & Gift Shop:  Available when you check in or at the River's Edge Snack Shack .
Security:  State-of-the-art control gate and video surveillance.
Food & Treats:
River's Edge Snack Shack:  Open Thursday to Sunday, offering candy, pop, ice cream, slushies, nachos, fries, onion rings, burgers, sausages, and more.
Activities:
Inner Tubing
Canoeing
Kayaking
Fishing
Swimming and playing on the Floating Dock and Water Slide  in the pristine waters of the Saugeen River.

Welcome to the 2026 season at Saugeen Cedars Family Campground!
Camping Season:  April to October
New This Year:
Back-In Sites:  New back-in sites for your convenience.
Water Hook-Ups:  Added to non-serviced sites.
Serviced Group Site:  A new serviced group site is now available.
We look forward to seeing you and hope you have a fantastic stay! If there's anything else you need to know, feel free to ask.
Come and Enjoy Your Stay
We are a seasonal park with limited sites for weekend and transient campers, but we continue to look forward to welcoming you for an enjoyable stay at Saugeen Cedars Family Campground.
Services
Electrical/Water Hook-Ups:
30amp electrical/water
Sites:
Over 200 Sites
2 serviced group sites and 1 non-serviced group site
13 back-in sites with water hook-ups (no hydro), some are waterfront
Water:
Tap water is potable—safe to drink and tested regularly
Pavilion:
Available for rental for family reunions, weddings, or casual gatherings
New covered shelter and community firepit area—use anytime
We are an Eco-park
We are officially labelled an Eco Park - 30% of our electricity needs are supplied by 36 solar panels.
Shuttle bus for Tubing
Our Complimentary Shuttle Bus takes you & your tubes, canoes, or kayaks up-river to your choice of trip length – you float back into the park.
Free Wifi
Our WiFi has been an issue - due mostly from the mature trees on the property.  We offer free WiFi hotspots are the shelters by the Snack Shack & Pavilion.
