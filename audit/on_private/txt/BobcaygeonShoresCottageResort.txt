Everything you need for a great summer vacation
Nestled along the serene shores of Pigeon Lake, welcome to Bobcaygeon Shores Cottage Resort, the seamless integration of Lilac Lodge and Green Acres, now unified under one name as of 2024.
Home to 244 tranquil sites for lakeside cottage and RV ownership, Bobcaygeon Shores is where relaxation meets recreation. Whether you’re looking to own or simply rent a resort cottage for a memorable Kawartha Lakes getaway, the resort offers something for everyone. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including brand new docks introduced in 2022, a new multi-sports court added in 2023, an inground heated pool, on-site café, and off-leash dog park, with even more exciting enhancements still to come.
Whether you’re unwinding from the second week of May through Thanksgiving weekend or making the most of the resort’s activities and amenities, Bobcaygeon Shores is the perfect place to relax, reconnect and make lasting memories.
We can’t wait to welcome you to Kawartha Lakes at Bobcaygeon Shores Cottage Resort!
