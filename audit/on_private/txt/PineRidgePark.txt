Year Long Cabin Rentals
Pine Ridge Park offers long-term cabin rental in Petawawa during the winter season. Book your stay in one of our modern and comfortable cabins providing fast Wi-Fi connection and easy access to snowmobile trails in the heart of the Ottawa Valley.
Laundry facilities on-site, recreation room with pool table, games, and more. Enjoy our water views, wood stoves, and modern 4-piece bathroom facilities. We have everything to make your getaway unforgettable. Contact us now for our availability!

In the Heart of the Ottawa Valley
Located on the Ottawa River, our family-friendly campground offers a variety of ways for you to enjoy time and make lasting memories with friends and family! From cozy rustic cabins to spacious trailer sites, we have everything you need for a comfortable, relaxing, and fun-filled time at our campground.
We’re happy to welcome guests for nightly, weekly, or seasonal stays.
