Tucked along the scenic Grand River in Cayuga, Ontario, Bruce’s Landing RV Resort offers a welcoming riverside escape where families, couples, and outdoor enthusiasts can relax and reconnect with nature. Located in beautiful Haldimand County, this seasonal resort blends peaceful riverfront surroundings with a friendly community atmosphere and plenty of activities for guests of all ages.
At Bruce’s Landing, every day brings something to enjoy. Spend your mornings fishing or boating on the Grand River, explore nearby trails and local attractions, or simply unwind at your RV site while taking in the calm, natural setting. The resort features convenient amenities including washroom and shower facilities, an on-site store, children’s play areas, a recreation centre, and a boat launch, making it easy to enjoy both relaxation and adventure.
Guests can choose from hydro and non-hydro RV sites, providing flexibility for a variety of camping styles. With a welcoming atmosphere, planned activities, and pet-friendly accommodations, Bruce’s Landing truly becomes a place where guests return year after year.
Whether you’re looking to enjoy quiet days by the water, take part in fun community events, or explore the charming town of Cayuga and the surrounding region, Bruce’s Landing RV Resort is the perfect home away from home for unforgettable summers.
