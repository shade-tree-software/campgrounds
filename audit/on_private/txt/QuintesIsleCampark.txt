Welcome to Quinte's Isle Campark
Welcome to     Quinte’s Isle Campark   , a   family-owned and operated campground     in Cherry Valley, proudly run by the   Ward Family   since 1983.  Our park offers the perfect blend of relaxation, fun, and stunning Lake Ontario views. Enjoy a meal at our Dockside Grill & Chill overlooking the Pebble Beach Marina, take a dip in our large pool and splash pad, or explore the grounds with our e-bike and golf cart rentals. With two playgrounds, sport courts, mini-golf, and a variety of scheduled activities and live music, there’s always something for everyone. Our complimentary rural WiFi keeps you connected, while our EV charging station, laundry facilities, and on-site stores add convenience. Whether camping in a tent or bringing a large rig, we have sites of all sizes — including some with breathtaking lakefront views. If you’re looking for a more permanent vacation spot, check out our trailer and cottager sales, or try a cottage rental before you buy.
The   Ward Family     takes pride in creating a   warm, welcoming environment for families and campers of all ages  ,  and we look forward to making your visit truly memorable!

Premium Pebble Beach Pull Thrus
These are fully serviced premium  “Big Rig” pull thru sites located on Lake Ontario. They come with water, sewer and electricity (30 or 50 amp).  The sites are very large (75 feet long) and sunny. On Pebble Beach there is a store, multi-sport court, washrooms with showers, two pools and Dockside Grill & Chill restaurant. One pool is a wading pool with sprinklers. The other pool has a rock climbing wall cascading into the pool and basketball nets.
Pull Thrus
These are fully serviced, easy on, easy off, pull thru sites. These sites are located in the main area of the park, near the playground, recreational hall, store, canteen, pool and splash pad. Located nearby, is a comfort station, with washrooms and showers. This is approximately 2km away from the Lake Ontario waterfront. The sites are large and offer varying amounts of trees.
Premium Serviced Sites
These are premium fully serviced back in sites located in the main area of the park.  They come with water, sewer and electricity (30 or 50 amp). Close by is   the playground, recreational hall, store, canteen, washrooms, showers, pool and splash pad.  This is approximately 2km away from the Lake Ontario waterfront. These sites are sunny and have trees.
Serviced Sites
These are fully serviced back in sites located in the main area of the park, near the playground, recreational hall, store, canteen, washrooms, showers, pool and splash pad. This is approximately 2km away from the Lake Ontario waterfront. These sites are sunny and have trees.
Unserviced Sites
These sites are non-serviced tenting or trailer sites, located in the main area of the park, near the playground, recreational hall, store, canteen, pool and splash pad. This is approximately 2km away from the Lake Ontario waterfront. They all have access to communal drinking water taps and close to the comfort station where you have access to washrooms and showers. If you reserve, you are guaranteed a site without a specific site number.  When you arrive, you will be directed to the camping area, and you may then choose a site with a picnic table and a fire pot.
