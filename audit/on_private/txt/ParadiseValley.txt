Welcome to Paradise Valley!
Located in Plympton-Wyoming, Ontario
At Paradise Valley, our goal stays simple. Relaxed camping, clean spaces, and friendly service.
Guests enjoy two pools, a sandy beach on Lake Huron, a pond, mini golf, disc golf, playgrounds, a bouncy pillow, and weekend activities through the season.
The campground sits close to local beaches, with quick access to shops, dining, and family outings.
Settle in and enjoy a place where families slow down, feel at ease, and spend easy days together.

Reservations
Making a reservation and planning your vacation with Paradise Valley is simple. We are excited to announce the launch of our new and improved online system RMS.
Enjoy all that Paradise Valley has to offer.  Please check our Rates and Rentals and Sales sections for available seasonal, sales or nightly rentals.  We also encourage you to add you name to our waiting list and we will contact you when a site becomes available.  We currently do have sites available for the 2025 season.
Book Now!
Please note our reservations guidelines:
Maximum Persons Per Site:  maximum number of persons per site is four (4), except for family’s (parents/guardians and dependent children under 18).  Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
Maximum Vehicles Per Site:  maximum number of vehicles allowed per site is one (1)
Maximum Camping Units Per Site:  maximum number of camping units per site is one (1)
Check in time: 2 pm
Check out time: 11 am
For further Reservation information, please contact us:
Telephone: 519-899-4080
E-mail:  paradisevalley@killamproperties.com
Deposit Policy:
For advance reservations, a deposit of 50% of the stay is required at time of booking, major credit cards accepted. Reservations made within 7 days of arrival date require full stay deposit. Deposit may also be in the form of money orders or cheques, but reservation is not guaranteed until funds have cleared. Please do not send cash in the mail. Balance of stay is due in full 7 days prior to arrival date.
Cancellation Policy:
We are not able to refund for inclement weather. Non-cancellation or failure to show constitutes forfeiture. Unfortunately, we cannot guarantee the weather, therefore once checked in, you are responsible for the entire length of stay you reserved.
To receive a  full refund  of your camping fees, less a $50. administration fee,  cancellations must be made more than 14 days prior to your originally scheduled arrival date .
Cancellations made between 14 days and before 48 hours in advance of your arrival will be charged  one-nights’ stay.
No refunds  for cancellations received within 48 hours of your arrival date.
Camping fee refunds are applied to the credit card used in the original booking.
