Welcome to ray's cottages
Ray’s Cottages is a family and pet-friendly campground and cottage resort located on the shores of West Lake in  Prince Edward County, Ontario . Our four-acre lakeside property is close to the villages of Wellington and Bloomfield, and just minutes from Sandbanks Provincial Park and North Beach. We offer lakeside cottages, glamping tents, and campsites in a relaxed setting where families can slow down and enjoy time together by the water.
Cottages
Our lakeside cottages offer a comfortable stay for families and couples looking to enjoy Prince Edward County at a relaxed pace. Each cottage provides indoor comfort with easy access to West Lake and the shared amenities of the resort.
Glamping Tents
Our glamping tents combine the feel of camping with added comfort and convenience. A great option for guests who enjoy being outdoors but prefer a ready-made setup.
Overnight Camping
Our overnight camping sites offer a traditional, non-serviced camping experience for tents and small trailers. Campers have access to shared washrooms, showers, and resort amenities close to the lake.
Seasonal Trailer Sites
Our seasonal trailer sites provide a summer home base for guests who enjoy returning year after year. Seasonal campers become part of a relaxed lakeside community with access to resort amenities.

Explore Prince Edward County
Discover the charm of   Prince Edward County  , known for its beaches, wineries, local food, outdoor trails, and small-town villages. From sandy shores to scenic cycling routes, there is something to explore in every season.
Explore Culture
Experience the creative and culinary side of Prince Edward County, from local wineries and breweries to artist studios, markets, and standout restaurants.
Explore on Your Bike
We are a 3-minute ride to the   Millennium Trail   with 46 kms of rejuvenated, multi use path. It offers more than 30 areas to hop on and off and enjoy County scenery or local businesses.
Explore the Lake
Enjoy direct access to West Lake with our on-site boat launch and parking, perfect for fishing and time on the water. For a change of pace, the famous sand dunes are a straight drive away for a classic County beach day.
Explore the Area
ACTIVITIES FOR EVERYONE
ENJOY THE LAKE
Explore the shores of West lake in a kayak, canoe or on a paddleboard all available for rental. Relax on one of our man-made beaches, or catch some fish off the dock.
Play Some Games
Come by and visit our office staff to borrow a lawn game or use the DVD lending library on a rainy day.
RELAX BY THE FIRE
Spend evenings with family around your personal bonfire pit or join in with a larger crowd at the communal bonfire area.

Camping
Un-Serviced Campsites:
Our un-serviced campsites are located along the cove waterfront, with most sites offering water views. Sites 7 and 8 are directly lakeside. Each campsite accommodates one sleeping tent or one trailer / motorhome measuring 25 feet or under, along with one vehicle. Every site includes a picnic table and fire pit, and pets are welcome with a maximum of two per site.
Communal washrooms with shower facilities, a dishwashing station, charging station for electronic devices, and potable water taps are located within close proximity to all campsites.
Important to Know
All campsites are  un-serviced . There is   no   hydro and   no   individual water hookups. Potable water is available on site for filling trailer holding tanks. There is   no   dumping station on the property, and  generators are not permitted .
Information:
Check in 1pm, Check out 11am.
All camping guests must check in prior to 9 pm.
Parking for  one vehicle  is included
High Season: Two night minimum to pre-book sites on a weekend.
Generators are not permitted.
Campsites do not have electricity and there are no sewage dumping facilities on the property
For more detailed information about reservations and camping rules and regulations download:  COTTAGE RENTAL & TRANSIENT CAMPING RULES & REGULATIONS
