O’Reilly Lake Resort offers seasonal owners and guests 3,500 feet of beautiful waterfront, loads of family-friendly facilities, organized activities, and best of all, an exciting inflatable water playground. Surrounded by rugged terrain, stunning views, and exposed Canadian Shield, it’s a true nature lover’s retreat and a favourite for mountain biking enthusiasts and star gazers alike.
Set on a small, spring-fed lake teeming with fish, O’Reilly Lake also features a private, forested island that adds to its natural charm. Guests can enjoy canoeing or kayaking around the shoreline in about half an hour, weather permitting, while paddle boards, kayaks, pedal boats, canoes, and life jackets make it easy to enjoy everything the lake has to offer. On shore, family-friendly amenities like playgrounds and a jumping pillow add even more fun for kids, while the Aqua Park promises excitement for all ages.
With stunning waterfront transient sites, incredible wooded sites, and a convenient on-site store stocked with ice, firewood, snacks, treats, and cottage essentials, O’Reilly Lake Resort is the perfect place to relax, explore, and make unforgettable memories.
Our pet-friendly resort opens annually on May 1.
