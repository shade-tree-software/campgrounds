IMPORTANT UPDATES YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT THE 2025 CAMPING SEASON AT HEIDI'S CAMPGROUND:
DO YOU HAVE SEASONAL SITES AVAILABLE?
Due to overwhelming demand, we only offer seasonal sites to those that have purchased their trailer from Heidi's, with a model not older than 10 years.  If you qualify you can inquire further at our campground office or by emailing  campground@heidisrv.com .
HOW CAN I RESERVE A SITE IN YOUR CAMPGROUND?
The recommended way is also the easiest and the fastest, would be to book online (  please click here  )
Easy, online reservations take just minutes, and you have the ability to choose your favorite site (pending availability).  You can reserve your site for the day, week or month.  Monthly rates start at  $1,572 + HST.  Please see our website for more details and rates.
We can easily change the date of your reservation if needed.  If you need to cancel, we require 7 days notice, and you will receive a full refund, minus a $7+HST booking fee.
DO YOU HAVE SITES AVAILABLE FOR THE LONG WEEKEND?
Yes we do!  We recommend you to book your campsite as soon as possible, as the other long weekends are filling up quickly.  We would like to remind you that all long weekends require a minimum 3 night stay.
ARE YOU ACCEPTING TENT RESERVATIONS?
At this time, we are only accepting reservations to those with self contained RV units. No tents.
WE LOOK FORWARD TO WELCOMING YOU TO OUR BEAUTIFUL CAMPGROUND THIS SUMMER!!
