We enjoy speaking with everyone in order to get a feel for what type of camping/cottage experience you are looking for.  We are not an online reservation system without personality.  Our goal is to help make your holiday enjoyable!  We do offer various availability and look forward to meeting everyone.  🙂
Guests can choose between 6 deluxe housekeeping cottages, 1 bunkie for those who like "Glamping" or a camping vacation. In the spring/fall, we cater to those who need a break from reality or just want that trophy fish.  Summer brings families and the young at heart to our resort to enjoy a memorable vacation!
We are a family/staff operated resort who put our hearts and souls in to this beautiful retreat.  We all strongly believe in  personal customer service .  It is always best to  CALL  us to confirm a camping site or cottage so that we can do our absolute best to accommodate your requests.  Unfortunately, a computer is NOT very good at conveying your thoughts and feelings however a quick call can make the process easy.  Feel free to email us however since we are family run, please be patient for a response.    We give site references for completed reservations with credit cards.     Please email us for written confirmations referencing your name and date .
Since we are a large resort, we offer "walk-in" site available on a first come, first serve basis.  If the store is closed, please complete the check-in form next to the store door, choose your site and enjoy!  If you would like a last minute cottage, please call us as availability changes.

STORE HOURS:
Our phone lines are open 7 days a week, all year.  Feel free to call us between 10am - 9pm.  If you leave a clear message , (without loosing signal half way and we can't hear you), I guarantee we will call you back.
July & August our store is open from 10am-12pm, 1-5pm & 6-9pm.  We aim for you to check-in from 1-8:30pm while our store is open for both camping and cottages.
May, June & September our store is open from 10am - 12pm & 3-5pm.  On Friday & Saturday evenings only, we are also open from 7-9pm.  If you arrive and the store is closed, a map will be on the door for you to go in, set-up and see us later for official registration.
Please be patient when we are closed during the day as we do need to eat too!  🙂  We prefer you check-in before we are closed for the evening however feel free to contact us if you need to make late check-in arrangements.  As long as you make it here safe, is the only thing that matters.
Keep in mind, there will be a 2.5% surcharge on all Visa and MasterCard payments.  $1.00 processing fee for debit cards and no additional charges for cash payments.  We truly apologize however we can no longer absorb the credit card company fees.
PEACEFUL HOLIDAY
Beauty surrounds you
Manitoulin is a unique place where ancient native legends combine with historic sites and beautiful scenery to provide an unforgettable vacation experience. The pace of life is slower and more simple here allowing for a peaceful relaxing holiday atmosphere. After a few days, the magic of Manitoulin takes over and you find yourself relaxing, slowing down, and taking time to enjoy the beauty which surrounds you. Crystal clear lakes, abundant wildlife, birds, and winding scenic roads lead to new discoveries at every turn.

PERFECT SPOT
On the shores of beautiful Lake Manitou
Manitoulin Resort is approximately 1 km off of Hwy 6 on Holiday Haven Road, located on the beautiful shores of Lake Manitou. All amenities are close by in the town of Manitowaning, located only 5 minutes from the resort.
Directions from Little Current:
Approximately a 20-30 minute drive.
After crossing the swing bridge, turn left onto Hwy # 6 South.
Drive South until you reach Bidwell Road.
Turn right on to Bidwell Road.
Take your first left on to Holiday Haven Road.
We are on the right near the end of the road.
Directions from The Ferry:
Approximately a 30-45 minute drive.
Take Hwy # 6 North until you reach Bidwell Road.  This is approximately a 5 minute drive past the town of Manitowaning.
Turn left on to Bidwell Road.
Take your first left on to Holiday Haven Road.
We are on the right near the end of the road.

TENTING SITES
Manitoulin Resort offers grassy spacious sites, shady or sunny, perfect for your camping experience. These are non-serviced sites with a shared water tap within the vicinity. All of these sites are located in our Maple Grove section #40-44 located on the top right of our map and are within a 2-3 minute walk to one of our washroom/shower facilities and approximately a 7 minute walk to the waterfront area.  Please keep in mind we do not permit the use of generators within the resort.
BACK-IN
Back-In Camping Sites
We offer 30 amp electricity and water back-in sites that are perfect for your stay. A variety of sizes, locations, sun or shade, grassy or gravel pads are options we can discuss while you choose your vacation spot away from home.  Keep in mind we do not have sewers however we do offer a free dump station on site or a pump out service twice a week for a small fee.  Please ask us for details.
PULL-THRU
Pull-Thru Sites
These sites are geared towards "big rigs" and larger units offering 30 amp electricity and water.  They are typically wide, open/sunny sites with gravel pads approximately 16-18 meters, (55-60 feet), long.  Pull-thru sites offer the convenience of driving in and out with ease and the use of a grey water ONLY tank on each site.  Keep in mind we do not have sewers however we do offer a free dump station or a pump out service twice a week for a small fee. Please ask us for details.
Camping Information
In order to place a reservation for a campsite you must be a minimum 18 years of age, we require a Visa or MasterCard number and date of expiry. We will not place any charges on your card, you will pay for your site upon arrival.  We cannot accept Debit Visa's or prepaid gift cards.  Upon arrival, you will be asked to confirm your Visa or MasterCard as we keep it on file in the event of damages or additional service fees.
We are a family/staff operated resort who put our hearts and souls in to this beautiful retreat. We all strongly believe in personal customer service.   It is always best to CALL us to confirm a camping site or cottage so that we can do our absolute best to accommodate your requests.   Unfortunately, a computer is NOT very good at conveying your thoughts and feelings however a quick call can make the process easy. Feel free to email us by clicking on the "Reservations" tab however since we are family run, please be patient for a response.
Camping check-in is 1:00pm or later and check-out is 11:00am. Late check-out fees may be applied to the credit card on file if this should occur.  Upon arrival, if you are not satisfied with your campsite, feel free to see us in the store for a list of other available sites.  It is very rare that we will move your reservation to a different site however if an issue arises, this may occur.  Please contact us if you will be arriving after 8:00pm in order to make arrangements for late arrival. We will not give your site away until the following day however you are responsible for payment for the nights you have reserved.  No refunds are given due to inclement weather or for issues beyond our control.
Please be advised we require a minimum 3 weeks notice for cancellations or changes. If the minimum is not met, you will be charged for your one or two night stay.  If your reservation is originally for longer than one night, you will be charged $100.00 per week and/or partial week reserved.  Taxes applicable and fees applied to your credit card per campsite.  (Ex. 10 nights cancelled = $200.00 + tax cancellation fee).   Upon arrival, you are responsible for payment in full regarding the nights you have reserved.
If you do not arrive for your site on your date scheduled for arrival and you have not called us at 705-859-3550 to update us, we will assume that you have chosen to cancel your reservation without notice.
Keep in mind that all of our electric camping sites offer 30 amp electricity & water. Generators are NOT permitted.  Since these outlets are larger than regular electrical plugs and similar in size to a dryer or stove plug , we offer a converter plug program. For $20.00 CASH we will lend you a converter plug. Once the plug is returned we will return your $20.00 CASH.  If you do not have cash, we will sell you a converter electrical plug + tax.
Please be advised all weekends are a minimum 2-night stay however the July, August & September long weekends require a minimum 3-night stay.
ACCOMMODATIONS
CAMPING
Manitoulin Resort offers spacious grassy sites most of which include 30-amp electricity and water however we do offer non-serviced tenting sites with a shared water tap nearby for those who like to rough it. Each site consists of a fire pit, picnic table and nearby clean, modern washrooms with free hot showers and flush toilets. Please keep in mind it is very seldom we switch a camping site location however if required, we will do so in order to accommodate all clientele to the best of our ability.  We also offer laundry facilities, campfire wood delivery and a free dump station for clientele to use after a minimum 1-night stay.  Upon check-in feel free to ask about our pump out service offered twice a week on select days for a small fee.  Purchase slushies, penny candy, ice cream, snacks, supplies, fishing gear, select grocery products and ice cubes/blocks in our variety store.
Pets are permitted but  must  be leashed when off your campsite. They may prefer a walk in our additional 49 acre forest or enjoy a swim at the public boat launch located next to the resort. Excessive barking, stoop/scoop policy is in effect.   Maximum 2 pets per site.
Manitoulin Resort’s water is Ministry approved and therefore safe to drink, shower and cook with. We follow and abide by the Ministry’s requirements. We maintain two top quality water systems which are equipped with dual filtration, ultraviolet and chlorination units to keep you safe.
Our camping season typically begins the May long weekend, (Mother Nature permitting), and completes towards the end of September.  Please contact us to confirm opening and closing dates for the current year.  We accept camping reservations starting January 1st of the current year.

Number of adults/children/pets? (Max 2 pets per site).
Total length of your RV once it's set up or number of people your tent can accommodate?
Is it a travel trailer, 5th wheel, hybrid, tent trailer, toy hauler, truck camper, motorhome or tent?
Do you require electricity and water?  A back-in site or pull-thru site?
Have you stayed with us before and have a preferred site or area?
Do you need to arrange for advance docking?
Name/cell phone number/Visa or MasterCard number and expiry date?  Sorry, we do not accept debit Visa's or gift cards.
We do offer "after hours" check-in for those campers arriving late.  Please feel free to fully complete the form located outside the store/office door in the blue box.  Once complete, insert your information in the store drop box.
For those who wish a longer stay, contact us regarding a seasonal site or purchasing a trailer for sale within the resort.
