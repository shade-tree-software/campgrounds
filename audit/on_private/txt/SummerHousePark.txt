Summer House Park
Camping, Cottage Rentals and B & B on the Saugeen Bruce Peninsula
Near Tobermory and the Bruce Peninsula National Park
Summer House Park  is situated at the heart of the   Saugeen Bruce Peninsula   on the pristine shores of Miller Lake, central to   Tobermory  , the   Bruce Peninsula National Park  , the   Bruce Trail   and many local attractions.
We offer family camping, cottage rental and bed & breakfast accomodations, nestled amoung 85 acres of beautiful hardwood forest.
Come experience all the activities and amenities of Saugeen Bruce Peninsula's premiere camping resort, described by many of our guests as "their happy place."  Take a moment to   Explore Our Park   online. We invite you to reserve your next family getaway with us.
Beautifully landscaped grounds, exciting children’s programs and special events, a safe sandy beach and a wide variety of recreational amenities are available for your whole family to enjoy!
Accommodations at Summer House Park
Looking for accommodation on the Bruce Peninsula? Summer House Park has the perfect place to lay your head, whether you enjoy sleeping in a tent or 'camping resort-style' in a housekeeping cottage, cabin or yurt!

Camping Cabin Rentals
Log Cabin Rentals on Miller Lake
Camping Cabins at Summer House Park
If you are looking for a camping experience but don't own a camping unit, why not try one of our camping cabins? These basic log cabin-style units offer an affordable, cozy and comfortable place to relax at the end of a busy day exploring the Bruce! Each cabin is equipped with electricity, bunk beds, a small bar fridge and counter space. Washroom facilities are available nearby.
Cooking is not permitted inside cabins (except Whip-poor-will Roost) but a fire pit, charcoal grill and communal outdoor kitchen with sink are included with your campsite. Pet friendly.
