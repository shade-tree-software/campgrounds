Reservation Instructions:  The Online form will be active beginning at 12:01 am on Monday, January 5th, 2026. Please submit ONE FORM per Family and complete ALL information for the Family staying on the site you are booking. If you are booking with friends then please create a Group Name and have all Families use it when making a booking.
If you prefer to talk to us in person then we will post our Office hours on our Calendar and our Saugeen Springs RV Park App.
Looking forward to hearing from you and joining us for 2026!
Our camping guests enjoy spacious campsites with many along the riverfront, 200 acres of rolling hills, grazing horses, hiking the many nature trails, relaxing with friends, making s’mores, fishing in the ponds or tubing the Saugeen River.
If you don’t have your own tent or trailer, we have a variety of rental units so that you can enjoy the camping experience. See  RENTALS .

Seasonal Camping
Join us this summer as a Seasonal camper….
Imagine sitting around the campfire in the evening…sticky marshmallows on your fingers…listening to the laughter of your kids as they fall exhausted into bed after a day of tubing, fishing, playing and making new friends. In the distance a guitar is playing as other families enjoy the quiet evening together as tales of the day unfold.
Each spring you wait with anticipation for the bulbs and hostas that you planted in your garden to poke their heads out of the ground….your garden decor is perfect and the little white lights that you hung up around your site have created a special oasis that is your “home away from home” escape…..
As transient campers are busy thinking about packing up…you know that all you have to do is pack up the kids to head home….and know that at the next chance you will be back….life as a Seasonal camper!
There is always room for more in the Saugeen Springs family! Our family would love to meet yours…

Spring Camping
Spring is a wonderful time to enjoy Saugeen Springs RV Park!  There is a smell in the air as buds are opening, tulips are popping up and baby birds are being born.
Tired of hauling your trailer back and forth?  Always wanting to extend your camping trips? Interested in what it would be like to become a Seasonal Camper?
Try a Mini Season Camping Package, stay as  MANY  nights as you like, make Saugeen Springs R.V. Park your home away from home, and leave your Trailer/Unit on site between visits…
SPRING CAMPING…
May 18th – June 21st, 2026
Hydro/Water Site:  $1299.00 + HST
Hydro/Water River Site:  $1449.00 + HST
Hydro/Water/Sewer Site:  $1449.00 + HST
Non-sewer sites include 4 pumpouts on May 27th, June 3rd, 10th & 17th
VICTORIA LONG WEEKEND ADD-ON May 15th – 18th, 2026:
Hydro/Water Site:  $280.00 + HST
Hydro/Water River Site:  $310.00 + HST
Hydro/Water/Sewer Site:  $285.00 + HST
Non-sewer sites include pumpout on May 20th
Proof of insurance for your trailer is required.
You are required to disconnect water and hydro between visits.

Day Guests
…Welcome to Saugeen Springs R.V. Park…
Our Park is  OPEN to DAY GUESTS from Sunday to Thursday each week , except Sundays of a Long Weekend.
We are happy to have you visit us for the day!  Full use of our  Park Amenities  from 10:00 am to 7:00 pm on  Sunday to Thursday .
Day Guests (13+ years) $12.00/day per person
Day Guests (4-12 years) 6.00/day per person
Day Guests (0-3 years) FREE
Vehicle Parking – $7.50/day per vehicle
Please register at the Office when you arrive and you will receive a parking tag for your vehicle.  Please park in designated areas.
Tubing Day Package – $200.00 + HST  .  Please click for information .
Want to visit us more often?  Looking for a safe, family-friendly Day Trip?
We now have Day Guest Memberships
Full use of our  Park Amenities  from 10:00 am to 7:00 pm on  Sunday to Thursday .
Day Guest Membership with a Site*
Guest Parking Membership – $25.00/Vehicle per Season + HST

Not valid on any Friday or Saturday (or Sundays on Long Weekends).
*Each site includes a parking space, picnic table and a fire ring.  We will assign you a site upon Arrival in the Office.  You are welcome to set up a dining tent on your designated site for the day.

Seasonal Camping
Join us this summer as a Seasonal camper….
Imagine sitting around the campfire in the evening…sticky marshmallows on your fingers…listening to the laughter of your kids as they fall exhausted into bed after a day of tubing, fishing, playing and making new friends. In the distance a guitar is playing as other families enjoy the quiet evening together as tales of the day unfold.
Each spring you wait with anticipation for the bulbs and hostas that you planted in your garden to poke their heads out of the ground….your garden decor is perfect and the little white lights that you hung up around your site have created a special oasis that is your “home away from home” escape…..
As transient campers are busy thinking about packing up…you know that all you have to do is pack up the kids to head home….and know that at the next chance you will be back….life as a Seasonal camper!
There is always room for more in the Saugeen Springs family! Our family would love to meet yours…
