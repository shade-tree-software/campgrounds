Full Service Sites
Newly Upgraded - gravel sites, all new plumbing & electrical (2020).
30 Amp Electrical Hook up, Water & Sewer (No public restrooms or showers on site), Free Wi-Fi
Rates: $60/night - $350/Week,  $1200/Month &  $3600/seasonal - HST included
Payment required on reservation
Camper Rentals
Multiple camper rentals located in Rabbit Lake RV Park for monthly/seasonal and short term stays. Campers are fully furnished including bedding & kitchen supplies.  Full hookups, fridge, propane stove.  WI-FI is included as well as a propane BBQ. Unfortunately camp fires are not permitted. This property overlooks Rabbit Lake and is within walking distances of two beaches.
Nearby Amenities
