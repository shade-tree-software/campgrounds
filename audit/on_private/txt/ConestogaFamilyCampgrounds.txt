Please Let us    Introduce    ourselves.   Once
you enter the gate you immediately sense a feeling of leaving
all your worries at home. When you drive into the grounds
your eyes automatically focus to the right to a fresh spring
fed lake      (Lake Rotten Ralph)   .   The wonderful
feeling of old mother nature captures you when your ears hear
the delightful sound of the birds chirping and flying from
one pine tree to another.
If you are looking for a place that
will keep the whole family busy for your    Overnight Camping    and    Seasonal
vacations or just need a quiet place to relax, then you have
come to the right place. We also offer a wide assortment of    New Trailers    and    Used Trailers
for sale.
Throughout the summer there are many
planned
activities   . I have included
this seasons summer    Calendar    to
help you decide when you would like to visit us.
Many of our events including our
regular Friday night    BINGO    are
held in our gorgeous    Recreation Hall   , however we also hold many activities
outside in the    Sports Field
(   Sports
Equipment    provided free of
charge at our store) or even on occasion in our 25' X 50'
heated    Swimming
Pool   .
I think young and old will all agree
that playing a round of golf or in our case    Mini Golf    is very enjoyable and relaxing. Our 18 hole
course is located at the front of the park near our    Recreation Hall   ,    Store   ,    washrooms    and    Laundry
facilities. Also located in the front area are our    Horseshoe    pits and a    Playground
for children ages 6 and under. Another playground for the
older children is also available in the park.
For those of you that enjoy canoeing,
please feel free to bring up your canoes, however if you do
not own one we have many    Boats
available for rental. Whether you go out on    Lake Rotten Ralph    for    Fishing   ,
boating or swimming you will find it invigorating and
stimulating to the mind and body.
As a final note I have also included
our    Rules
and Regulations   , a list of
our    Facilities   ,    Maps    of the
campground and some nearby    Attractions   .
