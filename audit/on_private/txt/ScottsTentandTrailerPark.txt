About
Scott's Campground is located in the heart of Niagara falls, with only being 8km away from the Golden Horseshoe Falls, Scott's Campground is the right choice for all your family camping needs.
The Niagara Regional transit Bus stops right in front of our campground to give guest easy access to the bus service.  The City bus services gives guest easy access to all attractions that Niagara Falls has to offer allowing you to be just a hop, jump and a skip away from all the action that Niagara Falls brings. You can then transfer to The WEGO bus service.
​
Scott's Family Campground prides itself with keeping all facilities and grounds kept clean and maintained with friendly service.

Before You Arrive
Check in is 2PM
Prior to check in, ensure all campers of age can provide one piece of valid photo ID
At least one member of a reservation must be over the age of 25
Reservations can only be guaranteed via deposit by valid Credit card
All campfires, by Niagara Falls Law, must be fully extinguished prior to 12AM, and not reignited until 8AM
We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone for any reason.
If you are local to the area which we are located, you may be subject to additional screening questions and/or approval as deemed necessary by our staff. this is to ensure a positive experience for all of our guests from across the globe
