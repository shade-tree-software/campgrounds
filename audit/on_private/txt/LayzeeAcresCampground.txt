Campground
Layzee Acres Campground
Layzee Acres is nestled on the beautiful shores of Lake Dalrymple offering the perfect family getaway. With over
nine hundred feet of shoreline, Layzee Acres Campground features a sandy beach, lakeside recreation and docking
facilities. Lake Dalrymple continues to be a favourite destination for angles especially those searching for the
elusive muskie.

Boating & Fishing
Lake Dalrymple offers great boating and superb fishing, and Layzee Acres has the facilities to launch and dock
your boat. Our 80 boat slips are located in a sheltered canal and are protected from the open water. Boat ramp
use is included with your camp[ing fee while boat docks are available for rent.

Camping Amenities
Whether you are planning a family vacation or a visit to lake country, Layzee Acres Campground offers amenities
and services you need to ensure a comfortable and quiet stay.
Main amenities at Layzee Acres include:
Full-service RV dealership
On-site general store
Laundromat
Control gates
Central washroom facilities
Full service sites with 30/ 50 amp hydro

Committee Events
