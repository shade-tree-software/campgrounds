FISH
Walleye and Northern Pike Fishing
Lac Des Mille Lacs is famous for it's trophy Walleye and Northern Pike. With over 45 miles of Canadian fishing paradise, Lac Des Mille Lacs will delight any angler's heart. We are conveniently located in the middle of the lake. You can purchase your fishing license, rent a dock space, or rent a fishing boat at the Pine Point Resort store.

HUNT
Moose, Black Bear, and Grouse Hunting
Add a trophy Canadian moose or black bear to your collection! Moose can be hunted by land or by water. Boats are available for rent or you may bring your own. ATVs are also welcome.
Check out our hunting packages.

Camping On Lac Des Mille Lacs
Get away from the hurried pace of every day life at our Ontario campground!
Enjoy a quiet, peaceful, stress free vacation  camping at Pine Point Resort campground. Located on a peninsula of land surrounded by water on three sides and tall, native pine forest on the other, our individual campsites are shaded along beautiful Lac Des Mille Lacs. Pitch your tent among the trees or park your RV facing the painted sky of an Ontario sunset! Experience the sights and sounds of nature all around you – the songs of the birds in the trees and the sounds of the night. Do some  star gazing . Yes, you really can see the stars up here!
Relax on the dock at water’s edge. And don’t forget your fishing rod! You’ll find deep water right off the end of our dock. Or bring your boat and spend your day on the water. Vacation during the right time of year and go  blueberry picking . The native sweetness of wild blueberries is a gourmet treat!
