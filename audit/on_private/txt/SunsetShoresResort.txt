About Us
🌞  Welcome to Sunset Shores Resort – A Geographical Gem!
Nestled where the river meets Lake Superior, Sunset Shores Resort offers an unforgettable outdoor experience for nature lovers, fishing enthusiasts, and adventure seekers alike.
—
🚙  Stay Your Way
• Seasonal & Overnight Camping
• Charming Cottage Rentals
🚣‍♂️  Explore the Waters and Park
• Canoe, Kayak, Bike & Motor Boat Rentals
• Ideal for peaceful paddling or thrilling lake adventures
🎣  World-Class Fishing
Enjoy direct access to both the river and Lake Superior for some of the region’s best fishing — reel in trout, salmon, pike, and more!
🍜  Dine On-Site
Open during July and August,  Pho-Licious Restaurant  serves mouthwatering Vietnamese cuisine to satisfy your cravings after a day on the water.
🏹  Exceptional Hunting & ATV Adventures Nearby
Just a 5-minute drive from the resort, you’ll find access to vast Crown Land — perfect for outdoor exploration and seasonal game hunts:
• Goose & Partridge Hunting
• Spring & Fall Bear Hunting
• ATV Riding  – Explore scenic trails and rugged terrain through the wilderness, right from the edge of nature
⚒️  Resort Amenities -  Everything you need for a comfortable and enjoyable stay:
• Beautiful, clean  drinking water
•  Modern comfort station  with flush toilets
•  Hot showers  for your comfort and convenience
•  Camper store  & with ice cream, snacks, groceries, worms, tackle, and more
•  24-hour coin laundry  — clean and convenient
•  Scenic beachfron t for sunbathing, swimming, and relaxing by the water •  Boat launch  for easy water access
•  Boat slips  available for secure mooring
•  Water toys  to keep kids entertained and smiling
•  Wi-Fi  available at the office to stay connected when needed
—
At  Sunset Shores Resor t, we blend natural beauty with comfort and community. Whether you’re here to fish, hunt, paddle, relax, or explore — we can’t wait to welcome you.
