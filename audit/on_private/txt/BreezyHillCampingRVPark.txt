About Us
About the Campground
Located close to trans-Canada highway 417, within 10 minutes of the town of Arnprior, and just 25 minutes from Canada's capital city of Ottawa, Breezy Hill Camping & RV Park Inc, formerly known as Grainger Park is a great family run campground that has been established for 25 years.
This is an excellent location to take advantage of nature in all its glory.
Campground Amenities
In addition to great camping there are many activities and amenities for everyone to enjoy:
-heated pool
-artificial lake and sandy beach area
-playground
-volleyball net
-pétanque
-convenient store
-coin operated laundry facilities (washer and dryer)
-washroom and shower facilities
In the summer months enjoy our complimentary weekend activities for children!

Cabin rental
***NEW*** Only available for monthly or seasonal rental
Seasonal
$4,000. (plus hydro and HST)
Monthly (4 weeks)
$1,800.  (plus hydro and HST)
Things to know
Important
What is Included and What to Bring
Included
•Each cabin includes a microwave, mini fridge, BBQ, fire pit and picnic table
•Bed sizes range from twin bunk beds to king size
•A two piece bathroom
What to Bring
•Any bedding or linens you may require
•Dishes and utensils
•Toiletries
•Any other personal items
Please note that there are NO pets allowed in the cabins.
Making a Reservation
•When reserving a cabin a deposit of 50% of your stay is required. (Credit card number is required for security purposes)
•Please note that prices are for 2 adults and their children, additional adults are $10 a night (tax included).
Cancellation Policy
•If you must cancel your stay with us, the cancellation needs to be made within 28 days (4 weeks), if not respected the deposit in non-refundable.
•We do reserve the right to check cabins before they are vacated and if there is any vandalism the costs of replacement or fixing will be charged to your credit card.

Rules and regulations
All visitors must register at the office
Quiet hours: 11pm to 8 am
Animals on leash at all times and pickup poop
No animals at the beach, pool, toilets, showers and playground
Music for your ear, please respect your neighbor
No excessive noise or vulgar language tolerated
Visitors are your responsibility
Speed limit is 10 km/h
No bicycles in the sand at the beach, playground, at the pool and on cement pathway at the office
One firepit per site
No motorcycles or 4 wheeler excursions in the park
Washing your vehicle or RV requires office permission
No shampoo and/ or bathing in the lake
No waste water on the grounds
No cutting or damaging trees
Use ashtrays for cigarette butts
Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult (18 or older) to the beach, pool, toilets, showers and should be on their site between 9pm and 8am.
No fireworks, fire arms, projectiles and drugs permitted on grounds
No clotheslines attached to trees
Beach and pool unsupervised. Hours and rules posted, please follow
One car per lot maximum
Eviction may occur if rules are not respected
