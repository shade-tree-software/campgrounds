ABOUT
Established in 1958, Lakeside Village is located approximately 9km East of Wheatley, Ontario on the Talbot Trail, and is a privately owned facility consisting of 104 campsites and 13 motel rooms.
Located High on a Bluff, this campground is ideal for a relaxing view of Lake Erie and the occasional fishing trawler plying their trade... or you can go down the lighted steps for a refreshing swim at the private beach.
​
At night the stars seem close and bright, and you can see the glow of the lights from Ohio across the lake. Come join us for a quiet, relaxing retreat!
