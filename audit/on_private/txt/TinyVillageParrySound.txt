What People Are Saying
"Our stay at the dome was perfect! Michael and his wife were very friendly and accommodating which made our checkin/stay a breeze. The dome was clean when we arrived and had everything we could have hoped for and more! The beach was a close walk and getting to the dome itself was very easy, you can drive your car right up to it for anyone wondering. A hidden gem in my opinion, and I would recommend this to anyone! Thanks again for all the hospitality!"
Brandon’s Airbnb Review (Terra Dome)
“Stayed here with my husband and we will definitely be recommending this spot to our friends! The view from our bunkie was gorgeous, steps from the beach area. Super comfortable, well equipped + clean. We will be back for sure!”
Brenda’s Airbnb Review (Red Pine Bunkie)
“Amazing place to stay in. From the moment you arrive to the sad moment of leaving this has been one of the best experiences for our family. The hosts are very friendly and welcoming. Really looking forward to come back some time this summer. This is truly a gem in Ontario”
Luis’ Airbnb Review (Evergreen Bunkie)
"Had wonderful experience staying at the Terra dome . Hosts are super cool . The dome itself is secluded from the rest of tiny village . It’s a very small walk to the beach. We had fun doing kayaking and riding ATVs. Since it’s far from cities or towns the night sky was surreal . Will definitely recommend"
Satya’s Airbnb Review (Terra Dome)
“Wife and I stayed for two nights in the dome by the beach. This was one of the best muskoka getaways we’ve ever had. Hospitality was great and the dome experience will be an annual event for us moving forward. Private, relaxing, and everything you’d need for a weekend away at the lake."
Colin’s Airbnb Review (Beach Dome)
"Beautiful little spot, wonderful and friendly staff/owners and the coolest tiny home! Would definitely recommend this spot!"
Leah’s Airbnb Review (Lake View Tiny Home)
"My family and I rented, 2 domes and a Loft and all had an amazing experience and are already talking about going back, they had so many amenities to use like a shower, kayaks, paddle boards, you can rent the ATV, canoe, and more. I had a blast and would suggest this place to anyone wanting to get away :)"
Brayden’s Airbnb Review
"Wonderful dome and beach area. The other accommodation options at the park are equally great (Tiny houses and bunkies). Awesome remote spot with pristine lake and great views (especially the night sky. Lots of activities with free to use kayaks and a canoe. Highly recommended and we'll definitely go again."
Gustavo’s Airbnb Review
"We stayed at the dome in the forest which is 2 mins from the beach and loved everything about it. It was secluded, clean, had its own outhouse, bbq grill, outdoor shower and sink. The inside of the dome was very spacious and comfortably accommodated family of two adults and two kids. The beach was beautiful, clear water and kids had a blast... Watching the stars through the domes ceiling at night was also a unique experience... I would highly recommend this place to anyone who wants to disconnect and unwind in nature. We would go back again"
Shamila’s Airbnb Review
"Everyone was extremely helpful and kind. When the owners learnt I had come alone, they ensured I had a walkie talkie in case I needed anything if my phone service didn't work. They even accomodated my late arrival due to crazy highway traffic. Despite the rain, the property is beautiful! The heaters kept the dome at a comfortable temperature and the added blankets gave an extra cozy feel. Really great view from the dome. Looking forward to coming back."
Taylor’s Airbnb Review (Beach Dome)
