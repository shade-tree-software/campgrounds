Welcome to Ontario's Camping &Fishing Capital
Our Campground and Fishing Resort
Our  family friendly campground and resort is  1 minutes off the Transcanada Highway in the Town of Spanish Ontario. Here we offer sport fisherman and nature lovers a spectacular paradise along the North shore of Lake Huron.
With accommodation options of cabins, bunkie, RV sites and tent sites, with either lake view or lake side sites directly along the waters edge.
Here you can enjoy a relaxing sunset and fish for  Walleye, Small Mouth Bass, Pike, Musky   and more  right from your site or our docks. Water sites are limited so book early!
Our fisherman approved cabins and bunkie are comfortable and clean, and our Rv trailer and camp sites book up fast. With Powers options of 15, 30 and even 50 amp service we can accommodate most sizes of Rv's and even Coaches. We also provide drinkable water hookups.  Come for a weekend or stay the entire summer. We know you will love it here at Spanish River Resort & Campground.

Amenities and Accommodations
Our resort offers guests  a private beach, a boat launch, boat slips, dock fishing, kids play areas and free kayak or canoe rentals  for those staying 3 nights or longer. Unique to our location, and our boat ramp, you can access the Spanish River and the North Channel all the way to Little Current and even Georgian Bay. Here you are able to angle in both zones 10 and 14. A very unique fishing experience.
We have 3 options for your stay; a cabin or bunkie, RV and trailer site, or tenting camp site. Please specify which one you're interested in and if you would like to rental a fishing boat or are bringing one and will need a boat slip.
​Spanish River Resort & Campground is Ontario's unofficial capital for fun, serving pro-fisherman, campers and families alike. For those staying in the campground there is hotwater showers, a fish cleaning station and WiFi readily available.

Location
We are only 1 km off the Trans Canada 17 HWY in the Town of Spanish and right on the waters edge of Lake Huron. Relax in one of our cabins or book a Rv site in our campground.
Here you will find both a fisherman's heaven as well as an outdoor playground for kids and families, that includes; swim area, beach volleyball, tree houses, dock fishing, canoe, kayak and stand-up paddle board rentals. For those that need to work while on vacation...we also have great WiFi!
To find us we are a short 65 minutes West of Sudbury, and a little over 2 hours East of Sault Saint Marie. Some call it the middle of nowhere, we call it Paradise!

Camping in Spanish Ontario doesn't get better than this. Since our Resort is located along the shoreline, every site has a view of the water!If you're lucky, you might even get a water's edge site!
If staying by Rv, 5th wheel, motorhome or tent you will find a friendly mix of Northern locals and Summer vacationers all catching up around the docks and crackling fires sharing stories of adventure and fun.
Our grassy treed sites are serviced with 15, 30, and 50 amp and all have drinkable water hookups. Our bathrooms include hot showers and campground has great Wifi. We also have a fish cleaning station for fishermen and an  UNSUPERVISED ​ swimming area available to all overnight campers. Always use caution around open water.
We have recently added 2 new sun pergolas as well as beach volleyball, tetherball and horshoes to our waterfront play areas.
