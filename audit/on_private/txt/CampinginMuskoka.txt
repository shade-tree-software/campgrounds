Winter in Muskoka
Camping in Muskoka is a third generation family owned and operated campground. We have been connecting campers to the great outdoors while helping friends and families make memories since 1971. With creeks, streams, towering pines, lush hardwoods and dramatic exposures of Canadian Shield granite, our location is quintessentially Muskoka. Our amenities, like our stocked trout pond, par 3, 9 hole golf course, or our canoe and rowboat rentals connect you to the unique beauty of our property, while our location, about half way between Gravenhurst and Bracebridge, connects you to the best of what the region has to offer. With campsites ranging from rustic, un-serviced tent sites, to full-service RV sites, to camping cabins, we've got camping options for all campers.

Cancellation policy  :
For cabins, we do require 7 days notice, prior to your reservation, to receive a refund. There will be a $10. admin fee witheld.
For campsites, we require 48 hours notice, prior to your reservation, to receive a refund. There will be a $10. admin fee witheld.
Guests are eligible for a refund when cancellations are made in accordance with the policy above.
No Hook up Tent Sites
$5  5   per night
Our no hook up tent sites are our most rustic camping option. These large, private sites are perfect for tents or small camper vans. There are no water or electrical hook ups on these sites. Potable water is available from close by access points. On these sites, we allow a maximum of two adults per site with one piece of camping equipment per site. Children belonging to adults who booked the site are free.
Electric Only Sites
$  60   per night
Our electric only sites have a 15 amp, household electrical outlet which is perfect for running small devices. These sites are perfect for tents, pop up trailers or small camper vans. Potable water is available from close by access points. On these sites, we allow a maximum of two adults per site with one piece of camping equipment per site. Children belonging to adults who booked the site are free.
Back In, Water and 15 amp Electric
$6  5   per night
Our back in water and 15 amp electric sites are   are perfect for tents, small trailers, pop up trailers and camper vans. Ideal for campers who want the convenience of water right on their site and who wish to power small household devices while camping. On these sites, we allow a maximum of two adults per site with one piece of camping equipment per site. Children belonging to adults who booked the site are free.
Pull through, Water and 30 amp Electric
$  75   per night
Our pull through sites are easy to get onto with roads on either side of the site. Just pull through, no backing up required. Centrally located and close to all of the campground's amenities.
Back In, Water and 30 amp Electric
$  75   per night
Our back in water and 30 amp electric sites are nice large sites with lots of trees and privacy.
Full Service
$  85   per night
Our Full Service sites are all back in sites with water, 30 amp electric, and sewer service.
Single Room Camping Cabin
$99 per night
Our single room camping cabins sleep 4 people. The cabins are equipped with a double bed and two single beds arranged as a bunk bed. Bring your own pillows, blankets and sheets. The cabins are located on campsites with a firepit, picnic table, and wood burning/charcoal grill for cooking. Campers staying in our cabins use our centrally located washroom building.
Pets are only allowed in cabins 1,2, and 3. Pets are not allowed in cabins 4-10.
All of our single room cabins have a lovely front porch. Perfect for relaxing!
Each single room cabin is located on a campsite equipped with a picnic table, a fire ring and a charcoal/wood burning grill for cooking.
Bunk bed, made up of two single beds.
All of our cabins have electrical outlets to power small household devices like radios, laptops, fans, or space heaters.
Each cabin is equipped with a double bed and a bunk bed, made up of two single beds. Perfect for a family of four, or four close friends.
Deluxe 2 Room Cabin
$165 per night
We have one air conditioned Deluxe Cabin with two rooms separated by a pocket door. One room has two bunk beds. The other room has a double bed, a mini fridge, a microwave, counters, and cabinets for storage. Bring your own pillows, blankets and sheets. This cabin is located on a site with a fire pit, a picnic table, and a gas barbeque for cooking. Campers staying in our deluxe cabin use our centrally located washroom building.
Pets are not allowed in the Deluxe Cabin.
Our Deluxe Cabin has a lovely front porch, perfect for relaxing!
The Deluxe cabin is equipped with a mini fridge and microwave for your convenience..
Bunk Beds.
Two bunk beds in the air conditioned bunk room.
the main room and the bunk room has a closable pocket door, perfect for putting children to bed earlier.
Dump Station
$44.25
Use of our dump station is included in the cost of booking a stay with us. For people passing through, not staying with us, we charge 44.25 to use our dump station, during store hours only.
***
All of our prices are subject to HST and an additional $5 booking fee.
Long weekends have a 3 night minimum stay.
Rates are based on two adults per site, per day. Additional adults are $  20   per adult, per day, to a max of 4 adults per site.
Extra adult fees are not available on tent sites. All tent sites have a max of 2 Adults per site.
Persons under the age of 18 must be accompanied by an adult.
Check-in time is 2pm.
Check-out time is 11am.
