Life’s Better at the Lake
Nestled on the beautiful Big Rideau, our Cottage and RV Resort offers spacious sites, including a select number of transient RV spots and rental cottages. Waterways is the perfect destination for families looking to relax and reconnect. Dock rentals are available for an additional fee.
Waterways RV Resort is a family-oriented resort that is a great getaway with lots to do both on and off the resort. Our updated beach hut area includes free wifi, our office/store offers treats for the kids, coffee and great Waterways swag wear! The resort features a pristine dock area for boating enthusiasts, lots of activities for families, a horseshoe pit area, a volleyball area, a children’s play area, and a large sports court that includes Pickleball, basketball and much more. Enjoy the convenience of our beautiful shower and laundry facilities, and lots of parking.
Weather permitting, our guests can enjoy their Resort Cottage or RV from May 1st to October 31st each season.
