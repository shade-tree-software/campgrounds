Embrace the Great Outdoors
If escaping the city and adventuring off the beaten path to seek outdoor fun is your ideal vacation, look no further than Whitewater Cottage & RV Resort. From a beautiful sandy beach, to a rushing river, mountain bike trails galore, and open fields perfect for yoga, you’ll find peace and tranquility among the trees and surrounded by nature next to the Ottawa River. We are located just minutes away from the world-renowned Wilderness Tours rafting experience and we encourage our guests, whether visiting for a few days or with a seasonal site here, to pay them a visit!  Only 75 minutes from downtown Ottawa.
Resort Cottage Rentals, RV Sites, Unique Glamping Accommodations & So Much More!
Choose Your Own Adventure for your Vacation Fun at Whitewater Cottage & RV Resort
A wide range of lodging options are available including chalets with a view of the river, our luxury beach house, log cabin and lake house, cabins and tiny homes!  Bring your own RV for the weekend and stay with us for a family fun experience you’ll remember forever.
INTRODUCING THE WHITEWATER BEERSPA (19+)
NOW OPEN
Relax with a beer-infused twist on the traditional Nordic spa experience at Whitewater BeerSpa, located onsite. Enjoy thermal cycles with hot tubs, saunas, dump buckets, river dashes, hammocks, campfires, and a traditional float pool experience, all in the heart of the Ottawa River Valley. Detox, retox, recover, and enjoy. Learn more at  WhitewaterBeerSpa.ca .

Rental Accommodation Options
Waterfront Resort Cottages
Experience the comfort of our modern resort cottage—designed with families and groups in mind. This spacious and stylish retreat features two cozy bedrooms: one with a queen bed and built-in storage, and the other with bunk beds that are perfect for kids or extra guests. The open-concept living area includes a large sectional sofa and an inviting fireplace, creating a warm space to unwind. A full kitchen with a central island offers everything you need to prepare meals and gather together. Additional perks include a full bathroom, walk-in closet, and contemporary design throughout.
Accommodates up to 6 guests
Waterfront Location: Enjoy stunning views and easy access to the water.
Fully Equipped Kitchen: Includes full-size appliances and a large island with seating.
Spacious Living Area: Open layout with a sectional sofa and fireplace—ideal for relaxing or entertaining.
Pet-Friendly: Furry friends are welcome to join your getaway.
Lake House
The Lake House is hands down our best location at Whitewater Cottage & RV Resort. Looking for an exceptionally peaceful getaway far from the hustle and bustle of crowds? The Lake House is even available for rent during the off season for an even more tranquil retreat. Equipped with three bedrooms and a loft, the lake house sleeps a maximum of 8 guests, with two bathrooms including a Jacuzzi bathtub for a luxurious spa-like retreat.
The Lake House is available for rent in the off season.
3 Bedrooms and loft – sleeps a maximum of 9 guests
1 one king bed on main floor with en-suite bathroom with Jacuzzi tub, 1 queen bed on second floor, 1 double bed with a bunked single on second floor. Pull out double sofa in loft

High Speed Internet in the unit

Crash Cabin
Looking for a comfortable rustic camping adventure near the Ottawa River?
The Crash Cabins at Whitewater Cottage & RV Resort offer a cozy and comfortable retreat for 1-6 guests. Each 12’x12’ cabin has been refreshed with brand-new flooring and paint, and features two double beds, a daybed, and a trundle bed tucked underneath—providing a total of four sleeping spaces.
Enjoy the outdoors from the charming front deck, perfect for relaxing with family and friends while taking in the beautiful views of the resort and its natural surroundings.
Designed to give you a true camping experience, these cabins do not have washrooms, but clean and well-maintained comfort stations are conveniently located nearby.
Each cabin has a Firepit, Smart TV, a single-serve coffee maker with packets, bedding, and a picnic table.
(Bathroom towels are not provided.) (There is not a kitchen.)
Bring your own beach towels, bathroom towels, toiletries and outdoor chairs.
Sleeps 1–6 guests
2 Double Beds
1 Daybed
1 Trundle Bed

The Bordeaux
Discover the charm of our locally crafted executive resort cottage, nestled in a private, tree-lined corner. This inviting retreat features two spacious bedrooms—each with ample closet space—one with a cozy queen bed and the other with a double bed topped by a single bunk. The living area offers a comfortable double sofa bed, perfect for relaxing or hosting additional guests. Enjoy the convenience of a bathroom equipped with a half tub and shower, and a full kitchen that includes a bar-style eating area. Step outside onto the expansive deck and unwind in the serene garden, an ideal spot for peaceful mornings or leisurely evenings.
Accommodates up to 7 guests
Modern Bathroom: Equipped with a convenient half tub and shower.
Fully Equipped Kitchen: Includes all necessary appliances and a bar-style eating area.
Expansive Outdoor Living: Large deck for outdoor relaxation and entertainment.
Tranquil Garden: Unwind in the serene garden setting, perfect for quiet mornings or relaxing evenings.
