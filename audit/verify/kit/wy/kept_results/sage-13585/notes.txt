13585 Sage Campground (Pathfinder Reservoir) - waterfront re-check 2026-11-22

Call: not waterfront -> lakefront (three westernmost sheltered shore-row sites), drawdown caveat in note.
Pin: keep (42.469,-106.8645 is inside the loop, ~70 m north of the shore row).

High water: Reclamation allocations sheet rev. 2016-05-19 (UNT archive 2016-12-24): top of active
conservation = uncontrolled spillway crest 5,852.49 ft = 1,783.84 m (1,070,185 af). Datum not stated
on the sheet; the 3DEP 1,783.84 m contour coincides with the vegetation/sand line (2022) and the
2011 high-water shoreline, so the field check agrees. 3DEP hydro-flattened water = 1,782.25 m.

Rec1 catalog (tab 12059 Pathfinder Reservoir): Sage group 24998 has a single facility "Sage
Non-Reservable Sites" - no site names. Diabase group 36963 = DB Site 01-11 (04 and 11 ADA).
