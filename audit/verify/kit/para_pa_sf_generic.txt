The twelve are PENNSYLVANIA: DCNR state forest roadside campsites booked one site at a time on ReserveAmerica (__LIST__). The Missouri and MDC passages elsewhere in these instructions do not apply to this batch; everything else does. These entries were added on 2026-10-10 from the site pages alone (the recipe, the cached per-site pages and the helpers are in audit/pa_sf_dispersed/: README.md, ra_sites.jsonl, sat.py for an Esri crop with the sites dotted, water_elev.py) and have never been looked at one by one, so this pass is the first real check. An entry may hold several sites: read EVERY site of it. For EACH entry: (1) re-read every one of its ReserveAmerica campsiteDetails pages today (plain urllib works on pennsylvaniastateparks.reserveamerica.com; the www.reserveamerica.com/explore links may need the browser) and confirm the site still exists and is bookable ONLINE - 'Walkup Only' with no price means it has left the booking system (p3-357 found three Akersville sites gone that way; report it, rewrite booking as first-come and flag it as a keep-or-drop question) - its Site Access (Drive-In), the equipment categories it takes (quote the list; sites listing 'Horse Site, Tent Site, Trailer Site' with no 'RV Site' have been kept with the fact in the flags - report them, do not drop on that alone), Max Vehicle Length (23 ft or more; under ~23 ft is a DROP candidate; say which sites of a multi-site entry fall short, and correct sites.count and max_rig_ft to the sites a 23-ft motorhome can use), 'Site Height/Overhead Clearance' (p3-358 found a site listing 10 ft: the rig is just under 10.5 ft, so quote any figure under 11 ft and say whether imagery shows open sky), driveway surface, length and GRADE (quote 'Moderate'/'Severe'; p3-357 found a ~13% driveway - check 3DEP along any driveway graded above Slight), pull-through or back-in (write sites.pull_through), and every line of site-specific text - 'tent and small RV', '4WD or high clearance recommended', 'camper trailers not recommended', equestrian-only, ATV-only or seasonal-use wording are drop grounds under the recipe and must be quoted; (2) the fee: stored $10 with a $5 non-resident surcharge; the site pages show 'Reservation Fees: $6.50' - confirm and write fees.reservation_fee 6.5; booking window (330 days), cut-off (noon of arrival), 7-night maximum, 3 pm check-in and check-out and the 48-hours-off rule go in booking and booking.note; (3) the forest district's own DCNR page and any advisory for road closures, seasonal gates, timber sales, bridge limits (the rig weighs up to 11,000 lb - a posted limit of 5 tons or less on the only way in is a drop, an unknown one is a caveat) and winter access: if DCNR states a closure period for the district's roads or campsites (Michaux: mid-December to April 1) that period is the season even when ReserveAmerica sells winter nights; where DCNR says only that roads are not plowed, the season stays year-round and the note says so; (4) the district phone: confirm the stored number on DCNR's page for that district; 888-727-2757 is the ReserveAmerica call centre and must be replaced by the district office (Tuscarora's is 717-536-3191); apply.py writes neither phone nor website, so flag them; (5) the ground itself on current imagery (sat.py) and the DCNR GIS campsite layer where it answers (it carries TrailerAccess and PicnicTables per site - report a 'No' against the booking page): is there a real pull-off, spur or old log landing a 23-ft motorhome can get into and level on, how long and wide, what surface, how far off the forest road, and whether the pin is on it - give old and new coordinates if the pin is more than ~30 m off the pad (for a multi-site entry the pin should sit on one of its sites, and say how far apart the sites are: sites more than ~1 km apart with their own access are a split to REPORT, not to make); under closed canopy say so plainly and do not invent a pad; (6) the access from the nearest paved road: name the roads, length and surface, and flag anything steep, narrow or signed against trailers; (7) what kind of place it is: a site in or beside a trailhead PARKING AREA is a numbered bay in a shared lot - say whether DCNR names it an ATV or snowmobile trail access (a camp that is OHV staging is a keep-or-drop question for the owner; report it, keep it with the fact in the note), and whether it is a horse-trailer area. Waterfront: all are stored not waterfront; check for a stream or pond within ~50 m with water_elev.py or NHD and a look at imagery - an upgrade is a LEAD only, reported and not written. Facilities: write potable_water, vault_toilets, flush_toilets, showers false and dump false where the page shows none. The note for each should say in plain impersonal words what each site is on the ground (landing, pull-off, lot, field), the road in and any caveat a booking page would not tell a visitor; it must not repeat what the fields already show (length, fee, hookups). policy_ref stays 'none' (no Pennsylvania state forest row exists). When ReserveAmerica throttles, slow down rather than sample: every site page must be read.
