The twelve are all ALBERTA (Canada) - the first full Alberta batch, so set up reusable notes in your scratch directory for the batches that follow. CANADA RULES: every money figure is in Canadian dollars - write fees with the schema's CAD marker (read docs/campground-schema.md for how `cad` is stored; never convert to US dollars); the private price gate is C$69 base nightly rate for a 23-ft motorhome (flag, do not drop; say whether a major park gateway is within 40 km); ownership values are `provincial` (Alberta Parks), `federal` (Parks Canada only), `local` (towns, counties, municipal districts, agricultural societies run for a municipality) and `private`; phone numbers stay in 10-digit North American form. ALBERTA PARKS entries (8849 Beaver Flats in Elbow Falls PRA, 8843 Beaver Mines Lake in Castle Provincial Park, 8891 Brazeau Reservoir PRA Reservoir Campground, 8930 Buck Lake PRA, 8994 Cold Lake Provincial Park, 8878 Cypress Hills Firerock Campground): read the park's albertaparks.ca information-and-facilities and camping pages and its advisories, and the booking site shop.albertaparks.ca (may refuse plain fetches - use bw.py under the browser lock; a refused page is UNREAD).
