Lessons from recent batches (IL/VT/WI): booking.fcfs values are always / after_cutoff (reservations close N days or hours before arrival and open sites then go walk-up - set reserve_until too) / some_sites / never; nothing else. fees.extra_person is an object. Some phones are stored as an empty string: give new phones as empty -> new. Check that stored inclusion_evidence, note and links describe THIS campground (one WI entry carried a Washington park's data); if not, give replacement text. An --AWH waterfront value is one signed --AWH in note or evidence. Season dates: give the 2027 date where it differs; a reservation window is not a season. Pins often sit at an office, entrance or day-use lot: place the camping loops. For any private, town or county park that looks seasonal, measure the nightly share and give the Google rating + count; when kept at 10-50% nightly, the note says so plainly ("Mostly seasonal: about N of its M sites are sold by the night"). A park whose only posted rate is seasonal fails test 2; under 1 site in 10 nightly fails test 4; a park that sells or leases lots fails test 5; a private park with no price anywhere and only a Facebook/directory page is a drop candidate; a mostly-seasonal private park needs a posted nightly rate or a booking site. Price gate: the test is the cheapest real RV night (an electric site a 23-ft rig can book) in ANY season - a park whose spring/fall or weekday RV nights are $50 or under passes even if summer weekends are higher (AWH rulings 2026-10-30, 2026-11-16). No-hookup or tent-only sites don't count as the RV night. Report the summer peak too; a park that passes only narrowly and has mediocre Google reviews (under ~4.5) is a drop candidate for the reviewer, not an automatic drop. Casino RV parking along a casino lot is not a campground. A fair association's campground stays local. A general campground with ATV trail access stays (just say so); an off-road park or OHV staging camp does not. Horse rule (any owner): horse sites mixed into the general loops with no separate horse area are a drop candidate. A source that refuses bots is not dead: keep its link unless it is actually gone. When a stored count, rig length or amperage has no source, say so and give the sourced value if you found one.
