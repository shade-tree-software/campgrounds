The twelve are all WISCONSIN (US; US dollars, the US$50 price gate for private parks). This is the first Wisconsin round of phase 7. Wisconsin batches mix owners: COUNTY and city campgrounds (local; the largest group - county park systems such as Clark, Marinette, Iron, Portage, Dodge, Waukesha, Marathon, Wood, Barron and Dane), Wisconsin DNR state parks, state forests and recreation areas, Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest and Corps of Engineers campgrounds (federal, mostly recreation.gov), and private parks. STATE (DNR): the state:WI policy row in /home/andrew/Dev/campgrounds/campground_policies.json already holds the GoingToCamp booking (wisconsin.goingtocamp.com; reservations mandatory but same-day allowed; 11-month window; the 2-night minimum that applies only to stays booked before May 15; 3-night holiday minimum), the $15 non-resident nightly surcharge, the $9.30 reservation fee, the vehicle admission pass and tax - do not write any of that per entry, and strip booking/fee/pass boilerplate from notes (the fields show it). Per DNR campground confirm from the property page (dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/<name>; replace dead or redirecting links, old -> new) and the GoingToCamp site list: the resident nightly price for a drive-in site and the electric surcharge (each property's own figures), which sites have electric (and amps), water/sewer, the site count, dump station, showers/flush toilets, season (record the published 2026/2027 dates; many loops close or go rustic in winter - say which stay open), and the per-site maximum equipment length (report how many sites take 23 ft+; a campground-wide cap under ~23 ft is a drop candidate; a uniform value on every site may be a placeholder - say so, and give the spread). DNR state forest and remote campgrounds may keep first-come or non-reservable sites the row does not cover: record what the property page says. Several DNR properties have a separate horse campground: the entry must be the general campground, and a horse area not explicitly separated from general camping is a drop candidate. FEDERAL: rec.gov / RIDB campsite catalog for fit, hookups, fees, season and first-come vs reservable (trust rec.gov over older notes and over the forest's own page); the federal:usfs / federal:usace rows cover generic agency rules. Small free or cheap primitive NF campgrounds stay on little evidence unless reports say closed or locked. LOCAL (county/city): the price gate does not apply, but give the current RV rate and source, who runs it, how it is booked (the county's own engine - often Campspot, ReserveAmerica-style county portals or a phone/office - or first-come/self-registration), hookups and amps, season, site count and max length, and whether it is a real public campground rather than a seasonal-lease park (many Wisconsin county parks rent most sites by the season: measure the nightly share, quote any wording that discourages nightly guests, and give the Google rating + count; a mostly-seasonal county park faces the long-stay tests like a private park). PRIVATE: price for a 23-ft motorhome (base rate under US$50, or a gateway within 40 km, a Good Sam Park at 10% off, or an operator-posted member discount; give the rate, date and source), Google rating and count (under 4.0 with 25+ reviews on any site = drop candidate), RV Life rating where shown, and the long-stay five tests (Wisconsin lake-country and Dells-area parks are often seasonal-heavy or resort-priced: measure how many sites sell nights; a waterpark/resort fee per person counts toward the price). Casino lots and casino RV parks that are parking areas are out; a real campground run by a casino is judged like any private park. Clearance: a posted height limit of 10.5 ft or more passes. Waterfront: Wisconsin lake campgrounds were swept with generous values; check each stored waterfront value on imagery and downgrade where the sites are not on open ground within ~50 m of the water (behind a tree band = a view value); an upgrade or a caveat needs site numbers placed on imagery (the campground map or reservation map numbers them). `coastal walk` is for Lake Superior / Lake Michigan only.
