The twelve are MISSOURI: eleven Missouri Department of Conservation (MDC) areas and accesses and one federal (USACE) campground. For every MDC area (13901 McKinny, 13808 McPike Access, 13854 Miami Access, 13762 Mineral Hills, 13841 Mokane Access, 13777 Moniteau Creek, 13795 Monkey Mountain, 13866 Montgomery Woods, 13822 Montrose, 13844 Moores Mill Access, 13984 Mound View Access): confirm the camping is at agency-designated drive-in sites, that a 23-ft rig reaches them (no 4WD, no low-water crossing or posted limit that excludes; a passable low-water slab is a caveat in the note, as John Alva Fuson), and that the pin sits on a camping spot or the drivable lot beside it (move it out of woods onto the lot where the GIS point is under canopy) - use MDC's area page, its 3CSR 10-11 area rules on the page (a 'camping by special use permit only' rule makes it a DROP, as Grand Bluffs; Rule 101 'not owned by the Missouri Department of Conservation' means the owner runs it - report ownership and the owner's phone, as Harrison County Lake; a bare 'Camping is prohibited.' line after the standard Rule 140 text, with camping listed under Things to Do, is a page artifact), the brochure (a 'walk-in campground' with no road or lot reaching it is a DROP, as Hamilton City Lake; a brochure camping season beats the stored year-round, as Huzzah), its area map PDF and its GIS 'Camping Sites' and 'Parking Lots' layers (gisblue.mdc.mo.gov Area_Feature_Layers FeatureServer 27 and 26). MDC camping is free until March 2027, first-come, 14 days in any 30, no hookups; the March 2027 $5 permit is already in the policy row - don't write it per entry. RULES (AWH 2026-11-09): camping on or beside a lot STAYS where MDC marks a designated camping point (GIS point or map camping symbol) there; where there is no designated point and the only place to camp is a boat-ramp or parking lot (or the brochure says camping is permitted in the parking lot, or 'not recommended as a camping destination'), it is a DROP (Cross Timbers, Henry Truitt, Girvin, Locust Creek; a GIS 'Designated Camping Area' point at a lot edge keeps it, as Lick Creek). Padless roadside camping that MDC designates stays with a caveat in the note. Where MDC labels a camp a horse camp, say whether it is plainly separate from a general camp; if the entry's camp cannot be distinguished from the horse camp, it is a drop; where the pin is on an equestrian-only camp and a general campground exists, move the pin there. Report clusters; propose a split only for a distinct camping area 1 km+ away on its own road or entrance with more than a single small lot, two or three entries at most (a shared entrance means no split), and BEFORE proposing one, check campgrounds.json for an existing entry within ~1 km of the split point; where the pin is on a minor spot and the area has a clear main camp, say so (move the pin there). Each MDC entry's stored phone: report whether it matches the area page's 'For more information call' number. 1888 Mill Creek (USACE, Table Rock Lake near Lampe, stored LAKEFRONT, no phone): rec.gov catalog and photos for season, fees, amps (from bookable sites), site lengths and phone; move the pin onto the loops if it is the rec.gov facility point; name which sites earn lakefront under the 50 m open-apron rule (at normal pool, not the imagery's) and downgrade to lakeview if none do.
