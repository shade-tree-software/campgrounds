## Minnesota patterns worth reusing

- **ATV wording is common in northern MN town campgrounds.** Babbitt (5603), Big Falls (5611), Bergamo (5515) and Birch Lake RV Park (5516) all let ATVs ride from the site to the trail. Babbitt and Big Falls are town campgrounds with no staging. Bergamo markets itself as an "ATV/OHV" base. Expect more of this on the Iron Range, near Bemidji and near the Koochiching trails.
- **ResNexus** (Jackson County, Babbitt): Incapsula refuses plain HTTP and the browser hits an hCaptcha, so treat it as UNREAD.
- **Firefly** (Bergamo) reads in the driver, with rate qualifiers visible. **WebTrac** (Wright County) is platform operator, with a url.
- **Campspot** (Big Falls): the park record reads, but availability gave CORE-0 errors with no 2027 dates open.
- **Local resident rate** (Wright County, Bertram): the base is the resident rate; non-residents pay +$8 as a surcharge.
- **Three Rivers Park District** (Baker): tax-included rates and an $8.25 reservation fee. A non-electric site takes "one tent and one RV", so it counts as the base.
- **City pages are the source for small towns:** Adams, Blue Earth and Big Falls. Several old notes said "reservable online" where the city takes phone or email only (Blue Earth).
- **Private parks:** Birch Lake RV Park is seasonal-only, with a waiting list and no nightly sale. Bluffview's rate PDF answered 404 and was not read.
- **rec.gov site photos settled a lakefront call under canopy** (Birch Lake 5454: sites 006, 012, 018 and 019).
- **The Big Falls riverview fell to the city's own rendered site plan:** the loop road and a tree band lie between every site and the river.
