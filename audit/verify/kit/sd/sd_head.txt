The twelve are all SOUTH DAKOTA (US; US dollars, the US$50 price gate for private parks). This is the first South Dakota round of phase 7 (the state was swept 2026-07-01, ids 5709-5879, plus the 2026-09-20 top-up 13153-13157 and later adds). South Dakota batches mix GFP state parks and recreation areas (the largest group, many on the Missouri River reservoirs), private RV parks (Black Hills tourist corridor, Rapid City, Sioux Falls, the I-90 towns), town/county campgrounds (local), and federal campgrounds (Black Hills NF, USACE on the Missouri, NPS at Wind Cave/Badlands, Buffalo Gap National Grassland). OWNERSHIP: `state` = South Dakota Game, Fish and Parks (gfp.sd.gov), including Custer State Park's campgrounds, the State Recreation Areas, Lakeside Use Areas (LUAs) and the SD State Fairgrounds; `federal` = USFS, USACE, NPS, BLM, Reclamation-run sites; `local` = town, city and county campgrounds, county fairgrounds, and tribal campgrounds on public land; `private` = commercial parks. Report any entry whose stored ownership breaks this. STATE PARKS: the state:SD policy row in /home/andrew/Dev/campgrounds/campground_policies.json already holds the Go Outdoors South Dakota booking (reservations.gooutdoorssouthdakota.com, platform operator), the 90-day window (Custer: one year, carried per entry), first-come always, the 14-night limit, 4 p.m. check-in/out, the Statewide Basic $16 / Modern $23 tiers, the $7 electrical fee, the $10 non-resident reservation fee and the $10/$15 park entrance licence - do not write any of that per entry, and strip booking/fee/permit boilerplate from notes (the fields show it). Per state entry confirm from the park's gfp.sd.gov/parks/detail/<slug>/ page (the authority: the sweep found the reservation portal's facIDs unreliable across agents) and its reservations.gooutdoorssouthdakota.com FacilityDetails.aspx?facID=<id> listing (check the facID really is this campground): which campground(s) the entry covers (several parks hold more than one campground area - measure each and name the one the pin and evidence describe), its own rate pair (basic/modern), electric sites (amps only if GFP or the engine publishes them - never guess), water/sewer, the site count, dump station, showers, reservable versus first-come (gfp.sd.gov/first-come lists the non-reservable parks), the 2026 season and any off-season camping with reduced services, and the per-site maximum equipment length where the engine shows it (report how many sites take 23 ft+; a uniform value on every site may be a placeholder). Lakeside Use Areas: gfp.sd.gov/lakeside-use-areas/ is the authority that camping there is a real fee campground, and those with a blank entrance-licence column carry fees.entrance 0. Links: about 20 state entries carry https://campsd.gooutdoorssouthdakota.com/, which no longer resolves - replace it with the park's gfp.sd.gov page and the facility's reservations.gooutdoorssouthdakota.com link (old -> new). FEDERAL: rec.gov / RIDB campsite catalog for fit, hookups, fees, season and first-come vs reservable (trust rec.gov over older notes and over the forest's own page; catx.py reads the public catalog without a key); the federal:usfs / federal:usace / federal:nps / federal:blm rows cover the generic agency rules. A forest or grassland campground with no recreation.gov listing is first-come only (AWH 2026-10-22). Small free or cheap primitive forest and grassland campgrounds stay on little evidence unless reports say closed or locked. ACCESS: never measure grades; an agency or operator statement that a road needs 4WD or high clearance (required or recommended), or that motorhomes/trailers are not recommended, is a drop candidate - quote it; a "steep" or "rough" road with no rig restriction is not. Custer State Park's Needles Highway and Iron Mountain Road tunnels have posted width/height limits: a campground reached only through one is a flag (say which tunnel and its posted clearance; EKKO is under 10.5 ft tall and about 8 ft 1 in wide with mirrors), but every Custer campground has a non-tunnel route - say which. A per-site or campground-wide length cap under ~23 ft is a drop candidate. NPS: Wind Cave (Elk Mountain) and Badlands (Cedar Pass via the concessioner, Sage Creek primitive) - confirm from nps.gov and the booking channel the 2026 season, site count, length limits and any closure; the price gate does not apply to federal campgrounds. LOCAL (town/county/fairgrounds): the price gate does not apply, but give the current RV rate and source, who runs it, how it is booked (online engine, phone, first-come honor box), hookups and amps, season, site count and max length, and whether it is a real public campground: many small-town South Dakota parks offer free or donation camping with a few electric posts - confirm designated campsites (not a parking-lot allowance) a 23-ft rig fits; a county fairground must be shown to rent ordinary nights outside events; a public campground with no agency or town page is kept when Google Maps lists it as a campground with a phone (the note then says to call that number to confirm it is open) or a recent Google review confirms overnight camping. PRIVATE: price for a 23-ft motorhome (base rate under US$50, or a gateway within 40 km via audit/price_research/gateways.py - Mount Rushmore, Badlands, Wind Cave, Custer SP and Devils Tower count; give the rate, date and source; a Good Sam Park at 10% off or an operator-posted member discount also passes), Google rating and count (under 4.0 with 25+ reviews = drop candidate), RV Life rating and price tier where stored, the price indicator and presence indicator, and the five long-stay tests. STURGIS: Black Hills private parks often post a separate Rally-week rate (early August) - the test is the ordinary nightly rate; a park that opens only for the Rally, or sells only Rally packages, is a drop candidate. Membership resorts (Thousand Trails, Coast to Coast, Rushmore Shadows, Hart Ranch) were skipped at the sweep - flag any that slipped in. ENERGY/PACKING-PLANT TOWNS (Sioux Falls, Huron, Aberdeen, Mitchell, Watertown) and the pipeline corridor: a park that sells mainly monthly to crews, or whose nightly sale is token, is a drop candidate - measure the nightly share and quote the wording. 'No posted nightly rate' alone is not a drop for an ordinary private park with a working website + phone (or Google 25+ reviews + phone) and an RV Life $/$$ tier. Clearance: a posted height limit of 10.5 ft or more passes. WATERFRONT (confirm, log a lead, or recommend a downgrade; never upgrade without named sites): South Dakota is inland - lakefront/riverfront/creekside/pond and the view values only, never coastal values. The sweep's waterfront audit UPGRADED 53 entries (21 lakefront), so treat every stored water value with suspicion: within about 50 m of the water with only open ground between (open grass or prairie counts as open ground); a cottonwood or tree band, cedars, marsh, riprap or a cut bank, a road or a trail between = a view value; a river or channel under ~30 m wide is creekside. The Missouri River reservoirs (Oahe, Sharpe, Francis Case, Lewis and Clark) and Angostura, Belle Fourche, Shadehill and Pactola draw down hard: measure to the high-water (full-pool) edge and say what lies between, with site numbers; Angostura's imagery vintages disagree by kilometres (the sweep rested Sheps Canyon on the GFP per-site map alone) - say which vintage you read. GFP's own GPS coordinates are often entrance or boat-ramp pins (Buryanek ~280 m off, Platte Creek ~400 m, Lake Carthage ~1.6 km). Read the pin against Esri imagery for all twelve and report one more than about 250 m off the campsites with corrected coordinates.
