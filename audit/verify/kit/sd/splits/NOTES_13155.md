# 13155 Sheps Canyon Recreation Area — split decision (2026-11-22)

**Decision: SPLIT.** The id stays on the electric main loop (Sheps Canyon Campground). Sheps South becomes one new entry.
`trips.campground_references(13155)` returned `[]`, so no trips are affected.

## Waterfront calls
**Main loop: lakeview (down from lakefront).**
- Sites 17E-20E and cabin C1 sit behind a continuous cottonwood band about 15-20 m deep, then a dirt track, then the bay channel. The channel was about 25-30 m wide in 2022, and 18E is about 30 m from it.
- No nightly site has open ground to the water at any pool level. The view is filtered, as at Pickerel 5751.

**Sheps South: lakefront, with a caveat naming the east-side shore row.**
- Shore row: 24, 26, 28, 30-33, 35, 37, 39, 41, 43, 45, 47, 50, 52, 54, 56, 59, 61.
- Distance from the pad ends to the vegetation/sand line (the high-water edge) is about 20-30 m.
- Distance to the 2022 drawn-down water is 45-62 m:
  - 35 ≈ 45 m, 37 ≈ 48, 39 ≈ 47, 41 ≈ 57, 43 ≈ 54, 56 ≈ 49, 59 ≈ 54, 61 ≈ 62.
- The ground between is open grass, a faint grassed two-track, a broken line of single trees with gaps, and then sand beach. There is no bluff, and the pads sit about 3 m above full pool.
- Clearest sites: 35, 37, 39, 41, 56. The inland row is a view.

## For AWH
- **Sheps South lakefront rests on the high-water measure.** It clears 50 m to the high-water edge, but not to the 2022 water at 41, 43, 59 and 61. The grassed two-track crosses between pads and shore.
  - Compare Hat Creek (5714 split), which was held at lakeview because today's water was 75-90 m away.
  - Here the drawn-down water is 45-55 m from the clearest sites, so I called it lakefront.
- **Sheps South max length 60 ft** is uniform across all 40 engine units and may be a template value. It is not the 75-ft default, so it was written.
- **The engine prices 1E, 21E and 22E (V1) at $37.** GFP's page says a flat $30. These are stored as nightly_high 30, which is $37 minus the $7 surcharge.
  - At Angostura, the $37 units were host sites. Here they are typed and sold as ordinary back-in sites.
- **Phone:** the GFP map prints 605.745.6996, while the page and the stored entry carry 605.773.2886. The phone was left unchanged.
- **Wifi** is listed park-wide on the GFP page. It was kept on the main loop and not asserted for Sheps South.
