North Dakota batches are mostly LOCAL (city park boards, county parks, the park boards and marinas around Lake Sakakawea, Devils Lake and Lake Ashtabula), with North Dakota state parks and recreation areas, federal campgrounds (Corps of Engineers on Lake Sakakawea / Lake Oahe / Lake Ashtabula and its other projects, Dakota Prairie Grasslands, Theodore Roosevelt National Park; recreation.gov where listed) and some private parks. STATE PARKS: the state:ND policy row in /home/andrew/Dev/campgrounds/campground_policies.json already holds the reserveNDparks.com (usedirect) booking, the 95-day window, the 14-nights-in-30 limit, the $6 reservation fee, the vehicle entrance permit and the change/cancel rules - do not write any of that per entry, and strip booking/permit boilerplate from notes (the fields show it). Per state park confirm from the park's page on parkrec.nd.gov and the reserveNDparks.com listing: each loop's nightly price by site class (primitive / standard / modern / premium; Saturday nights are $5 more on standard, modern and premium sites - record that on the entry), which sites have electric (and amps), water, sewer, the site count, dump station, showers, season (published 2026/2027 dates; many parks go to limited services Oct-May), and the per-site maximum equipment length the reservation system posts (report how many sites take 23 ft+; a uniform value on every site may be a placeholder - say so). FEDERAL: rec.gov / RIDB campsite catalog for fit, hookups, fees, season, first-come vs reservable (trust rec.gov over older notes and over agency pages); the federal:usace / federal:usfs / federal:nps rows cover the generic agency rules. LOCAL (city/county/park board): the price gate does not apply, but give the current RV rate and source, who runs it, how it is booked (online engine, phone, first-come honor box), hookups, season, and whether it is a real public campground rather than a seasonal-lease park (a city park or marina whose sites are mostly rented by the season, with a few nightly sites, faces the long-stay five tests like a private park; quote any wording that discourages nightly guests). Many small-town ND city parks are free or donation camping with a few electric posts: confirm they are designated campsites (not a parking-lot allowance) and that a 23-ft rig fits. PRIVATE: price for a 23-ft motorhome (base rate under US$50, or a gateway within 40 km, a Good Sam Park at 10% off, or an operator-posted member discount; give the rate, date and source), Google rating and count (under 4.0 with 25+ reviews = drop candidate), RV Life rating where shown, and the long-stay five tests. WESTERN ND OIL-PATCH RV PARKS are often workforce housing (monthly crews, man camps): a park that sells mainly monthly to workers, or whose nightly sale is token, is a drop candidate - measure the nightly share and quote the wording. Clearance: a posted height limit of 10.5 ft or more passes. Waterfront: Lake Sakakawea, Lake Oahe and Devils Lake levels swing by many metres; apply the open-apron rule at normal summer pool (measure to the high-water edge, not a drawn-down shoreline) and downgrade where sites sit behind a tree band or above a dam berm; an upgrade or caveat needs site numbers placed on imagery.
