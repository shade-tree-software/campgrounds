Pins tend to sit at the entrance or contact station: place the camping loops and give a move over 250 m. Read each park page's alerts box for road warnings, bridge weight limits (EKKO is ~11,000 lb GVWR: a 5-ton limit excludes, 6+ tons passes) and closures; a warning the agency posts is what counts (do not measure grades). LOCAL (city campgrounds): the price gate does not apply, but give the current RV rate and source, how it is booked, hookups, season, site count and max length. PRIVATE: price for a 23-ft motorhome (base rate under US$50, or a gateway within 40 km, a Good Sam Park at 10% off, or an operator-posted member discount; give the rate, date and source), Google rating and count (under 4.0 with 25+ reviews on any site = drop candidate), RV Life rating where shown, and the long-stay five tests (New England private parks are often seasonal-heavy: measure how many sites sell nights). Clearance: a posted height limit of 10.5 ft or more passes. Waterfront: check each stored waterfront value on imagery and downgrade where the sites are not on open ground within ~50 m of the water; an upgrade or a caveat needs site numbers placed on imagery (the park's Map & Guide or ReserveAmerica's map numbers them; RA's "Near Water" tag is only a hint).
