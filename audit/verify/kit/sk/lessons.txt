Lessons from recent batches (IL/VT/WI, and the Alberta round): booking.fcfs values are always / after_cutoff (reservations close N days or hours before arrival and open sites then go walk-up - set reserve_until too) / some_sites / never; nothing else. fees.extra_person is an object. Some phones are stored as an empty string: give new phones as empty -> new. Check that stored inclusion_evidence, note and links describe THIS campground (a Wisconsin entry carried a Washington park's data); if not, give replacement text. An --AWH waterfront value is one signed --AWH in note or evidence. Season dates: give the 2027 date where it differs (2027 dates are rarely out in November: write 2026 and say so); a reservation window is not a season. Pins often sit at an office, entrance, gate or day-use lot: place the camping loops. For any private, regional, town or RM park that looks seasonal, measure the nightly share and give the Google rating + count; when kept at 10-50% nightly, the note says so plainly ("Mostly seasonal: about N of its M sites are sold by the night"). A park whose only posted rate is seasonal fails test 2; under 1 site in 10 nightly fails test 4; a park that sells or leases lots fails test 5; a private park with no price anywhere and only a Facebook/directory page is a drop candidate; a mostly-seasonal private park needs a posted nightly rate or a booking site. Price gate: the test is the cheapest real RV night (an electric site a 23-ft rig can book) in ANY season - a park whose shoulder-season or weekday RV nights are C$69 or under passes even if summer weekends are higher (AWH rulings 2026-10-30, 2026-11-16). No-hookup or tent-only sites don't count as the RV night. Report the summer peak too; a park that passes only narrowly and has mediocre Google reviews (under ~4.5) is a drop candidate for the reviewer, not an automatic drop. A fair or exhibition association's campground stays local. A general campground with ATV trail access stays (just say so); an off-road park or OHV staging camp does not. Horse rule (any owner): horse sites mixed into the general loops with no separate horse area are a drop candidate; a mere amenity line or a single sold horse site in a general campground is not. A source that refuses bots is not dead: keep its link unless it is actually gone. When a stored count, rig length or amperage has no source, say so and give the sourced value if you found one. A Google rating shared with a golf course, bar or attraction is not the campground's alone - say so. Saskatchewan regional parks often bundle a golf course, a beach and a seasonal section: the campground's own nightly sites are what count. When an operator's own site refuses, still look for it on Let's Camp, Campspot, RoverPass, Facebook or Google, and say what the earlier captures under audit/sk_private/ and audit/price_research/ show. Saskatchewan sources that refuse (don't retry): reservation.pc.gc.ca (403). Overpass refuses (406): don't use mirrors. Never run git, even read-only.
