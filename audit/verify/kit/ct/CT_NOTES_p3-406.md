# CT notes, p3-406 (first CT batch)

- ctparks park pages are the freshest DEEP source (2026 + 2027 seasons, office phones). DEEP's fee table can disagree on non-resident rates (Macedonia: table $24, park page $17).
- ReserveAmerica Max Vehicle Length is often uniform 35 ft (Hammonasset, Hawes, much of Hopeville) - possibly a default; Salt Rock, Macedonia, Housatonic and Waramaug have real per-site spreads, with many 16-20 ft sites.
- RA site TYPE matters for fit: POPUP/TENT sites are not RV sites (Macedonia: only the 21 STANDARD sites suit an RV; West Thompson's nonelectric sites allow only pop-up/tent).
- RA 'Near Water' attribute is a useful pointer to the pond/river-side sites, but not proof of waterfront; the DEEP map + imagery decide.
- Many CT campgrounds had empty phones; ctparks lists a seasonal campground office number for each.
- state:CT policy row (RA, $9 fee, 2-night minimum, 14-night max) matched every park page read; Hammonasset and Rocky Neck state a 21-day maximum (already per-entry).
- Pins: Hopeville stored pin ~500 m off on the wrong side of the pond (fix 41.6015,-71.9269). Others within 250 m.
