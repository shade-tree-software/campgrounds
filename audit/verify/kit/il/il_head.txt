The twelve are all ILLINOIS (US; US dollars, the US$50 price gate for private parks). Illinois batches mix Illinois DNR state parks, state recreation areas and state fish & wildlife areas (the largest group), LOCAL campgrounds (county forest preserve districts, county conservation districts, park districts, city parks and fairgrounds), FEDERAL campgrounds (Corps of Engineers at Carlyle Lake, Lake Shelbyville, Rend Lake and the Mississippi/Illinois River lock-and-dam recreation areas; Shawnee National Forest; recreation.gov where listed) and private parks. STATE: the state:IL policy row in /home/andrew/Dev/campgrounds/campground_policies.json already holds the camp.exploremoreil.com (usedirect) booking, the three-days-out reservation cutoff with first-come sites after it, the 14-night limit, holiday minimum stays, the reservation fee, no entry fee, the resident-only discount and the 2027 fee and holiday-fee changes - do not write any of that per entry, and strip booking/fee boilerplate from notes (the fields show it). Illinois paused reservations Sept 1 - Dec 31, 2026 for its new 2027 fee schedule: price each IDNR campground from the 2027 schedule (dnr.illinois.gov 2027-camping-changes pages and fee PDF) by its site classes (Premium = electric + water + sewer; Electric; Non-electric; Walk-in/primitive - give each class's nightly rate present at that park) and say which year the stored rate reflects. Per IDNR campground confirm from the park's dnr.illinois.gov page and its camp.exploremoreil.com listing: site classes and counts, which sites have electric (and amps), water, sewer, showers, dump station, season (many IDNR campgrounds close or turn off water and showers Nov-April; some close entirely; give the published dates), first-come vs reservable sites, and the per-site maximum equipment length the reservation system posts (report how many sites take 23 ft+; a uniform value on every site may be a placeholder - say so). Several IDNR sites are equestrian or have a separate horse campground: the entry must be the general campground, and a horse area not explicitly separated from general camping is a drop candidate. State fish & wildlife areas often have only primitive Class C/D camping: confirm designated drive-in sites a 23-ft rig fits, not a parking-lot allowance. FEDERAL: rec.gov / RIDB campsite catalog for fit, hookups, fees, season and first-come vs reservable (trust rec.gov over older notes and over agency pages); the federal:usace / federal:usfs rows cover the generic agency rules. Shawnee NF campgrounds are often small, free or cheap, first-come and seasonal; free primitive NF sites stay on little evidence unless reports say closed or locked. LOCAL (forest preserve / conservation / park district, city, county, fairgrounds): the price gate does not apply, but give the current RV rate and source, who runs it, how it is booked (online engine, phone, first-come, honor box), hookups, season, and whether it is a real public campground rather than a seasonal-lease park (a park whose sites are mostly rented by the season faces the long-stay five tests like a private park; quote any wording that discourages nightly guests). Fairgrounds must be shown to rent ordinary nights outside events. Some county forest-preserve campgrounds restrict camping to county residents or to organized groups: say so and quote it. PRIVATE: price for a 23-ft motorhome (base rate under US$50, or a gateway within 40 km, a Good Sam Park at 10% off, or an operator-posted member discount; give the rate, date and source), Google rating and count (under 4.0 with 25+ reviews = drop candidate), RV Life rating where shown, and the long-stay five tests (many lake parks near Chicago and the Fox Chain O'Lakes are mostly seasonal). Clearance: a posted height limit of 10.5 ft or more passes. Waterfront: the Mississippi, Illinois and Ohio rivers run behind levees in many places - a campground behind a levee or below a dam berm earns no river or lake value even if the water is close; compare DEM to the levee crest. Carlyle, Shelbyville and Rend Lake pools swing several feet: apply the open-apron rule at normal summer pool. Lake Michigan (Illinois Beach State Park and nearby) is the only Great Lake: coastal walk rules apply there only. An upgrade or caveat needs site numbers placed on imagery.
