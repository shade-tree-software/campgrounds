The twelve are PENNSYLVANIA: DCNR state forest roadside campsites booked one site at a time on ReserveAmerica (Buchanan 15204, 15207, 15216, 15217, 15218, 15225; Delaware 15234; Michaux 15266, 15267, 15268 Piney Mountain Parking Area, 15269 Teaberry Parking Area; Tuscarora 15332). The Missouri and MDC passages elsewhere in these instructions do not apply to this batch; everything else does. These entries were added on 2026-10-10 from the site pages alone (the recipe, the cached per-site pages and the helpers are in audit/pa_sf_dispersed/: README.md, ra_sites.jsonl, sat.py for an Esri crop with the sites dotted, water_elev.py) and have never been looked at one by one, so this pass is the first real check. For EACH entry: (1) re-read every one of its ReserveAmerica campsiteDetails pages today (plain urllib works on pennsylvaniastateparks.reserveamerica.com; the www.reserveamerica.com/explore links may need the browser) and confirm the site still exists and is bookable online, its Site Access (Drive-In), the equipment categories it takes (an RV or trailer category must be listed), Max Vehicle Length (23 ft or more; under ~23 ft is a DROP candidate; say which sites of a multi-site entry fall short), driveway surface and grade, pull-through or back-in, and every line of site-specific text - 'tent and small RV', '4WD or high clearance recommended', 'camper trailers not recommended', equestrian-only, ATV-only or seasonal-use wording are all drop grounds under the recipe and must be quoted; (2) the fee as the engine quotes it for one night for a motorhome (the stored $10 with a $5 non-resident surcharge, plus any transaction fee - report what is mandatory), the booking window, same-day cut-off and maximum stay; (3) the forest district's own DCNR page and its current alerts for road closures, seasonal gates, timber sales, bridge limits (the rig weighs up to 11,000 lb - a posted limit of 5 tons or less on the only way in is a drop, an unknown one is a caveat) and winter access (state forest roads are not plowed - 'year-round' means bookable all year, so say whether the road is gated or simply unmaintained in winter and put that in the note, not the season); (4) the district phone (15332 stores the ReserveAmerica call centre 888-727-2757 - replace it with the Tuscarora district office from DCNR's page; confirm the others); (5) the ground itself on current imagery: is there a real pull-off or spur a 23-ft motorhome can get into and level on, how long and wide, what the surface is, how far off the forest road, and whether the pin is on it - move the pin onto the pad if it is more than ~30 m off, with old and new coordinates; under closed canopy say so plainly and do not invent a pad; (6) the access road from the nearest paved road: name it, give its length and surface, and flag anything steep, narrow or signed against trailers. Look hard at: 15268 Piney Mountain Parking Area (7 sites 002D-002J) and 15269 Teaberry Parking Area (5 sites) and 15266 site 022A at Peggy's Hill Parking Area - these are numbered spaces in or beside trailhead parking lots, popular with ATV and mountain-bike users in Michaux: say exactly what a booked 'site' is on the ground (a marked bay in a shared gravel lot, or its own spur), whether the lot is an ATV or snowmobile trailhead (a camp that is an OHV staging area is out of scope; a general lot that riders also use stays with a caveat), and whether overnight campers share it with day-use parking; 15225 (three Akersville sites on grass driveways - soft ground after rain is a caveat); 15218 SH017 (grass driveway); 15234 E012 Flat Ridge (30 ft) and 15332 site 071 (25 ft - the margin is thin, so read its text closely and measure the pull-off); 15217 SH008 Culvert Field (75 ft pull-through in a field). Waterfront: all twelve are stored not waterfront; check for a stream or pond within ~50 m with water_elev.py or NHD and a look at imagery - an upgrade is a LEAD only, reported and not written. The note for each should say in plain impersonal words what the site is on the ground, the road in and any caveat a booking page would not tell a visitor; it must not repeat what the fields already show (length, fee, hookups). policy_ref is stored as 'none' on all twelve: report whether audit data has a Pennsylvania state forest policy row they should point at, but do not invent one. These twelve were opened recently on the campground map by a viewer planning a visit, so accuracy on access and on what the ground really is matters more than speed.
