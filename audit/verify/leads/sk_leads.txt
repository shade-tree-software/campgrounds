Saskatchewan phase 7 leads (campgrounds found mid-pass that are not in the DB). One line per lead: NUM. slug: what the verifier saw. ALL MONEY IN CAD: write fees with "currency": "CAD"; the private price gate is C$69 (not US$50). Ownership (SK): `provincial` = Sask Parks only (provincial parks, provincial recreation sites, and recreation sites the province leases to an operator); `local` = every Regional Park and every town/village/RM/First Nation/non-profit campground on public land; `private` = commercial parks. Read audit/verify/kit/sk/sk_head.txt and audit/verify/kit/sk/lessons.txt for the province's sources and refusals. Before adding, search campgrounds.json by name AND within ~2 km of the pin; an existing entry = skip "duplicate of <id>".

1. sandy-beach-rp: Sandy Beach Regional Park, SK, 19.3 km N of Lloydminster on Hwy 17, 306-825-2092 (named on the Lloydminster city campgrounds page; found p3-521). Regional park: who runs it, how booked, rate, hookups, season, nightly share vs seasonal (Google rating + count), presence (AWH: a local campground with no current presence beyond directories is a skip).
2. rolling-greens-fairways: Rolling Greens Fairways RV park, ALBERTA side of Lloydminster (1.6 km W on Hwy 16, then 0.8 km N on Range Road 13; 780-808-1667; from the Lloydminster city campgrounds page; found p3-521). state "AB". Private: C$69 gate on the cheapest electric night, presence, Google rating, long-stay tests in full (golf-course RV parks are often seasonal).
3. woodlawn-boundary-dam: Woodlawn Boundary Dam campground, same operator as 9287 Woodlawn Regional Park, 8 km S / 2 km W of Estevan on Boundary Dam Reservoir; woodlawnregionalpark.com/woodlawn-boundary/, Let's Camp slug woodlawn-boundary-dam, 306-637-3837 (found p3-524). Local. Reservoir: measure to the high-water edge.
4. landing-view: Landing View private campground inside Saskatchewan Landing Provincial Park (old pins of 9205 sat on it; found p3-526; "looks seasonal"). Private: gate, presence, Google, long-stay tests in full; quote any wording that discourages nightly guests.
5. cactus-bloom: Cactus Bloom (park map spells it "Cactus Blume") private campground inside Saskatchewan Landing Provincial Park, between the Nighthawk and Riverside campgrounds on Hwy 261 (found p3-526 and the 9205 split; "looks seasonal"). Private: as lead 4.
6. cowan-lake-big-river: Cowan Lake Campground, Big River Regional Park's second campground (~53.828,-107.035, end of Main Street on Cowan Lake; 14 x 30 A + 2 x 50 A + non-serviced; 306-469-2232; found p3-527). Check it is a separate place from the existing Big River Regional Park entry (search the DB) - if it is the same campground, skip "duplicate".
7. ruby-lake-hudson-bay: Ruby Lake Recreation Site, Hudson Bay Regional Park's second site 15 km N of Hudson Bay east of Hwy 9 (2 x 30 A, 2 x 15 A, 5 unserviced; beach, boat launch; 306-865-4144; map townofhudsonbay.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Hudson-Bay-Ruby-Lake-Site.pdf; found p3-528). Local.
8. shamrock-rp: Shamrock Regional Park (RM of Shamrock, ~20 min NE of Gravelbourg; shamrockregionalpark.ca, 306-648-3594; found p3-529). Local regional park: as lead 1.
9. peck-lake: Peck Lake campground, Bronson Forest Recreation Site / Sask Parks park 290510 (41 electric sites per the SKPP park list; found p3-530). Provincial: the provincial:SK policy row covers booking/fees/permit; read its campgroundDetails.do park page and loop site lists. Provincial entries store fees.reservation_fee 12 and fees.entrance {11,11,vehicle_day} - but recreation sites carry no entry permit (the row says so).
10. little-shell: Little Shell Rec Site (Resort Village of Chitek Lake; letscamp.ca/camps/little-shell-rec-site; 9 non-electric sites; found p3-530). Local. Check 9 sites take a 23-ft rig.
11. anglers-trail-resort: Anglers Trail Resort on Lac la Plonge (may be the resort at 9185 Lac la Plonge Recreation Site's old pin; found p3-532). Private: a fishing resort - check it sells RV nights (not just cabins), the gate, presence, Google, long-stay tests.
12. dore-lake-fishn-camp: Dore Lake Fish'n Camp RV Park, dorelakervpark.ca, 54.62859,-107.39338, 306-832-2042, Google 4.8 (14) (found p3-533 near 9303 Michel Point). Private: as lead 11.
13. sawmill-bay: lodge/cabin complex on Sawmill Bay west of Hwy 167 (~54.6834,-102.0790; found p3-531; "probably cabins"). First establish whether any RV camping is sold; if cabins only, skip "not a campground".
14. candle-lake-causeway: unnamed trailer park + marina at the Candle Lake causeway (~53.7561,-105.2852; found p3-520; "looks seasonal"). Identify it; if it is a seasonal-lease or cottage-lot park, skip with the evidence; otherwise vet in full.
