Mantago Bay RV Park & Campground

Family operated since 1995. 

Where family and fun meet.

Nestled at the mouth of the Manigotagan River we are a private campground that is the destination for all of your outdoor fun needs. Boasting a mile long beach with a view across Lake Winnipeg, we are the ideal location for fishing, boating, swimming and any type of water fun. We have both daily and seasonal rentals that are backed onto our 160 acres of the Boreal Forest where you can enjoy peaceful trail walking, bird watching and exploring our beautiful Manitoba landscape. We pride ourselves in being an inclusive campground for families at any stage with our amenities including a shower house, laundry, playgrounds, life sized chess set, horseshoes and more.

Campground Information & Rules

All campers and guests must be registered and fees paid upon entry.

Check in is 3pm, check out is 11am. If you’d like a different check in/out time please check with management.

Speed limit throughout campground is 10km/hr and is strictly enforced.

Quiet time is between 11pm-8am. (No generators or loud parties/music, please be considerate of your neighbours).

Pets are welcome, dogs must be leashed and under control at all times as well as cleaned up after.

Limited ATV use in campground. Campground speed limits must be followed at all times. Stay on the road (do not drive on grass or over trees and bushes). Failure to follow rules will result in banning of ATVs.

Come join the family in Sunset Country! With over 100 seasonal sites (and growing) there is plenty of room for you and your friends. Our new seasonal campsites offer 30 amp service in an approximate 80ft x 80ft lot. 

Seasonal Rental
Rental Info

Yearly rental rate is $1701.00/year (including tax) with invoices sent out in March due April 15th.

We require a $150 deposit to hold a lot (which will be applied to rental).

Incoming trailers cannot be older than 15 years old. Once trailer is in the campground it is grandfathered in.

Includes winter storage.

You’re rental covers six persons admission into campground, additional guests are subjected to $3/day or $5/night fee.

Off season camping is allowed at a $10/day fee, use of electrical heaters discouraged.

We allow buildings on site no larger than 300 sq/ft. 

Lots are roughed in at 80ft x 80ft. You are able to make the lot your own, however any changes must be approved by management.

We require a site map of any plans for changes to lot.

Limited utility use throughout campground. Golf carts encouraged! ATV’s strictly prohibited from riding on beach.

No Excessive Noise between the Hours of 11 p.m. and 8 a.m. Please turn radios off and keep voices down

Beach is public area, it is for everyone’s use.

FAQs
I would like to bring my trailer to camp at Mantago Bay, but in one year it will be past the 15 year age limit. Will I have to replace it with a newer trailer?

No you will not have to replace the trailer! Once your trailer is on your lot and entered into the rental agreement it is ‘grandfathered’ in, meaning you will not have to remove it once it is past the 15 years. However, if you decide to sell your belongings on the lot and your trailer is older than 15 years you will have to sell it on the condition the trailer is removed, or remove it yourself and sell what remains. 

Do you have water hook-ups?

We do not offer direct water hook-ups currently. You are responsible for your own water. Most of our current campers have 250 gallon tanks that they have filled at the beginning of the year by a water truck and then top up their water from home. 

What are our options for sewage?

We do offer two central dumping stations, plenty of our campgrounds have the carts that hook up to a hitch to empty their trailer. You are also more than welcome to have a Port-A-Potty on your lot or an above ground sewage tank. We have a local truck come to pump out trailers/outhouses/tanks for a fee that you can contact!

I have friends coming for the weekend, can they set up camp in my lot?

If they are tenting, most definitely! They will still have to pay our nightly fee ($20/night) and register at the office but as long as all tents/vehicles fit on your lot it is allowed. Unfortunately we do discourage having trailers double up in lots. We always give our seasonal campers first priority if they have friends wanting to camp with us for a weekend and do our best to fit them in so please contact us if you wish to reserve a site.

There’s so much green space along the beach, can I set up a spot for just my family to use?

You are more than welcome to use the green space daily, we encourage people to make use of every inch since it is a public area. However, if you decide to keep belongings along the shore such as a bench or table, it does become public use. The entire beach and green space is for everyone to use, nobody ‘owns’ a section of the beachfront. 

I have two vehicles, a boat, a quad, etc… do you have parking/storage?

If your belongings don’t fit in your lot we unfortunately don’t have addition parking/storage. We do have campers who rent double lots for parking and guests.

My dog is my favorite family member, is he welcome?

Absolutely! All pets are welcome. They must be leashed, under your control and picked up after at all times. 

Is there a waitlist for seasonal rentals?

We currently have available sites so please contact us!

When can we come view the campground and choose our lot?

We typically have people come to view beginning in March. We don’t plow our roads in the winter so we do need to wait until it has melted off a bit before showing lots. When you do decide to come visit us please keep in mind we are typically two weeks behind Winnipeg weather wise so dress warmer and expect more snow! Also please ensure you contact us before coming so we are available to show you the lots for rent.
