OUR STORY

Sandy Lake Motel & Campground is located in picturesque lakeside town of Sandy Lake MB, less than 20 minutes from Riding Mountain National Park.

​

We offer the option of motel or campground accommodations, as well as an onsite laundromat and seasonal greenhouse. Local shops, restaurants, golf course and the beautiful beach are all located within walking distance.

​

Fishing, boating, snowmobile trails, seasonal hunting, golfing, bird watching, summer lake sports and beach fun-  Sandy Lake has everything anyone needs to enjoy every season Canada has to offer!!
