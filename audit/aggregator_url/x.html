Minnesota’s Best Casino Action

Minnesota’s best casino action is at Prairie’s Edge Casino Resort.

Just 2 hours west of the Twin Cities of Minneapolis and St. Paul you will find Prairie’s Edge Casino Resort in Granite Falls. 

Step onto our gaming floor and discover one of the few places in America where you can still play authentic coin slot machines, and the largest collection in the Midwest, a true throwback for slot enthusiasts. Alongside these timeless favorites, you’ll find the latest and most popular video slots, plus live blackjack, virtual roulette, video keno, and video poker, offering endless ways to play and win.

For your comfort, Prairie’s Edge features both smoking and non‑smoking casino areas, so everyone can enjoy the excitement in the atmosphere they prefer. Whether you’re chasing the thrill of a jackpot or enjoying a night of cards with friends, our casino delivers nonstop entertainment.

Guests must be 18 years or older to gamble.
