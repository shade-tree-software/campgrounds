Make a Reservation

Booking a Site: a 1 night deposit will be required to reserve a site.

​

Weekly Rate: Stay 7 nights for the price of 6 nights

​​​​

Cancellation Policy:

Cancellations must be RECEIVED AT LEAST 4 DAYS before arrival to receive refund of deposit, less a $25 cancellation fee and any associated booking fees. Cancellations with less than 4 days’ notice and no-shows will result in the loss of deposit. Sorry, no refunds or credits due to sickness, work schedules, or weather changes.

​

Other Information:

Rates for trailer sites include two adults per site.

Additional adults are subject to a charge of $5+HST per person, per night. 

Maximum of four adults per trailer site

One trailer or RV per trailer site ​

*Tent sites are not available for booking online

Tents are NOT permitted on trailer sites

Maximum of one tent per tent site

Maximum of two adults per tent site

 

Maximum of 2 vehicles are permitted per site

​

Two night minimum booking on weekends

Three night minimum booking on long weekends

​

Must be 19 years of age or older to rent a site.

​

Access to the sewage dumping station provided to our campers at no additional charge.

Non-campers are subject to a $20 charge.

**No seasonal sites currently available
