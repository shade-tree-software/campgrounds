The Story of Maison Touristique Dugas

1977 - Present

Ever since Martina Dugas opened our doors as a tourist house to the public in 1977, we have had one goal in mind: offering our guests , comfortable, affordable and all-around exceptional accommodation. We are now 3 generations in   and we have grown beyond comfy beds and delicious breakfasts. We have camping & cottages, self catering suites, and shared spaces made purely for the comfort & tranquillity of our guests. 

​

Find out more about how MTD has grown since our ancestor Henri Dugas built our beautiful house in the 1920's

Here at MTD, we offer a tranquil camping experience that allows you to get back to nature and enjoy the great outdoors. Our campsite is nestled in the heart of Acadia and provides a perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. We are situated near the beach and the Veloroute cycle path. So if you’re looking for a peaceful getaway this summer why not choose us!
