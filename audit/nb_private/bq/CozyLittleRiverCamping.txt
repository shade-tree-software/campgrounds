 Ocean River RV Resort
and Campground
Escape to Ocean River RV Resort and Campground for the ultimate camping experience in New Brunswick! Nestled in the picturesque tidal headwater region of the Tabusintac River, our resort offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Choose from spacious RV and tent sites with various amenities, including fire rings and picnic tables. Our newly renovated washrooms and hot showers are always complimentary, ensuring your comfort throughout your stay.

Enjoy a range of outdoor activities such as fishing, canoeing, kayaking, and paddle boarding. Relax by the sparkling pool, sip on local craft beer in the Clubhouse or patio bar, or explore our onsite camp store for souvenirs and camping essentials. With options for cabin rentals, bunkies, and RV accommodations, we cater to every camper's needs.

And, the onsite clubhouse and pavilion are perfect for all your special occasions from elegant weddings and lively family reunions to fun-filled birthday parties and professional corporate events.  Book your event with us today and create unforgettable moments in a picturesque location that caters to all your event needs.  Our venues and bathrooms are fully accessible!

Let Ocean River RV Resort and Campground be your gateway to the Acadian Peninsula - where memories are made and adventures begin!

Inspired by the surrounding nature, Ocean River RV Resort and Campground was designed to make your visit a unique, relaxing and adventurous experience. Our RV sites are generous, our amenities are varied and clean. Take a walk along the nature trails; stop and listen to birds providing background music, or explore the beautiful Tabusintac River in one of our kayaks or canoes available for rent at the Camp Store. The pool provides for a cooling break, and in the evening join fellow campers at the wine and beer bistro.

The Camp Store is your spot to find that item you forgot to bring, and is also stocked with confections, chips, beverages and extras.​Our team knows that traveling can be exhausting, and will do their best to make your stay easy and satisfying. We look forward to welcoming you soon!

Our Story

What to do after you retire?  Find a new adventure, of course!  Like many who jumped into a second passion after retirement,  our family (the Sweets) bought a campground and now enjoy the outdoors, our guests, and physical work.

​

David retired from politics in September 2021, where he served as a Member of Parliament for 16 years.  Almut was a financial controller for almost as many years, retiring in January 2020.  What to do next was our big question:  it had to be something we enjoyed, would allow us to travel and keep active.  With years of RVing as a family, it was not a big leap to consider being campground owners.  We acquired the campground in the fall of 2021 and over the winter and through the Spring worked on learning the operational side, while working on some of the improvements we felt were necessary.

​

Our son, Christopher, daughter-in-law, Annie and family joined us in the move from Ontario, looking for their next big adventure and some wide open spaces to raise their boys.  As a writer, Chris looked forward to working hard in the campground during summer, and writing and supply teaching during the off season.  Making good use of snow bound days and an inspiring environment his first novel, The Boy in the Canvas, was published in March 2022, quickly followed by two novellas, Something Sweet and Satisfaction Guaranteed, and the epic Orchid Room in 2024.  Annie who retired from her long time position at John Deere spends time with their boys and hones her creative talents as our photographer and facebook expert.

​​

For all of us, the lifestyle adjustment from urban living in Hamilton was a welcome one: from white pristine snow in the winter to hot summer days by the sparkling Tabusintac River - adjusting was not difficult at all.

Our Team
DAVID SWEET

David served as a Member of Parliament for Ancaster-Dundas-Flamborough-Westdale (2006 - 2015) and Flamborough-Glanbrook (2015 - 2019).  Throughout his tenure he held  positions as chair and vice chair of various committees,  served as chair of the National Conservative Caucus, and Shadow Minister for International Human Rights and Religious Freedom.   Prior to politics, David had early career successes in real estate sales, then manager of over 100 agents, before stepping out on his own to provide consulting, speaking, and strategic development to high profile clients. His entrepreneurial skills, success in strategic development and leadership gifts then landed him to serve as CEO of a national charity.

ALMUT SWEET

Almut spent early years raising five children, then returned to a career in financial and systems management as Director of Finance and Administration of a large charity,  then fifteen years as the  financial controller and governance officer at Cardus, a think tank dedicated to strengthening the ways in which society’s institutions can work together for the common good. 

CHRISTOPHER SWEET

Christopher studied Broadcast Journalism and screen writing and has  spent over twenty years providing excellence in customer service finding himself in leadership and management roles in each of his work places.  He has, to date, published two novels and two novellas, contributed to several published anthologies, and has two new novels in the works. Christopher's books are in stock at the camp store and on Amazon, and  check out his website for more information on his creative journey:  www.authorchristophersweet.com

Chris enjoys spending time with his wife, three boys, three dogs, and cat when he's not working or writing.

ANNIE SWEET

Annie worked as a manager in customer service for six years prior to a twelve year career with John Deere.   When the opportunity to move to New Brunswick came about, she was eager to start this new life with her family. With an education in marketing and graphic design, Annie created our logo and is instrumental in managing our social media presence and any design elements we may need ... even while raising three high energy boys! Annie has always been creatively inclined in music, art, and theatre and met Chris on stage where they took lead roles in numerous musical stage productions including Young Frankenstein, Heathers, and The Little Mermaid. The rest, they say, is history!
