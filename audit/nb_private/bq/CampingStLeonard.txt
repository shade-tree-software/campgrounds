St. Leonard, New Brunswick is a small town located in Canada's eastern province. It is known for its rich French heritage, outdoor recreation opportunities, and stunning natural beauty. Here are some things that someone visiting St. Leonard could do: 


Explore Grand Falls Gorge - The Grand Falls Gorge is a stunning natural attraction located just a short drive from St. Leonard. The gorge is formed by the Saint John River and offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape. 

Go fishing or boating on the Saint John River - The Saint John River is one of the largest rivers in eastern Canada and offers excellent fishing and boating opportunities. You can rent a boat or hire a local guide to take you out on the water.

Visit the New Brunswick Botanical Garden - This beautiful garden is located just outside of St. Leonard and features over 80 acres of gardens, walking trails, and educational exhibits. 

Take a hiking or biking tour of the area - There are numerous hiking and biking trails in the area that offer stunning views of the local landscape. You can rent a bike or go on a guided tour to explore the trails. 

Visit the local museums and cultural centers - St. Leonard is home to several museums and cultural centers that showcase the history and heritage of the area. Some of the most popular include the Acadian Historical Village, the Village Historique Acadien, and the Musée du Madawaska. 

Attend a local festival or event - St. Leonard and the surrounding area are home to several festivals and events throughout the year. Some of the most popular include the Festival des Rivières, the Madawaska River Run, and the Grand Falls Potato Festival. 

These are just a few of the many things that someone visiting St. Leonard, New Brunswick could do. Whether you're interested in outdoor recreation, cultural activities, or simply exploring the natural beauty of the area, St. Leonard has something to offer everyone.
