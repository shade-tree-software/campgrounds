About us
Danny and Jo-Anne Lagacé

Rooted in the vibrant Acadian culture and breathtaking landscapes of New Brunswick, we take pride in sharing the warmth, creativity, and authenticity of our region with travelers from around the world.

Since 2019, Old Church Cottages has redefined luxury stays for adults by blending distinctive architecture, boutique-style comfort, and thoughtful privacy. In 2023, we expanded with Appalaches Domes & Spa, a nature-inspired retreat designed to help guests unwind, reconnect, and experience the beauty of the Appalachian region.

Our story continues in July 2026, with the addition of a new accommodation that will once again bring history, design, and tranquility together in a uniquely Acadian way.

As bilingual Acadian hosts, we’re passionate about breathing new life into forgotten spaces and crafting soulful places to relax, connect, and celebrate local culture. Danny’s craftsmanship and Jo-Anne’s dedication to hospitality come together in every detail — from restored churches to inspired domes — offering guests tranquil, romantic settings designed to inspire connection and peace.

We look forward to welcoming you and sharing the stories, warmth, and spirit of our Acadian community.

What is Lodgify?

Lodgify is an all-in-one vacation rental platform that helps hosts and property managers manage bookings, automate guest communication, sync calendars across channels, process payments and build a direct booking website—all from one place.

Who is Lodgify for?

Lodgify is built for independent vacation rental hosts and property managers. Whether you manage one property or a growing portfolio, Lodgify gives you the tools to save time, avoid double bookings and earn more.

Does Lodgify connect to Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com?

Yes. Lodgify's channel manager syncs your calendars, rates and availability across major booking platforms—including Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com—so everything stays up to date automatically.

Can I get direct bookings with Lodgify?

Yes. Lodgify includes a website builder that lets you create your own direct booking website. Guests can browse your properties, check availability and book directly without third-party commissions.

Do I need technical skills to use Lodgify?

No. Lodgify is designed to be easy to use, even if you're not tech-savvy. The website builder uses drag-and-drop templates, and features like automated messaging and payment processing are simple to set up.

Can I try Lodgify before committing?
