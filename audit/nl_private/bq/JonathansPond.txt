Campground Amenities
EVERYTHING YOU NEED FOR THE PERFECT OUTDOOR GETAWAY. FEATURING RUSTIC CHARM PLUS MODERN CONVENIENCES.
Spacious Campsites

Well-maintained sites with fire pits, picnic tables & garbage cans. Perfect for tents and RVs.

Pondfront Access

Direct access to pristine waters for swimming, fishing, and kayaking. Also a small private beach which is perfect for relaxing, or having a picnic.

Modern Facilities

We provide Wi-Fi Internet from the check-in facilities and laundromat.

Hiking Trails

A 4 Km scenic walking trail through  forests. Evening views of Jonathan's pond are spectacular!

RV Hookups

173 sites have both electrical & water servicing. The other 21 have water only. For your safety, all our water is chlorinated. We also have a limited number of full-service 3-way hookups, as well as a dumping station which is accessible to everyone. Call for availability.

Showers & Washroom

For your convenience, all sites access to our comfort station which offers hot showers + washrooms.

About Our Campground

Located on Route 330, 15 minutes from Gander. Our campground is a 250 acre natural environment campground. Campsites are nestled among beautiful white birch trees. It is the perfect place for a vacation. We are in an ideal location for tourist visiting central Newfoundland who wish to experience our culture, partake in event functions or just visit historical sites such as the Aircraft Museum,  Barbour House, see Icebergs off Twillingate, as well as enjoying access to all the Town of Gander has to offer.
