THE GROUNDS
A Haven for the Open Road

Pull in, hook up, and settle into the heart of central Newfoundland. Our family-built RV park sits among tall trees with full-service sites, an in-ground heated pool, and crackling fire pits under big northern skies. Centrally located between St. John's and Gros Morne, it is the perfect basecamp for whale watching, iceberg spotting, hiking, and every adventure the island has to offer.
