Custom Tours

If our themed tours aren't what you are looking for, check out one of our custom tours. We can design a tour to accommodate adventurers of all ages and skill levels. We have a six seat ATV, where your taken on a guided tour where tales of the land are told to you by a True Pirate. This ATV takes up to four riders, great for couples, families, seniors, physically challenged & non drivers.

Getting Here

South to North from the Marine Atlantic Ferry in Channel-Port-aux-Basques, travel East on the TCH (Trans Canada Highway) 115 Km, exit on Route 404 to Robinson’s and McKay’s.

North to South from Stephenville Airport, travel West on the TCH 65 Km, exit on Route 404 to Robinson’s and McKay’s.

North to South from Deer Lake Airport, travel West on the TCH 167 Km, exit on Route 404 to Robinson’s and McKay’s.
