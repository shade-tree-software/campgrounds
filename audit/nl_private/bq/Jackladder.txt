The Jackladder
Stay, Eat & Play at
The Jackladder!

We’re located in the heart of Western Newfoundland on Route 430 (also known as the Viking Trail) and just 10 minutes from the wonders of Gros Morne National Park.

Situated across from Big Bonne Bay Pond with its mountain fed waters, our neck of the woods is a great place for boating, fishing, swimming, mountain biking, ATVing, side by sides, hiking and just relaxing in the summer. Be sure to make time to check out the famous Lomond Sinkhole!

In the winter months, The Jackladder is ideally situated along the vast groomed snowmobile trail network traversing Newfoundland’s Great Northern Peninsula. The snow is plentiful and the adventures are too! We offer guided tours to the two most popular snowmobile destinations in Newfoundland – Western Brook Gorge and the Lomond Sink Hole.

Stay

A good night’s sleep is waiting for you at The Jackladder. Our peaceful, clean and beautiful chalet accommodations and RV Park are conveniently located on Route 430 (also known as the Viking Trail), just 10 minutes from Gros Morne National Park and 20 minutes from Deer Lake Regional Airport. It’s the ideal home base for your vacation in Western Newfoundland.

 

Settle in at your private chalet or set-up camp in our RV park and head out on countless adventures in all seasons. In summer, you’ll have a 5-minute walk to the beach at Big Bonne Bay Pond and in winter you’ll have direct access to Western Newfoundland’s snowmobile and ATV trail network.

Before You Book


Things to know before you book your stay at The Jackladder.

A deposit equivalent to one night (based on double occupancy) is required to hold your accommodation. The deposit will be non-refundable in the two (2) weeks prior to arrival. For all cancellations we charge $30.00 per booked resource. This covers cost of bookings, credit card charges and banking fees.
Children under 16 years old stay for free
Daily maid service is available for an additional fee
Towels and linens are included for each guest for the stay period. Additional linens are only provided through the optional daily maid service. Please call us to arrange for this service.
Meal plans through our on-site restaurant are available for an additional fee. Please call us to arrange for this service.
Bookings which include weekends are subject to a minimum three night rental during our Winter Season (January to April). All other bookings are subject to a two night minimum.
