Kilmory
YMCA Camp

Nestled in the Piper's Hole River Valley, just outside Swift Current in Placentia Bay, Kilmory YMCA Camp offers waterfront cabin accommodations surrounded by forest, mountains, and natural beauty.
Just 25 km off the Trans-Canada Highway, Kilmory provides private, self-catering accommodations with access to recreational activities year-round, making it an ideal destination for family vacations,
group retreats, and outdoor getaways.

Kilmory's 20 log cabins are situated on individual treed lots, offering privacy and waterfront views overlooking the river estuary and surrounding mountains. Each cabin features its own driveway and private deck complete with a barbecue, picnic table, and seating, providing the perfect setting to relax, reconnect, and enjoy the natural surroundings.
