Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping 
Oceanfront lots with a million-dollar view

Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping is located in the picturesque community of Rattling Brook and King's Point on Newfoundland’s north-east coast, nestled on 1600 ft of oceanfront shoreline. Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping offers an unforgettable retreat tucked away amongst mountainous cliffs, mature forests, breathtaking oceanfront views, one of the region's most spectacular 800 ft waterfalls, and world-class hiking trails

​​​

We offer premium-size oceanfront camping sites. Sites are fully serviced with 50 amp power, spring water, sewer, full size washrooms with showers and laundry facilities, and access to Wi-Fi. Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping property is able to host campers looking for seasonal RV lots, as well as nightly camping. Each site services both RV's and tenting guests.

 

Designed for luxury views, and comfort in mind, our waterfront campsites provide generous room to relax, with convenient access to many hallmark hiking trails and waterfront access for kayaking and paddle boarding.

Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping 
Oceanfront lots with a million-dollar view

Wake up to the sound of the ocean and enjoy Newfoundland’s natural coastal beauty right outside your RV.

EXPERIENCE OCEANFRONT CAMPING ON OUR UPPER AND LOWER DECKS

Upper Deck Oceanfront Sites

Our Upper Deck Oceanview Hillside sites overlook the bay and feature a beautiful backdrop of mountains and mature trees. These spacious lots offer a peaceful setting where guests can take in the natural beauty of the surrounding landscape. With sweeping views of the ocean, this area is also an ideal spot for whale-watching enthusiasts hoping to catch a glimpse of whales passing through the bay.

​

The Upper Deck is home to our gatehouse, laundry facilities and full-service - mobility friendly washrooms.

Lower Deck Oceanfront Sites

Our spacious Lower Deck Oceanfront Beach sites have direct access onto the 1600 ft oceanfront, offering beautiful views and convenient access to the beach.​These sites are ideal for kayakers and paddleboarders who want to launch directly from the shoreline, as well as guests who enjoy peaceful morning walks along the beach.​

Premium Amenities

All sites, on both the Upper and Lower Decks, include 50-amp power, fresh spring water and sewer connections.  Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the campground as part of our premium guest amenities.​

 

The Upper Deck is home to our gatehouse, laundry facilities and full-service - mobility friendly washrooms.

Getting Here / Directions 
Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping

Finding Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping is easy!

​

Follow the instructions listed below - Google Maps is not great at at bringing you to our park. 

 

We are located just minutes from King's Point, Newfoundland.

 

GPS navigation works well for our address: 

​GPS coordinates: 49.61308N, 56.17745W​

 

​​

​

​

​

​

​

 

 

 

 

 

 

Directions:

 

If you're travelling from the Trans-Canada Highway (Route 1):

Take Route 390 toward Springdale/King's Point. Turn onto Route 391 North toward King's Point.

​

Continue into King's Point on Main Street (Highway 80).

​

Stay on Main Street, and you'll find Rattling Brook Falls RV Camping on the right-hand side on your way towards the village of Rattling Brook. 

 

The drive from Route 1 to the campground is approximately 19 km (about 18 minutes).
