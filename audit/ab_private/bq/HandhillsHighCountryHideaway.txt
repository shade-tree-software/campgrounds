Experience the beauty of prairie nature at Handhills High-Country Hideaway. Nestled in the heart of a large property of nearly 70 acres, our 30 acre campground offers a tranquil escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. With large treed lots with plenty of space between neighbors you'll love our park and your stay.

Whether you're a seasoned adventurer or a first-time camper, our amazing private sites, comprehensive list of amenities and activities ensures that your stay is comfortable and enjoyable. We have Seasonal sites available for move in on May 1st 2027. Immerse yourself in the stunning landscapes of the Hand hills, hike through scenic trails, and parklands or simply relax by the campfire under the dark starry sky. We are located 20 minutes east of Drumheller on Hwy 851 at the top of the Handhills. Book your stay today and embark on an unforgettable private, or group camping experience.

Weather & Nature

We are blessed with some of the most beautiful weather in Southern Alberta and are generally about the same temperatures as Drumheller year round

We regularly see Moose, Deer, Eagles, Woodpeckers, Bats and Owls living in the park, and inform and encourage people to take photos when we see them. In Fact some call it a bird watchers dream and have seen as many as 42 bird species in a single weekend.

Our nights are free from light pollution so we have incredible star gazing and Meteor shower viewing. As well as late season Auroras in August and September.

Our park is built into the existing natural area and we strive to protect the animals that call our property home.

Our Story

The Hansen Family built Handhills High-Country Hideaway RV and Tenting campground in the late 90's, in a poplar grove on generational family farm land and operated for about 8 years. The Sopp family then purchased campground in the early 2000's and ran it for the next 18 years. During all that time the original Handhills High-Country Hideaway has remained a favorite among Travelers and locals alike. 

Now the Dollimount Family has purchased the park and Reopened the park to the public, for all to enjoy! 

As newer owners but 16 year tenants at HHH we have been camping seasonally here and have watched our children grow into adults and learn many new skills at the park. The park reopened on May 1 2024. HHH will again be fully open to the public with fully serviced RV weekend & Seasonal Sites. As well as a number of beautiful private tenting sites. Rental RVs and Rental Inflatable Tents.
