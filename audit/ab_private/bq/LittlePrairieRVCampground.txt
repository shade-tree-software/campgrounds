Little Prairie RV Park
Business typeAccommodations
AddressTownship Rd 912, North Star, AB
Phone780-836-3150
Little Prairie RV Park is located just 1 km south of the Town of Manning. Enjoy scenic views, and quiet and relaxing surroundings. Little Prairie RV Park offers 30 & 50-amp power, portable water, sewer, free firewood, horseshoe pits, and a playground! Little Prairie can also accommodate group camping and events, chat with them today to book your family reunion or special celebration!

Camping

There's nothing quite like camping in Alberta...unless you're camping in northern Alberta! Enjoy 15-17 hours of daylight in the summer months, and when night falls, it's time to gather around the campfire and spend the evening star gazing; perhaps the northern lights will come out and put on a show!

Remember to be fire smart! Ensure that you check the Alberta Fire Bans website, or download the app, to see if there are any fire restrictions or bans in place before having a campfire. 

Note that the County does not own or operate any of the campgrounds below. Please contact them directly for bookings or further facility information.

https://www.campspot.com/park/condy-meadows-golf-course-and-campground?adults=2&children=0&pets=0&fbclid=IwY2xjawJaUedleHRuA2FlbQIxMAABHUWGb-VkuwUsHuEv5CTbaqbcsKQRFJYKdXoUo2mijxcifSsETGxuZ23mqA_aem_yUSfVWgOKbUEG9F5cch2qA
Condy Meadows
Camp and golf to your hearts content at the Condy Meadows campground in Hotchkiss! Take a break from hot dogs and treat yourself to lunch at the clubhouse
https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/north/figure-eight-lake-pra/information-facilities/
Figure Eight Lake
Figure Eight Lake Provincial Recreation Area offers camping, birding, fishing, hiking, canoeing, kayaking and more! 
https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100049127597619
Leddy Lake
Located in Weberville and minutes away from the Town of Peace River, Leddy Lake offers camping, interpretive trails, beach volleyball and much more!
https://maps.app.goo.gl/XqhR9qDETWgC4ezD6
Little Prairie RV Park
Situated minutes north of Manning, Little Prairie RV Park offers full service site for short or long-term stays!
https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/north/notikewin-pp/information-facilities/camping/notikewin/
Notikewin Provincial Park
Unplug and unwind at Notikewin Provincial Park! While the camping spots are unserviced, the beauty serenity of makes up for it!
https://dixonvillelifeag.wixsite.com/dixonvillelifeag/facilities
Shady Lanes
Nestled in the hamlet of Dixonville, Shady Lanes offers bathrooms, showers, and close access to the community walking trail!
https://albertaparks.ca/parks/north/twin-lakes-pra/information-facilities/
Twin Lakes Provincial Park
Set up camp at Twin Lakes and then hit the walking trail and make your way around the lake!
https://mightypeace.com/places/the-creek-campground/
The Creek Campground
Stay a night, week or month at the Creek Campground! Powered sites, showers, group sites and more await!
