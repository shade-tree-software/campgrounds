Welcome to
Mink Lake RV Park & Campground
Plan Your Adventure
Your Family’s Gateway to Nature and Tranquility

Our campground provides a range of options for outdoor enthusiasts, perfect for families and group gatherings. Here, you can reconnect with nature, unwind by the lake, and enjoy quality time with loved ones in a tranquil and welcoming environment.

What sets us apart is our dedication to creating a warm, community-focused experience. Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, you’ll find plenty of opportunities to relax and explore. Create lasting memories at Mink Lake RV Park & Campground, your go-to destination for a peaceful retreat in Alberta’s great outdoors!

Where Relaxation Meets Adventure

The friendly and attentive staff at Mink Lake RV Park & Campground are always ready to assist you, ensuring your stay is as enjoyable and stress-free as possible. Whether you’re looking to take a refreshing swim, cast a line for some fishing, soak up the sun, head out on the water for a boating adventure, or simply relax and unwind on the beach, we’ve got everything you need for a perfect getaway.

At Mink Lake RV Park & Campground, we strive to create a welcoming atmosphere where every guest can find their own version of outdoor bliss. Whatever your plans, we’re here to make them happen!

Our Facilities

NON POWERED CAMPING SITE

Reconnect with nature at our family and group-friendly non-powered camping sites. Enjoy a rustic outdoor experience with plenty of space to relax and make memories by the lake.

Book now!
POWERED CAMPING SITE

Enjoy the great outdoors with the convenience of 15 AMP and 30 AMP powered sites. Perfect for RVs and campers, our sites provide comfort and easy access to the lake and amenities.

Book now!
HALL RENTAL

Our hall rental accommodates up to 150 guests for seated dining—perfect for weddings, reunions, parties, or special events. The space includes a complete kitchen, dance floor, stage, private shower and washrooms, plus a front deck with picnic tables and a fire pit.

Book now!
SEASONAL LOTS

Enjoy the beauty of Mink Lake all season long! Our spacious lots offer easy lake access, modern amenities, and a welcoming community.

Book now!
