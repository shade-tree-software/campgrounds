Welcome to the lovely 11 Bridges Campground.  This campground is 40+ years old and situated in the Drumheller valley, along the Rosebud river.   We have 200+ year old cottonwood trees, providing a true nature experience and a secure home to hundreds of song birds.

Find us 5 mins south of the town of Drumheller.  The area offers Coal mine tours, Suspension Bridge, 11 Bridges to Wayne, the famous Dinosaur Royal Tyrrell Museum, and much more.

We offer a full service shower house, laundry, sani dump, town water, dish wash station, emergency AED, and more. 

Please enjoy your stay in one of the beautiful treed sites or the rustic cabins.  Remember to check out the T-Rex fire pit, free for all campers to use and share!
