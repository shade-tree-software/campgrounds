ABOUT RIVERBANK GOLF & RV PARK
ABOUT US

Riverbank Golf & RV Park is a premier golf resort destination located just 1.75 hours south of Fort McMurray, Alberta. Situated approximately 2 km south  of Wandering River, the park offers a thriving summer playground for Fort McMurray Oil Sands residents. Come experience the perfect getaway for golf enthusiasts and nature lovers alike.

FACILITY
GOLFING EXCELLENCE

Tee off amidst breathtaking views and meticulously maintained greens at our picturesque golf course. Whether you’re a seasoned golfer or just starting out, our 9 hole course promises an enjoyable round for all skill levels. Enjoy the tranquility of the river nearby as you perfect your swing and soak in the beauty of the surrounding landscape. 

RV PARK OASIS

Park your worries and unwind in our spacious, fully serviced Seasonal RV Park. With 50 sites available, Riverbank provides a comfortable and welcoming environment for RV travelers seeking a home away from home. Each site offers full hook-ups and ample space to relax, ensuring a peaceful stay surrounded by nature. 

OUTDOOR RECREATION

 Beyond golf and RV relaxation, Riverbank invites you to explore a variety of outdoor activities. From hiking trails to fishing spots, there’s no shortage of ways to enjoy the natural beauty of  Wandering River, AB, Canada. Gather with friends and family at our outdoor seating areas, perfect for picnics and evening gatherings under the stars. 

RIVERBANK DINING DELIGHTS

 Our restaurant offers a delightful dining experience that perfectly complements your day on the greens or stay at our RV park. Our menu features a variety of mouth-watering options, from hearty breakfasts to energize your morning, to delicious lunch specials that satisfy every palate. Don't miss our handcrafted cocktails, expertly mixed to refresh and relax you after a round of golf. Whether you’re a local resident or visiting from Fort McMurray, enjoy a family-friendly atmosphere where great food, scenic views, and warm hospitality come together in Wandering River Alberta.

Riverbank Golf and RV Park

Welcome to Riverbank Golf and RV Park, your ultimate destination for relaxation, recreation, and unforgettable outdoor experiences. Nestled alongside the Wandering River, our park offers a perfect blend of nature and leisure, catering to both avid golfers and RV enthusiasts.




RV Park Amenities

Our park features 50 fully serviced seasonal sites, each designed to provide you with the comfort and convenience you deserve. Experience the charm of Riverbank by reserving a spot in our unserviced half acre dry camping area. This area is ideal for those who enjoy a more rustic camping experience, allowing you to immerse yourself in the great outdoors without sacrificing proximity to our amenities.

For Full Service Seasonal Site inquires, send us an email at (riverbankgolfandrv@gmail.com).



Future Developments

Exciting developments are on the horizon at Riverbank Golf and RV Park! We are thrilled to announce the upcoming Dome Development project, which will enhance our facilities and provide guests with even more recreational options. Stay tuned for updates as we embark on this exciting new chapter.
