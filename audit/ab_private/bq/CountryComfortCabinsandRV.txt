WELCOME TO COUNTRY COMFORT CABINS & RV!
AN EXPERIENCE AS UNIQUE AS YOU

Get away from everyday life, relax, and rejuvenate your soul.

We are a family run campground with a goal of providing a unique camping experience. Our cabins are set up and waiting for you – just bring your food, bedding, and personal items. Or you can roll in with your RV or tent and set up in one of our private, tree-lined sites. Also, our group area is open, with more upgrades to come next season.  Lots of space for family reunions, staff gatherings, groups functions or weddings.

Most of our sites are non-serviced (no power/water/sewer) but:

– there is a main wash house conveniently located on-site, with running water, flush toilets, showers and outlets to charge your devices.

– solar lighting and potable water is also supplied in all of the cabin units.

Check in, set-up, and settle in…to the country comfort.

How to Find Us

We are located at 2325 Twp 572, Barrhead County

 

From Edmonton
West on Yellowhead Highway
North on Highway 44 (26 miles/42 km)
West on Highway 651. Go 16 miles/26 km (thru Busby and past Dunstable School)
South on Range Road 24, south 2 miles/3 km
East at intersection on Twp 572
Entrance is ¼ mile on the right
From The West
North on Highway 43
North on Highway 33 (past Rich Valley School)
East on Twp 570 (Lac La Nonne Road)
North at first intersection and follow road around south tip of Lac La Nonne
East on Twp 572 (past St. Peter’s Church)
