Reservations
To make a reservation for camping, please register by clicking the registration tab. You will not know your row, lot, or meter number put in a 0.  When you arrive, email a picture of your lot number and power meter reading to thelewykpark@gmail.com.​​ Payment is not required until arrival.​

Rates
$35 per day  plus GST plus power.  If you provide us with a picture of your driver's license, we will not require a security deposit.  We prefer payment by etransfer but accept credit card payments.
Amenities while Camping
All stalls are fully serviced with 20,30,50 amp electrical, year-round water and sewer
Coin-operated laundry and showers
Large RV sites to allow for 45-foot trailers
Activities in the camping area include:

Fishing
Snowmobiling
Hiking 

ATV'ing
 Site Seeing
 Canoeing

The rules are for the safety and enjoyment of all campground guests.  Please be considerate of others and respect the property.  Enjoy your stay!

- All fees must be paid in advance.  Late charges are $25 per day from your move in date.
- Power is charged at .40/kw/hr & includes the Atco line charges.

- A picture of the electrical meter in your site, including the site number, must be emailed to thelewykpark@gmail.com upon arrival and upon departure.  Always pay attention to the amount of power you are using, electric heat and A/C units use a lot of power.
- Children must be supervised at all times. Parents are fully liable and responsible for the conduct of their children.
- The speed limit is 15 km/hour everywhere in the campground.  Violation may lead to eviction.
- Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am. No visitors after 10 pm. Respect your neighbours. Please keep voices, radios, music, and TVs low enough so as not to disturb your neighbours. 

​- All garbage must be bagged and put in the secure, animal resistant garbage compactor located across from the blue laundry building.  Do not put household garbage in bathroom or laundry room cans. 

- Any recyclables left in bags or bins in stalls will be collected and donated to students. 
 ​​



-  No porches, decks, additions or additional roofs are allowed.  
- Dogs are allowed if arrangements are made with management. Dogs must be on a leash at all times and owners must pick up after their dogs.  
- We are not responsible for any lost, damaged or stolen property.
- No smoking or outdoor footwear permitted in laundry or shower facilities or the admin building.
- Do not repair, service, or wash vehicles or RV's at your site or anywhere in the campground. 
- ATV's are allowed in the Park.
-  Occupant must clean up site on vacating and anything left on site after 30 days, including camper, will become the property of Lewyk Park and disposed of in a manner decided by management.
- Our campground does NOT go under the landlord and tenant act!!! Evictions are immediate, when you sign in you agree to these terms. All agreement are day to day only! You may leave with one days notice and vice versa.​


Breaking the rules may result in eviction.  Management has complete discretion. Rules may change at any time without notice. Thank you! 

Fire bans may be in effect. Please check local forestry signage.
