Why Choose Bar W RV Resort for Your Next RV Camping Adventure?

Bar W RV Resort is more than just an RV campground – it’s a lifestyle. Located just 2 hours from Calgary, 2 hours from Edmonton, and 45 minutes from Red Deer, our resort is easily accessible for campers across Alberta. Here’s what makes us stand out as one of the top RV parks in the region:

Unmatched Location

Situated on Buffalo Lake, one of Alberta’s most pristine lakes, Bar W offers 1,000 meters of sandy beach, clear waters, and stunning views. You’ll love the convenience and beauty of our Central Alberta location.

Modern RV Sites

Our spacious 60×40 foot RV sites come with full hookups, including 50-amp power, city water, and underground sewer systems. Plus, enjoy no-charge winter RV storage.

Family-Friendly Amenities

From our water park complex to pickleball courts and playgrounds, BarW is designed for campers of all ages.

Community Focus

At Bar W, we’re more than just an RV campground – we’re a community. Join us and connect with fellow campers who share your love for the outdoors.

Explore Our RV Camping Amenities

Bar W RV Resort is proud to offer some of the best amenities in Alberta for RV camping enthusiasts. Here’s what you can enjoy during your stay:

Water Park Complex: Featuring a heated swimming pool, waterslide, splash park, and wading pool, our water park is perfect for families.
Private Beach: With 1,000 meters of sandy shoreline, our beach is ideal for swimming, paddling, and building sandcastles.
Playground & Pickleball Court: Keep the everyone entertained with our playground, climbing dome, and pickleball court.
Dog-Friendly Spaces: We even have one private dog beach for your furry friends!
Convenience Store: Stock up on essentials, grab an ice cream, or pick up firewood for your campfire.
Other Activities: 8-hole Mini Golf, Beach Volleyball, and Basketball
