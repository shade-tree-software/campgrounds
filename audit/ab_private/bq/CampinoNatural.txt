CAMPING AREA

Campino Natural is situated on a gated lot and provides grassy parking stalls for our guests. A common area fire pit is available for your use. We have a capacity for 14 RV's (up to 50 feet) and a grassy space for up to 10 tents..

No Footprint Left Behind

Camping stalls are not equipped with power or water/sewer. In addition to providing a serene experience for our guests, we strive to reduce our impact on the land by offering dry stalls only.

Our campground also includes a full service washroom, outdoor shower, a common area with fire pit and a picnic table.

NATURE TRAIL

Our beautiful 1.5km trail is open as soon as the leaves are in full bloom. Pack some comfy shoes and enjoy a beautiful walk through our nature trail while taking in the sights of the resident birds and wildlife.

We not only provide a tranquil space to roam and enjoy, but we also have bug spray at the gate if you need it!
