Corridor Campground
Contact this Campground
505 Elevator Road, Beaverlodge, AB, Canada

The Corridor Campground has 25 fully serviced sites nestled amongst the trees and gardens of an old farmstead. All this sites have municipal water and sewer and 30 amp power. Also 8 power and water sites are available for tenters and RVs. Max 2 pets welcome pr. site.

Full Service Rates 45.00 + gst pr. day 265.00 + gst pr. week plus power usage 800.00 + gst pr. 4 weeks plus power usage

Power and Water Rates 40.00 + gst pr. day

Tenting Rates 25.00 + gst pr. day

The Corridor Campground is right in the town of Beaverlodge within a short walk of many amenities such as Food stores, pharmacies, liquor stores, hardware lumber and outdoor stores. Services also include gas stations, mechanical shops, veterinarian, upholstery, seamstresses and more all in walking distances of the Corridor. The campground itself has fire pits at each site, except some that can not be used due to fire risk. For these sites, there will be a common fire pit made available. We also have Garden spots available for the people who are going to be with us all summer. A horseshoe pit, volleyball net for the older kids and a sandbox and the natural woods for the younger ones, this should help keep them occupied. We also have a 5G tower very close, so we have excellent cell coverage, just in case your kids have devices. PUBLIC WASHROOMS and SHOWERS. I hope if you choose Corridor Campground for a night a day a week or more that you enjoy your stay and enjoy the trees that were planted here on this farmstead decades or more ago.

Note: For stays of 14 nights or more, a refundable electricity deposit is required. Any electricity usage during your stay will be deducted from your deposit, and the balance will be refunded. If electricity usage is more than your deposit, this will be added to your invoice.

Refund Rules

For stays 28 nights or longer, cancellations made 7 or more days before arrival will receive a 97.2% refund, minus a $100 cancellation fee and the non-refundable reservation fee.

The 2.8% credit card processing fee is not refundable (which results in the 97.2% refund total).

For stays 28 days or longer, cancellations made 2 days or more before arrival will receive a 97.2% refund, minus a $200 cancellation fee and the non-refundable reservation fee.

The 2.8% credit card processing fee is not refundable (which results in the 97.2% refund total).

For stays 7 nights or less, cancellations made 2 days or more before arrival will receive a 97.2% refund, minus a $5 cancellation fee and the non-refundable reservation fee.

For stays 7 to 28 nights, cancellations made 7 or more days before arrival will receive a 97.2% refund, minus a $45 cancellation fee and the non-refundable reservation fee.

Let’s Camp’s Refund Policy

Let’s Camp reserves the right to refund reservations that were canceled due to extraneous circumstances, such as public health orders or government mandates.

Aside from these circumstances, Let’s Camp’s individual campgrounds have final authority over all cancellations and refunds, and are not required to give refunds for inclement weather, wildlife, air quality, fire bans, or other inconveniences.

Modification of Terms

Let’s Camp and its individual campgrounds reserve the right to update their refund policies at any time in accordance with this provision.
