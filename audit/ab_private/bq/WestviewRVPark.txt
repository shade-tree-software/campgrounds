After seven wonderful years, we are sharing that Westview RV Park and Willow Inn have been SOLD and are closing our doors effective immediately.

​

All of us here have truly enjoyed the hard work of bringing this property to life and watching it grow into what it has became. We are proud of the improvements made and the awards earned along the way, but most importantly, we are grateful for the people we have met and the relationships we formed. Those friendships and memories will always mean the most to us.

​

As Dr. Seuss said, “Don’t cry because it’s over. Smile because it happened.” This chapter is ending much sooner than expected, and it’s a reminder to appreciate the places and people that matter while we have them.

​

We understand this news may come as quite a shock—not only that the property was for sale, but that it sold so quickly (the sale was finalized December 23rd) and also that the property will no longer be operating as a public business. 

​

We are truly sorry for the disruption in peoples plans and lives. While we cannot control the direction the new owners choose to take, we are truly excited about their plans and wish them nothing but success as they move forward.

​

To answer a few questions you may have, we don’t have any firm plans of what the next chapter in our lives will look like. But two of the six of us are retiring for good (this time) and the remaining four are taking some time off before deciding what’s next. These coming weeks will be very busy as we pack up our lives and closing this chapter, so if you have questions, please feel free to email, message, or text if you have our number. We will respond as we are able to during this very busy transition.

Thank you for being part of our journey and for sharing in this place with us.

Love you all, and be blessed.
